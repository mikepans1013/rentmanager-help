# Zego

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/MicroContent/Resources/MicroContent/Improve-Search/zego.htm

Rent Manager is designed to help property management companies succeed. By using integrations with Zego , you have access to a seamless connection to modernized resident payments and utility management. These features eliminate manual workarounds and processes that slow you down. Zego is a Rent Manager -preferred partner for digital rent payments, resident billing, and managing utilities; the combined integrations and features increase property performance and improve the resident experience.

 More Information

 To use Zego in Rent Manager , you must set up an account with Zego and enter that information into Rent Manager . For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 The following features are available with Zego :

 Feature
 Description

 CashPay

 Allows tenants and prospects to make payments in cash at select retail locations within the United States. For more information, refer to Zego CashPay .

 ePay

 Allows the automatic transfer of money either from ePay payments you made or that you receive from others in the real world and deposits them into the actual bank account(s). For more information, refer to Set Up Zego ePay .

 Lockbox

 Allows tenants to make physical payments that are mailed to a P.O. box managed by Zego . For more information, refer to Set Up Zego Pay Lockbox .

 PayDirect

 Allows you to make outgoing payments for various property management expenses to owners and vendors. For more information, refer to Zego PayDirect .

 Resident Billing

 Allows you to automate the process of billing your tenants rent and utility charges. For more information, refer to Zego Resident Utility Billing .

 Utility Expense Management

 Allows you to offload the property-level utility billing, invoicing, and vendor payment process to Zego and Rent Manager . For more information, refer to Zego Utility Expense Management .

 Zego Resident Payout

 Allows you to refund security deposits directly to tenants' bank accounts via ePay , eliminating the need to issue paper checks. For more information, refer to Zego Resident Payout .
