# Smart Suite Powered by Orion AI

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/MicroContent/Resources/MicroContent/Improve-Search/smart-suite.htm

Using the Smart Suite powered by Orion AI , Rent Manager can streamline a variety of accounts receivable (AR) and accounts payable (AP) processes. Smart Suite features can automate data entry for bills, invoices, and checks, process reimbursements or company credit card expenses with rmAppSuite Pro , and synchronize transaction history with your real-world banks and cards.

 More Information

 These features are licensed and must be purchased. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 Bank Sync

 The Bank Sync feature allows you to seamlessly sync and reconcile real-world transactions with your Rent Manager data using AI-assisted technology.

 For more information, refer to Set Up Bank Sync .

 Smart Bills

 The Smart Bills feature allows you to eliminate the hassle of manual invoice entry by using Orion AI to automatically capture relevant data from invoices and categorize them. Smart Bills also includes an intuitive review process, allowing you to ensure the accuracy of transactions before posting them as actual bills.

 For more information, refer to Smart Bills .

 Smart Check Scanning

 Rent Manager allows you to quickly scan checks and money orders from tenants or prospects, and Orion AI automatically populates transaction details.

 For more information, refer to Scan Tenant/Prospect Checks .

 Smart Receipts

 The Smart Receipts feature makes tracking expenses a breeze. Using rmAppSuite Pro , simply snap a photo of a receipt, and Orion AI will extract the relevant transaction details.

 For more information, refer to Smart Receipts .
