# Smart Receipts

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/MicroContent/Resources/MicroContent/Improve-Search/smart-receipts.htm

Rent Manager 's Smart Receipts feature allows you to easily streamline your expense management process. With this feature, you can upload images of receipts via rmAppSuite Pro  allowing Orion AI to extract the relevant data and autofill transaction details. Smart Receipts also includes the ability to view receipts that were uploaded, but not posted through a dashboard tile. Additionally, you can link a user to a vendor so it's clear what user is being reimbursed for personal charges.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 How It Works

 Upload images of your receipts through rmAppSuite Pro , then Orion AI extracts the relevant information and autofills the transaction details in Rent Manager . Once the details are filled in, all you need to do is verify that the details are accurate and record some other quick information about the transaction. You can classify charges as either company credit card charges or reimbursements based on the credit card information in Rent Manager and quickly create the corresponding credit card transactions or bills.

 Add Receipts from rmAppSuite Pro

 You or your technicians can snap a photo using rmAppSuite Pro to capture a receipt in the field as soon as a purchase is made using the Smart Receipts feature and then easily classify the purchase as either a company credit card charge or a personal charge that needs reimbursement. Once a photo is taken and you've selected the entities related to the receipt, Orion AI extracts all the information and autofills the transaction details. Then in Express , verify that the details are correct, fill in additional information like a memo, and save the receipt for later or post it directly as a credit card transaction or as a bill to reimburse the employee who made the purchase.

 Next Steps

 After uploading receipts using the Smart Receipts feature, there are few additional things you can do. The following pages allow you to upload and manage Smart Receipts.

 Page
 Description

 Smart Receipts

 The Smart Receipts page lists any receipts uploaded in Rent Manager or through rmAppSuite Pro . For more information, refer to Smart Receipts Page (Page) .

 Smart Receipts Review

 Once receipts are uploaded, review the information Orion AI pulls from the receipt. For more information, refer to Review Smart Receipts .

 Vendor Details

 If you have employees that make purchases with a personal payment method, their user account can be linked to a vendor making it seamless to submit a reimbursement via rmAppSuite Pro .

 More Information

 You can use aliases to improve Smart Receipts matching by identifying additional property addresses and vendor names that display on the invoices and matching them to existing Rent Manager records. Aliases can be automatically created through the Smart Receipt Review page, or added manually to ensure accurate matching. For more information, refer to Manage Aliases (Page) .

 Link Credit Card Numbers

 On Credit Card type general ledger (GL) accounts, you can add the last 4 digits of the corresponding company credit card number(s). These numbers are referenced during Smart Receipt uploads to determine if a company credit card was used for purchase. For more information, refer to Chart of Account Details (Page) .
