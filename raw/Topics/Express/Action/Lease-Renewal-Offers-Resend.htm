Resend a Lease Renewal Offer

To ensure you give your tenants the best options for renewing their leases, Rent Manager allows you to resend renewal offers to tenants. This saves you time because you don't have to go through the process of creating a new renewal offer from scratch just to change the expiration date. Renewal offers can be resent only if the status is Offer Sent and the delivery method of the offer is a Blue Moon lease or a signable document published to Tenant Web Access (TWA).

Option 1: Resend a Renewal Offer Individually

Rent Manager allows you to resend a lease renewal to an individual tenant. For example, if your tenant needs more time to decide if they are going to renew their lease, you can change the existing offer expiration by resending the initial offer. To resend a lease renewal offer to a single tenant, you can do so from the tenant's card on the board.

To resend a renewal offer to an individual tenant, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Lease Renewal Board.
    The Lease Renewal Board page displays.

  2. In the Sent Offer column, on the card of the renewal offer you want to resend, click  arrow_forward Resend Offer.

  3. In the Select Signers column, select or deselect the signers needed to sign the renewal offer.

  4. Optionally, in the Offer Expiration field, change the date the renewal offer expires.

  5. Click Send.
    The renewal offer is sent to the tenant again.

Option 2: Resend Renewals Offers in Bulk

Rent Manager additionally allows you to resend lease renewal offers in bulk. For example, if you plan to offer a universal extension to lease renewal offers for all tenants, you can resend the offers in a single action. Tenants that do not have the Resend Offer option available on their individual card or do not have a valid email address are excluded from this bulk action.

To resend renewal offers in bulk, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Lease Renewal Board.
    The Lease Renewal Board page displays.

  2. In the Sent Offer column heading, click arrow_forward Resend Offer.
    The Bulk Resend Offer pop-up displays.

  3. Optionally, to update the expiration date for the new lease renewal offers, select Yes. Then, in the New Offer Expiration Date drop-down, select a relative date or a Custom Date on which the resent offer expires. If No is selected, each new offer’s expiration date is the same as its original offer's.

  4. Click Next.

  5. In the Select Signers column, select or deselect the signers needed to sign the renewal offer. Only signers with a valid email address can be selected.

  6. Click Send.
    The renewal offers are sentto all respective tenants again.
