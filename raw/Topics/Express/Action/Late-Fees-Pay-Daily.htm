Pay Off Daily Late Fees

Daily (also called per-day) late fees accrue each day a tenant is delinquent on a rent charge. Daily late fees are not included when posting late fees in Rent Manager, and are instead posted automatically at the moment you receive a full payment from a delinquent tenant. Rent Manager examines the payment date and uses that to calculate the late fee amount. Once a full payment is received and applied to the rent charge(s), Rent Manager applies a new late charge to the tenant's transactions for the calculated amount of all the per-day fees.

Tenants can view their pending per-day late fee charges in Tenant Web Access (TWA). Rent Manager users can view the pending per-day late fee charges from the Add Payment pop-up in the New Late Fees column.

More Information

Daily late fees are calculated only on rent charges. The charge types counted as rent charge types are established on each property's details page. For more information, refer to Property Details (Page).

Partial payments do not stop daily late fees from accruing; daily late fees are applied until the rent is paid in full.

To process daily late fees while accepting a payment, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant.
    The tenant's details page displays.

  2. On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Add Payment.

  3. In the Payment Info tile, enter the following information:

    Field Description

    Amount

    The full dollar amount of the payment received.

    Date

    The payment method used for this payment: MO (money order), CC (credit card), Cash, or enter the check number.

    If the option Process via ePay is selected below, this field displays ePay.

    Memo

    A brief comment about this payment. Payment memos display in the Comment column of the tenant's list of transactions.

    Reference #

    The date on which the payment was received. This date determines the per-day late fees calculated in the Open Charges section's New Late Fees column.

  4. In the Open Charges section, enter information into the available columns:

    Column Description

    New Late Fees

    By default, this column displays the calculated amount of per-day late fees based on the date the charge was posted, the payment Date above, and the daily late fee settings established for the tenant or property. You can manually enter a different amount.

    Current Payment

    The total amount of the charge and accumulated daily late fees combined.

    Current Payment = Original Amount + New Late Fees

    Pay?

    If checked, the payment is allocated to this open charge.

  5. In the Payment Summary tile, you review the payment amounts, opt to print a receipt, or process the payment via ePay. For more information, refer to Add a Payment.

  6. Click Add Payment.
    The Confirm Late Charge Assessment pop-up displays.

  7. To proceed with making the payment and adding the late charge, click Yes.
    The payment and late fee charge are added to the tenant's list of transactions.
