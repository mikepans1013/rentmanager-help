Add a Credit

You can add a credit for your tenants or prospects to apply discounts on their open charges. You can add a credit to a current tenant for referring a friend to your property. Or, you can add a credit to a prospect to represent a discount for a move-in special. To add a credit, add a charge with a negative amount to the account (e.g., -200.00). Credits can be preallocated to apply automatically to open charges of a specified charge type, or credits can be left unallocated and apply to any open charges.

To add a credit to a tenant or prospect account, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants|Prospects and select a tenant or prospect from the list.
    The details page of the tenant or prospect displays.

  2. On the Transactions tile, click Add Charge.

  3. Enter the following information about the credit:

    Field Description

    Charge Type

    The charge type associated with this credit. If you need the credit to impact your expenses, select a charge type linked to an expense GL account. If you need the credit to count as income, select a charge type associated with an income GL account.

    Unit

    The unit with which the credit is associated. By default, this is the unit associated with the first lease listed on the tenant's Leases tile or the unit listed in the prospect's Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile displays.

    Date

    The date that the credit posts to the account.

    Amount

    The amount of the credit to apply to the tenant or prospect account. To add a credit, you must enter a negative value in the Amount field (e.g., -200.00).

    Comment

    A note describing or explaining the credit. The comment displays on the View Transactions pop-up in Rent Manager, the tenant or prospect's Transactions page in Tenant Web Access, and any printed statements.

    Preallocations

    When you enter a negative value in the Amount field, the Preallocations section displays.

    To create an unallocated credit that can be applied to any open charge, leave the Preallocations section blank. Unallocated credits are applied to open charges or when new charges are added for this tenant or prospect.

    To preallocate the credit to charges of a specific charge type, click Add Item and enter the charge type(s) and amount(s) for the allocation. Preallocated credits apply automatically to the chosen charge type for open charges or the next time a payment is made that pays charges of the chosen charge type. Additionally, they can be manually applied to open charges when recording a new payment.

    Reference #

    An optional name or number to track and organize the credit in Rent Manager.

  4. Click Save.
    The credit is added to the tenant or prospect account and displays as a negative value in the View Transactions pop-up. The credit amount is also subtracted from the tenant or prospect's Balance Due amount on their account scoreboard.
