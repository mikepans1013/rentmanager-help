12.241100 Release Notes

The release begins rolling out on November 14, 2024.

New Features

Use the new Prospect Leasing Board in Express to improve your leasing process and boost conversion rates!

Express

The Prospect Leasing Board was added to Rent Manager Express, giving you a central hub to manage and monitor your entire leasing process. Utilizing a Kanban-style board, this new resource provides a panoramic view of your prospect landscape, enabling you to instantly identify where each prospect stands in their leasing journey, organized by your existing prospect stages. The Prospect Leasing Board also makes it easy to spot when a prospect’s Next Contact is due, and flags when an action is overdue, ensuring timely engagements that keep momentum going. What’s more, you can customize prospect card details for improved insight and execute critical actions (write letters, send emails, run and view screenings, etc.) directly from the board.

For more information, refer to the Prospect Leasing Board (Page) topic in Express help.

To make automating you leasing workflow easier, the following prospect stage triggers were added:

  • Apply Now Application Started, which automatically updates the prospect’s stage when their Apply Now application status is In Progress.

  • Expected Move In Date Set, which automatically updates the prospect’s stage when a Move In date is entered on their account.

  • Signable Document Pending Acceptance, which automatically updates the prospect’s stage when their published signable document status is Pending Acceptance.

  • Appointment Added, which automatically updates the prospect’s stage when a saved appointment is linked to their account.

Additionally, for stage advancements linked to specific documents, a Signable Document(s) field was added for the Signable Document Published trigger, which allows you to select from a list of available signable documents.

Lastly, the Prospect Stage Change automated notification was added, which allows you to automatically send an email or text message to prospects, property managers, and/or Rent Manager users when a prospect’s stage changes.

Simplify invoicing with Smart Bills, powered by Orion AI.

Express

The Smart Bills feature was added to Rent Manager Express, allowing you to significantly streamline your invoice management process. Simply upload your invoices to Rent Manager via a PDF or image format, and Orion AI will extract the relevant data and create draft transactions automatically. Even better, vendors can now send email invoices directly to a dedicated inbox, allowing for seamless integration into your workflow without any extra steps. Smart Bills also includes an intuitive review process, enabling you to ensure the accuracy of transactions before posting them as actual bills.

More Information

Smart Bills is a licensed feature—for more information, please contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

For more information, refer to the Smart Bills (Page) topic in Express help.

Enhancements

Standard Management Fees have been significantly enhanced in Rent Manager.

Express Rent Manager 12

To make handling your management fees more intuitive and user-friendly, some exciting enhancements were made to Standard Management Fees! These updates include the integration of partial pass-throughs, seamless fee collection when credits apply to charges, and the ability to deactivate fee templates for both standard and advanced structures. Additionally, the Management Free Breakdown report was updated with an Include Pass Throughs option. Changes were also made to how data displays on the Management Fees Templates page, the Fees Setup pop-up, and the Convert wizard, improving organization, spacing, and legibility.

The Management Fee Template Listing report was also added to Rent Manager with this update, which allows you to quickly identify the management fee templates used for each property.

Streamline communication with your tenants when they are charged a late fee with the new Late Fee Posting automated notification!

Express Rent Manager 12

The Late Fee Posting automated notification was added, which allows you to automatically send notice letters via email or text message to tenants when late fees are posted using task automation or the Post Late Fees page.

Easily manage multiple bills for a single order with Rent Manager’s enhanced purchase order (PO) tools!

Express Rent Manager 12

To eliminate the hassle of creating multiple purchase orders and ensure the accurate tracking of billed quantities in Rent Manager, you can now effortlessly link multiple bills to a single PO. Additionally, new system preferences and user privileges were added, preventing over-payments and safeguarding your finances.

Additional Express reports were updated with a modernized design and improved export formatting!

Express

An additional 28 reports (including 9 payables reports) received our new streamlined design in this release. Headers that include the report’s name, date information (if applicable), and specified report options were added to all pages of the updated reports. Changes were also made to the way data is displayed in these reports, improving organization, spacing, shading, and legibility.

In addition to the new styling, we’ve significantly improved the format of Excel exports—removing merged cells and improving the overall alignment—making it much easier to tweak the exported data to fit your needs.

Custom layouts now deliver enhanced control over the visibility of specific menu options on an entity’s page for a tailored Rent Manager user experience.

Express

An Edit Action Bar Access button was added to the Layout designer, which allows you to tailor Action Bar menu options that are not hidden or restricted by a user’s privileges. This new option ensures a customized user experience that aligns with your organizational security objectives and user privileges.

The Invoice and Memorized Invoice details pages were updated with improved styling and usability.

Express

In our continuing efforts to enhance the Rent Manager Express user experience, several exciting changes were made to the Invoice and Memorized Invoice details pages. These updates have made information easier to read, improved navigation, and added or repositioned sections and fields to simplify performing popular actions.

The Make Deposit page was improved to make tracking and managing your bank deposits in Rent Manager Express a breeze!

Express

To make viewing and managing bank deposits in Express more intuitive, several changes were made to the Make Deposit page, including format adjustments that make important deposit information easier to read and update. The deposit workflow was also streamlined, and tiles and pop-ups were revamped to make performing popular actions—like adding other income and printing deposit tickets—much easier and more efficient by requiring fewer clicks.

The Rent Manager Express Move In wizard was updated with Deposit and Charges tabs.

Express

The Move In wizard was updated in Express, allowing you to easily link an existing prospect’s security deposit, contact, recurring charge, and one-time charge information to their new tenant account.

rmResident was updated with handy FAQ pages, expanded help text, and a Rate Us pop-up.

rmResident

To improve the tenant experience with rmResident, the app was updated with two FAQ pages and expanded help text/messages to guide users through routine actions, like setting up the app, making payments, etc. One of the FAQ pages is accessible from the Need help? link on the login page, while the other is available from the More menu. Tenants can reach out to Rent Manager support after three unsuccessful login attempts or directly from the FAQ pages via a Contact Us form. For other questions, tenants should first reach out to their property manager for assistance. Finally, a Rate Us pop-up was added, which allows users to easily rate and review the app.
