12.880 Release Notes

The release begins rolling out on September 20, 2022.

New Features

The Short Term Rentals (STR) feature was updated to include rate schedules. Please note that currently rate schedules are available only for properties with a Property Type of RV/Campground.

With rate schedules, you can easily manage the various fees and charges related to your STR reservations and quickly calculate the amount a guest pays for their stay. Rate schedules are managed on the Property Details page in the STR Rate Schedules tile.

Before you can use rate schedules, you must establish your property-level STR Settings, which include the following:

  • On the Rate Method tab, choose how rates are calculated when a stay spans multiple rate schedules and set up Site Groups, which consist of units that share the same rates.

  • On the Lengths of Stay tab, select the lengths of stay available at your property for which you would like to create rate schedules.

  • On the Taxes and Discounts tabs, set up tax types and create discounts that you can apply to rate schedules or to a specific guest’s reservation.

For more information, refer to the Property Short Term Rental (STR) Settings.

After you have established your property-level STR settings, click Add Rate Schedule to create a rate schedule. As part of managing your rate schedules, you should do the following:

  • In the Schedule Information, Rate Information, and Extended Stay Rates sections, enter basic information about the schedule, such as defining nightly rates and extended stay rates.

  • In the Special Rates section, determine how much a guest should pay when they stay at your property during holidays or events. These rates will override any standard rates applied to the unit.

  • In the Fees section, specify additional fees associated with the rate schedule, including guest fees, pet fees, vehicle fees, cleaning fees, and so on.

For more information, refer to the Short Term Rental (STR) Rate Schedules.
