12.260700 Changelog

The release begins rolling out on July 21, 2026.

New Features

Quickly review and manage your rmVoIP voicemail messages with Orion AI Smart Voicemails.

Express Rent Manager 12

The Orion AI Smart Voicemails feature was added to Rent Manager Express, which automatically transcribes and categorizes rmVoIP voicemails, giving you a faster, clearer way to manage daily communications. This feature allows you to quickly listen to messages, scan summaries, dig into transcriptions, and easily identify urgent or repeated issues directly from Rent Manager. Additionally, follow-up actions can be added from the voicemail record, even on historical calls. By enhancing the communication and review prioritization, Smart Voicemails saves you valuable time, reduces missed issues, and focuses your attention on high-value tasks.

For more information, refer to the Set Up Smart Voicemails topic in Express help.

More Information

Orion Smart Voicemails and rmVoIP are licensed features—for more information, please contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Revolutionize your security deposit management with a faster setup, clear statuses, and seamless deposit workflows.

Express Rent Manager 12 rmAppSuite Pro rmResident

Security Deposit Management, powered by Rentable, is now available in Rent Manager. This built-in integration is designed to streamline the entire security deposit life cycle—from collection through refunds and withholdings—while ensuring compliance with complex state and local regulations. By eliminating the hassle of managing separate bank accounts for security deposits and minimizing manual accounting work, you can reduce legal risk, simplify reconciliation, and offer your tenants flexible payment options in one linked workflow.

For more information, refer to the Set Up Security Deposits with Rentable topic in Express help.

Simplify your security deposit refund process with Zego's Resident Payout feature.

Express Rent Manager 12

Zego now offers Resident Payouts, a payment option that transforms the security deposit refund process from a manual, check-based workflow into a secure, digital experience. You can initiate refunds directly within Rent Manager, and tenants receive an email invitation to choose how they want to receive their funds. By digitizing your payout workflow, you reduce your operational burden, improve transparency, and deliver a faster, more flexible refund experience.

For more information, refer to the Zego Resident Payout topic in Express help.

Take control of your data entry with Custom Wizards.

Express

The Custom Wizards tool was updated in Rent Manager Express, allowing you to create streamlined data entry processes tailored to your exact needs. With this update, you can fully customize your Express Move Out add wizard workflow, guiding your team step-by-step, displaying only the essential fields for each task. You can design wizards from scratch, customize existing ones, or import wizards from Rent Manager 12. Unleash new levels of productivity and ease for your entire team by eliminating sifting through unnecessary fields and tedious training.

This release introduces the Move Out custom wizard, with custom wizards for Issue, Property, Unit, and Asset coming soon.

Enhancements

Streamline your leasing process and quickly identify prospect rent quote information with prospect stages updates.

Express Rent Manager 12

The Add Prospect Stage pop-up's Trigger field was updated with two rent quote options (Rent Quote Created and Rent Quote Expired). By tying rent quote activity to prospect stages, this enhancement gives you an automated, accurate reflection of a prospect's movement through the Prospect Leasing Board—eliminating manual tracking and enabling more consistent, timely leasing workflows.

Transform your rent quote process with the ability to add move dates to each quote with seamless efficiency.

Express

The rent quotes tool was updated so you can easily add move-in dates to each quote. These new updates streamline the move-in process by pulling move-in dates directly from Apply Now and iloveleasing, automatically applying lease dates and terms once a unit is reserved. This update eliminates typing the same information twice and provides a smooth, instant workflow from quote to signed lease—keeping your data accurate every time.

As part of this update, the following changes were made:

  • The rent quote wizard's Add Rent Quote pop-up was updated with a Move In field, which allows you to establish the date on which the tenant officially moves into the unit.

  • The rent quote wizard's Additional Charges pop-up was updated with an Add & Reserve Unit button, which, if a move-in date was provided on the quote and the prospect doesn't already have a reserved unit, allows you use the quote's Move In date to populate the unit's Reserved Move In date and the prospect's Expected Lease Start date.

  • The Prospect details page's Rent Quotes tile was updated with a Move In field and Reserve option, which allows you to enter the prospect's move-in date to reserve the unit or change the reserved unit if they already have a different unit reserved, depending on the scenario.

  • The Tenant Web Access - Apply Now - GeneralReserve unit upon prospect application completion and Create a rent quote with application using market rent system web preferences were enhanced to automatically apply lease dates and terms once a unit is reserved.

  • The MoveInDate function is now available for RentQuotes scripts.

Easily track prospect communication with last contact date enhancements.

Express Rent Manager 12

The tools for tracking prospect communication have been enhanced, allowing you to automatically capture every email and text along with the option to sync phone calls. The prospect's Last Contact field now reflects all interactions across emails, texts, rmVoIP calls, call notes, phone broadcasts, and bulk communications. These updates eliminate the burden of manual data entry and replace it with crystal-clear visibility into every lead.

Revolutionize your lease renewal process with our latest enhancements.

Express

The lease renewal tool was updated, giving your lease renewal workflow even more control, clarity, and organization. This enhancement allows you to Publish Signable Documents in bulk for tenant signatures directly from the Lease Renewal Board's Needs Document column. The publishing process is further enhanced by automatically adding the documents selected during the create renewal offer process. Each document also retains the specific data (e.g., expiration dates, signatures) established when creating the renewal offer, removing the need to reselect them each time.

The Lease Renewal Board's Sent Offer column was also updated with a Resend Offer option, which allows you to easily resend lease renewal offers to your tenants in bulk. Additionally, this update allows you to clearly see which tenants can receive the resent offer and those that are excluded because they do not have an email address recorded in Rent Manager.

The site classification tool received enhanced customization.

Express Rent Manager 12

This release brings significant upgrades to manufactured housing property management focused on improving efficiency, data accuracy, and in-depth tracking. These updates allow you to rename your homeowner statuses and their associated site classifications for a more tailored classification system. Additionally, you now have the ability to deactivate homeowner statuses that may not be relevant to your home management workflow.

As part of this update, the Asset Management privilege group was updated with a Manage Homeowner Statuses & Site Classifications privilege, which allows you to access the new Homeowner Statuses & Site Classifications page, which allows you to deactivate and rename Homeowner Statuses and rename Site Classifications.

For more information, refer to the Homeowner Statuses & Site Classifications topic in Express help.

The scripting tool was significantly enhanced for site classification and home-type assets.

Express Rent Manager 12

The scripting tool was updated so the Home and Asset classes can now be used to seamlessly pull details like homeowner statuses and property images into tools such as bird's eye view (BEV) maps and letter templates.

As part of this update, the following changes were made:

  • The Home class is now available anywhere the Asset class can be used.

  • The Home class was updated with new functions for HomeImage, HomeownerStatusCount, ImageCount, and ImageURL.

  • The Unit class function for SiteClassification was updated with a SiteClassificationCount parameter.

  • The scripts for [Asset.FinancialUnit.UnitAvailability()] and [Home.FinancialUnit.UnitAvailability()] now return Occupied or Vacant.

Express Rent Manager 12

TheUnit Turnover, Unit Type Vacancy, Unit Availability Listing, and Move In/Move Out reports were updated with site classification report options. This update eliminates manual searching by instantly connecting occupants, homes, and lots—providing you with a unified view of your assets. By incorporating site classifications breakdowns, you can easily analyze occupancy trends and make data-driven decisions through cleaner, more intuitive reporting.
