Make a Subsidy Payment

A subsidy is a form of financial assistance offered to qualifying tenants. The most common subsidy examples are HUD, Section 8, and FEMA. In Rent Manager, subsidies can be tracked, and payments made by the subsidy can be recorded. A single subsidy payment can pay off charges on multiple tenant accounts.

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Step 1: Filter Tenant List

To make a subsidy payment, go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Payments arrow_forward Make Subsidy Payment. Then you can filter the list of tenants by subsidy or by property and balance due.

Subsidy

In most cases, when making a regular subsidy payment, you filter the list by subsidy. This is best used for subsidy payments that are regularly applied to the same list of tenants.

On the first tile, in the Subsidy field, select the subsidy for which you are making a payment. The Charge Type and Payment From fields automatically populate with the information entered on the subsidy's details page.
The list of tenants at the bottom of the page displays all tenants included in the selected subsidy.

Property and Balance Due

In some instances, you may wish to manually filter for tenants with a balance on a specific subsidy-related charge type. This is best used for one-off subsidy payments that need to be applied to multiple tenants, but don't occur frequently enough to warrant creating a subsidy for them.

On the first tile, enter information into the available fields. The list of tenants at the bottom of the page displays all tenants who meet the filter criteria.

Field Description

Balance Due

Includes tenants with an open charge balance of the selected charge type that is greater than (>), equal to (=), or less than (<) a specified amount. In the available field, enter the specified amount to filter by.

For example, if you select > and enter 100, then only tenants with over $100 due in open charges display in the list.

Charge Type

The charge type to which you are applying a subsidy payment. The amount that displays in each tenant's Balance column below is the total of all their open charges of this charge type.

If you are filtering for tenants with a balance less than (<), tenants with a balance of 0 for this charge type also display.

Properties

Includes tenants from the selected properties or a property Group in the list.

More Information

This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property.

Tenants to Include

Includes tenants with a status of Current, Past, and/or Future in the list of tenants.

Step 2: Select Tenants and Enter Payments

Once you have set your filter fields, the tenant list populates with all tenants who meet the filter criteria.

The following columns display:

Column Description

Balance

The total balance due on the tenant's account for the selected charge type.

Payment

The amount to pay towards the tenant's open charges on this subsidy payment.

Tenant

The full name of the tenant receiving a subsidy payment.

Unit

The name of the unit associated with the tenant's lease. For tenants with multiple leases, <Multi> displays.

To enter subsidy payments for tenants, do the following:

  1. In the first column, check the box for each tenant that is having a subsidy payment applied.
    Once checked, the amount in the tenant's Balance column populates in the Payment column.

  2. In the Payment column, if the payment amount for the tenant is different from the Balance, enter the payment amount to apply to the account.

  3. Repeat for each selected tenant.

Step 3: Enter Payment Information

In the second tile for payment and deposit details, enter information into the available fields. The tile also displays the number of tenants selected in the list and the current sum of all those tenant's payment amounts.

Field Description

Create Deposit

Deposits this subsidy payment in Rent Manager. This should be checked only if the subsidy payment is also deposited into the bank in the real world.

If you selected a subsidy in the Subsidy field, the Create Deposit field is checked automatically and cannot be unchecked.

Deposit Comment

If you are depositing the payment, an additional note to record on the deposit regarding the subsidy payment deposit.

Deposit Date

The date on which the subsidy payment is deposited into the bank in the real world.

Payment Comment

An additional note to provide additional context or information regarding the subsidy payment.

Payment Date

The date on which the payment for the subsidy was received.

Step 4: Apply the Payment

To complete making the subsidy payment, do the following:

  1. In the first tile, next to the Amount field, click to pull in the total amount of all values in the Payment column. This determines the subsidy payment total.

  2. Enter information into the available fields:

    Field Description

    Bank

    The bank account into which the payment is being deposited.

    Payment From

    The name of the person, organization, or program providing the subsidy payment.

    Reference #

    The payment method of this subsidy payment. Select Cash, MO (money order), CC (credit card), or enter another method such as a check number ACH.

  3. Click Save.
    The subsidy payment is entered in Rent Manager.
