Owner 1096 (Report)

The Owner 1096 report is a summary tax form that shows the total amount of earned gross income that owners received from properties you managed during the tax year and the number of 1099 forms you will be submitting to the IRS to record that income. The Owner 1096 report allows you to print information from Rent Manager directly onto the preprinted IRS 1096 form for owners who require a 1099.

To complete your tax forms as accurately as possible, generate the Owner 1099 forms first and then generate the Owner 1096 using the same report options as the 1099 forms. For more information, refer to Owner 1099 (Report).

For information on purchasing Safeguard printable tax forms, refer to Safeguard Tax Forms.

More Information

As of 2023, if you have ten or more recipients, your must eFile your owner 1099 forms. For more information, refer to Export 1099 Owner.

Related Preferences

If your printer is not aligning with the IRS 1096's margins when printing the report, you can adjust the margins through system preferences. For more information, refer to Preprinted Forms (System Preferences).

Additionally, on the Reports tab, you must have access to Owner 1096.

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view the Owner 1096 report, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Owner 1096.
    The Reports: Owner 1096 page displays.

  2. Adjust the report options as desired. Each option is described below.

  3. Generate the report in your desired file format(s).

Report Options

The report options described below determine what data displays in the report.

Owners to Include

Select each owner or an owner Group to be included in the report.

More Information

Only information related to the properties to which you have access displays. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property.

Optionally, check Inactive Owners to include owners that are no longer active.

Optionally, check Show Inactive Properties to include properties that are no longer active.

Exclude Total Values Less Than $600

Check to display forms only for owners who earned more than $600 of gross income in the selected year in the report results.

Year

Select the desired calendar year to display the gross income earned by the selected active owner(s) during that year.

Report Results

The form displays as shown below when printing the Owner 1096 report on its preprinted form.

Field Descriptions

Each owner is displayed in their own subsection in the report so that it can printed on separate 1096 forms. Each field printed on the form is described below:

1096 Form Field Description

FILER'S name

The name and default address of your property management company as entered on the property management company's details page.

Name of person to contact

The name of the Manager or accountant who is managing your taxes as entered on the on the property management company's details page.

Email address

The Email for the manager as entered on the property management company's details page.

Telephone number

The Default phone number for the property management company entered on the property management company's details page.

Fax number

The fax number for the property management company entered on the property management company's details page.

1 Employer identification number

The Property Tax ID for the property management company.

3 Total number of forms

The total number of 1099 forms that are summarized by this 1096 form.

5 Total amount reported

The total amount of earned gross income for owners.

6 Enter an "X"

An X displays in the checkbox for the appropriate type of tax form that this 1096 summarizes.
