Tenants/Prospects Privilege Group

Tenants/Prospects privileges provide access to features such as adding or updating tenant and/or prospect information, entering lead source information, viewing rental applications, and using the screening features.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
System Manage assigned users and privileges View, Edit
Manage all users and privileges View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To manage these privileges, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then go to Users arrow_forward Users. Select a user and click the Privileges tab.

  2. Click next to the privilege group to expand the privilege list.

  3. To enable a privilege, check the box. Each privilege is described below.

  4. Click Save.

Privilege Descriptions

Each privilege is described below.

Privilege Description

Tenants

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Tenant accounts. Tenants are individuals or businesses who lease rental units from you.

Prospects

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Prospect accounts. Prospects are individuals or businesses who express interest in your properties or rental units but have not yet signed a lease.

Lead sources

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Lead Sources. Lead sources are the ways in which prospects learned about your company and can be used to track the effectiveness of your marketing.

Sales

Add, view, edit, and/or delete retail sales data for commercial tenants if the tenant's rent is based upon their monthly sales. Sales information is entered on the Retail Sales window on the commercial leases.

Related Preferences

Sales information is available only when Track tenant retail sales is enabled in Commercial (System Preferences).

Application

View, edit, and/or delete in progress or completed rental Applications submitted by prospects through Apply Now.

Pet Types

Add, view, edit, and/or delete pet types in Rent Manager, allowing users to define charge information for pets that are owned by your tenants.

Lease Terms

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Lease Terms to describe the duration of a lease.

Confirm move out

Allows a user to Move Out tenants from the tenant details page Leases tile. in order to stop recurring charges from posting.

Related Preferences

If the Require moveout confirmation to stop recurring charge posts option in General Options (System Preferences) is checked, recurring charges continue to post until the Move Out field is checked, confirming the move out.

Modify tenant move in/move out dates

Provides the ability to update the Move In and Move Out dates on the tenant's Lease Details pop-up.

Modify move in/out dates before accounting close

Provides the ability to update the Move In and Move Out dates on the tenant's Lease Details pop-up prior to the accounting close date.

Related Preferences

The accounting close date is set in Accounting Close (System Preferences) or overridden on the property level through Property Accounting Close (Pop-Up).

If the accounting close date is split by selecting the Split Accounting Close for AR, AP, Journals/Adjustments option, the latest of the three dates is used for this privilege.

Modify lease start/end dates before accounting close

Provides the ability to update the Lease Start and Lease End dates in the Lease Details on the Tenant Details (Page) to dates prior to the accounting close date.

Related Preferences

The accounting close date is set in Accounting Close (System Preferences) or overridden on the property level through Property Accounting Close (Pop-Up).

If the accounting close date is split by selecting the Split Accounting Close for AR, AP, Journals/Adjustments option, the latest of the three dates is used for this privilege.

Create multiple leases on a tenant

Provides the ability to add more than one Lease on a tenant account if the tenant rents more than one unit at any property.

Change account status to tenant

Provides the ability to use Move In on the Status section of Prospect Details (Page) to change a prospect account into a tenant account.

Change account status to prospect

Provides the ability to use Change on the Status section of Prospect Details (Page) to change a prospect's status back to Prospect from status options of Tenant, Lost, or Lost-Rejected.

Change account status to Lost/Lost - Rejected

Provides the ability to use Change on the Status section of Prospect Details (Page) to change a prospect's status to Lost or Lost-Rejected.

Modify charges in the move in wizard

Provides the ability to add, delete, and inherit/uninherit recurring charges in the Move In wizard. Additionally, this privilege provides the ability to add, modify dates, inherit/uninherit, and delete one-time charges in the Move In wizard.

Set Renewal Rent Increases

Provides the ability to set rent increases prior to generating renewal offers.

Approve Renewal Rent Increases

Provides the ability to modify and approve rent amounts for renewal increases.

Related Preferences

This privilege displays only if Require approval of Renewal Rent Increases before sending offer is checked in arrow_forward Administration, then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward General Options arrow_forward Lease Renewals.

Lease Renewal Offers

Provides the ability to generate new lease renewals in Create Renewal Offers and change renewal statuses on the Lease Renewal Board.

Manage Prospect Leasing Board

Provides the ability to customize and manage actions on the Prospect Leasing Board. For more information, refer to Prospect Leasing Board (Page).

Manage Lost Reasons

Add, view, edit, and/or delete the reasons a prospect decided not to rent from you, or the reasons you did not allow a prospect to rent from you, listed on the Lost Reasons register.

Manage Prospect Stages

Add, view, edit, and/or delete prospect stages listed the Prospect Stages register. These stages allow you to track the progression of a prospect through the leasing process, and filter and report on prospects assigned to those stages.

Manage Stage History

Provides the ability to manually add, edit, or delete the historical information for Prospect Stages on a prospect's account.

Change reserved unit with transactions prior to close

Provides the ability to change the Reserved Unit listed on the Reservation/Expected Lease Information section of Prospect Details (Page) for a prospect that has transactions prior to the property’s accounting close date. This also applies to using Move In on the Status section of Prospect Details (Page) to change a prospect account into a tenant account.

Related Preferences

The accounting close date is set in Accounting Close (System Preferences) or overridden on the property level through Property Accounting Close (Pop-Up).

If the accounting close date is split by selecting the Split Accounting Close for AR, AP, Journals/Adjustments option, the latest of the three dates is used for this privilege.

Change account name

Provides the ability to change the name of tenant and prospect accounts.

On the Tenant Details (Page) and Prospect Details (Page), change the First Name and Last Name or Company Name in the General Information section. On the Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up) and Prospect Contacts (Pop-Up), change the primary contact’s First Name and Last Name.

Change primary contact status

Provides the ability to assign the Primary tenant status to a different contact on the Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up) and Prospect Contacts (Pop-Up).

View complete tenant SSNs

View the complete social security number of tenants and prospects on the Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up) and Prospect Contacts (Pop-Up) by clicking .

View annual income

View the complete Annual Income of tenants and prospects on the Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up) and Prospect Contacts (Pop-Up) by clicking .

Access Screenings

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Screenings to check tenant or prospect credit, criminal, and eviction histories.

Related Preferences

The screening results you receive are generated based on the types of reports that you have contracted with AmRent as well as the types of databases and report options you defined in Screening Settings (System Preferences).

Access other users’ screenings

View the complete list of credit, criminal, and eviction history Screenings and view all non-protected screenings, including those run by other users on the Screenings register.

View Protected Screenings

View Screenings that have a check in the Protected column on the Screenings register.

More Information

This is a legacy feature from AmRent no longer available in Rent Manager. This privilege is intended for use only if you have run protected screenings in the past when the feature was available and still need to view these screenings.

Override workflow solution from screening

Provides the ability to override the Declined recommendation of an AmRent workflow solution Screening report on the Prospect Details (Page).

Related Preferences

To override a screening workflow solution, the Minimum decision to convert prospect to tenant must be set to Approved in Screening Settings (System Preferences).

Income Verifications

Add, view, or delete Income Verifications on the Screenings page as part of the prospect screening process.

View others' Income Verifications

View the complete list of Income Verification screenings and details of Income Verification screenings run by other users.

Merge Prospects

Provides the ability to merge duplicate prospect accounts by converting a Source Prospect into a Destination Prospect or Destination Tenant. Additionally, you may choose to delete the source prospect or update the source prospect’s status to Lost or Lost-Rejected with the option to move that prospect’s transactions to the destination prospect or tenant.

Manage other users appt / tasks for Prospect

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Appointments (Page) and/or Tasks (Page) for other Rent Manager users as well as remove yourself from the appointment or task list.

More Information

This privilege must be enabled in order for a user to see the Show All Appt/Tasks option on a prospect’s History/Notes pop-up.

Application Templates

Create application templates in Rent Manager to be used for Apply Now applications.

Violations

Add, view, edit, and/or delete Violations (Page) used track and manage tenants with violations, including the penalty for the infraction and any escalating penalties for failing to comply.

Guest Card Templates

Add, view, edit, and/or delete guest card templates to place a contact form on your Web Template Suite or custom website.

Edit prospects converted to tenants

Edit the prospect account of an account that has a status of Tenant on the Prospect Details (Page).

Allow to delete prospect contacts

Delete any additional contacts created for a prospect on the Prospect Contacts (Pop-Up).

Allow to delete tenant contacts

Delete any additional contacts created for a tenant on the Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up).

Allow to not require insurance

Grants the ability to select or deselect the Don't require insurance option on a tenant's lease. For more information, refer to Tenant Leases (Pop-Up).

Export Minnesota CRP

The user can use the Export Minnesota CRP page to export the form for electronic filling.

Access limited occupancy information for all properties

When using the Unit Picker, the user can select and view occupancy information for any active property in your Rent Manager database regardless of which properties they have the ability to access.

Tenant Eviction

Add allows the ability to start the eviction process for a tenant. Edit allows the ability to advance existing evictions and edit information in existing evictions. Delete allows the user to delete any existing evictions on an account.

Override payment options when tenant is in eviction

The user can enable or disable payments options on the Miscellaneous tile for a tenant who is in an eviction process. When disabled, the payment options cannot be used for the duration of the eviction process.

Rent Quotes

Add, edit, and/or delete rent quotes. A rent quote is the unit rent offered to prospects during the leasing process and can be incorporated in applications, screenings, and recurring charge setups.
