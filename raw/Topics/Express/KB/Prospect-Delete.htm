Delete a Prospect

Rent Manager gives you three options to handle prospects you no longer manage in your database. The first option, and best practice, is to make the prospect inactive. A prospect is considered inactive with a status of Lost or Lost/Rejected. This can be managed on the prospect's details page. Information about inactive prospects remains in Rent Manager and may be included in reports wherever the Prospects to Include option is available and Lost and/or Lost/Rejected is selected. For example, you can use the inactive option for prospects that choose not to rent with you in order to preserve valuable historical and financial data. Additionally, you can also track which prospects ultimately choose not to rent with you and use reports like Lost Prospect Breakdown and Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

Should you find that deleting a prospect is the best option for your business, there are two deletion methods available in Rent Manager: complete or merge.

If you created a prospect account by mistake and do not need to maintain any historical data or financial records for the prospect, the complete method is the best option. Alternatively, if you have duplicate prospect accounts for the same individual, those accounts can be merged together. Rent Manager can combine accounts using the Merge Prospects pop-up. The Merge Prospects pop-ups' main purpose is to merge a prospect account into a tenant account or another prospect account.

Option 1: Make a Prospect Inactive

Making a prospect inactive ensures that all the information regarding the prospect remains in Rent Manager for you to access if you need it again. Inactive prospects can also be included in reports wherever the Prospects to Include option is available and Lost and/or Lost/Rejected is selected. Additionally, you can also track which prospects ultimately choose not to rent with you and use reports like Lost Prospect Breakdown and Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Prospects View, Edit
Change Account Status to Lost/Lost-Rejected Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To change a prospect's status, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select the prospect account for which you want to change status.
    The prospect's details page displays.

  2. At the top right, click Update Status.
    The Change Prospect Status pop-up displays.

  3. In the New Status drop-down, choose the Lost or Lost/Rejected status and enter the related information. See the table below for more information about these prospect statuses:

    Status Description

    Lost/Rejected

    If you choose not to proceed with leasing to this prospect, select Lost/Rejected from the New Status drop-down and enter the following information:

    Lost Date

    The date on which the prospect's tenancy was declined. The Lost Date defaults to today's date. You can edit the date as needed.

    Reason

    The reason you decided that the prospect should not lease with you. Active lost reasons of only the Lost-Rejected type display in the drop-down when you select this status. For more information, refer to Lost Reasons (Page).

    Description

    A description explaining the prospect's status. The description displays in the prospect's history/notes and in reports, such as the Lost Prospect Breakdown report.

    Lost

    If a prospect chooses not to rent with you, select Lost from the New Status drop-down and enter the following information:

    Lost Date

    The date on which the prospect declined to rent with you. The Lost Date defaults to today's date. You can edit the date as needed.

    Reason

    The reason the prospect decided not to rent with you. Active lost reasons of only the Lost type display in the drop-down when you select this status. For more info, refer to Lost Reasons (Page).

    Description

    A description explaining the prospect's status. The description displays in the prospect's history/notes and in reports, such as the Lost Prospect Breakdown report.

  4. Click Save.
    The prospect's status is updated.

Option 2: Merge Prospect Accounts

Rent Manager provides the ability to copy one prospect and all contacts on that account into another current prospect or current tenant account. The source prospect and each associated contact are transferred as new contacts on the Contacts tile of the destination account, and after the merge is completed, history/note items are generated on both accounts to document the merge. Additionally, Tenant Web Access (TWA) users who do not exist on the destination account have their web user credentials transferred.

This tool is helpful if, for example, you have contacted a prospect and taken detailed notes and then that same person submits an online application and becomes a second prospect in your Rent Manager database. If a prospect account was created in-office or via Apply Now that has a screening or charges/payments (e.g., an application fee) tied to it, but a separate tenant account was created when they signed their lease, you can use this tool as a way to merge these accounts.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Prospects View, Edit
Merge Prospects Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Warning

Data not copied in when merging prospects includes the following:

  • ePay information

  • Values for prospect-type user defined fields (UDFs)

  • The values on the prospect's details page

  • The Primary designation of a contact

  • The Show on Statements/Labels designation of a contact

To merge prospect accounts, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and click Merge.
    The Merge Prospects pop-up displays.

  2. In the Select the Source and Destination Accounts section, enter the following information:

    Field Description

    Merge Prospect To

    Whether the prospect account is being merged into another Prospect account or a Tenant account.

    Source Prospect

    The prospect account whose information is being merged. If both prospect accounts have the same name, click and enter the prospect's name in the Search field, then select the correct account from the list.

    More Information

    Prospects with an application status of In Progress cannot be merged until their application is completed or canceled.

    If the source prospect has screening results below the minimum acceptable standards, the following privilege is also required:

    Related Privileges

    Group Privilege Column
    Tenants/Prospects Override Screening Decisions Enabled

    For more information, refer to Control User Access.

    Destination Prospect/Tenant

    The account that the Source Prospect is being merged into.

  3. In the Source Prospect Options section, ensure Delete the Source Prospect is checked.

  4. Click Merge.
    The Source Prospect account is merged into the Destination Prospect account, and the Source Prospect account is updated with the selected status.

Option 3: Delete a Prospect

If you created a prospect by accident and do not wish to maintain any historical data or financial records for the prospect, the complete method is the best option. Deleting a prospect from Rent Manager is final and cannot be undone. Prospects can be deleted only if they do not have financial data, issues, screenings, or utilities associated with their account.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Prospects View, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To delete a prospect, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects.
    The Prospects page displays.

  2. On the prospect you want to delete, select arrow_forward Delete.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  3. Click Yes.
    The prospect is deleted from your Rent Manager database.

To bulk delete multiple prospects, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects.
    The Prospects page displays.

  2. At the top left, click Bulk Actions arrow_forward Manage Prospects arrow_forward Delete.

  3. In the far left column, check each prospect you wish to delete.

  4. Once you are satisfied with your selections, click Delete Prospects.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  5. Click Yes.
    The prospects are deleted from your Rent Manager database.
