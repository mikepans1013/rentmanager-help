Change an Owner Prospect Status

Owner prospects in Rent Manager are potential owners. You can track which owner prospects choose to sign a contract with your management company by changing an owner prospect's Status. All new owner prospect accounts start with the Owner Prospect status but can be changed to Owner, Lost, or Lost/Rejected. For example, if an owner prospect decides to sign with you, you can update their status to Owner and convert their prospect account to an owner account with a contract. You can also track which owner prospects ultimately choose not to sign with you and use reports like Owner Prospect Listing and Owner Prospect Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

Change an Owner Prospect Status to Lost

To change an owner prospect's status to lost, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Prospects.
    The Owner Prospects page displays.

  2. To the right of the owner prospect, click  arrow_forward Change Status.

  3. If an owner prospect chooses not to sign a contract with you, select Lost from the New Status drop-down list.

  4. Click Save.
    The owner prospect's status is updated.

Change an Owner Prospect Status to Lost/Rejected

To change an owner prospect's status to lost/rejected, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Prospects.
    The Owner Prospects page displays.

  2. To the right of the owner prospect, click  arrow_forward Change Status.

  3. If you choose not to proceed with this owner prospect, select Lost/Rejected from the New Status drop-down list.

  4. Click Save.
    The owner prospect's status is updated.

Change an Owner Prospect Status to Owner

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To change an owner prospect's status to owner, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Prospects.
    The Owner Prospects page displays.

  2. To the right of the owner prospect, click  arrow_forward Change Status.

  3. Select Owner in the New Status drop-down list.

  4. Select the Owner Contract Start date for each property in the owner's portfolio.

  5. To help identify owners of certain types, groups, or any other specifications you prefer, select the Display Color. The color displays as a bar to the left of the owner in some areas of Rent Manager, such as the Owners page.

  6. In the Properties to be created for this owner section, enter the Name, Short Name, and select the Property Type for each property in the owner's portfolio. To add additional properties, click Add Property.

  7. In the Users with access to created Properties section, select the user(s) that require access to the Properties to be created for this owner. Their level of access is determined by their user permissions.

  8. Click Save.
    The owner prospect account becomes an owner account and any properties listed are created and associated with the owner's contract.

Return a Lost, Rejected, or Converted Owner Prospect to an Active Owner Prospect Status

To return a lost, rejected, or converted owner prospect to an active owner prospect status, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Prospects.
    The Owner Prospects page displays.

  2. To the right of the owner prospect, click  arrow_forward Change Status.

  3. Select Owner Prospect in the New Status drop-down list.

  4. Click Save.
    The owner prospect's status is updated.
