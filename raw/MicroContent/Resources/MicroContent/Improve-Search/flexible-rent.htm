Flexible Rent

Zego offers a service, through their partner Flex, called Flexible Rent. Flexible Rent is a flexible payments solution that provides tenants access to a credit line that allows them to split their rent into two payments, while ensuring properties get paid in full when rent is due. This option is integrated directly into Tenant Web Access (TWA) and the rmResident mobile app.

More Information

The Flexible Rent service is free to Rent Manager customers, but tenants must pay a monthly subscription fee and a bill payment fee of 1% of their total rent to Flex to use the program.

Benefits of Flexible Rent

Sometimes your tenants may run into a period of financial difficulty, or maybe there are simply timing issues with their pay schedules and banks. Flexible Rent alleviates this problem by allowing them to split up their rent payments over the course of a month so the full balance isn't due on the same day.

Your tenants will benefit not only from the flexibility of the schedules, but also having smaller, more affordable payments and positive credit reporting. As a property management company, you will benefit too by seeing fewer late payments, saving on administration fees, and not having to worry about platform fees.

How do tenants enroll?

Tenants can sign up for Flexible Rent via their Tenant Web Access (TWA) portal or the rmResident mobile app. Once they enroll, a soft credit pull is performed to determine if their enrollment is approved or denied. If approved, they set up a payment schedule, connect their bank account, and can even sign up for Flex's Auto Pay. Flex does not perform a hard credit pull. Instead, they evaluate each applicant to determine their eligibility for a Flex credit line based on information from the tenant's credit report, payment history, and other financial factors.

How It Works

With Flexible Rent, tenants pay part of their rent to Flex by 11:59 PM EST on the 5th of the month. If set up, autopay attempts to start on the last day of the prior month. Once that payment clears, your property management company receives the full rent amount from Flex and the tenant pays the remaining balance to Flex later in the month.

What happens if a tenant misses a payment?

If the tenant doesn't pay their upfront payment for any given month—or that payment fails—their Flexible Rent account is paused. Flex notifies them via email so they can make their full rent payment directly to your property management company. If they miss their second scheduled payment, Flex may allow a one-time catch-up payment. However, to keep their account active, the full Flex balance must be paid by the end of the month.

Enable or Disable Flexible Rent

Related Preferences

Zego ePay must be enabled in system preferences to use Flexible Rent. For more information, refer to General ePay (System Preferences) and Tenant ePay (System Preferences).

If Flexible Rent is enabled, tenants can enroll in the service via Tenant Web Access (TWA) or rmResident. If it is disabled, this option does not display in TWA or rmResident. This setting affects all tenants at all properties in Rent Manager that are not one of the following property types:

  • Association

  • Commercial

  • Short Term Rental

  • RV/Campground

Warning

Flexible Rent does not support account groups. If a tenant is enrolled in Flexible Rent and then added to an account group, they are unenrolled from Flexible Rent. For more information, refer to Manage Account Groups.

Additionally, the ePay AutoPay feature is not compatible with Flexible Rent. If Flexible Rent is enabled for a tenant account, their ePay AutoPay is canceled.

To turn Flexible Rent on or off, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Web Preferences arrow_forward Tenant Web Access arrow_forward Financial.

  2. Check or uncheck Allow tenants to enroll in Flexible Rent.

  3. Click Save.
    If checked, Flexible Rent is enabled. If unchecked, Flexible Rent is disabled.

More Information

Once enabled in system web preferences, Flexible Rent is available at all properties that have ePay enabled except for properties with a property type of Association, Commercial, Short Term Rental, or RV/Campground.

To disable Flexible Rent at the property level, you must disable ePay for the property, but this should be done only if you intend to stop all ePay payments at the property.

To disable Flexible Rent for an individual tenant, you can check the options Do Not Accept Payments or Do Not Accept TWA Payments on the tenant's account.

Check Tenant's Flexible Rent Status

For tenants that enrolled in Flexible Rent, the current status of their Flexible Rent account displays on their tenant details page. To check this status, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant. In the Transactions tile header's Flexible Rent field, one of the following statuses displays:

Status Description

Active

The tenant is currently enrolled in Flexible Rent and Flex pays the tenant's balance after their upfront payment clears.

Deactivated

The tenant was enrolled in Flexible Rent, but their account was disabled due to a change made in Rent Manager that changed their eligibility, such as ePay being disabled for the tenant or property, or the options Do Not Accept Payments or Do Not Accept TWA Payments being checked on the tenant's account.

Paused

This status is set by Flex when they are working with the tenant to resolve an issue, such as the tenant being behind on payments. While this status is set, Flex does not pay the tenant's balance.
