Owner Web Access

Owner Web Access (OWA) is a web portal that allows your owners to manage their properties by logging in with their Rent Manager credentials. Owner Web Access (OWA) can be customized to meet the specific needs for each owner so they only have access to the information they care about. Owners can view bills, inspection reports, management fees, history/notes, and distributions for each of their properties. OWA users can also generate reports directly from the portal so the information that matters most to them is always available. In addition to viewing their contributions, if owner ePay is enabled with Zego, they can make contributions directly from Owner Web Access (OWA).
