# rmVoIP Owner Call Details (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/rmVoIP-Call-Details-Owner.htm

When you receive a phone call through rmVoIP , Rent Manager attempts to any account in your database with that phone number to immediately provide you with information about the caller. Call records that are linked to owners include basic details about the caller, notes describing the call, and the ability to view the owner details and view or edit history notes for the owner.

 More Information

 You must be subscribed to the rmVoIP service in order to utilize this integration within Rent Manager . For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 To view the details of an rmVoIP call linked to an owner, go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward   rmVoIP Incoming Call History . Then, select a call with the Link Type of Owner from the list.

 The Call History tile displays any additional information to explain the call. This information also displays on the owner's History/Notes .

 Field
