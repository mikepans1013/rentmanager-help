# rmVoIP Incoming Call History (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/rmVoIP-Incoming-Call-History.htm

rmVoIP is the fully independent, business-class phone system offered by LCS . rmVoIP provides enterprise-grade phone features like auto attendants, hunt groups, conference calls, call forwarding, call recording, and more. The main advantage to using the rmVoIP phone system is that it fully integrates with Rent Manager , allowing you to make and receive phone calls directly through the software and have those conversations saved in the history notes of the associated accounts.

 The Incoming Call History page provides information about recent phone calls, such as the caller's number and ID, the date and time of the call, and the extension number receiving the inbound call.

 More Information

 You must be subscribed to the rmVoIP service in order to utilize this integration within Rent Manager . Please contact your Sales Representative for more information on how to begin the process of purchasing the rmVoIP phone system.

 To view the Incoming Call History page, go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward rmVoIP Incoming Call History .
