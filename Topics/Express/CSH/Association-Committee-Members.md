# Association Committee Members (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Association-Committee-Members.htm

If your property has an association, you can designate tenants as committee members, manage or remove existing committee members, or send an email to everyone on your committee from the Committee Members pop-up.

 Related Privileges

 View

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To view the Committee Members pop-up, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties and select an association-type property. Then, on the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Committee Members .

 Filter Information

 The following filtering options are available on this pop-up.

 Field
 Description

 Search

 Enter the desired search criteria to display results match the criteria.

 Committees

 Select committees to narrow down the results to only members of the selected committees. By default, all committees are selected.

 Show Inactive

 Check to include active and inactive committee members in the list.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on the pop-up.

 Column
 Description

 Contact Name

 The name of the committee member.

 Committee Name

 The title of the committee (e.g., Finance , Elections , Communications , Social ).

 Email

 The contact email address for the committee member.

 Comments

 A note or comment associated with the committee member (e.g., Available by email only ).

 Active

 A displays if the committee member is active.
