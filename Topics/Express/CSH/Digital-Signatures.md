# Digital Signatures (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Digital-Signatures.htm

Digital signatures are an image of a handwritten signature that can be saved and placed on printed checks to save time. A digital signature is still recognized by banks, making it a valid and secure alternative to handwritten signatures.
