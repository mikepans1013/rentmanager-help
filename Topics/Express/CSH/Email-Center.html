Email Center (Page)

The Email Center allows you to review emails that have been sent from Rent Manager as well as their subsequent replies.

To access the Email Center, go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Email arrow_forward Email Center. You can view, delete, print, reply to, or forward emails directly from the Email Center. Correspondence found in the Email Center can be viewed from the entity's History/Notes pop-up. To check for newly received emails, click to refresh the Email Center.

Related Privileges

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

More Information

Emails sent from Rent Manager Express that contain attachments can be opened, saved, or forwarded in Rent Manager. However, emails with attachments that were sent before June 2024 must be viewed and managed from your native email client.

Set Up and Customize Emails

Email correspondence is managed in Rent Manager through system preferences. You can use the Rent Manager-provided email server to send emails, which works well for sending correspondence like localized maintenance notices or community newsletters that do not require a response. For more information, refer to Set Up Email Using Rent Manager's Default Mail Server.

Rent Manager also offers powerful tools for emails, such as using your business email to send correspondence and email reply tracking. Email reply tracking controls whether replies to your emails go back to your email account or pull into Rent Manager. For more information, refer to Set Up Email and Email Reply Tracking.

Additionally, if enabled in system preferences, each user can override system level settings to display their own email address by setting up their personal preferences. For more information, refer to Set Up User Email.
