Accounts Receivable Automation Schedules (Page)

You can use this automation to send notices via email, text, phone broadcast, and Virtual Post Office (VPO) to a list of tenants at your properties who have open or upcoming charges without worrying about human error or meeting deadlines. Each alert is personalized for various intervals of delinquency, allowing you to easily change the tone of the alerts the longer the tenant is delinquent. These alerts can make actively seeking payments from tenants a completely automatic process to streamline workflows for your business. After a notice is sent, a record of that notice is added as a history/note item on the tenant's account.

Each property can have its own AR automation schedule, allowing you to accurately adhere to your real-world collections policies that may differ between properties. A property's default AR automation schedule can be configured on the property's details page. These automations include all tenants, past, current, or future, with a charge or balance due on their accounts. For example, if you have an automation set for five days past due only tenants who have received a recent charge to be five days past due on is notified. Unless a past tenant is receiving a new charge, their charges will likely not meet those criteria for a charge that is five days past due.

Related Preferences

To set up automation schedules, you must have the appropriate automation enabled in system preferences. For more information, refer to Task Automation (System Preferences).

To view an accounts receivable automation schedule, go to  arrow_forward Administration, then go to Automation arrow_forward Task Automation arrow_forward Accounts Receivable and select a schedule from the list on the left.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available on this page.

Column Description

Balance Filter

Determines the total balance each charge type must reach in order to receive the notification.

For example, if a notification's Balance Filter is set to > Greater than $0.00 for your rent charge type, then all tenants at the selected properties that owe more than $0.00 in rent charges will receive the notification on the specified Due Date or Day of Month.

Enabled

A displays if the posting schedule is currently enabled.

Message

A preview of the message's written content, the selected letter template's name, or the selected phone broadcast's name.
Schedule Type

The following options determine when notifications will be sent:

Day of Month

. Notify tenants that meet the filter criteria on the specific day of the month

Due Date

Notify tenants that meet the filter criteria relative to the date that unpaid charges are due.

Scheduled Day

The day that notifications are sent to tenants who meet the Balance Filter criteria based on the Schedule Type of the notification.

Day of Month

The calendar day the notification is sent (e.g., 1 for the first day of the month, 2 for the second day, etc.) displays.

Due Date

Displays either On Due Date or the number of days before or after the charge's due date (e.g., 1 (Day After)) that the notification is sent.
