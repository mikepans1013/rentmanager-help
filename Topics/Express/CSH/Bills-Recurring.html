Recurring Bills (Page)

Recurring bills are bill templates that can be posted as one-time bills on a recurring basis. If you manage properties that receive bills on a regular cycle, you can save time by creating recurring bills and posting them when necessary. Common examples of recurring bills include trash service, cable service, car loans, mortgages, and so on.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Recurring Bills View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view your recurring bills, go to  arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Recurring Bills.

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on this page. By default, some columns display only by using .

Default Column Description

Invoice #

The bill's reference number or invoice number

Vendor

The name of the payee of the bill, which can be a vendor or a owner.

Automate

A displays if the bill is set to post automatically.

Task Automation is only available for Rent Manager Online users.

Frequency

How often a new one-time bill is created from this template based on the Period of the bill. For example, if the Frequencyis set to 1 and the Periodis set to Month(s), the recurring bill should be posted once every month.

Period

How often a new one-time bill is created from this template based on the Frequency of the bill.For example, if the Frequency is set to 3 and the Periodis set to Week(s), the recurring bill should be posted once every three weeks.

Terms

The amount of time before the bill is considered due. For example, if the term is set to ON THE 15th, the bill is due on the 15th day of the month.

Times Left

The number of remaining times the bill is created before the recurring bill posting ends.

Last Post Date

The last time this recurring bill was posted.

Next Post Date

The next date this bill is due to post.

Due Date

The calculated due date of a bill. This date is calculated by adding the Terms to the Post Date. For example, a bill that is posted on 10/13 with a Term of Net 15 shows a due date of 10/28. A bill that is posted on 8/1 with a Term of On The 15th shows a due date of 8/15.

Amount

The dollar amount of the recurring bill.

Property Short Name

The short name(s) of the property or properties that are expensed for this bill.
Available Column Description

Bank

The bank account from which the recurring bill is paid.

Created By

The user who created the recurring bill.

Created Date

The date on which the recurring bill was created.

Detail Memo

Additional information regarding the recurring bill as entered on the Recurring Bill details page in the first Memo field.

Expense Account

The expense account debited for the bill. If multiple expense accounts are debited, this displays <Multiple>.

Expense Account Number List

The general ledger (GL) account number(s) of the debited expense accounts.

Memo

Additional details regarding each line item of the recurring bill as entered on the Recurring Bill line item Memo field.

Notification Email

The email address that receives an email notification for each successful or failed automatic posting of this bill.

Pay Method

The method by which the bill is paid. For example, if the bill is being paid with check, this column displays Check.

Properties

The properties that are expensed for this bill.

Unit

If applicable, the unit(s) associated with the line items on the bill.

Updated By

The user who most recently updated and saved the recurring bill.

Updated Date

The date on which the recurring bill was most recently updated and saved.
