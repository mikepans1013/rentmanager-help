# Memorized Comments (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Memorized-Comments.htm

Memorized comments are custom messages that you can save and add in various places in Rent Manager . For example, when creating tenant statements, filling out a bill's or check's details, or processing a batch payment, you can insert a memorized comment. Depending on the location in Rent Manager , memorized comments can be inserted into fields like Comment or Memo by clicking and selecting the memorized comment name.
