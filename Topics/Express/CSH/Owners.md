# Owners (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Owners.htm

In Rent Manager , owners are the people or organizations that own the properties you manage. If you are a fee-based management company, you will utilize the owner features in Rent Manager to perform tasks like receive owner contributions, collect management fees, and pay owners their equity distributions.

 On the Owners page, every owner record displays on a row with information organized into columns. Clicking on any owner opens the owner's details page, where information specific to that owner is available.

 Send Text

  

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 Texting/Broadcast
 Send/Receive texts
 Enabled

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 Send a text message to the owner through the text messaging center. This option displays only if there is a text-enabled phone number listed on the account.

 More Information

 The first text sent to a recipient from the text messaging center and any texts sent via text broadcast or automated notification include an opt-out message. If a recipient opts out, that phone number cannot receive texts from Rent Manager unless they choose to opt back in. Recipients can opt out or opt in at any time. If a recipient wishes to opt back into text messaging, they must text Start from the phone number they opted out with to the number they originally requested to opt out of.

 Phone numbers that have opted out of text messaging are identified on the account's View Contacts pop-up, where the checkbox in the T column is replaced with the words Opted out in red text.
