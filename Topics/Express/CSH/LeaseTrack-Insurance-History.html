Insurance History (Pop-Up)

The Insurance History pop-up allows you to view a detailed breakdown of any changes made to your insurance setup.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage Insurance Setup Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view the Insurance History pop-up, go to arrow_forward Administration, then select Rental Info arrow_forward Properties/Units arrow_forward Insurance. Then, on the Insurance page, click Insurance History.

Select a property from the list on the left to view the property's policy. Check Show Inactive Properties to include those in the list.

Active Policies

This section displays the Master policies (100K Renters/Homeowners Insurance and/or 300K Renters/Homeowners Insurance) established with LeaseTrack for any of your properties.

The following columns display in this section.

Column Description

# of Tenants with Policy

The number of tenants or homeowners at the property currently enrolled in and covered by the Master policy. These numbers update at the end of the month when LeaseTrack refreshes policies.

Charge Amount

The dollar amount of the charge posted each month from LeaseTrack to apply to the property, as displayed on the Insurance page.

Policy

The liability covered by the Master policy: 100K Renters/Homeowners Insurance or 300K Renters/Homeowners Insurance.

History

This section displays any changes made to the property's Master policies, the date on which the update(s) occurred, and the Rent Manager user who made the change(s).

The following columns display in this section.

Column Description

Date

The date that the change was made to the Master policy.

Event

A system note describing the change made to the Master policy.

Update User

The Username of the Rent Manager user who made a change to the property's Master policy.
