# Prospect Leasing Board (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Prospect-Leasing-Board.htm

The Prospect Leasing Board provides a panoramic view of your prospect landscape, enabling you to instantly identify where each prospect stands in their leasing journey, organized by your existing prospect stages. Additionally, this tool also makes it easy to spot when a prospect’s Next Contact is due and flags when an action is overdue, ensuring timely engagements that keep momentum going. What’s more, you can customize prospect card details for improved insight and execute critical actions (write letters, send emails, run and view screenings, etc.) directly from the board.

 Filter Options

 The following filtering options are available on this page.

 Option
 Description

 Search

 Enter the desired search criteria to display results containing the entered criteria.

 Saved Filters

 Use to narrow the list of prospects that display in the list by using other criteria.

 Show Quick Filters

 Filter the list of prospects to narrow down how many prospects display. Click Show Quick Filters to access the filters described below.

 Leasing Agent

 Select the leasing agent(s) to include their associated prospects on the board.

 Stages

 Select the prospect stages and associated prospects to include on the board.

 Properties

 Select the properties and associated prospects to include on the board.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Last Contact

 Select the date to filter the board based on the number of days prior to the current date that prospects have not been contacted. For example, to view prospects that have not been contacted in past month, enter the date 30 days prior to the current date in the From field.

 More Information

 The prospect's last contact date is established on the most recent date that a call-, email-, or visit-type history/note item was added to the prospect's account. For more information, refer to Add a Prospect Call to History/Notes , Add a Prospect Email History/Note , or Add a Prospect Visit to History/Notes .

 Next Contact

 Select the date to filter the board based on the number of days until the prospect is contacted again. For example, to view prospects that should be followed up with in two weeks, enter the date 14 days from the current date n the From field.

 More Information

 The prospect's next contact date is established on the Prospect Stage Details pop-up for the prospect's current stage, based on the number of days entered in the Set the Next Contact Date to X days after the Last Contact Date field.

 Sort

 Indicate the order in which prospect cards display in each column. The following sort options are available:

 Application Date - Newest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the newest to oldest Application Date .

 Application Date - Oldest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the oldest to newest Application Date .

 Last Contact - Newest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the newest to oldest Last Contact date.

 Last Contact - Oldest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the oldest to newest Last Contact date.

 Name - First, Last

 Prospect cards are sorted alphabetically by First Name .

 Name - Last, First

 Prospect cards are sorted alphabetically by Last Name .

 Next Contact - Newest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the newest to oldest Next Contact date.

 Next Contact - Oldest

 Prospect cards are sorted by the oldest to newest Next Contact date.

 Property, Unit

 Prospect cards are sorted alphanumerically by property Name , then the property's units.

 Stage

 Prospect cards are sorted by stage order, as defined on Prospect Stages page.

 Unit

 Prospect cards are sorted alphanumerically by unit Name .

 Card Information

 Each prospect in the leasing process displays on the board with their own card.

 You can drag and drop these cards to move a prospect to a different stage column. Additionally, you can change the size of the cards by clicking Small or Large at the top right of the board. Clicking on a card expands it to display all assigned fields. The fields that display vary depending on how the board was set up. The available fields are described below.

 Field
 Description

 Agent

 The name of the Leasing Agent as displayed on the prospect's details page in the Lead Information tile.

 Application Date

 The date the prospect's application was received.

 Application Status

 The current status of the prospect's application ( In Progress , Completed , or Canceled ).

 Balance Due

 The prospect's unpaid balance as of today's date as displayed on the prospect's details page.

 Expected Move in Date

 The date on which the prospect is expected to move in to the unit.

 Interest Level

 The prospect's interest level ( 0 through 10 , with 10 being highest) in your property.

 Last Contact

 The date of the prospect's last contact. The last contact date is determined by the call, email, or visit history/note item on the account with the most recent Start Date .

 Related Preferences

 Call history/note items must have Spoke with prospect checked, and/or the system preference to Always update Last Contact Date when a call note is added enabled, to be considered when determining last contact date. For more information, refer to Prospect (System Preferences) .

 Lead Source

 The main source of information that describes how this prospect learned about your company.

 Lease Start

 The date on which the lease is expected to begin as displayed on the prospect's details page.

 Market Rent

 The market rent of the unit associated with prospect as established on the unit's details page.

 Next Contact

 The date of the prospect's next contact as established on the Prospect Stage Details pop-up for the stage associated with the prospect. If the Next Contact action is behind schedule, a icon displays to the right of the date.

 Original Contact

 The date on which the prospect was first contacted by your company and the communication method used for that contact (such as email or phone call). If the prospect's original contact comes from a guest card/ Apply Now , the type is Web . If the prospect original contact comes from Apply Now , the type is Application .

 Preferred Move in Date

 The date on which the prospect wishes to move in to the unit.

 Rent Quote Amount

 The rent amount for the prospect's reserved rent quote as displayed on the prospect's details page.

 Rent Quote Expiration

 The date on which the rent quote is set to expire and no longer be valid. If there is not an expiration, the field is blank.

 Rent Quote Status

 The status ( Reserved , Expired , or Active ) of the rent quote, which can help property managers keep track of quotes that have been offered, expired, or accepted.

 Screening Status

 The prospect's screening Status as displayed on the prospect's details page in the Screening tile.

 Stage

 The prospect's current stage and the date they were moved to that stage.

 User Defined Fields

 Any prospect-type user-defined fields (UDF) set to display for the prospect stage.
