Unit Occupancy (Pop-Up)

The View Unit Occupancy pop-up displays the occupant name, move-in and move-out dates, and status of past tenants, tenants currently occupying the unit, and/or prospects that have reserved the unit.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Properties/Units Units View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view a unit's occupancy information. go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Unitsand select a unit from the list. Then on the Occupancy tile, click .

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on this pop-up:

Column Description

Occupancy Name

The name of each tenant who leased the unit, is currently leasing the unit, or the prospect who has reserved the unit.

Move In Date

The date when the tenant moved in as entered on the tenant's lease.

Move Out Date

The date when the tenant moved out or will move out as entered on the tenant's details page.

Status

The occupancy status of the unit (e.g., Current Occupant, Past Occupant, Reserved - Current, Reserved - Past).
