# Surveys (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Surveys.htm

Surveys allow you to gain insight and gather opinions from your communities by asking tenants about your properties and units, request suggestions for areas of improvement, and collect feedback for specific service issues. You create surveys to be published to the Tenant Web Access (TWA) portal for tenants to submit their answers, or create surveys with public survey links which allows tenants to submit answers through a portal without needing a TWA account.

 After the survey is published or sent, you may view the individual answers submitted by each tenant and the total results sorted in a bar or donut graph.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this page. By default, some columns display only if added via .

 Default Column
 Description

 Name

 The name of the survey that displays within Rent Manager .

 Display Name

 The name of the survey that displays to tenants.

 Survey Type

 The kind of survey ( Tenant or Service Issue ).

 Properties

 Each property at which the survey is being conducted. This column displays only for surveys that were created to be published to TWA.

 Status

 The current state of the survey.

 Active

 The survey can be distributed to tenants by email and/or text message. This status applies only to surveys that were not created to be published to TWA.

 Completed

 The survey was published to TWA and its End Date passed.

 Draft

 The survey was created and saved but has not been published to TWA.

 Inactive

 The survey cannot be distributed to tenants by email and/or text message. This status applies only to surveys that were not created to be published to TWA.

 In Progress

 The survey was published to TWA and has not yet reached its End Date .

 Voided

 The TWA survey was manually voided by a Rent Manager user.

 End Date

 The final date that the survey allows responses in TWA.

 Number Completed

 The total number of tenants that submitted responses to the survey.

 Available Column
 Description

 Created By

 The user who created the survey.

 Created Date

 The date the survey was created.

 Description

 Additional information about the purpose or goals of the survey to display in Rent Manager .

 Number of Questions

 The total number of questions in the survey.

 Number Sent

 The total number of surveys sent via email and/or text message, or published to TWA.

 Publish Date

 The date the survey was published to TWA.

 Published By

 The user who published the survey to TWA.

 Updated By

 The last user to modify and save the survey.

 Updated Date

 The date the survey was last modified and saved.

 Void Date

 The date the survey published to TWA was voided.

 Voided By

 The user who voided the survey published to TWA.
