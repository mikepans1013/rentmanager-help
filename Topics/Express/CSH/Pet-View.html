Pets (Pop-Up)

The View Pets pop-up allows you to view all the pets associated with the selected tenant or prospect account. In addition to viewing the pets, you can also add and delete any pets associated with the tenant or prospect account.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Tenants View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To manage pets for a tenant, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants|Prospects. Select a tenant or prospect from the list and click on the Pets tile.

When more than one pet is linked to the tenant or prospect account, each pet displays in its own tab on the left of the pop-up.

Field Descriptions

The following fields are available on the View Pets pop-up.

Field Description

Pet Type

The kind of animal, such as a dog or cat, allowed at the property. The pets that are available to add depend on the pet types for the property.

Name

The name of the pet associated with the account.

Weight

The weight of the pet associated with the account. This can be helpful for properties that may have weight limits to abide by.

Description

An optional message about the pet (e.g., Does not play well with other dogs).

Breed

The specific group of animals the pet belongs to. For example, American Bulldog can be entered as a dog breed.

Age

The age of the pet associated with the account. Age can be entered alphanumerically for accuracy (e.g., 5 and 1/2 years).

Color

The color of the pet associated with the account (e.g., White with light beige spots).
