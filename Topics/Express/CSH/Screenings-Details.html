Screening Details (Page)

Rent Manager's screening feature provides the ability to check a prospect's credit, criminal, and eviction history prior to entering a rental agreement. Screening an applicant is one step in determining if an individual has a stable financial and behavioral history.

The Screening Detail page allows you to view information about a completed screening. Additionally, you can resubmit the screening and upgrade the existing screening report by ordering extended manual reference checking based on your specifications with AmRent. For example, you may need to request that a landlord, employment, or criminal reference be checked manually.

More Information

AmRent Screenings can pull credit information from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. All three credit bureaus offer preventive measures to tenants/prospects to help protect credit information. If you are generating a screening via AmRent on a tenant or prospect using one of these preventative measures, the credit portion of the screening comes back as frozen or locked.

A credit freeze must be initiated by the tenant/prospect, through a credit bureau, who provides them a 10-digit code. Access to their credit report is restricted by the credit agency unless the entity generating the screening has been given the 10-digit code by the tenant/prospect. Credit locks are like credit freezes in that they help protect credit information, but they are not regulated by federal law.

To view the Screening Detail page, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Screenings and select a screening from the list.

Applicants

In the Applicants section, if more than one applicant has been screened, select the desired applicant from the list on the left. The selected applicant's screening details display in the summary (i.e., middle) section.

Summary

This section displays general information about the screening. Each column is described in the table below.

To view the tenant or prospect's full screening report from AmRent, click View Report. From this format you can print, export, or download the report.

Column Description

Vendor

The name of the credit bureau (e.g., AmRent, Trans Union, Experian) who performed the screening.

Date

The date the screening was completed.

Factors

The factor(s) that adversely affected the credit score.

Maximum

The highest possible credit score in the range.

Minimum

The lowest possible credit score in the range.

Score

The numerical assessment of the person's creditworthiness.

Information

The Information section displays the automated Workflow Solution based on the results of the screening and the criteria you set up with AmRent. In addition, you can use letter templates to generate a letter that communicates your decision to the person(s) screened.
