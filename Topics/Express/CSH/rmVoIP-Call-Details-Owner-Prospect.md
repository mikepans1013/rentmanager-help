# rmVoIP Owner Prospect Call Details (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/rmVoIP-Call-Details-Owner-Prospect.htm

When you receive a phone call through rmVoIP , Rent Manager attempts to any account in your database with that phone number to immediately provide you with information about the caller. Call records that are linked to owner prospects include basic details about the caller and notes describing the call.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 To view the details of an rmVoIP call linked to a property, go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward rmVoIP Incoming Call History . Then, select a call with the Link Type of Owner Prospect from the list.

 Basic Information

 At the top of the page, the following read-only details about the incoming call display.

 The address marked as Default on the owner prospect account associated with the phone number that placed the call.

 Identifying information provided by the caller's phone service, such as their name, company name, or phone number.

 The date (in MM/DD/YYYY format) and time that the call took place.

 The Rent Manager entity (i.e., Owner Prospect ) linked to the call.

 The ten-digit phone number of the caller.

 Owner Prospect Name

 The name of the owner prospect's Rent Manager account.

 Call History

 The Call History tile displays any additional information to explain the call. This information also displays on the owner prospect's History/Notes .

 Field
 Description

 Category

 The history category related to this comment.

 Comment

 A note explaining the call, such as the purpose for the call, any next steps that need to be taken, the emotional state of the caller, or the resolution.

 Spoke with prospect

 Indicates that the call was answered and a conversation took place with the caller.
