# Asset Title Statuses (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Asset-Title-Statuses.htm

Asset title statuses are conditions that you create to describe the state of the asset's legal ownership (e.g., Clear Title , No Title , Title with Lien ), which helps you track the title regardless of where it is in the ownership process and allows you to filter your assets list by title status.
