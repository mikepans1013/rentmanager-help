# Appointments (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Appointments.htm

Appointments allow you to maintain your busy schedule with Rent Manager . You can even create appointments to recur on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. You can then easily view or access your appointments on the Dashboard with the Appointments tile.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this page:

 Column
 Description

 Title

 The topic of the appointment (e.g., Open House , Owner Visit , Prospect Tour , Cleaning Services ).

 Start Date

 The date and starting time of the appointment.

 End Date

 The date and ending time of the appointment.

 Reminder Date

 The date and time before the scheduled appointment to receive a reminder.

 If the appointment is recurring, the Tasks privileges to Add , View , and Edit are also required.

 View or edit appointments. If this is a single appointment, the details open in the Edit Appointment pop-up. If this is a recurring appointment, choose Open the Series to view the original recurring appointment, or Open the Event to view the selected appointment. Click Save on the Edit Appointment pop-up to save any changes made to the appointment.
