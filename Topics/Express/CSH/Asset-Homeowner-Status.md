# Homeowner Statuses (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Asset-Homeowner-Status.htm

For manufactured housing-type properties, units (or sites) are assigned a classification based on who owns the site, the type of asset(s) present at that site, occupancy status, and other considerations. To ensure that these classifications are correct for home-type assets, such as manufactured homes and lots, the current legal ownership status needs to be tracked and updated for those assets.

 More Information

 This option is available only for assets associated with an Asset Type that is marked as Is Home . For more information, refer to Asset Types (Page) .

 To view an asset's record of homeowner statuses, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Assets and select an asset. Then, in the General tile, click History .

 To determine what site classification to apply to a unit, Rent Manager considers multiple factors, including what Homeowner Status is applied to the associated asset on the unit's lease. To apply a homeowner status, click Add Homeowner Status and select from the prepopulated list of statuses.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on this pop-up.

 Column
 Description

 Start Date

 The date that the homeowner status begins.

 Status

 The legal ownership status (i.e., Community Owned , Employee Owned , etc.) that corresponds with the site classification Rent Manager  automatically applies to the associated unit. For more information, refer to Site Classification .

 User

 The user who originally added the homeowner status.
