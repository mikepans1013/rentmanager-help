# Purchase Order Bill Links (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Purchase-Order-Bill-Links.htm

Purchase orders (POs) are used to request permission to purchase items, prevent unauthorized purchases, and maintain a historical record of items purchased. To bill tenants, prospects, and owners for items purchased through a purchase order, you can create a bill that auto-populates with information from the purchase order and link it to the purchase order or link an existing bill to the purchase order. On a purchase order's details page, the Bills pop-up allows you to view information about bills linked to your POs.
