# Screenings (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Screenings.htm

Rent Manager 's Screenings feature provides the ability to check a prospect's credit, criminal, and eviction history prior to entering a rental agreement. Screening an applicant is one step in determining if an individual has a stable financial and behavioral history.

 A screening can reveal potential credit problems, such as late payments or accumulated debt, criminal-related information that could pose safety concerns, or housing evictions or debts that might reveal a propensity to pay rent late. It can also provide a score and/or decision message for aiding in the conversion from a prospect to a tenant or in renewing.

 More Information

 Although the Screenings feature is accessible within Rent Manager , the company that provides the screening service is AmRent . Registered users can retrieve screening reports that are based on their account screening requirements and can be instantly viewed or printed. For information about setting up your account, contact AmRent .

 More Information

 If you screen your applicants, it is important to know the federal laws and regulations surrounding this process. Did you know that you must obtain authorization from each applicant to collect their consumer information? You can easily do this using Apply Now applications and setting up your application template to include an additional applicant section. Creating a sub-application allows you to collect the required signatures from each applicant, ensuring your compliance when accessing their consumer information. Creating the sub-application also offers the ability to select additional options for screening preferences and application fees for the co-applicant(s). For more information, refer to Apply Now Applications .

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this page.

 Column
 Description

 Name

 The tenant or prospect who was the subject of the screening or income verification request. If the screening or income verification request was run on a person who does not have a tenant or prospect account, this column is blank.

 Regarding

 The name of the person who was screened or whose income verification was requested, and the type of report that was generated. If a screening or income verification request was submitted for multiple people, a semicolon (;) separates the names.

 Date

 The date the screening report or income verification request was submitted.

 User

 The user who ran the initial screening report or income verification request.

 Upgrade User

 The user who ran the upgraded screening report. For income verification requests, this column is blank.

 Upgrade Date

 The date the upgraded screening report was submitted. For income verification requests, this column is blank.

 Status

 The current status of the income verification request. For screenings, Received displays.

 Each status is described below:

 Canceled

 The income verification invitation can no longer be completed by the tenant or prospect. Links expire forty-eight hours being sent.

 Completed

 The income verification report is available on the tenant or prospect account.

 Error

 AmRent encountered an issue during the income verification process and a new income verification invitation link must be sent.

 Expired

 The income verification invitation is no longer available for the applicant to complete.

 If the income verification invitation has expired, but the applicant has requested that they be sent a new link to complete verification, Expired - Link requested displays.

 Invitation Error

 AmRent was unable to send an income verification invitation link to the tenant or prospect. This error is typically caused by an invalid phone number or email address.

 Submitted

 An income verification invitation link has been delivered to the tenant or prospect.

 Report Type

 The type of screening report run on the account. For income verification requests, Income Verification displays.
