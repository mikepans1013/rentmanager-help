# Contact Types (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Contact-Types.htm

Contact types are categories that can be assigned to the people/contacts that are associated with a prospect, tenant, or vendor and can provide a more regimented breakdown of these individuals as your business requires.

 For example, you may want to identify a tenant contact as a roommate or a vendor contact as a manager. If contact types are defined, you may assign each contact listed on an account to a contact type in the Contacts tile on the Tenant and Vendor details page.
