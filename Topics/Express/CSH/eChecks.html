eChecks (Page)

Safeguard eChecks streamline bill payments and financial transactions by eliminating the need for physical checks. By utilizing eChecks in Rent Manager, you can electronically write and deliver checks to your recipient, who can then receive and print the check via an email link.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Filter Options

The following filtering options are available on this page.

Option Description

Filter By Check Date

Enter a From and/or To date to display only those transactions dated within the range.

Saved Filters

Use the drop-down to select a saved filter or click to create a filter that narrows the list of entities that display in the list by using other criteria.

Search

Enter the desired search criteria to display results that include the criteria.

Show Sent Status Only

Check to display only eChecks currently assigned to the status Sent.

Column Descriptions

Column Description

Amount

The total dollar amount of the eCheck.

Bank Account

The bank account from which funds are drawn.

Check No.

The identifying number of the eCheck.

Date

The date on which the eCheck was issued to the payee.

Memo

An optional message providing additional information about the eCheck.

Payee

The recipient of the payment.

Payee Email

The vendor, owner, prospect, or tenant email address that is associated with the payee of the eChecks.

Status

The current status of the eChecks.

Each status is described below:

Expired

The eCheck expired. eChecks more than 90 days old without being cashed will expire. Expired eChecks can be reissued from the eChecks site.

Retrieved

The payee received the eChecks email and retrieved the eCheck.

Returned

Safeguard attempted to process the eCheck, but an error prevented it from being sent to the payee.

Sent

The check was processed by Safeguard and sent to the recipient, but the recipient has not yet retrieved the eCheck.

Voided

The eCheck was voided.
