# Inventory Item Notes (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Inventory-Item-Notes.htm

Notes allow you to create categories for inventory items listed on an invoice or estimate. These notes display as headings on the invoice or estimate to preface a breakdown of the items being billed to the tenant, prospect, or owner.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on this page.

 Column
 Description

 The name of the note to display on the inventory item listed on the invoice or estimate.
