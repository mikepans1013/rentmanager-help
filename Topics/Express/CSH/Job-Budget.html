Job Budget (Pop-Up)

The job costing feature tracks transactions associated with specific jobs completed by your business. On a job's Budget pop-up, you can plan budgeted amounts for general ledger (GL) accounts to assist with measuring expenses versus projected cost for the job.

Related Preferences

In order to use the job costing tool in Rent Manager, Enable job costing must be checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences).

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting Jobs View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Jobs and select a job from the list. Then, on the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Budget.

In the Comment field on the left, you can add additional explanations or notes about the budget.

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on the pop-up:

Column Description

Account

The GL account under which the budgeted item is recorded.

Type

The type of the GL account.

Comment

An additional explanation or purpose for the budget item. These comments display on the job's details page in the Budget tile.

Amount

The total dollar amount budgeted for this item.
