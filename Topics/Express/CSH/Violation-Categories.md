# Violation Categories (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Violation-Categories.htm

To make the most out of using violations in Rent Manager , you need to create categories as a way to organize collections of violation codes that are similar. Since rules and regulations can be extensive, assigning a category such as Parking or Lawn Care can help you arrange your codes.
