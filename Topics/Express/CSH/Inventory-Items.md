# Inventory Items (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Inventory-Items.htm

Inventory items are products you sell or services you provide. They can be tracked items (tangible items that you hold in stock) or non-tracked items (such as services you provide or items you do not stock or track). In Rent Manager , you can maintain a list of products and services for which you can submit an invoice, issue, bill or purchase order. Maintaining such a list of items helps to ensure consistency when entering data and makes it easier to search for items when they are used in various places.

 Because each inventory item is part of a sales transaction, each one requires a corresponding income charge type and expense chart account. You also determine if each item is active (selectable) and/or taxable.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this page. Columns can be added or removed from this page by clicking .

 Default Column
 Description

 Name

 The name of the item as it displays in lists and on estimates, purchase orders, service issues, and invoices.

 Description

 A brief description of the product or service. This is displayed in the memo field on estimates and purchase orders.

 Quantity

 The number of available inventory items.

 Markup

 The additional percentage or fixed amount that is added to the cost of the product or service as a markup.

 Cost

 The purchase price (the price you pay) per unit or item.

 Expense Chart Account

 The account that is expensed whenever this inventory item is purchased.

 For example, this expense account would be used if this item is included in a purchase order or vendor bill from a service issue.

 Income Charge Type

 The charge type that displays on service issues, invoices, etc. This charge should be linked to an income account that collects the money received from the sales of your inventory items.

 Available Column

 Description

 Active

 The inventory item is marked as active on the inventory item's details page.

 Asset Chart Account

 The general ledger (GL) account is used to track the value of the inventory that is held on hand.

 COGS Chart Account

 The GL account is used to track the cost burden of selling inventory items on hand.

 Location

 The physical location of the item, such as the bin number.

 PO Workflow

 The workflow that determines the sequence of events an inventory item follows for reviews, approvals, and purchase order fulfillment.

 Reorder Quantity

 The minimum number of items to keep in stock that is entered on the inventory item's details page.

 Taxable

 The item is marked as taxable on the inventory item's details page.

 Inventory Status Descriptions

 Items that have been added to Rent Manager are color-coded to reflect their inventory status:

 Color
 Description

 Red

 No Quantity. The item has no units in stock.

 Black

 Non-inventory. The item is not tracked (on the item's details page, Track Inventory is not checked and the Inventory Transactions section displays no information).

 Blue

 Reorder. The quantity on hand is less than the Reorder Qty .

 Green

 In Stock. The item has at least one unit in stock.
