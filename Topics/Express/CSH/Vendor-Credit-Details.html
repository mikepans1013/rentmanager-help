Vendor Credit Details (Pop-Up)

Vendors can give you credits for various reasons, such as when you overpay a bill, when services are not performed, or when goods are not available. On the Vendor Credit detail page, you can view who created a vendor credit as well as edit the vendor credit. Creating a vendor credit in Rent Manager allows you to apply that credit to bill(s) that you pay to the vendor. The page also includes a section that displays general details about the credit as well the Previous Allocations tab, which shows bills you have already applied part of this credit toward, and the Available Bills tab, which shows unpaid bills that you can apply the credit to.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Vendor credits View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view the Vendor Credit detail page, go to  arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Vendor Credits and select a credit from the list.

Credit Details

The following basic information displays on the vendor credit:

Fields Description

Vendor

The vendor who issued the credit.

Property

The property where the credit can be applied.

Unit

The unit to receive the credit. If the credit applies to more than one unit, <Multiple Units> displays.

Account

The general ledger account in which the credit is entered. The account selected should correspond with the account on the bill for to which the credit is applied.

For example, if the vendor credit is created using 6402 Materials expense account, the bill being paid should have a corresponding expense account such as 5105 Maintenance.

Job

If applicable, the job associated with the credit to ensure it displays within the transactions associated with the job.

Related Preferences

In order to use the job costing tool in Rent Manager, Enable job costing must be checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences).

Date

The date the credit was issued.

Amount

The total amount of the credit issued by the vendor

Remaining

The amount of the vendor credit that is available and can be applied when paying a bill.

Attachment

Any attachments relevant to this vendor credit.

Click to browse your computer for a file to attach to the note.

Reference

The reference number (e.g., invoice or account number).

Comment

An optional comment.

Previous Allocations

If this credit was already allocated to a bill, click the Previous Allocations tab to view information regarding how the credit was applied. The following information displays on the tab:

Column Description

Bill Reference

The Invoice number of the bill that the vendor credit was applied to.

Bill Comment

The message in the Memo field on the bill that the vendor credit was applied to.

Property

The property that the bill expensed.

Unit

If applicable, the unit that the bill expensed.

Expense Account

The GL account the bill expensed.

Allocation Date

The date when the vendor credit was applied to the bill.

Allocation Amount

The amount of the vendor credit that was applied to the bill.

Detail Comment

The message in the Memo column entered on the bill that the vendor credit was applied to.

Available Bills

If this vendor has unpaid bills to which this credit can be applied, click the Available Bills tab to view information about the remaining amount of the credit and to allocate the credit to the bill. The following bill information displays on the tab:

Column Description

Vendor

The vendor to be paid by the bill.

Reference

The reference number (e.g., invoice or account number) as entered on the bill.

Bill Date

The date the bill was created.

Due Date

The date by which this bill must be paid.

Bill Amount

The total dollar amount of this bill.

Amount Due

The amount of the bill remaining to be paid after any allocations have been applied.

Allocation Date

The date the credit allocation is created. By default, today's date displays.

Amount To Credit

The amount of the credit to apply to the bill payment.

Amount Allocated

If applicable, the amount of the credit that was already allocated to this bill.

Post Date

The date this bill posts to the associated GL account.

Memo

The message in the Memo field entered on the unpaid bill that is eligible to receive a vendor credit.

Properties

The property expensed by the bill.

Units

If applicable, the unit expensed by the bill.
