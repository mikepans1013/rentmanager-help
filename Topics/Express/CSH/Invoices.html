Invoices (Page)

Invoices are bills that are sent to tenants or owners for products and services. For example, you can create invoices when you charge owners for management fees or when tenants reimburse you for upgrades to their units. Invoices display as new one-time charges on a tenant, prospect, or owner's accounts.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Sales/Invoicing Invoices View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view your list of invoices, go to  arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Invoices.

On the Invoices page, each invoice displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on an invoice to open the Invoice details page for that account, where information specific to that invoice is available.

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on the page. By default, some columns display only by using .

Default Column Description

Account

The owner, tenant, or prospect who receives the invoice.

Date

The date that the invoice was created. This should match the date that the invoice was sent to the recipient.

Invoice Number

The system-generated number that identifies the invoice.

Property

The property to which the invoice is linked. By default, the property management company specified in system preferences displays.

Unit

For a Tenant invoice, the unit to which the invoice is linked.

Total Amount

The final total dollar amount of all items for the invoice.

Account Type

The account type (Tenant, Owner, or Prospect) that is receiving the invoice.

Amount Paid

The total dollar amount that was paid toward the invoice.

Balance Due

The total dollar amount of the remaining balance.

Available Column

Description

Account #

The system-generated ID of the tenant, prospect, or owner account that receives the invoice.

Address

The address marked as Default on the recipient's details page.

City

The city of the address marked as Default on the recipient's details page.

Due Date

The date by which the invoice must be paid by the recipient.

Postal Code

The zip or postal code of the address marked as Default on the recipient's details page.

Property Short Name

The Short Name of the property associate with the invoice.

Sales Rep

The user with the Sales Rep/Leasing Agent option enabled who is selected on the invoice.

State

The state of the address marked as Default on the recipient's details page.

Street

The street of the address marked as Default on the recipient's details page.

Term

The amount of time after creation before the invoice must be paid by the recipient.
