# Estimates (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Estimates.htm

Estimates allow you to record the anticipated parts and labor needed to complete a project, itemize the costs, and provide a total amount due including any markups and/or taxes you add. Estimates can also be linked to an invoice, service issue, and/or purchase order.

 On the Estimates page, each estimate displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on an estimate to open the details page for that estimate, where information specific to that estimate is available.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on the page.

 Column
 Description

 Account

 The owner, tenant, or prospect account to be expensed by bills associated with this estimate.

 Active

 A displays if the estimate is active and can be used to record expected labor and parts expenses.

 Create Date

 The date that the estimate was created.

 Date

 The date that the estimated work is planned to take place.

 Estimate #

 The system-generated number for the estimate.

 Job

 If applicable, the Name of the job, as it displays on the job's General tile, associated with the estimate. For more information, refer to Jobs (Page) .

 Related Preferences

 In order to use the job costing tool in Rent Manager , Enable job costing must be checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences) .

 Reference

 A reference number or additional identifier for the estimate.

 Total Value

 The total dollar amount of all inventory items, including any added markup or tax.
