# Asset Locations (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Asset-Locations.htm

From the asset's details page, you can define the physical location of an asset and link it to a property and/or unit. Additionally, you can track the dates on which the asset is at a location as a history of where and when the asset was moved between properties.

 Filter Options

 The following filter options are available on this pop-up.

 Option
 Descriptions

 Service Life Start

 The date on which the asset was first placed at a unit or property.

 Service Life End

 The date on which the use of this asset by your company ends, or is expected to end. The asset can no longer be used past this date.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this pop-up.

 Column
 Description

 Property

 The property where the asset is physically located.

 Unit

 The unit where the asset is physically located.

 From

 The first date on which the asset is located at the property and unit.

 To

 The last date on which the asset is located at the property and unit.
