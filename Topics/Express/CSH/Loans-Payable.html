Loans Payable (Page)

Rent Manager provides a loan management tool to help users create and manage loan payments. Payable loans are typically used for mortgages but can also be used for other loan types such as for land, equipment, appliances, auto, and so on. For example, loan payables are used to track your expense loans (e.g., with vendors), while loan receivables are used to track loans you are managing (i.e., charging) for your tenants and/or clients.

The Loans Payable page allows you to track outstanding loan balances, manage a loan's details, and easily record loan payment transactions.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available on this page. By default, some columns display only if added via .

Column Description

Name

The unique name used to identify the loan.

Loan Number

The number of loan assigned by the lender, which can typically be found in the loan's paperwork.

Property

The name of the property that is financially impacted by the loan.

Lender

The financial institution, associated with a vendor account, who provided the loan and receives payments.

Automation

A displays for loans with automated loan payments as established on the loan's details page on the Payment Info tile.

Rate

The interest rate percentage used to calculate interest for monthly payments.

Remaining Principal

The loan's principal remaining balance.

Payment

The monthly payment amount of the loan using the following equation:

Payment = Monthly Principal + Interest + Monthly Escrow/Other Costs

Available Columns

Description

Asset

The name of the asset associated with the loan.

Bank

The bank account used to pay the loan.

Due Day

The day of the month that the loan payment is due to the lender as established on the loan's details page on the Payment Info tile.

Interest Method

The method (30/360, 30/365, Actual/360 (365/360), Actual/365 (365/365), Actual/Actual) used to calculate interest for the loan.

Interest Type

The loan's interest type (Fixed or Variable).

Maturity

The date, in a MM/DD/YY format, the final payment of the loan is due.

Next Payment

The date, in a MM/DD/YY format, the next monthly payment is due.

Original Principal

The total amount distributed to the borrower and is expected to be paid back over the term of the loan.

Origination

The date, in a MM/DD/YY format, the lender distributed the finds to the borrower.

Payment Method

The payment method (Check, ACH, EFT, Journal) used to record payments on the loan.

Refinanced

Indicates if the loan was refinanced.

Term

The total number of months in the term of the loan.
