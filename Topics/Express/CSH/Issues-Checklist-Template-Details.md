# Checklist Template Details (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Issues-Checklist-Template-Details.htm

Checklist templates are saved lists of to-do items that you can use to easily populate the Checklist section of issues in Rent Manager . Checklist templates are helpful for tasks that always require the same steps to complete, such as painting interior walls or changing locks.

 The Checklist Template details pop-up allows you to view the list of items on the template, the user responsible for those items, and modify or add information as needed.

 To view the Checklist Template details pop-up, go to arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Checklist Templates . On the template you wish to view, click arrow_forward Details .

 The following fields are available on this page:

 Field
 Description

 Name

 The unique name used to identify the checklist template.

 Is Active

 Indicates if the template can be used to populate the Checklist section of an issue.
