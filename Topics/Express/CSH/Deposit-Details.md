# Deposit Details (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Deposit-Details.htm

The Deposit details page allows you to view and manage deposit information and, when applicable, make additions or changes to the deposit to match what occurred outside of Rent Manager . From this page, you can view details on individual deposits, including the bank account, date, deposit amount, breakdown of payments, and associated properties.

 To view a deposit's details, go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Banking arrow_forward Bank Register and select a deposit from the list.

 General Information

 In this tile, the bank account, deposit date, transaction types, and any comments or attachments associated with the deposit display.

 The following fields are available in this section:

 Field
 Description

 Attachments

 Any images or documents relevant to the deposit, such as a digital copy of the original invoice. To upload a file, click Upload or to paste an image from your clipboard, click Paste .

 Bank Account

 The bank to which this deposit is made.

 Comments

 Any description or notes you want to add about the deposit.

 Deposit Date

 The date the payments were deposited into the bank account.

 Payment Method

 The breakdown of payment methods that make up the deposit.

 Cash

 The total amount of physical cash that was deposited.

 Check

 The total amount of physically routed checks that were deposited.

 Money Orders

 The total amount of money orders that were deposited.

 Other

 The amount of any other form of payment included in the deposit.

 Payments

 In this tile, the individual payments associated with the deposit display, including information about the properties, tenants, units, etc. associated with the deposit.

 To add any additional line items to the deposit, click Add Payments .

 The following columns are available in this section:

 Column
 Description

 Address

 The address associated with the deposit.

 Amount

 The total dollar amount of the deposit.

 Check #

 If applicable, the number of the check that was deposited.

 Comment

 Any notes or other information you wish to add about the deposit.

 Date

 The date the funds were deposited into the selected bank account.

 Property

 Any property associated with this deposit.

 Search

 Type in the Search field to narrow the list to match your search criteria.

 Tenant

 Any tenants whose payments are associated with this deposit.

 Unit

 Any unit associated with this deposit.

 Other Income

 In this section, information about other forms of income associated with the deposit display, including property, unit, account, and amount information. Other income can include payments such as vendor refund checks or laundry room income.

 Related Privileges

 This section displays only if other income is added to the deposit, otherwise the Add Other Income button displays instead.

 The following columns are available in this section:

 Column
 Description

 Amount

 The total dollar amount of this income to deposit for the property/unit.

 Income Account

 The general ledger (GL) account from your chart of accounts used to track this income.

 Job

 If this income can be associated with a job, select the job from the drop-down list. For more information, refer to Jobs (Page) .

 Related Preferences

 In order to use the job costing tool in Rent Manager , Enable job costing must be checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences) .

 Memo

 An optional message explaining this income item. This memo displays on various deposit reports.

 Property

 The property that earned or collected the income.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Reference

 The payment type. You can choose CASH , MO (money order), CC (credit card), or, if paying with a check, enter the check number.

 Unit

 The unit that earned or collected the income.

 Vendor/Owner

 If applicable, the account type and specific account that provided the income.
