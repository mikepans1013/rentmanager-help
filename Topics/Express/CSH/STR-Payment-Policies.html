STR Payment Policies (Page)

Short term rental (STR) payment policies allow users to establish sets of rules for how guests can pay for their reservations. For example, some of your STR properties may require the guest to pay the full amount at the time they book the reservation, while other STR properties only require a partial payment during booking and allow the guest to pay the rest at a later date.

STR payment policies are only available for RV/Campground type properties.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage STR Payment Policies Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Warning

To access the STR Online Booking tool, at least one of your properties in Rent Manager must have the RV/Campground property type assigned.

To view and manage STR payment policies, go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward  Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Short Term Rentals arrow_forward STR Payment Policies.

To include STR payment policies that are no longer active in your search results, check Show Inactive.

Column Descriptions

Columns on the STR Payment Policies page display the following information:

Column Description

Active

A displays when the policy is active.

Name

The title used to describe the payment policy.

Due At Booking

The flat dollar amount the guest is required to pay when the guest reserves their spot, or a percentage of the total cost that the guest is required to pay when a reservation is set. If the entered number is a percentage, check Percent.

Remainder Due

The number of days prior to a guest's stay that they must pay the rest of the balance due for the reservation. For example, if the Remainder Due is set to 2 and the reservation's Arrival date is on 5/20/2026, then the remaining balance must be paid on or before 5/18/2026.
