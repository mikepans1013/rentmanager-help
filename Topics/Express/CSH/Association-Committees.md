# Association Committees (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Association-Committees.htm

If your property is an association such as a homeowners association (HOA), you may have committees of people related to various aspects of the association (e.g., a social committee). If you have a committee that reviews requests for architectural changes to a home such as planting trees, adding gardens, or painting, committee members may view and vote on tenant requests in the Tenant Web Access (TWA) portal.

 The Committees page allows you to add new committees, manage your active committees, and deactivate committees that no longer exist. Once you've created your committees on this page, you can assign homeowners to a committee on the property's Committee Members pop-up. Additionally, you can establish Association Settings , which determine what all committee members see on the Architectural Requests page in Tenant Web Access .

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on the page.

 Column
 Description

 Active

 Determines whether or not you are able to assign members to the committee on the Committee Members pop-up.

By default, this box is checked.

 More Information

 If there are already members assigned to a committee, you cannot mark the committee as inactive until you remove the members.

 Name

 The name of the committee that displays in Rent Manager .

 The name of the committee that displays in Tenant Web Access .

 A note to explain the purpose of the committee.
