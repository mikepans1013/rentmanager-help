# Rent Quotes (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Prospect-Rent-Quotes.htm

Rent quotes allow you to offer prospective tenants a price for units they are interested in with various criteria. You can view and edit the details of a prospect's existing rent quote. If you have created multiple quotes for different units, amounts, or lease terms for the prospect, you can view an organized grid of each quote.

 To view a prospect's rent quote(s), go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select a prospect. Then, on the Rent Quotes tile, click .

 If the prospect has only one rent quote, the Rent Quote Details pop-up displays automatically. If the prospect has multiple rent quotes, a tile displays for each quote. To view the details pop-up for a quote, click arrow_forward Details . For more information regarding the fields in this pop-up, refer to Add a Prospect Rent Quote .

 Field Descriptions

 The fields that display vary depending on whether the prospect has one or multiple rent quotes associated with their account.

 Multiple Rent Quotes

 If the prospect has multiple rent quotes, the Rent Quotes pop-up displays with a tile for each rent quote. The name of the property and unit associated with the quote displays at the top of each tile.

 The following fields display on each tile:

 Field
 Description

 Additional

 The total of all recurring charges with non-rent charge types in the rent quote’s Recurring Charges section.

 Date Quoted

 The date the quote was given to the prospect.

 Lease Term

 The duration of the lease for which the quote price is applicable. For more information, refer to Lease Terms (Page) .

 If the quoted unit is reserved, in the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, the Lease Term field is pre-populated with this selection.

 Move In

 The date on which the prospect plans to move into the unit.

 If the quoted unit is reserved, in the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, the Expected Lease Start ,  Reserved Move In , and Expected Lease End fields are pre-populated based on this date and the selected Lease Term .

 Offer Expiration

 The date that the rent quote is no longer valid. To view expired quotes, uncheck Hide expired quotes . This date displays in red for expired quotes.

 One-Time Charges

 The total of all charges selected in the rent quote’s One-time Charges section.

 Rent

 The dollar amount quoted for rent to the prospect for the corresponding Unit and Lease Term .

 Total

 The total of all recurring charges in the rent quote’s Recurring Charges section.

 Single Rent Quote

 If the prospect has only one rent quote or you opened the details of one of their multiple rent quotes, the Rent Quote Details pop-up displays with editable fields.

 The following fields display:

 Field
 Description

 Amount

 The dollar amount to offer the prospect for the selected other rentable item (ORI) unit. To use the value established for the unit's Market Rent or a rent recurring charge amount set up on the unit, unit type, or property level, click Select Amount , choose one of the listed options, and click Save .

 Charge Type

 The rent charge type to use for the quoted recurring charge on the unit or ORI unit. The charge types displayed are based on the charge types selected on the property's Rent Charge Types field. This field displays in the Primary Unit and Other Rentable Item (ORI) Units sections.

 Comment

 An internal note to provide additional information about the rent quote, such as the procedure if the quote expires.

 Date Quoted

 The date the quote was given to the prospect.

 Lease Term

 The duration of the lease for which the quote price is applicable. For more information, refer to Lease Terms (Page) .

 If the quoted unit is reserved, in the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, the Lease Term field is pre-populated with this selection.

 Move In

 The date on which the prospect plans to move into the unit.

 If the quoted unit is reserved, in the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, the Expected Lease Start ,  Reserved Move In , and Expected Lease End fields are pre-populated based on this date and the selected Lease Term .

 Property

 The property associated with the unit or ORI unit associated with the rent quote. This field displays in the Primary Unit and Other Rentable Item (ORI) Units sections.

 Rent

 The dollar amount quoted for rent to the prospect for the corresponding Unit and Lease Term .

 Unit

 The unit or ORI unit for which the prospect has been offered a rent quote. This field displays in the Primary Unit and Other Rentable Item (ORI) Units sections.

 To view a filtered list of the property's units or ORI units, click . For more information, refer to Other Rentable Item (ORI) Unit Selector (Pop-Up) .
