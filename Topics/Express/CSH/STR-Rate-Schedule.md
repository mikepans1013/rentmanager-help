# STR Rate Schedules (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/STR-Rate-Schedule.htm

Short term rental (STR) rate schedules allow you to maintain rates used to charge for reservations, providing you with an easy way to calculate STR rates. The process of charging rates can be complex due to the variety of reservation fees, seasonal fees, pet fees, and so on. STR rate schedules define each rate within the schedule on a property and automatically apply to reservations that fit the requirements set up on the schedule. It also allows for multiple schedules per property, as you may have one schedule for in-season rentals, another for off-season rentals, and anything in between.

 Warning

 To access the STR Online Booking tool, at least one of your properties in Rent Manager must have the RV/Campground property type assigned.

 To view an STR property's rate schedules, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties and select a property with a Property Type of RV/Campground from the list. On the STR Rate Schedules tile, click to open the Rate Schedule pop-up.

 To view past and inactive rate schedules, at the top of the pop-up, check Show History

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on this pop-up.

 Option
 Description

 Name

 The unique name of the rate schedule.

 Start Date

 The first day the rate schedule is considered active. Reservations for dates on or after the start date are eligible for the rate schedule.

 End Date

 The last day the rate schedule is considered active.
