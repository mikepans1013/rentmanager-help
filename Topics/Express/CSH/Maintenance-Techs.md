# Maintenance Techs (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Maintenance-Techs.htm

The Maintenance Schedule feature gives you the ability to turn your Rent Manager users into maintenance techs that can be easily assigned to issues. From the Maintenance Techs page, you can set what time blocks they are available to work each day and what time off they have planned to have an accurate view of which users are available to complete work. Only the users that you designate as maintenance techs display with their hours of availability and can be added to maintenance groups so their assigned issues can be scheduled through the Maintenance Schedule page.

 More Information

 The Maintenance Schedule feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. However, a license is not required to use the Maintenance Techs page. Additionally, if you are licensed for rmAppSuite Pro , users added to this page have access to the application's Service feature. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 Hours Tab

 Each maintenance tech can be assigned their own unique set of available working hours. This tab displays the time blocks during the week the maintenance tech can be assigned scheduled issues.

 Time Off Tab

 To help you keep up with which maintenance techs won't be available for a certain period of time, you can use the Time Off tab.

 The following columns are available on this tab:

 Column
 Description

 Date

 The full date, including the month and day of the week, of the schedule time off for the maintenance tech.

 Duration

 The time off, in hours, for the maintenance tech.

 Description

 Details regarding the reason for the time off for the maintenance tech.
