# Lease Renewal Board (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Lease-Renewal-Offers-Board.htm

The Lease Renewal Board provides a panoramic view of your lease renewal landscape, enabling you to instantly identify where each tenant stands in their renewal process, organized by the offer's workflow status. Additionally, this tool also makes it easy to spot when a renewal offer requires a signature and flags when a tenant's current lease and/or renewal offer has expired, ensuring timely engagements that keep momentum going. What’s more, you can execute critical actions (write letters, send emails, add history/note items, void or delete offers, etc.) directly from the board.

 More Information

 Lease renewals are generated only for residential leases, so any leases marked as a Commercial Lease do not generate lease renewals.

 Card Information

 Each tenant in the lease renewal process displays on the board with their own card. You can change the size of the cards by selecting Small or Large in the View drop-down at the top right of the board. If a tenant has multiple units or assets linked to their renewal, you can click the icon to the right of the primary unit to view all associated units and assets. Clicking on a card expands it to display all assigned fields.

 The fields that display vary depending on the renewal's status. The available fields are described below.

 Field
 Description

 Current Lease End

 The date on which the tenant's lease officially ends, as displayed on the lease details pop-up Lease End field. If the action is behind schedule, a icon displays to the right of the date.

 Current Lease Term

 The tenant's current lease term (e.g., MTM , 12 Months , 24 Months , etc.), as displayed on the lease details pop-up Lease Term field.

 Delivery Method

 The delivery method selected for the tenant's renewal offer (e.g., Write Letters , Compose Email , etc.).

 Document Expiration

 The date the document expires, as displayed in the document's Expiration Date field.

 Document Status

 The status ( Published or Expired) of the tenant's lease renewal offer.

 Last Updated

 The date the tenant's account was last updated.

 Number of Signers

 The number of assigned signers who have signed the lease renewal offer.

 Offer Expiration

 The date the tenant's lease renewal offer expires, as displayed on the Renewal Offer Details pop-up. If the action is behind schedule, a icon displays to the right of the date.

 Renewal Start Date

 The date the tenant's lease renewal offer starts, as displayed on the Renewal Offer Details pop-up.

 Renewal Status

 The current status ( Waiting Tenant Signature , Blue Moon Pending , Offer Accepted , etc.) of the tenant's renewal offer.

 Terms Offered

 The lease term (e.g., MTM , 12 Months , 24 Months , etc.) for the tenant's lease renewal offer.

 Terms Selected

 The lease term (e.g., MTM , 12 Months , 24 Months , etc.) selected by the tenant.

 Updated By

 The name of the user who last updated the tenant's account.
