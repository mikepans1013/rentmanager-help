# Property Owners (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Property-Owners.htm

From a property's details page, you can assign the owners of a property and determine the percentage of their ownership. You can also set start and end dates for their contract(s) and establish a reserve amount for the property.

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To view the owners assigned to a property, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties and select a property from the list. Then, on the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Owners .

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on the page:

 Column
 Description

 Primary Owner

 The owner with the principal ownership share for this property or, if the owners have equal shares, the main owner contact for the property.

 Owner

 The name of each owner with an ownership stake in this property.

 Start

 The date on which the owner's contract with this company begins. This is also the date the owner is considered active in Rent Manager .

 End

 The date on which the owner's contract with this company ends. When an owner's contract ends, rather than delete them from this page, enter the end date of their contract. This maintains your records related to the owner.

 %

 The percentage of the property belonging to this owner.

 Reserve

 The minimum amount of money that must remain in the bank to cover expenses for each property. This reserve amount can have an impact on owner distributions.
