AvidInvoice (Page)

Once vendors submit their bills to AvidXchange, you can load these invoices into Rent Manager. For your convenience, AvidInvoice prepares groups of invoices which can be imported into Rent Manager as batches. Each invoice in the imported batch creates a one-time bill.

To ensure your vendors and bills are up to date and match AvidXchange's records, you can sync your Rent Manager vendor data with AvidXchange's records at any time.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

To view the AvidInvoice Batches page, go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward AvidXchange arrow_forward AvidInvoice.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available on this page.

Column Description

Batch Name

The name of the invoice batch from AvidXchange.

Date

The date on which AvidXchange collected and downloaded the invoice batch.

Bill Count

The total number of invoices in the batch.

User

The name of the AvidXchange user who created the batch.

Amount

The total dollar amount of all invoices in the batch.
