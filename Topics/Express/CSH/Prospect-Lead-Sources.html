Prospect Lead Sources (Page)

On the Lead Sources page, you can manage the different ways a prospect could learn about your business and available properties. Tracking these sources can be beneficial for certain prospecting reports (e.g., Prospect Box Score) that assess the marketing strategies you have employed.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Lead sources View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view a list of your prospect lead sources, go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Lead Sources.

On the Lead Sources page, each lead source displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on a lead source to open the details page for that lead source, where information specific to that lead source is available.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available the page:

Column Description

Name

The unique name used to identify the lead source.

Call In DID

The phone number associated with the lead source so Rent Manager's integrated phone service, rmVoIP, can track call activity with the lead source.

Active

A displays when the lead source is active and can be used for tracking prospects for reporting purposes.
