Post AvidPay (Page)

AvidXchange, a provider of accounts payable (AP) and payment automation solutions, and Rent Manager have partnered to provide a better accounts payable (AP) experience. If you manually send bill payments to vendors through AvidPay, you need to post those bills in Rent Manager to initiate payment.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Submit user's own AvidPay check batches Enabled

To post AvidPay check batches created by another user, the Submit other user's AvidPay check batches privilege must also be Enabled.

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view the Post AvidPay page, go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward AvidXchange arrow_forward Post AvidPay.

Bills that are paid on the Pay Bills page and also have AvidPay selected as the Pay Method, as well as checks that have AvidPay selected as the Pay Method, display on the Post AvidPay page.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available on this page.

Column Description

A displays if the check is marked to be paid.

Date

The date on which the check was issued to the payee.

Vendor

The vendor receiving the payment.

Bank Account

The bank from which funds are drawn.

Memo

Any additional information about the check. The memo displays on the printed check and on the associated transaction pages for the payee.

Check No.

The check number, which begins with AV to designate it as an AvidPay check.

Amount

The total amount of the check.
