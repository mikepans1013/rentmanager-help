Bank Sync Setup Page (Page)

The Bank Sync feature allows you to import transactions directly from your real world bank or credit card account into Rent Manager during your reconciliation process. This allows you to easily import these transactions instead of obtaining a monthly statement from the bank to import or enter manually.

This feature uses a built-in integration with Plaid to safely and securely connect with your bank or credit card institution. You can add any accounts you have open with that institution so that no matter what bank or credit card general ledger (GL) account you perform a reconciliation on, you can sync your transactions in real time.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting Manage Bank Sync Setup Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view and manage your bank and credit card connections, go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Accounting Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Bank Sync Setup.

Column Descriptions

The following columns are available on this pop-up:

Column Description

Institution

The name of the financial institution that holds your real world bank and/or credit card account(s).

Description

The unique description for the institution that holds your accounts. This is for your reference within Rent Manager to identify the institution associated with the accounts.

Account No

The account number of the associated bank or credit card account.

Linked Account

The general ledger (GL) account to which the bank or credit card is mapped.

Account Type

The type of institution account (Bank or Credit Card).

Row Actions

The following row actions are available from the menu of the institution:

Option Description

Details

View the name of the Institution and the unique Description of its associated accounts.

Manage Connected Accounts

Launch the Plaid Link integration in order to add an existing account to the associated institution.

Delete Connection

Delete the institution's connection and remove all its associated accounts.

Update Linked Account

View the institution linked to the account, its account number, and optionally edit its associated GL account. This action is available only from the menu of the account.
