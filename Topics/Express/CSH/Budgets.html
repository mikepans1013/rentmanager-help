Budget (Page)

Creating and tracking budgets is important for measuring actual income and expenses versus projected revenue and costs. Rent Manager makes budgeting easy by allowing you to enter data manually, copy a budget from the previous year, or import a budget file.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Budgets View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view your budgets, go to  arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward  Accounting Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Budget. In the Property field, select the property whose budget you wish to review.

More Information

Only properties to which you have access display. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property.

Related Preferences

You may set Budget Exceeded Warnings to display on financial transactions that may approach or exceed your budget goals in system preferences. For more information, refer to Budget (System Preferences).

Profit & Loss

This tab displays the existing income and expense accounts in the leftmost column and a column for each month in sequential order from left to right. Based on the amounts you enter in the budget, Rent Manager calculates and displays the yearly and monthly Total Expenses and Net Income at the bottom of the budget.

Balance Sheet

This tab displays existing asset, liabilities, and equity accounts in the leftmost column, followed by a Final column and a column for each month in sequential order from left to right. At the bottom of each section, Rent Manager calculates and displays the monthly Total Assets, Total Liability, Total Equity, and Liability + Equity based on the amounts you enter in the budget.
