# Owner Prospect Lead Sources (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Owner-Prospect-Lead-Sources.htm

On the Owner Prospect Lead Sources page, you can manage the different ways an owner prospect could learn about your business and available properties. Tracking these sources can be beneficial for certain prospecting reports (e.g., Owner Prospect Box Score ) that assess the marketing strategies you have employed.

 On the Owner Prospect Lead Sources page, each lead source displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on a lead source to open the details page for that lead source, where information specific to that lead source is available.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on the page:

 Column
 Description

 Name

 The unique name used to identify the lead source.

 Call in DID

 The phone number associated with the lead source so Rent Manager ’s integrated phone service, rmVoIP , can track call activity with the lead source.

 Active

 A displays when the lead source is active and can be used for tracking owner prospects for reporting purposes.
