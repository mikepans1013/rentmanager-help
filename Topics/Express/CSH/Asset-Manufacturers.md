# Asset Manufacturers (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Asset-Manufacturers.htm

Asset manufacturers are the companies that produce and supply your assets. Creating asset manufacturers and then assigning an asset to a manufacturer allows you to provide more information about the asset, filter your assets list by manufacturer, and run reports on assets based on selected manufacturers.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this page.

 Column
 Description

 Name

 The name of the manufacturing company.

 Phone

 The primary phone number used to contact the manufacturing company.

 Email

 The email address used to contact the manufacturing company.

 Website

 The URL of the manufacturing company's website.

 Comment

 An additional comment about the manufacturer (e.g., Dishwasher manufacturer for Riverview property ).
