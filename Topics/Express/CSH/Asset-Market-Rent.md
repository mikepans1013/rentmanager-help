# Asset Market Rent (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Asset-Market-Rent.htm

The View Market Rent pop-up allows you to view and manage the market rent value of the currently selected rentable asset, such as a dryer or a manufactured home. Market rent is the estimated value of the asset relative to similar rentable assets in that area.

 More Information

 To update the market rent value of multiple assets at the same time, you can use the Modify Market Rent tool. For more information, refer to Modify Market Rent .

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this pop-up:

 Column
 Description

 Amount

 The dollar amount of the market rent for this asset.

 Comment

 An optional note that provides further context on the market rent amount.

 From Date

 The date on which this market rent amount becomes active.

 This market rent amount is active from the specified date until the date of the next entry.
