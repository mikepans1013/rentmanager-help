# Consumer Price Index (CPI) (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/CPI.htm

Consumer Price Index (CPI) reports are issued by the Bureau of Labor Statistics and can be viewed on their website. CPI adjustments can be tracked in Rent Manager and then used to modify recurring charges in a batch at the tenant level or modify market rent at the unit level in proportion to fluctuations in the CPI.
