# Unit Market Rent (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Units-Market-Rent-View.htm

The View Market Rent pop-up lets you view and manage the market rent value of the currently selected rental unit. Market rent is the estimated value of the unit in relation to similar rental units in that area.

 For more information on adding or modifying market rent values for multiple units refer to Modify Market Rent .

 To view or edit the market rent of a unit, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Units and select a unit from the list. Then on the Current Market Rent tile, click .

 Type in the Find a market rent field to narrow the list to match your search criteria.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns are available on this pop-up:

 Column
 Description

 Comment

 An optional note describing the market rent amount.

 From Date

 The date from which this market rent amount should be active.

 This market rent amount is active from the specified date until the date of the next entry.

 Amount

 The amount of the market rent for this unit.
