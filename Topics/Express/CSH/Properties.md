# Properties (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Properties.htm

Most people think of properties in terms of real estate. You may own or manage a variety of real estate: single-family homes, duplexes, apartment buildings, strip malls, storage facilities, manufactured home sites, etc. These properties offer rentable spaces to tenants. In Rent Manager , the definition of property is expanded to include any entity, such as a management company, that has its own financial data (i.e., generates revenue and incurs expenses).

 On the Properties page, each property displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on a property to open the Property details page, where information specific to that property is available.

 More Information

 Only properties to which you have access display. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .
