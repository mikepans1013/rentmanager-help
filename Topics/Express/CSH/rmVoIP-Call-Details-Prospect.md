# rmVoIP Prospect Call Details (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/rmVoIP-Call-Details-Prospect.htm

When you receive a phone call from a prospect through rmVoIP , Rent Manager attempts to locate a prospect account in your database with that phone number to immediately provide you with information about the caller. Call records that are linked to prospect accounts allow you to take several actions, such as creating a service issue or adding a history/note item with information provided from the call. It also allows you to greet the prospect by name and with information specific to them when you answer the call because their account details are linked to the call.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 To view the details of an rmVoIP call linked to a prospect, go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward rmVoIP Incoming Call History . Then, select a call with the Link Type of Prospect from the list.

 Call History

 This tile displays information about the purpose and outcome of the call. This information also displays on the prospect's History/Notes .

 Field
 Description

 Category

 The history category that identifies what the call was about.

 Comment

 The explanation of what you discussed with the caller.

 Spoke with prospect

 If checked, signifies that you spoke with the caller, as opposed to a voicemail message or receiving no answer from the caller. By default, this is checked if an incoming call is answered (establishing a start and end time to the call) or unchecked if an incoming call is not answered or is missed.
