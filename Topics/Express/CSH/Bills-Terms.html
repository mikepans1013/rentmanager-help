Bill Terms (Page)

Bill terms are specified amounts of time given before a bill is considered due. Rent Manager includes some common bill terms, such as Immediately (payment is due upon receipt of the bill), NET 15 (payment is due within fifteen days of the bill date), NET MONTH (payment is due within one month of the bill date), and ON THE 15th (payment is due on the fifteenth of the month).

You are able to add custom terms which can then be selected on bills in the Terms drop-down list.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on this page.

Column Description

Name

The name of the defined bill term (e.g., 1st of Month).

Day of Month

The day of the month that the bill is due.

Net Days

The number of days before the bill is due based on the bill's Bill Date. For example, if the term's Net Days is set to 30, selecting that term on the bill's details page automatically sets the Due Date to 30 days after the Bill Date.

Net Months

The number of months before the bill is due based on the bill's Bill Date.

Active

A displays to indicate the bill term is active.
