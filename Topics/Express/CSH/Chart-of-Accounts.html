Chart of Accounts (Page)

The chart of accounts acts as the backbone of any financial entity, helping you determine where money comes from, where money is held, how money is disbursed, how expenses are paid, and what is used to pay for them. The Rent Manager chart of accounts is comprised of general ledger (GL) accounts, or chart accounts, which allow you to track the financial aspects of your business. The same chart of accounts is used by every property to support property-specific accounting, which means every transaction is linked to at least one property.

Rent Manager includes a default system GL account list organized in the following categories:

The chart of accounts can be changed to fit the needs of a business. A Certified Public Accountant (CPA) can provide guidance concerning common chart account practices for businesses in your specific industry. Rent Manager also gives you the ability to export your chart of accounts into a format that can be printed. You can choose the export format that best fits your needs whether that be a PDF that can be printed or an Excel spreadsheet or CSV file that can be shared.

On the Chart of Accounts page, each GL Account displays on a row with information organized into columns. Click on a GL account to open the Chart of Accounts Details page for that account, where information specific to that GL account is available.

Column Descriptions

The following columns display on the page:

Default Column Description

Account

The unique account name and number used as the system-wide identifier for the GL account.

Type

The account type that most closely defines the purpose of the GL account (e.g., Bank, Expense, Equity, etc.).

Subaccount Of

If applicable, a GL account that acts as the parent account for this GL account. Certain reports allow subaccounts to be collapsed into parent accounts, showing all GL subaccount totals as one line item.

Active

A indicates that the GL account is active and can be used for tracking transactions in Rent Manager.

More Information

Inactive accounts cannot be used to record future transactions in Rent Manager, but the historical record of the account's use remains viewable.

Charge Types

For non-bank and non-credit card GL accounts, the charge types linked to the GL account.
