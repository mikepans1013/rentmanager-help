# Tenant Contacts (Pop-Up)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Tenant-Contacts.htm

The View Contacts pop-up lets you view and manage information about the people associated with your tenant's account. Contacts could be other occupants added to the tenant account, co-signers, business contacts, etc. The primary tenant automatically displays first on this pop-up as a contact.

 To view a tenant's contacts, go to arrow_forward General arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant from the list. Then, on the Contacts tile, click . To view contacts associated with the prospect that are no longer active, click Show Inactive .

 The yellow tag is the assigned Type that represents the tenant contact assigned in the Type field. The email that displays beneath the yellow tag can be found in the Email Address field. Additionally, the phone number that displays is the number selected as Default in the Phone Numbers section. If you have an image of your tenant contact, you can add it to the contact.

 The following status options are available for the contact:

 Field
 Description

 Active

 The contact is currently associated with the tenant. This allows you to deactivate a contact, such as in the event of a roommate backing out of the lease, without deleting the contact's historical record.

 Board/Committee Member

 If the tenant is associated with an Association -type property and is designated as a board and/or committee member, an indicator ( Board Member , Committee Member , or Board Member | Committee Member ) displays above their name.

 Primary

 Indicates that the contact is the primary person your property management company communicates with. One contact must be designated as Primary on each tenant's account. When you add a tenant, the first contact on the account is always the tenant.

 Show On Statement/Labels

 Includes the contact's name on tenant statements or labels. Only two contacts may have Show On Statement/Labels checked on each prospect account. The first contact on the account is always the tenant, so by default, the tenant has Show On Statement/Labels checked.
