# Eviction Reasons (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Eviction-Reasons.htm

The eviction process is time consuming and requires a variety of different steps and jurisdiction-based requirements. Missing or incorrectly performing a step can result in wasted time. In Rent Manager , eviction workflows provide a streamlined solution for handling evictions efficiently. Eviction reasons allow you to create categories to track the reasons for a why a tenant is in the eviction process. Providing standardized reasons (such as Lease Violation or Non Payment of Rent ) to select from during an eviction process makes it easy to provide reporting and track trends on why tenants are being evicted.
