# Accounting Periods (Page)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/CSH/Accounting-Periods.htm

Accounting periods are the lengths of time for which financial statements are typically prepared. Some statements may need to be prepared on a yearly, monthly, quarterly, or semiannual basis. However, in some cases financial reports may be generated for atypical date ranges. For example, perhaps your company reports on a quarterly basis, or a fiscal month begins on the 15th and ends on the 14th. With custom accounting periods, you can create and implement these periods for use in financial reporting.

 Related Preferences

 To enable accounting periods, you must first check the Enable accounting periods option in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Ledger Settings (System Preferences) .

 Filter Options

 In the Series field, select the accounting period series to view. The accounting years and periods associated with that series display in the list below. To the right of the field, the Pattern field displays whether the selected series is Monthly , Quarterly , or Semiannual , and the start and end dates of the series.

 Column Descriptions

 The following columns display on this page:

 Column
 Description

 Year Name

 The name of each period year in the selected series, such as Fiscal Year 2026 .

 Period Names

 The names of each period in the period year (such as 1st Quarter , 2nd Quarter , and so on), followed by the date range that applies to that period.

 Start Date

 The date on which the first period in the year begins. This is also the date on which the series begins.

 End Date

 The date on which the last period in the year ends. This is also the date on which the series ends.
