STR Payment Policy Details (Pop-Up)

Short-term rental (STR) payment policies allow users to establish sets of rules for how guests can pay for their reservations. For example, some of your STR properties may require the guest to pay the full amount at the time they book the reservation, while other STR properties only require a partial payment during booking and allow the guest to pay the rest at a later date.

STR payment policies are only available for RV/Campground type properties.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage STR Payment Policies Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Warning

To access the STR Online Booking tool, at least one of your properties in Rent Manager must have the RV/Campground property type assigned.

To view the details of an STR payment policy, go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Short Term Rentals arrow_forward STR Payment Policies and select a policy from the list.

At the top of the pop-up, the policy Name displays. In addition, a next to Active indicates that this policy can be applied.

Standard Rule

The Standard Rule section displays the basic rule for determining the guest's total charges under the payment policy.

The following fields display in this section:

Field Description

Due at Booking

A flat dollar amount the guest is required to pay when the guest reserves their spot, or a percentage of the total cost that requires payment when a reservation is set.

Remained Due

How many days before arrival that the guest must pay the remaining amount. To make the remainder due the day of arrival, enter 0.

Advanced Rules

This section displays conditional rules for the payment policy. You can set up variable rules based on the number of nights in the stay or the days before the guest arrives. For example, you can create an advanced rule that charges 100% of cost due at booking if a guest makes a reservation for longer than five days or if they make the reservation more than fourteen days in advance.

Column Description

Due At Booking

A flat dollar amount the guest is required to pay when the guest reserves their spot, or a percentage of the total cost that requires payment when a reservation is set.

Remainder Due

The number of days prior to a guest's stay that they must pay the rest of the balance due for the reservation. For example, if the Remainder Due is set to 2 and the reservation's Arrival date is on 5/20/2026, then the remaining balance must be paid on or before 5/18/2026.

Nights In Stay

This rule applies to stays of a set minimum length. The value determines the length of stay necessary for the advanced rule charges to be applied. For example, 8 applies this rule to any reservations associated the payment policy so that when a guest reserves a stay for longer than eight days, the advanced rule charge is applied.

Days Before Arrival

This rule applies to stays reserved a set length of time in advance. The value determines the number of days before an arrival date that triggers the advanced rule charges. For example, 8 applies this rule to any reservations associated with the payment policy so that when the guest reserves a spot eight days in advance, the advanced rule charge is applied.
