# Change a Prospect Status

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Prospect-Change-Status.htm

Prospects in Rent Manager are potential tenants. You can track which prospects choose to rent with you by changing a prospect's Status . All new prospect accounts start with the Prospect status but can be changed to Tenant , Lost , or Lost/Rejected . For example, if a prospect decides to rent with you, you can update their status to Tenant and convert their prospect account to a tenant account with a lease. You can also track which prospects ultimately choose not to rent with you and use reports like Lost Prospect Breakdown and Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

 To change a prospect's status, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select the prospect account for which you want to change status.
The prospect's details page displays.

 -
 On the action bar on the right, click arrow_forward Prospect Status .
The Prospect Status Change pop-up displays.

 -
 Choose a status in the New Status drop-down and enter the related information. See the table below for more information about each prospect status:

 If you choose not to proceed with leasing to this prospect, select Lost/Rejected from the New Status drop-down and enter the following information:

 Lost Date

 The date on which the prospect's tenancy was declined. The Lost Date defaults to today's date. You can edit the date as needed.

 Reason

 The reason you decided that the prospect should not lease with you. Active lost reasons of only the Lost-Rejected type display in the drop-down when you select this status. For more info, refer to Lost Reasons (Page) .

 Description

 A description explaining the prospect's status. The description displays in the prospect's history/notes and in reports, such as the Lost Prospect Breakdown report.
