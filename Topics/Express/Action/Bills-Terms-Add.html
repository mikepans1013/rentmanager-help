Add a Bill Terms

Bill terms are specified amounts of time given before a bill is considered due. Rent Manager includes some common bill terms, such as Immediately (payment is due upon receipt of the bill), NET 15 (payment is due within fifteen days of the bill date), NET MONTH (payment is due within one month of the bill date), and ON THE 15th (payment is due on the fifteenth of the month).

You can add custom terms which can then be selected on bills in the Terms drop-down list.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Bill terms Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a new bill term, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Accounting Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Terms.
    The Terms page displays.

  2. At the top of the page, click Add Term.
    The Add Term pop-up displays.

  3. Enter a Name to describe the term. This name displays on the Terms page

  4. From the Options field, make one of the following selections.

    Option Description

    Day of Month

    The day of the month that the bill is due.

    Net Days

    The number of days before the bill is due based on the bill's Bill Date.

    Net Months

    The number of months before the bill is due based on the bill's Bill Date.

  5. Click Save & New to continue adding terms. Otherwise, click Save & Close to close the pop-up.
    The new term is added to the page.
