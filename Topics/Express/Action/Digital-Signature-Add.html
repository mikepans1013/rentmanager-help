Add a Digital Signature

Upload an image of a handwritten signature to create a digital signature that you can apply to checks printed from Rent Manager. Digital signatures are recognized by banks, making them a valid, secure, and efficient alternative to handwritten signatures for large batches of checks.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Banks/Checks Digital signatures Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a digital signature, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Payables Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Digital Signatures.

  2. Click Add Digital Signature.

  3. Enter a Name and Description, such as who the signature belongs to and when to use it.

  4. In the Users drop-down, select the users who are allowed to add this signature to documents.

  5. In the Signature field, click and browse your device to add the signature image.

  6. To finish, click Save & Close, or to create additional digital signatures, click Save & New.
    The digital signature is added, and the users with access can apply it when printing checks from Rent Manager.
