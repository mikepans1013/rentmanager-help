# Add an Owner Prospect Lead Source

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Owner-Prospect-Lead-Source-Add.htm

Adding a lead source allows you to track the different ways an owner prospect can learn about your business and available properties. Tracking these sources can be beneficial for certain prospecting reports (e.g., Owner Prospect Box Score ) that assess the marketing strategies you have employed.

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 Owners
 Owner Prospect Lead Sources
 Add, View

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To add an owner prospect lead source, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Owner Prospect Lead Sources .
The Owner Prospect Lead Sources page displays.

 -
 Click Add Lead Source .
The Owner Prospect Lead Source Detail pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter a Name to describe the lead source.

 -
 Enter a Phone Number for the lead source.

 -
 Check Is Active to make this lead source active.

 -
 To finish, click Save & Close or to create additional lead sources, click Save & New .
The new lead source is added to the list.
