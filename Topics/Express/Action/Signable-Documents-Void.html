Void a Signable Document

Signable documents are personalized forms delivered electronically to tenants, prospects, owners, or owner prospects for their signature. They are generated from signable templates, which are imported PDF documents or letter templates that include signable fields. If a signable document was sent out before it was ready, Rent Manager gives you the option to void the document before it can be signed by the recipient.

Warning

While a signable document can be deleted, this should be done only if the document was created in error. To maintain historical record of a signable documents, the best practice is to void the document.

To void a signable document, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Signable Documents arrow_forward Signable Documents.
    The Signable Documents page displays.

  2. To the right of the signable document you want to void, click arrow_forward Void.
    The Confirm Void pop-up displays.

  3. In the Reason for void field, enter information regarding why you want to void the document.

  4. Click Yes.
    The document is void and cannot be signed.
