# Add an Amenity Reservation

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Amenity-Reservation-Add.htm

When you receive a request from a resident who wants to use a reservable amenity such as a club house at your property, you can schedule the request directly in Rent Manager . Once approved, reservations can be added to the community calendar so that residents at your property know when certain amenities are unavailable.

 To add a new amenity reservation, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Community arrow_forward Amenity Reservations .
The Amenity Reservations page displays.

 -
 Click Add Reservation .

 -
 Enter or make selections in the following fields:

 Field
 Description

 Property

 The property at which the amenity reservation request was made. To display in this list, the property must be Active and have at least one reservable amenity defined.

 Amenity

 The name of the amenity for which the reservation is requested. The reservable amenities at the property you selected display in this field.

 Tenant

 The name of the tenant making the reservation request. Tenants with active leases at the property you selected display in this field.

 More Information

 You can leave this field blank to reserve an amenity for a community event or for staff use only if the reservable amenity is not tied to a charge. For more information, refer to Add a Reservable Amenity .

 Status

 The desired status, Pending , Approved , Denied , or Canceled , for the reservable amenity request. Only requests with the status Approved display on the community calendar. Should you decide to deny or cancel a reservation request, enter an optional reason for why you did this in the Reason field.

 More Information

 To notify tenants when their amenity reservation request has been denied or canceled, you can configure the amenity reservation canceled and amenity reservation denied automated notifications. For more information, refer to Amenity Reservation Canceled (Automated Notification) and Amenity Reservation Denied (Automated Notification) .

 More Information

 To allow requests submitted from Tenant Web Access (TWA) to be automatically approved, go to the Property Details page of the property where the reservable amenity is located, and on the Reservable Amenities tile, click to open the Reservable Amenities pop-up. Choose the amenity that you wish to change and in the TWA Settings section, select Automatically approve requests submitted from TWA .

 Date

 The date for which the reservable amenity is requested.

 Time

 The start time and end time that determines how long the requested amenity is reserved.

 -
 Optionally, in the Attachments section, select Upload Files to attach more information, such as waivers or permits, to the reservation request.

 -
 When you are satisfied with the information entered, click Save .
The amenity reservation is added to Rent Manager and can be viewed and managed on the Amenity Reservations page.
