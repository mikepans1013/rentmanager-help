# Void a Bill

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Bills-Void.htm

You can void a bill to reverse the expense transaction without deleting the original bill. For example, you may choose to void a bill if it contains errors, such as incorrect amounts, dates or vendor details. When a bill is voided, it shows as paid in Rent Manager and the Memo displays as Void . Another bill is then created for the exact same amount of the original bill, but the amount is negative. The reversal bill is also considered paid and the Memo also says Void .

 Warning

 You cannot void a bill if it has a payment from a check that also paid other bills.

 To void a bill, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Bills .
The Bills page displays.

 -
 To the right of the bill you wish to void, click arrow_forward Void .
The Confirm Void pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter the Bill Reversal Date . The bill is voided for reporting purposes as of this date.

 -
 Click Accept .
A pop-up displays, confirming that the bill was voided, and the details page updates with the date of the void.
