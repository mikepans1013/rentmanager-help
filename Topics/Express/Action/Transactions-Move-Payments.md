# Move a Payment in the Same Property

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Transactions-Move-Payments.htm

Handling tenant and prospect transactions is a big part of your business, so you need to be able to quickly and accurately record transactions on the correct accounts. If a payment is added to the wrong account, you can easily transfer payments from the incorrect account to the correct tenant or prospect account, as long as they are both at the same default property. You can move payments this way even if they were already deposited in Rent Manager .

 More Information

 This topic covers moving a payment between tenants who are at the same property. To correct a payment between two tenants at the different properties, refer to Move a Payment between Different Properties .

 Warning

 If the payment has been marked as NSF or Void , any fees assessed are not moved to the new account. You must manually delete these fees from the source account and apply them to the destination account.

 If the payment was made through ePay and the option to Have Zego manage platform fees is not enabled in system preferences, you must manually delete any associated platform fees from the source account and apply them to the destination account. For more information, refer to General ePay (System Preferences) .

 To move a tenant or prospect payment, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants|Prospects and select a tenant or prospect from the list.
The details page of the tenant or prospect displays.

 -
 On the Transactions tile, click .
The View Transactions pop-up displays.

 -
 Locate the payment that needs to be moved and click   arrow_forward   Move Payment .
The Move Payment pop-up displays.

 More Information

 If the payment is associated with a deposit, the Payment Deposited pop-up displays to inform you transferring the payment does not remove it from the deposit. If the payment needs to be associated with a different deposit, it needs to be moved manually.

 -
 Verify the payment information is correct.

 -
 Click  Move .
The payment is transferred from the source account to the destination account.

 Warning

 If the property uses Standard management fees, any payments tied to management fee postings cannot be transferred until the fee posting is rolled back. For more information, refer to Management Fee Setup Comparison and Roll Back a Posting .
