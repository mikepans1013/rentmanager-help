# Delete Master Meters

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/MU-Master-Meter-Delete.htm

Master meters are utilities that record usage for an entire property (e.g. the water meter for an apartment building). The primary reason to delete a master meter is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the master meter is unavailable for use. If a master meter is linked to a bill, it cannot be deleted.

 Warning

 Deleting a master meter is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the master meter, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the historical information associated with that master meter while making it unavailable for future utility billing. To make a master meter inactive, on the Master Meters pop-up, uncheck Active .
