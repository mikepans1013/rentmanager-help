Disinherit a Recurring Charge

While viewing the recurring charges for a tenant or unit, you may notice some with a checkbox beside them, which indicates a recurring charge inherited from a higher-level entity (the property, unit type, or unit). A check mark displays on active inherited recurring charges. In some instances, you may need to exclude or remove inherited charges for some entities. For example, if a tenant inherits a property-level recurring charge for a parking space fee but they do not have a car, you can disinherit the parking space fee so they are not charged for the space they are not using. If you need to make exceptions and charge a different amount for the same thing, you can disinherit a recurring charge and create a tenant-level charge for the new amount.

To disinherit recurring charges, do the following:

  1. Go to the details page for the entity (tenant, unit, property, or unit type) that needs the recurring charge removed.

  2. On the Recurring Charges tile, click .
    The View Recurring Charges pop-up displays.

  3. Uncheck any recurring charges you wish to disinherit.

  4. Click Save.
    The unchecked recurring charges are considered exceptions. This removes the recurring charge from the list of active recurring charges, but you can view exceptions by selecting the Exceptions filter at the top.
