# Add Market Rent

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Units-Market-Rent-Add.htm

You can track the market rent to determine if your leasable spaces are priced accurately compared to similar spaces. Keeping the market rent up to date helps you set rental charge rates when you lease a unit to a new tenant or renew a lease.

 Market rent can also be used to set up initial charges for new tenants in system preferences, which are automatically charged based on the unit's market rent. For more information, refer to Tenant Charge Setup (System Preferences) .

 To add market rent for a unit, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Units and select a unit from the list.
The Unit details page displays.

 More Information

 Only information related to the properties to which you have access displays. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 -
 On the Current Market Rent tile, click .
The View Market Rent pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Add Market Rent .

 -
 Enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 From Date

 The date on which this amount becomes the current market rent for this unit.

 Amount

 The dollar amount of the market rent. This displays on the unit's scoreboard and in reports such as the Market Rent Analysis report.

 Comment

 An optional note explaining the change in market rent. For example, you can enter a comment explaining that the market rent increased due to renovations or due to a yearly reappraisal.

 -
 Click Save .
The unit's market rent is added and displays on the Current Market Rent tile on the Unit details page and on the unit scoreboard.
