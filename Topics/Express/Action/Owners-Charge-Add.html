Add an Owner Charge

Charges created on owner accounts are linked to the management company. This means the management company is the recipient of any payment made toward these charges. For example, you may have a one-time bill to charge the owner or the charges for a specific owner do not occur on a regular interval.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Receivables Owner transactions Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a new one-time charge to the owner's account, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owners and select an owner from the list.
    The owner's details page displays.

  2. On the Management Company tile, click .
    The Management Company pop-up displays.

  3. Click Add.

  4. Enter the following information:

    Field Description

    Amount

    The total amount that is charged to the owner.

    More Information

    To prorate this charge, enter the full amount of the charge and then click Prorate.

    Rent Manager automatically prorates the charge based on the charge date. In the Days Prorated field, the number of days left in the month automatically populates. This number can be adjusted if needed. The Full Charge Amount displays the charge entered, with the prorated calculation appearing in the Prorated Amount field. If the Full Charge Amount is changed, the Prorated Amount updates to show the calculated amount that will be charged to the owner.

    When finished, click Save.

    Charge Type

    The charge type to use when this charge is posted. The posted charge impacts the general ledger (GL) account linked to the charge type.

    Comment

    A descriptive phrase or note regarding the charge. If entered, the comment displays on the account's Management Company pop-up.

    Date

    The date on which this charge is posted.

    Mgmt. Co.

    The management company that receives the payment(s) made to this charge.

    Related Preferences

    The property management company that displays in this drop-down list is the property established as the management company in system preferences. For more information, refer to Management Company (System Preferences).

    Reference #

    A unique number or phrase to help further identify the charge. If entered, the reference displays on the account's Management Company pop-up.

  5. Click Save.
    The charge is added to the owner's transaction history on the account's Management Company pop-up.
