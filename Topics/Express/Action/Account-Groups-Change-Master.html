Change the Master Account of an Account Group

Account groups allow you to group together two or more tenant accounts for transactional and reporting purposes, while maintaining individual account records for each tenant. Each account group has a tenant account that serves as the master account while the other tenants in the group are considered subaccounts.

From the Account Groups page, you can change the master account and remove tenants from an account group. You only need to change the master account if the tenant assigned as the Master is being removed from the account group. Otherwise, you can just remove the tenant from the group. Selecting which tenant to use for the master account can be based on which tenant in an account group is the preferred contact. Charges can be posted to each member the account group, or you can elect to post charges to the master account if that tenant manages the finances for the group.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Tenants View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To change the master account on an account group, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Account Groups.
    The Account Groups page displays.

  2. Select the account group you wish to update from the list at the left.

  3. Check Master next to the tenant account you wish to make the master account.

  4. Click Save.
    The name of the account group is immediately updated in the left panel to reflect the new master account.
