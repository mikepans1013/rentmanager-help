# Delete an Inventory Item

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Inventory-Items-Delete.htm

When an inventory item is no longer in use, you can choose to delete it. Once deleted, it is no longer available for selection when creating purchase orders, service issues, or estimates. The primary reason to delete an item is that it has been added in error and you want to erase the entry so that the item is unavailable for use.

 Warning

 Since items that have an inventory greater than zero or that are included in estimates/POs cannot be deleted, making the item inactive is a useful alternative. The item remains in the system but can no longer be selected. In addition, you can always go back to an inactive item and make it active again.

 To permanently delete an inventory item, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Inventory Items .
The Inventory Items page displays.

 -
 To the right of the inventory item, click   arrow_forward   Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The inventory item is removed from Rent Manager .
