# Delete a Batch of Scanned Checks

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Checks-Scan-Delete-Batch.htm

Rent Manager allows you to scan and record multiple checks or money orders in a batch and apply them to tenant or prospect accounts from one page. If a batch of checks or money orders is scanned into Rent Manager and contains errors, such as a duplicate batch of payments that were already scanned, you can delete the batch all at once from Scan Checks page.

 More Information

 London Computer Systems, Inc. offers Epson single-feed and multi-feed check scanners to meet your check-scanning needs. To learn more about check scanner options and how to set them up in Rent Manager , refer to Set Up Epson Check Scanner .

 To delete a batch of scanned checks, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Payments arrow_forward Scan Checks .
The Scan Checks page displays.

 -
 In the Batch field, select the batch you wish to delete.

 -
 At the top of the page, click Delete Batch .
The Confirm Batch Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The batch and all the checks or money orders in the batch are removed from Rent Manager .
