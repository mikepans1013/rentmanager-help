# Delete a Lease

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Leases-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete a lease is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry from Rent Manager .

 Warning

 Deleting a lease is permanent action and cannot be undone. If a lease has expired, but the tenant still remains at your property, considering renewing the current lease instead of creating a new one. For more information, refer to Manually Renew a Lease .

 To delete a lease, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant from the lease.
The tenant's details page displays.

 -
 On the Lease tile, click .
The View Leases pop-up displays.

 -
 On the lease you wish to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The lease is deleted from Rent Manager .
