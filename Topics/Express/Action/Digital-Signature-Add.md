# Add a Digital Signature

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Digital-Signature-Add.htm

Upload an image of a handwritten signature to create a digital signature that you can apply to checks printed from Rent Manager . Digital signatures are recognized by banks, making them a valid, secure, and efficient alternative to handwritten signatures for large batches of checks.

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To add a digital signature, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Payables Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Digital Signatures .

 -
 Click Add Digital Signature .

 -
 Enter a Name and Description , such as who the signature belongs to and when to use it.

 -
 In the Users drop-down, select the users who are allowed to add this signature to documents.

 -
 In the Signature field, click and browse your device to add the signature image.

 -
 To finish, click Save & Close , or to create additional digital signatures, click Save & New .
The digital signature is added, and the users with access can apply it when printing checks from Rent Manager .
