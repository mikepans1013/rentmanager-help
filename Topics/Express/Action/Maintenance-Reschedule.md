# Reschedule Maintenance

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Maintenance-Reschedule.htm

When you need to reschedule a planned maintenance visit, such as when a tenant asks to reschedule or when a maintenance technician has an unexpected absence, you can quickly move the issue to a new time block from the Maintenance Schedule page.

 More Information

 To reschedule a maintenance time block, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Maintenance Schedule .
The Maintenance Schedule page displays.

 -
 On the maintenance issue you want to reschedule, go to arrow_forward Reschedule .

 -
 You can select a new time from the list, or you can click Choose a Date and use the calendar to select a new time block or unlink the issue from any maintenance schedule time block.

 -
 If the tenant requested to be present for the maintenance visit, the Reschedule Issue pop-up displays. You can select Confirm Now to confirm the issue and send the tenant a notification email with the updated details, or select Confirm Later to move the issue in your maintenance schedule and leave the status as unconfirmed.
The issue is moved to the new time block, and the associated maintenance tech receives a notification about the rescheduled visit.
