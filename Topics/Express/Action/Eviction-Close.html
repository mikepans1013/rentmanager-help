Close an Eviction

Once the eviction process is completed, the eviction can then be closed in Rent Manager. Closing an eviction allows you to mark the tenant as not evicted or evicted based on the eviction outcome selected. Once closed, on the tenant's details page, the Evictions tile is removed from the top of the page and an All Evictions tile displays to keep a historical record regarding the evictions status, close date, outcome, start date, and eviction reason.

To close an eviction, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants and select the tenant in the eviction process.
    The tenant's details page displays.

  2. On the Eviction tile, click Close.

  3. Select the applicable resolution the eviction process in the Outcome field.

  4. Enter the date on which the eviction is closed in the Close Date field.

  5. To create a custom note about the closed evictions, click Add Eviction Note. The note is saved on the tenant's History/Notes pop-up and on the All Evictions pop-up, in the Eviction Notes section.

  6. If you changed any payment settings during the course of the eviction, click View Payment Settings to view the current payment settings and settings as they were before the eviction process . To continue using the modified settings, click Keep Current Settings, or to reset the settings to what they were before the eviction, click Revert to Pre-Eviction Settings.

  7. Click Close.
    The eviction is closed. The Eviction tile is removed from the top of the tenant's details page and the All Evictions tile displays the record of the eviction.
