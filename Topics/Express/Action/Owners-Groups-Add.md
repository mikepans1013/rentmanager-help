 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To create a new owner group, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward Owners Setup arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Groups .
The Owner Groups page displays.

 -
 Click Add Owner Group .

 -
 Enter the Name of the group.

 -
 Select the owners that should be included in this group.

 -
 Click Save & Close to complete the owner group creation process and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to finish adding the owner group and refresh the pop-up to create another group.
