# Add a Job Budget

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Jobs-Budget-Add.htm

The job costing feature tracks transactions associated with specific jobs completed by your business. On a job's Budget pop-up, you can add budgeted amounts for general ledger (GL) accounts to assist with measuring expenses versus projected cost for the job.

 Related Preferences

 In order to use the job costing tool in Rent Manager , Enable job costing must be checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences) .

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To add GL account budgets to a job, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Jobs and select a job from the list.

 -
 On the action bar on the right, click arrow_forward   Budget .
The Budget pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Add Budget .
The Add General Ledger Budget pop-up displays.

 -
 Select the GL Account for which you are entering budget data.

 -
 Enter the Amount that is budgeted for the selected GL account for this job.

 -
 Enter an optional Comment about the budgeted amount for this GL account.

 -
 Click Save .
The budget data for the selected GL account is added to the job's Budget pop-up.
