# Delete a Prospect Lead Source

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Prospect-Lead-Source-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete an existing prospect lead source is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the lead source is unavailable for use. You can delete a lead source only if there are no prospects linked to it.

 Warning

 Deleting a prospect lead source is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the lead source, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the information attached to that prospect lead source while removing the option when entering new prospect information. To make a prospect lead source inactive, on the Prospect Lead Source Detail pop-up, uncheck Is Active .

 To permanently delete a prospect lead source, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Lead Sources .
The Lead Sources page displays.

 -
 To the right of the lead source, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The lead source is removed from the Lead Sources page.
