Cancel an Amortization CAM Expense Adjustment

If you previously added an amortized commercial area maintenance (CAM) expense adjustment and need to stop it, you can cancel the amortization. This prevents the amortization expense adjustment from being applied to the tenant lease(s) in the future while also keeping a historical record of the amortization adjustment. Tenant leases with previously reconciled adjustments are not affected by this process.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Commercial Recoverable Expenses (CRE) CAM Expense Adjustments View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To cancel an amortization expense adjustments, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Commercial arrow_forward CAM Expense Adjustments.
    The CAM Expense Adjustments page displays.

  2. Click the Amortization type expense adjustment you wish to cancel.
    The Amortization Details pop-up displays.

  3. At the bottom of the pop-up, click Cancel Amortization.
    The Confirm Cancel pop-up displays.

  4. In the Reason for Cancelation field, enter an explanation for stopping the amortization for internal historical reference.

  5. Click Yes.
    The amortization CAM expense adjustment is canceled. To view the canceled expense adjustment details on the CAM Expense Adjustments page, on the top of the page, check Show Canceled.
