Delete an Asset

The primary reason to delete an asset is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry from Rent Manager so that the asset is unavailable for use. An asset with financial tracking enabled cannot be deleted if there are current tenant leases associated with asset.

Warning

Deleting an asset is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use an asset, instead of deleting it, consider making it inactive by entering a Service Life End date. Information about inactive assets is retained in Rent Manager and, if needed, you may make that asset active again in the future. For information on how to make an asset inactive, refer to Asset Locations (Pop-Up).

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Asset Management Manage Assets View, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To permanently delete an asset, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Assets and select the asset you wish to delete.
    The Asset details page displays.

  2. On the action bar to the right, click .
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  3. Select Yes to confirm that you want to delete the asset.
    The asset is deleted from your Rent Manager database.
