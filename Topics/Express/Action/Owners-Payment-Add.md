# Add an Owner Payment

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Owners-Payment-Add.htm

Once an owner has made a payment in the real world, this payment can be entered in Rent Manager using the Add Payment pop-up. This allows you to record payments and apply credits to the selected owner account. For example, you may need to enter a payment for an invoice or a payment that was missed.

 To add a payment directly from the owner's Management Company page, complete the following steps:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owners and select an owner from the list.
The owner's details page displays.

 -
 On the Management Company tile, click .
The Management Company pop-up displays.

 -
 Click the drop-down arrow next to Add arrow_forward Add Payment .

 -
 In the Payment Info tile, enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Amount

 The total amount being paid.

 More Information

 If the Amount entered is more than the Current Payment total when you add the payment, the Prepay Allocations pop-up displays. This allows you to allocate the remaining funds to a specific charge type(s).

 Date

 The date on which the payment posts to the account.

 Memo

 A comment describing or explaining the purpose of the payment.

 Reference #

 The following forms of payment to describe the transaction are available to select:

 Cash

 Record a cash payment.

 CC

 Record a credit card payment.

 Check

 Manually type the check number into the Reference # field to record a check payment.

 ePay

 This displays automatically if, in the Payment Summary tile, Process via ePay is selected.

 MO

 Record a money order payment.
