# Forgot Rent Manager Password

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Users-Password-Forgot.htm

If you forget your Rent Manager password, you can reset your password from the login screen.

 To reset your Rent Manager password, do the following:

 -
 On the Express login screen, click Forgot Password? .

 -
 Enter your username and the email address associated with your account. If required, complete the CAPTCHA.

 -
 Click Send Reset Link .

 -
 Check your email for a reset link from Rent Manager . In the email, click Reset Your Password .

 -
 Enter a new password in both fields. Your new password must meet the following criteria:

 -
 At least 8 characters long.

 -
 Contain at least 1 number (0-9).

 -
 Contain at least 1 lowercase alphabetic character (a-z).

 -
 Contain at least 1 uppercase alphabetic character.

 -
 Click Change Password and then click Return to Login on the next pop-up.
You can now log into Rent Manager with your new password.
