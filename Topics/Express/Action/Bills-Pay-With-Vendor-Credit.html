Pay Bills with a Vendor Credit

Vendors can give you credits for various reasons such as when you overpay a bill, when services are not performed, or when goods are unavailable. You can apply these credits to pay bills in full, or you can apply credits for partial payment of bills.

More Information

Vendor credits work differently on an accrual basis depending on your system preferences. For more information, refer to Vendor Credits in Accrual Basis Accounting.

To pay a bill with a vendor credit, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills.
    The Pay Bills page displays.

  2. Next to the bill to which you want to apply the credit, select arrow_forward Apply Vendor Credits. For bills to which a credit can be applied, displays in the Has Credit column.

  3. If there is more than one credit available for this vendor, in the Credit Information section, the Credit field displays. Select the credit you want to apply to this bill and the amount of the credit displays in the Amount to Credit column.

  4. Check Limit by credit's property to filter the list to display only the bills associated with the property listed on the credit's details page.

  5. In the Bill Allocation, section, enter the following information:

    Column Description

    Allocation Date

    The date on which the credit is applied.

    Amount To Credit

    The amount of the credit you want to apply to this bill. By default, the full amount of the credit populates. You can enter an amount lower than the available credit, but not an amount greater than the available credit.

    Additionally, you can apply only one credit at a time. For example, if you have two credits for $150.00 each, you must apply each $150.00 credit separately.

  6. Click Apply & Close.
    If the credit covers the full amount of the bill, the bill is paid and is removed from your list of open bills. If the credit does not cover the full amount of the bill, the bill remains in your list of open bills, with the remaining balance listed on the Pay Bills page in the Amount Due column.
