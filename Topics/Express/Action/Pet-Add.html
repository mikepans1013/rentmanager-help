Add a Pet

If your properties allow tenants to keep dogs and cats, Rent Manager makes it easy for you to add and track them directly from their account. Whether your tenants have one or three pets, they can all be added and tracked from the tenant or prospect account. For every pet that you allow at your property, you must also create matching pet types that can be used to identify what properties allow pets and to track recurring or one-time charges associated with them. The Pets tile displays on the tenant account only if at least one pet type is selected for the property. For more information, refer to Pet Types (Page).

To add a pet, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants|Prospects and select the tenant or prospect account you wish to add a pet to.
    The tenant's or prospect's details page displays.

  2. On the Pets tile, click Add Pet.
    The View Pets pop-up displays.

  3. Enter the following information in the available fields:

    Field Description

    Pet Type

    The kind of animal, such as a dog or cat, allowed at the property. The pets that are available to add depend on the pet types for the property.

    Name

    The name of the pet associated with the account.

    Weight

    The weight of the pet associated with the account. This can be helpful for properties that may have weight limits to abide by.

    Description

    An optional message about the pet (e.g., Does not play well with other dogs).

    Breed

    The specific group of animals the pet belongs to. For example, American Bulldog can be entered as a dog breed.

    Age

    The age of the pet associated with the account. Age can be entered alphanumerically for accuracy (e.g., 5 and 1/2 years).

    Color

    The color of the pet associated with the account (e.g., White with light beige spots).

  4. Click Upload Photo to add a picture of the pet.

  5. Click Save.
    The pet is added to the account.
