Consolidate Vendor Bill Payments

When you use the Pay Bills page to process payments for bills recorded in Rent Manager, you can pay multiple bills with a single check or payment transaction. By default, Rent Manager creates a separate check or payment transaction for each paid bill but you can consolidate the payments by selecting Consolidate By Vendor when paying bills.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Allow user to pay bills Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To consolidate payments, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills.
    The Pay Bills page displays.

  2. Select the bills you want to pay and click Pay.
    The Payment Info pop-up displays.

  3. Check Consolidate By Vendor.

  4. Click Pay.
    The selected bills with the same Vendor, Bank, and Pay Method are paid together with a single check or payment transaction.
