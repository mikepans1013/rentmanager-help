# Add a Vendor Credit

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Vendor-Credit-Add.htm

Vendors can give you credits for various reasons, such as when services are not performed or when goods are unavailable. Adding a vendor credit allows you to apply that credit to bills from that vendor. You can use credits to pay the full balance of a bill or decide to only use it to cover part of the payment. However you decide to put your vendor credits to use, Rent Manager 's vendor credits tool makes entering a vendor credit quick and easy.

 More Information

 Vendor credits work differently on an accrual basis depending on your system preferences. For more information, refer to Vendor Credits in Accrual Basis Accounting .

 To add a new vendor credit, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Vendor Credits .
The Vendor Credits page displays.

 -
 Click Add Vendor Credit .

 -
 Enter the desired information into the available fields below.

 Field
 Description

 Vendor

 The name of the vendor issuing the credit.

 Date

 The date on which the vendor issued the credit.

 Property

 The property to receive the vendor credit.

 Related Preferences

 If Prevent vendor credits from being applied across properties is checked in system preferences, the property selected here must match the property listed on the bill to which the credit applied. For more information, refer to Checks/Bills General (System Preferences) .

 Amount

 The total amount of the credit issued by the vendor. This amount must be positive.

 Unit

 The specific unit to receive the credit. If the credit can be applied to any unit, select <All Units> .

 Reference

 The reference number related to the credit. For example, enter an invoice or account number.

 Account

 The general ledger account in which the credit is entered. The account selected should correspond with the account on the bill for to which the credit is applied.

 For example, if the vendor credit is created using 6402 Materials expense account, the bill being paid should have a corresponding expense account such as 5105 Maintenance.

 Job

 If applicable, the job associated with the credit to ensure it displays within the transactions associated with the job.

 Related Preferences

 This field displays only when Enable job costing is checked in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences) .

 Comment

 Additional details regarding the credit that you want to document. You may find it helpful to use this field to address specific details regarding the vendor credit such as the name of any other properties that work with the vendor.

 Attachment

 Click to browse your computer or network for a file to attach to the credit. Additional information that may be useful to attach includes an email, bill, or invoice.

 -
 Click Save & Close to complete the vendor credit creation process and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to finish adding the vendor credit and refresh the pop-up to create another credit.
