# Apply Credits to Open Charges

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Transactions-Apply-Credits.htm

Tenants and prospects may have unapplied credits on their accounts due to preallocated credits on specific charge types, having more credits than charges on their account, or after clearing allocations on a transaction. These credits can be allocated to open charges on the entity's account.

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 Receivables
 Take tenant payments
 Enabled

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To apply a credit to open charges, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Payments arrow_forward Receive Payments .
The Receive Payment pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Tenant field, enter the name, unit, or account number of the tenant or prospect account you wish to apply credits to.

 -
 Click Apply Credits Now .

 -
 In the Unapplied Credits section, select from the tenant or prospect's available credits. Any unapplied payments, unapplied credits, and preallocated prepayments display.

 -
 In the Open Charges section, check the box under the Pay? column to select the open charge(s) to which you want to apply the credit.

 -
 Under the Current Payment column, enter the amount from the selected credit you want to apply toward each open charge you selected.

 More Information

 You can apply a partial allocation if the total credit amount is not needed to pay the charge(s). You can also split the credit across multiple charges by entering an amount in the Current Payment column on each charge for the amount you want to apply to each.

 -
 Click Allocate .
The credit is applied to the selected open charge(s) for the amount entered.
