# Delete Loans Receivable

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Loans-Receivable-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete a owner-financed loan is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry from Rent Manager or if you need to make major changes to the original loan that would make it necessary to create an entirely new loan.

 Warning

 Deleting a loan is permanent action and cannot be undone. All charges posted to tenant accounts from the loan are also deleted, however, payments and deposits related to that loan remain in the database. If you received payments, made deposits, etc. on a loan, consider performing a loan default to terminate that loan. Defaulting a loan retains the transactions and writes off the remaining principal as a bad debt. For more information, refer to Default a Loan Receivable .

 To delete a loan receivable, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Loans Receivables arrow_forward Loans Receivables .
The Loans Receivables page displays.

 -
 To the right of the loan you wish to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The loan is deleted from Rent Manager and all charges posted to tenant accounts from the loan are also deleted. Payments and deposits related to the loan remain in the database.
