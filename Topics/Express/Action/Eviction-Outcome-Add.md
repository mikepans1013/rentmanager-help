# Add an Eviction Outcome

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Eviction-Outcome-Add.htm

Eviction outcomes are results of an eviction process and can vary based on decisions made by the parties involves, such as a court or landlord. You can create eviction outcomes to group the records of eviction processes together and make them easier to organize based on their result. Each outcome can be categorized as Not Evicted or Evicted to have a clear distinction between outcomes that ended in the eviction of the tenant and ones that did not. Once an eviction is closed, the outcome of the eviction is logged in the tenant's history/notes.

 More Information

 Before you create your first eviction outcome, you first want to set up your eviction reasons and workflows. For more information, refer to Set Up Evictions .

 To add an eviction outcome to your list of available outcomes, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Evictions arrow_forward Eviction Outcomes .
The Eviction Outcomes page displays.

 -
 Click Add Eviction Outcome .

 -
 Enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Type

 The category of process represented by this eviction outcome.

 Evicted

 The eviction process ended with the eviction of the tenant from the property.

 Not Evicted

 The eviction process ended with the tenant complying to terms of the property management company without the need for eviction. For example, if the tenant paid the amount they owed or if the left the property on their own will.

 Name

 The unique identifier for the eviction outcome.

 Description

 An explanation of the eviction outcome and when to use it.

 Active

 Determines if the outcome is available for users to select when closing an eviction. By default, this field is enabled.

 -
 Click Save .
The new outcome is added to the list and can be assigned to closed evictions.
