# Reopen a Purchase Order

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Purchase-Order-Reopen.htm

After a purchase order is complete, you mark it as closed in Rent Manager . Once closed, it cannot be edited. If you need to edit a closed purchase order, such as when you need to add an invoice or change vendor information, you must first reopen it.

 Related Privileges

 POs/Estimates

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To reopen a closed purchase order, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Purchase Orders and select a closed purchase order from the list.

 -
 In the Status field, click Reopen .
The Reopen Purchase Order pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes on the confirmation pop-up.
The purchase order is reopened, and the status is changed to the order's most recent status before it was closed.
