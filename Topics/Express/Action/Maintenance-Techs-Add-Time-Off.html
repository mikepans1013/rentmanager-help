Add Maintenance Tech Time Off

After you add scheduled hours for your maintenance technicians, you can record techs' time off in Rent Manager to ensure that techs are not booked for service appointments when they are not available to work. When a tech is scheduled for a full day off, their time column on the maintenance schedule is fully blocked. For partial days off, the tech remains on the schedule, and the time frame displays in the tech's column header.

More Information

The Maintenance Schedule feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. However, a license is not required to use the Maintenance Techs page. Additionally, if you are licensed for rmAppSuite Pro, users added to this page have access to the application's Service feature. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

To add time off for a maintenance tech, do the following:

  1. Go to one of the following pages:

    Page Description

    Maintenance Techs

    arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Setup arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Maintenance Techs. Select a maintenance tech and click Add Time Off.

    Maintenance Schedule

    arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Maintenance Schedule. Click Time Off and then, on the Time off/Exceptions pop-up, select the Tech Time off tab. In the Tech field, select the maintenance tech to whom to add time off.

  2. Enter the applicable information on the pop-up.

    Field Description

    From Date

    The day on which the time off is scheduled to begin.

    To Date

    The day on which the time off is scheduled to end.

    Start Time

    The time of day on which the scheduled time off takes effect.

    Hours per Day

    The total number of labor hours that are scheduled off per day.

    All Day

    Check to block the full day for each day between the From Date and To Date.

    Description

    An optional explanation of the reason for the maintenance tech's time off.

  3. Click Save to complete the tech's time off addition and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to finish adding the tech's time off and refresh the pop-up to add more time off.
    The time off is recorded and displays on the tech's Time Off tab. The maintenance tech's column on the Maintenance Schedule page displays the time off, preventing them from being booked during this time.
