Roll Back a Subsidy Payment

A subsidy is a form of financial assistance offered to qualifying tenants. The most common subsidy examples are HUD, Section 8, and FEMA. If you need to revert a subsidy payment that you have already added to Rent Manager, you can use the subsidy payment rollback tool to remove all transactions and deposits associated with a subsidy payment.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Subsidies Manage View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To roll back a posted subsidy payment, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Payments arrow_forward Subsidy History and select a payment from the list.
    The payment's details page displays.

  2. Click Rollback Subsidy.
    Transactions are removed from each tenant that received money from this subsidy, and the bank deposit for this payment is deleted.
