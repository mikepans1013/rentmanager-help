Add a Loans Receivable Rate Schedule

Rent Managerprovides a loans receivable management tool to help users create and manage owner-financed loans. Owner-financed loans are typically used for home sales but can also be used for other loan types such as for land, equipment, appliances, auto, and so on. If the loan has interest rates that increase or decrease over time, you can create a rate schedule for that loan and determine the rate escalation by year and payment number. It is important to note that while the interest calculation is determined when creating the loan, the rate schedule cannot be established until after the loan itself is added.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Loans Receivable Loans Receivable View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a rate schedule to a loan receivable, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Loans Receivable arrow_forward Loans Receivable and select a loan.
    The loan's details page displays.

  2. On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Rate Schedule.

    On the first line, the first payment's Date, Interest Rate (%), and Payment Amount populate based on the loan's Initial Interest Rate, Payment Start Date, and monthly payment amount entered during the loan's creation.

  3. Click Add Rate.

  4. In the new line, enter the following information into the available columns:

    Column Description

    Starting Payment Number

    The payment number when the rate is scheduled to change. When you enter a payment number, the date on which that payment number is due populates in the Date column.

    Interest Rate (%)

    The new interest rate percentage that takes effect for this payment number and date.

    Payment Amount

    The new payment amount based on the entered interest rate populates in this column. You can manually adjust the payment amount, but it does not affect the Interest Rate (%) column.

  5. Add additional lines until all rate adjustments are added.

  6. Click Save.
    The rate schedule is saved to the loan.
