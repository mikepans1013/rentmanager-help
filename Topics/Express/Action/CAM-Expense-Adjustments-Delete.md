# Delete a CAM Expense Adjustment

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/CAM-Expense-Adjustments-Delete.htm

If you previously added a common area maintenance (CAM) expense adjustment to a reconciliation that needs to be removed, you can delete it from the CAM Expense Adjustments page. One-time adjustments cannot be deleted after they are reconciled, and Amortization adjustments cannot be deleted if an adjustment period is reconciled for at least one tenant associated with the adjustment.

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 Warning

 Deleting an Amortization type CAM expense adjustment also deletes all adjustment periods in the amortization schedule. Alternatively, you can cancel the amortization schedule on the Amortization Details pop-up. For more information, refer to Cancel an Amortization CAM Expense Adjustment .

 To delete a CAM expense adjustment, do the following;

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Commercial arrow_forward CAM Expense Adjustments .
The CAM Expense Adjustments page displays.

 -
 On the expense adjustment you wish to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The CAM expense adjustment is deleted.
