# Update a TWA Username

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Tenant-TWA-Username-Update.htm

Tenants and prospects with Tenant Web Access (TWA) accounts, use their email addresses as their username to log into the web portal. If the tenant or prospect want to change the email address they use to log in, you can make the update for them directly from Rent Manager using the Web Access Settings pop-up on the tenant or prospect's details page.

 To update a tenant or prospect's TWA username, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants | Prospects and select a tenant or prospect account.
The details page displays.

 -
 On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward TWA Settings .
The Web Access Settings pop-up displays.

 -
 Click on the email address and select Update Username .
The Add Web User pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter the Email Address to be used as the new username.

 -
 Click Save & Close .
The username is updated and an email is sent to the new username, prompting them to log into TWA with the new email address. The next time the tenant or prospect logs into TWA, they can use their updated username.
