# Complete a Signable Document

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Signable-Documents-Complete.htm

Signable documents are personalized forms delivered electronically to tenants, prospects, owners, or owner prospects to collect a signature such as lease agreements. These documents are generated from custom letter templates with signable fields and published to Tenant Web Access or Owner Web Access . This allows recipients to provide their digital signature from any computer with an internet connection. For more information, refer to Create a Signable Document Template .

 After the recipient signs a document, its status on the Signable Document Packets page is updated to Pending Acceptance . To accept the document review and verify that each signature is accurate (e.g., spelling, correct name), then mark the signable document as complete, which finalizes the document as legally binding. An email notification is also sent and provides a copy of the document to the recipient(s). If the recipient(s) entered data in any Signer Fields , that information is imported to the associated field(s) on their account.

 To complete a signable document, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward  Signable Documents arrow_forward Signable Documents and select the document or packet you wish to complete.
The Signable Document Packets page displays.

 -
 Click Complete Packet .
The Confirm Complete pop-up displays.

 More Information

 If the Landlord Signature or Company Representative Signature field was checked when the document was published, the landlord or company representative signature (the user who published the document) was automatically applied to the document at the time it was published.

 If the Landlord Signature or Company Representative Signature field was not checked, using Complete Packet applies the landlord or company representative signature (the user who completed the document) at the time the document is completed.

 -
 Click Yes .
The Packet Completed pop-up displays.

 -
 Click OK to acknowledge the completion of the signable document(s).
The document's status is updated to Completed .

 Fields filled out by you and the recipient signed Signer Fields are automatically imported to Rent Manager and are available in the document packet from the Signable Documents page or the recipient's history/notes.

 More Information

 Once completed, copies of the signed document(s) are sent to the recipient(s). This makes the document(s) binding in most municipalities as of the date/time stamp that the landlord/company representative signature is applied.
