# Add a Tenant/Prospect Contact

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Contact-Tenant-Prospect-Add.htm

Rent Manager allows you to add contacts to each tenant and prospect account you have in your database. On the tenant or prospect View Contacts pop-up, you can view and manage information about the people associated with your tenant and prospect accounts. Contacts can be other occupants of the unit, cosigners on the lease, business contacts, or other associated individuals. By default, the tenant displays as the Primary contact and is listed first in the list of contacts. By default, the primary prospect or tenant automatically displays first on this tab as a contact.

 To add a new contact to a prospect or tenant account, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants | Prospects and select a tenant or prospect from the list.
The tenant or prospect's details page displays.

 -
 On the Contacts tile, click Add Contact .

 More Information

 Click   Copy to duplicate the selected contact locally in this account or copy the contact to another account.

 -
 If this is the primary contact for the account, check Primary . A contact can be made Primary to make them the main point of contact. Only one contact can be selected as Primary for each account. Primary is checked by default for the first contact entered for the account.

 -
 To display this contact’s name on statements and printed labels, check Show On Statement/Labels . Only two contacts can have Show On Statement/Labels checked on a single account.

 -
 Enter any of the following Personal Information for the contact.

 -
 In the Addresses section, select a user-created address type from the drop-down menu and enter the address into the field.

 -
 In the User Defined Fields section, enter a Value for any applicable UDFs.

 -
 In the Phone Numbers section, enter phone numbers (including area code) and extensions associated with user-created phone number types such as Home , Work , Cell , etc. Next to the number that you want Rent Manager to use in reports, bills, letters, scripting, or when you use rmVoIP to call the contact, check Default . If a phone number can be used for text messaging, check the box in the T  column.

 -
 Click Save .
The contact is added to the tenant or prospect account.

 More Information

 Once the contact is saved, you can add an image of the contact. Back on the on the Contacts tile, click and select the contact from the tabs on the left. Click and drag an image to the center section, or click Upload to browse your computer for a file to attach. When finished, click Save .
