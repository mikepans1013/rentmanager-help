# Schedule Issue Visits via Maintenance Scheduling

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Maintenance-Schedule-Add-Visit.htm

The Maintenance Scheduling feature allows you to easily coordinate your day-to-day maintenance to make the process easier when it comes to scheduling issues, confirming time windows, and managing daily routines. Using the Maintenance Schedule page, you can manually schedule technicians to address their assigned issues during specific time windows.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 Before scheduling visits for issues, you must first create the issues in Rent Manager and complete the setup for the Maintenance Scheduling feature. For more information, refer to Add an Issue and Set Up Maintenance Scheduling . If you are rescheduling a maintenance visit that is already assigned to a time block, refer to Reschedule Maintenance .

 To schedule a visit for an issue, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Maintenance Schedule .
The Maintenance Schedule page displays.

 -
 In the Maintenance Group field, select the group for which to schedule a visit.
The maintenance technicians that are part of the selected group populate on the board.

 -
 Click Show Unscheduled .
A column displays on the left listing all unscheduled issues at the associated properties.

 -
 Select the issue you wish to schedule and click arrow_forward Schedule .
The Select a Time Block pop-up displays.

 -
 Select a date from the calendar, then click an available time slot on the right for that date.

 -
 Click OK .
If the option Allowed to Enter is checked on the issue's details page, the visit is scheduled for the selected time block. If Allowed to Enter is not checked, the Schedule Issue pop-up displays.

 -
 If the Schedule Issue pop-up displays, select on of the options below:

 Option
 Description

 Confirm Now

 An email confirming the time slot for the maintenance issue is immediately sent to the tenant.

 Confirm Later

 The issue has a new time slot assigned in Rent Manager , but a confirmation email is not yet sent to the tenant.

 More Information

 To confirm a scheduled visit at a later time, select the issue on the Maintenance Schedule page and click arrow_forward Confirm . The visit is confirmed for the selected time slot and a confirmation email is sent to the tenant with the visit information.
