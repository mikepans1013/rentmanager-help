# Add a Prorated Charge

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Transactions-Prorate-Charge.htm

When you need to calculate a prorated charge for tenants or prospects, such as when a tenant begins a lease mid-month, Rent Manager automatically calculates the prorated charge amount based on the number of days you want to prorate.

 Related Preferences

 By default, Rent Manager prorates based on the actual days in a month. To always prorate based on a 30-day month, enable the Always prorate based on 30 days in a month option in system preferences. For more information, refer to General Options (System Preferences) .

 To add a prorated charge to a tenant or prospect account, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants|Prospects and select a tenant or prospect.
The Tenant or Prospect details page displays.

 -
 On the Transaction tile, click Add Charge .
The Transaction Detail pop-up displays.

 -
 To create the charge, enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Charge Type

 The charge type that is associated with this charge. Each charge type is linked to one of the general ledger (GL) accounts from your chart of accounts.

 Unit

 The unit with which the charge is associated. By default, the unit associated with the first lease listed on the tenant's Leases tile or the unit listed in the prospect's Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile displays.

 Date

 The date on which this charge is posted to the account and available to be paid.

 Amount

 The full amount of the charge that is owed by the tenant or prospect.

 Comment

 Descriptive information about the charge.

 Reference #

 An optional name or number to track and organize the charge in Rent Manager .

 -
 To prorate the charge, click Prorate .

 -
 To calculate the Prorated Amount , enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Days Prorated

 The number of days in the month that are being prorated. For example, if a tenant moves in on the 15th of the month, enter 15 in this field. This field is automatically calculated based on the number of days left in the month.

 Full Charge Amount

 The total charge before the prorated amount is calculated. If a charge amount is entered on the Transaction Details pop-up, it populates here by default.

 -
 Click Save .
The Prorated Amount populates in the Amount field on the Transaction Detail pop-up.

 -
 Click Save on the Transaction Detail pop-up.
The prorated charge is added to the tenant or prospect account.
