# Delete a Job

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Jobs-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete a job is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the job is unavailable.

 Warning

 Deleting a job is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the job, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the information attached to that job while removing it from available jobs. To make a job inactive, on the Job details page, uncheck Active .

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 Accounting
 Jobs

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To permanently delete a job, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Jobs .
The Jobs page displays.

 -
 To the right of the job, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The job is removed from the Jobs page.
