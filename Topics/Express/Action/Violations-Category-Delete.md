# Delete a Violation Category

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Violations-Category-Delete.htm

With Rent Manager violations, violation categories are a way to organize collections of violation codes that are similar. Since rules and regulations can be extensive, assigning a category such as Parking or Lawn Care can help you arrange your codes. If no existing violations or violation codes are associated with a violation category, the category can be deleted.

 Warning

 Deleting a violation category is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use a violation category, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain historical data attached to that category while removing it from future violations. For information on how to make a category inactive, refer to Violation Categories (Page) .

 To permanently delete a violation category, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Violations arrow_forward Violation Categories .
The Violation Categories page displays.

 -
 On the violation category you wish to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The violation category is removed from Rent Manager .
