# Delete a Reservable Amenity

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Amenity-Reservable-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete a reservable amenity is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the amenity is unavailable for use.

 Warning

 Deleting a reservable amenity is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the reservable amenity, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the information attached to that reservable amenity while removing it from available reservable amenities. To make a reservable amenity inactive, on the Reservable Amenities pop-up, uncheck Active .

The property's details page displays.

 -
 On the Reservable Amenities tile, click .

 -
 To the right of the reservable amenity, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The reservable amenity is removed from the property and can no longer be reserved by tenants.
