Send an Email

Rent Manager allows you to compose and send emails directly to contacts selected from your Rent Manager database, such as when you need to send lease renewal offers or notifications about community events. Additionally, you can even compose emails to prospects after they visit your property or send an email to a select group of tenants alerting them to a road closure.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
System Compose Email Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To write and send emails from Rent Manager, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Email arrow_forward Send Email.

  2. In the email header, enter the following information:

    Field Description

    From Name

    The name entered in this field allows the recipient to identify the sender.

    From Address

    The email address entered here displays as the sender’s address.

    To

    The email addresses for each recipient email. If you deselect the default Send Individually, each recipient's address displays in the email for other recipients to see.

    To select recipients from your Address Book, click To.... The address book contains all the contacts in your database that have an email address and allows you to create additional email contacts.

    More Information

    If you access the Compose Email pop-up from a tenant or prospect account, you can click next to the To... field to select the contact type(s) to receive the email. In the Contact Types pop-up, check each contact type to which to send the email, then click OK. To send the email to all contact types, select <All Types>. For more information, refer to Contact Types (Page).

    Subject

    The topic of the email that recipients see in their email inbox and at the top of the email.

    Attachments

    To browse your computer for a file to attach to the email, click . To remove an attachment, click .

    More Information

    After entering the email recipients email in the To field, click Insert Email Template to use the contents of an already created email template as the body of the email.

  3. In the word processor below, enter the body text of the email.

    More Information

    The Orion Writing Assistant is a versatile tool which can assist with altering your email and letter templates to fit unique individual situations, or to efficiently create persuasive marketing descriptions with slight variations between properties. In areas of Rent Manager where the feature is available, click the icon to open the Orion Writing Assistant and start working with Orion AI. For more information, refer to Writing Assistant Powered by Orion AI.

  4. By default, Send Individually is checked, meaning Rent Manager sends the email to each recipient separately so that each recipient does not see the email addresses of other recipients in the To... field.

  5. Click Send.
    The email is sent to the specified recipient(s) and a record of the email is added to the History/Notes tile of each recipient's account. Additionally, you can view the sent email on the Email Center page.
