# Reserve a Unit for a Prospect

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Prospect-Unit-Reserve.htm

You can reserve specific units for prospects who intend to lease with you. Reserving a unit prevents it from displaying as available to be reserved by or leased to other prospects or tenants. Once a unit is reserved, the reservation information displays on the prospect's Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, and the unit's Occupancy tile displays with a Status of Reserved . When you convert a prospect to a tenant, all lease information entered on the reservation transfers to the tenant account, reducing the time needed to finish creating the tenant's lease.

 More Information

 If the prospect already has a unit reserved, rent quotes are always created with a Reserved status and the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile updates with the selections made when adding the rent quote. For more information on prospect rent quotes, refer to Add a Prospect Rent Quote .

 To reserve a unit for a prospect, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select a prospect account.
The prospect's details page displays.

 -
 On the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile, enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Due Day

 For monthly and weekly rent periods, the day of the week or month on which rent is due for this tenant. To have monthly rent payments due on the last day of each month, enter 31 .

 Expected Lease End

 The date on which you expect the planned lease to end.

 Expected Lease Start

 The date on which you expect the planned lease to begin.

 Lease Term

 The length and rental terms of the prospective lease. For more information, refer to Lease Terms (Page) .

 Rent Period

 The frequency of rent payments for this prospective tenant, which determines if charges are posted to their account on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis.

 Reserved Move In

 The date on which the prospect plans to move into the unit.

 Reserved Move Out

 The date on which the prospect intends to move out of the unit, if known. It is recommended that you leave this field blank until the prospective tenant is officially moving out of the unit, as move out dates can change.

 Reserved Unit

 The name of the unit that is reserved for the prospect. You can search a unit name in the Reserved Unit field, or click to open the Unit Selector pop-up and choose from available units. On the Unit Selector pop-up, the Show Vacant Units Only field is checked by default. To display both occupied and vacant units, uncheck this field.

 -
 Click Save .
The unit is reserved for this prospect and displays in the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile on their details page.
