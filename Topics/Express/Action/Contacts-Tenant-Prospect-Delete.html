Delete a Contact

The View Contacts pop-up allows you to delete a tenant or prospect contact account. For example, you might choose to delete a contact account if it was added in error. If a contact is designated as Primary, you cannot delete it until another contact for this prospect is marked as Primary.

Warning

Deleting a tenant or prospect contact is permanent and cannot be undone so it's only advised in situations where a contact was added in error. If you no longer wish to use the contact, mark it as inactive. To mark a contact as inactive, on the View Contacts pop-up, uncheck Active.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
  Tenants/Prospects Tenants View, Edit
Prospects View, Edit
Allow to delete tenant contacts Enabled
Allow to delete prospect contacts Enabled

If you need to change the Primary designation, the following privilege is required.

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Change primary contact status Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To permanently delete a tenant or prospect contact account, do the following:

  1. Navigate to the desired entity whose contact account you wish to delete.

    Entity Navigation

    Prospect

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select a prospect.

    Tenant

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant.

  2. On the Contacts tile, click .
    The View Contacts pop-up displays.

  3. To the right of the contact account, click  arrow_forward Delete.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  4. Click Yes.
    The contact is permanently removed from the tenant's or prospect's account.
