# Delete a Journal Entry

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Journal-Delete.htm

Journal entries are manual accounting entries that debit the balance of a general ledger (GL) account and credit the balance of another. If a journal entry is created in error or duplicates a transaction that exists in Rent Manager , you can delete the journal entry to remove any erroneous adjustments made to your GL accounts.

 Warning

 Please speak with your accountant about the financial implications to ensure this is the best course of action for your business.

 More Information

 Deleting a journal entry is permanent and cannot be undone. If you need to undo a change made by a journal entry, it is typically recommended to reverse the entry rather than deleting it to keep a historical record of the original change. For more information, refer to Reverse a Journal Entry .

 To delete a journal entry, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Journals arrow_forward Journals .
The Journals page displays.

 -
 On the journal entry you wish to delete, select arrow_forward   Delete .

 -
 Click Yes to confirm the action.
The journal entry is deleted.
