# Add a Prospect Lead Source

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Prospect-Lead-Source-Add.htm

Adding a prospect lead source in Rent Manager helps you track how prospects found your business. Tracking these sources can be beneficial for certain prospecting reports (e.g., Prospect Box Score ) that assess the marketing strategies you have employed.

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 Tenants/Prospects
 Lead sources
 Add, View

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To add a prospect lead source, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Lead Sources .
The Lead Sources page displays.

 -
 Click Add Lead Source .
The Prospect Lead Source Detail pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter a Name to describe the lead source.

 -
 Enter an optional Call in DID for the lead source. This is the phone number associated with the lead source, allowing Rent Manager ’s integrated phone service, rmVoIP , to track call activity with the lead source.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 -
 Verify that Active is checked in order to make this lead source active.

 -
 To finish, click Save & Close or to create additional lead sources, click Save & New .
The new lead source is added to the list.
