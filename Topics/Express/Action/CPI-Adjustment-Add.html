Add a CPI Adjustment

Consumer Price Index (CPI) reports are issued by the Bureau of Labor Statistics and are used to measure inflation. CPI adjustments can be tracked in Rent Manager and then used to modify tenant-level recurring charges or unit-level market rent in proportion to the fluctuations in the CPI.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup CPI Adjustments Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a new CPI adjustment, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then select Accounting arrow_forward CPI.
    The CPI page displays.

  2. At the top of the page, click Add.

  3. Enter information into the available field described below.

    More Information

    To view a data table of recent CPI information, visit Consumer Price Index Historical Tables for U.S. City Average.

    Field Description

    Year

    The year during which the adjustment occurred.

    Month

    The month during which the adjustment takes effect.

    Value

    The dollar amount of the adjustment according to the Consumer Price Index.

  4. Click Save.
    The CPI adjustment is added and can be applied on the Modify Market Rent and Modify Recurring Charges pages.
