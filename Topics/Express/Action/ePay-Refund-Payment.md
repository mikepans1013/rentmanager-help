# Refund an ePay Payment

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/ePay-Refund-Payment.htm

If a tenant or prospect made a payment through ePay and needs to receive a refund, you can process that refund directly through Rent Manager .

 More Information

 This process is used to fully refund an ePay payment. If you need to make a partial refund for an ePay payment, you must process the refund manually.

 To refund an ePay payment, do the following:

 -
 Navigate to the desired entity receiving the refund.

 Entity
 Navigation

 Prospect

 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select a prospect.

 Tenant

 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant.

 -
 On the Transactions tile, click .
The View Transactions pop-up displays.

 -
 On the ePay transaction being refunded, select arrow_forward ePay Refund .

 -
 If prompted, enter your ePay password.

 -
 In the Refund Date field, enter the date that the refund is issued in Rent Manager .

 -
 In the Reason field, enter an explanation for the refund.

 -
 To create a history/note item on the account to document the ePay refund, select Add History Note .
