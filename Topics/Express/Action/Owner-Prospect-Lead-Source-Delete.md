# Delete an Owner Prospect Lead Source

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Owner-Prospect-Lead-Source-Delete.htm

The primary reason to delete an existing owner prospect lead source is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the lead source is unavailable for use. You can delete a lead source only if there are no owner prospects linked to it.

 Warning

 Deleting an owner prospect lead source is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the lead source, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the information attached to that owner prospect lead source while removing the option when entering new owner prospect information. To make an owner prospect lead source inactive, on the Owner Prospect Lead Source Detail pop-up, uncheck Is Active .

 To permanently delete an owner prospect lead source, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Owner Prospect Lead Sources .
The Owner Prospect Lead Sources page displays.

 -
 To the right of the lead source, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The lead source is removed from the Owner Prospect Lead Sources page.
