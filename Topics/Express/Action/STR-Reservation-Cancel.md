# Cancel a Short-Term Rental (STR) Reservation

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/STR-Reservation-Cancel.htm

If a short-term rental (STR) reservation was made in error or the guest no longer needs the reservation, Rent Manager gives you the ability to cancel it on behalf of the guest.

 Warning

 To access the STR tool, at least one of your properties in Rent Manager must have the Short Term Rental or RV/Campground property type assigned.

 To cancel an STR reservation, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Short Term Rentals arrow_forward STR Reservations .
The STR Reservations page displays.

 -
 To the right of the reservation you wish to cancel, click arrow_forward Cancel Reservation .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The reservation is canceled.
