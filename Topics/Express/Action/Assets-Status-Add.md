# Add an Asset Status

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Assets-Status-Add.htm

Asset statuses are conditions that you create to describe the state of the asset (e.g., Inventory , In Use , Out for Repair , Out of Service ). Creating asset statuses and then assigning a status to an asset as part of asset management can help you provide more information about the asset and allow you to filter your assets list by status. Asset statuses are also used in conjunction with asset workflows so that assets move through statuses that match your real world procedures.

 To add a new asset status, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Rental Info arrow_forward Assets arrow_forward Asset Statuses .
The Asset Statuses page displays.

 -
 Click Add Asset Status .
The Add Asset Status pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter the following information:

 Field
 Description

 Description

 Additional information about the status.

 Name

 The name of the status to display on the Asset Statuses page (e.g., Inventory , In Use , Out for Repair , Out of Service ).

 -
 Click Save & Close to add the asset status and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to add the asset status and begin adding another asset status.
