# Delete an Accounting Period Series

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Accounting-Period-Delete.htm

Accounting periods are the lengths of time for which financial statements are typically prepared. An accounting series establishes the set of rules to be followed every created accounting period. A series can be deleted to completely remove an accounting series along with its subsequent period years and periods.

 Warning

 Deleting an account series is permanent and cannot be undone. If the series is no longer in use, instead of deleting it, consider deselecting Active instead. This allows you to retain historical data of the accounting series while preventing new periods from being created with it. For more information, refer to Accounting Periods (Page) .
