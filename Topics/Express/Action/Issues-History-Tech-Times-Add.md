# Add Service Tech Times

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Issues-History-Tech-Times-Add.htm

To help your maintenance techs keep track of the hours they spend completing issues, Rent Manager allows you to enter the time directly on the issue. Track maintenance time by adding hours on the issue's details page or from the issue using rmAppSuite Pro .

 To add service tech times, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Service Manager arrow_forward Issues and select an issue from the list.
The issue's details page displays.

 -
 On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Add Hours/Check In .
The Tech Time Details pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter information in to the following available fields:

 Field
 Description

 Service Tech

 The user working on the issue.

 Check In

 The date and time when the work was started.

 In the first field, enter the date or click to select a date. In the second field, enter the 12-hour time in four digits (e.g., 06:45 ) and AM  or PM .

 Check Out

 Check to enter the date and time when the work was completed.

 In the first field, enter the date or click to select a date. In the second field, enter the 12-hour time in four digits (e.g., 06:45 ) and AM  or PM .

 Hours

 The total service hours for the work session. Work can be logged using the Check In and Check Out fields or the Hours field, not both.

 Comment

 Additional details regarding the time, such as the work intending to be done when the user adds a Check In time or the work that was done by the Check Out time.

 -
 Click Save when finished.
The total time spent on the issue is updated in the Details section of the issue, and can also be viewed in the Service Tech Hours report.
