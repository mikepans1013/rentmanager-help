Delete an Eviction Outcome

Eviction outcomes are results of an eviction process and can vary based on decisions made by the parties involved, such as a judicial court or landlord. You can delete eviction outcomes that were created by mistake but eviction outcomes that are were used to close an eviction case cannot be deleted. In addition, you must have at least one outcome, so you cannot delete an outcome if it is the only one in your list.

Warning

Deleting an eviction outcome is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use an eviction outcome, mark it as inactive. To mark an outcome as inactive, on the Eviction Outcome Details pop-up, uncheck Active.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage Eviction Reasons and Outcomes Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To delete an eviction outcome, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Evictions arrow_forward Eviction Outcomes.


  2. Next to the outcome you want delete, click arrow_forward Delete.
    The Confirm Delete Eviction Outcome pop-up displays.

  3. Click Delete.
    The outcome is deleted from Rent Manager and can not be selected in future eviction processes.
