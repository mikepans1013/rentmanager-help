Delete a Bill Term

The primary reason to delete a bill term is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry from Rent Manager so that the term is unavailable for use. Terms can be deleted only if there are no bills or recurring bills associated with the term

Warning

Deleting a bill term is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use a term, uncheck the Active box for that term to deactivate it. The term remains in the Rent Manager database and historical bills, but can no longer be selected on open or new bills.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Bill terms View, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To delete a bill term, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then go to Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Terms.
    The Terms page displays.

  2. On the desired bill term, click arrow_forward Delete.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  3. Click Yes to confirm the action.
    The term is removed from the list.
