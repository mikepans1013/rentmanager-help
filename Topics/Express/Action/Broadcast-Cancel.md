# Cancel a Broadcast

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Broadcast-Cancel.htm

With Phone Broadcasting and Text Broadcasting , you can send audio and text messages to owners, tenants, prospects, or vendors. Additionally, you can build Numbers Lists of phone numbers that make it easier to broadcast your messages to multiple recipients at once. If a message you scheduled no longer needs to go out, Rent Manager allows you to cancel the broadcast.
