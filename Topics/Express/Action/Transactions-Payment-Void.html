Void a Payment

You can reverse payments recorded in Rent Manager to reflect real-world transaction activity, such as insufficient funds or bank errors. Voiding keeps a record of the payment on the account, but it also reverses the payment so a balance is still due. This option is primarily used as an alternative to deleting a payment.

To void a payment, do the following:

  1. Go to the corresponding page below and select the tenant or prospect account:

    Entity Type Description

    Tenant

    arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants

    Prospect

    arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects

    The Tenant or Prospect details page displays.

  2. On the Transactions tile, click .
    The View Transactions pop-up displays.

  3. On the payment, click  arrow_forward NSF.

  4. In the Reversal Type field, select Void.

  5. Review the Payment Information and Charges paid by payment sections.

  6. In the Reversal Options section, enter the applicable information for the reversal in the following fields:

    Field Description

    Date

    The date the payment was returned.

    Reason

    A note as to why the payment was reversed.

    Add History/Note

    Related Privileges

    Group Privilege Column
    History Tenant History/Notes Add
    Prospect History/Notes Add

    For more information, refer to Control User Access.

    Check to add a history/note documenting the reversal to the tenant or prospect History/Notes tile.

  7. Click Apply.
    A reversal transaction is added to the entity account and displays on the View Transactions pop-up.
