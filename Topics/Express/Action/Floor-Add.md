# Add a Floor

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Floor-Add.htm

Floors in Rent Manager allow you to accurately record the number of floors at your properties. Once you enter floors into the database, you can assign them to rental units to make it easier to find units on specific levels or floor types when a prospect is looking for a unit on a specific floor.

 To add a new floor, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Properties/Units arrow_forward Floors .
The Floors page displays.

 -
 At the top of the page, click Add Floor .
The Add Floor pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Name field, enter unique information to identify the type of floor being added, such as 1st Floor .

 -
 In the Associated Properties field, select the properties that have units with this type or level of floor.

 -
 Click Save & Close to complete the addition of the new floor and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to finish adding the floor and refresh the pop-up to create another floor.
