Delete an Owner Prospect

The primary reason to delete an owner prospect is that they were added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the owner prospect is unavailable. If an owner prospect has transactions tied to their account, they cannot be deleted.

Warning

Deleting an owner prospect is permanent and cannot be undone. To maintain the owner prospect record and historical data, change the owner prospect's status on the Owner Prospect Status Change pop-up to Lost or Lost-Rejected.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Owners Owner Prospects View, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To permanently delete an owner prospect, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owner Prospects.
    The Owner Prospects page displays.

  2. Select an owner prospect from the list.
    The owner prospect's details page displays.

  3. On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Delete Owner Prospect.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  4. Click Yes.
    The owner prospect is removed from the Owner Prospects page.
