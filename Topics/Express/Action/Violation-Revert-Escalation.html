Revert a Violation Escalation

Violations are used to track and document when tenants break the rules and regulations established for your properties. By replicating your rules and regulations in Rent Manager, you can track and manage tenants with violations, including the penalty for the infraction and any escalating penalties for failing to comply. If you find that a violation has been escalated to another stage in error, Rent Manager enables you to deescalate it back to the correct stage.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Violations View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To revert a violation to a previous stage, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Violations and select a violation.
    The violation's details page displays.

  2. On the Stage Information tile, click Revert.
    The Revert pop-up displays.

  3. Click OK.
    The violation is reverted to its previous stage.

    More Information

    Reverting an escalation does not automatically remove any charges associated with that escalation from the tenant's account. If there was a charge applied that needs to be removed, you must do so from the tenant's transactions. For more information, refer to Tenant Transactions (Pop-Up).
