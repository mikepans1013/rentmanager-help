Delete a Short Term Rental (STR) Payment Policy

The primary reason to delete a short term rental (STR) payment policy is that it was added in error and you want to remove the entry so that the payment policy is unavailable for use. You can delete an STR payment policy only if the payment policy is not currently used by any STR properties.

Warning

Deleting an STR payment policy is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use the payment policy, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the information attached to that policy while removing it from available payment policies. To make a payment policy inactive, on the Payment Policy Details pop-up, uncheck Active.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage STR Payment Policies Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To permanently delete an STR payment policy, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Short Term Rentals arrow_forward STR Payment Policies.
    The STR Payment Policies page displays.

  2. To the right of the payment policy, click arrow_forward Delete.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  3. Click Yes.
    The payment policy is removed from the STR Payment Policies page.
