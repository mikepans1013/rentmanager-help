# Delete an Association Committee

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Associations-Committee-Delete.htm

You can delete an association committee only when there are no active members associated with the committee. If you want to delete an association committee with active committee members assigned to it, you must deactivate each committee member before you are able to delete the selected association committee.

 Since this process requires updating much of your homeowners association (HOA) information, there are very few situations where deleting is necessary. For example, you may delete an association committee because it is a duplicate of an existing committee.

 Warning

 Deleting an association committee is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use an association committee, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain the HOA information attached to that committee while removing it from reviewing future architectural requests. To make a committee inactive, on the Committee Detail pop-up, uncheck Active .

 To permanently delete an association committee, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Properties/Units arrow_forward Association Committees .
The Committees page displays.

 -
 To the right of the association committee, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The association committee is removed from the page and is no longer available.
