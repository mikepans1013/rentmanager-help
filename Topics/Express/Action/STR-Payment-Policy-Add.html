Add a Short Term Rental (STR) Payment Policy

Short term rental (STR) payment policies allow users to establish sets of rules for how guests can pay for their reservations. For example, some of your STR properties may require the guest to pay the full amount at the time they book the reservation, while other STR properties only require a partial payment during booking and allow the guest to pay the rest at a later date.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Setup Manage STR Payment Policies Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a payment policy, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Short Term Rentals arrow_forward STR Payment Policies.
    The STR Payment Policies page displays.

  2. Click  Add Payment Policy.

  3. Enter a Name for this policy.

  4. In the Standard Rule section, enter the needed information into the available fields.

    Field Description

    Due at Booking

    A flat dollar amount the guest is required to pay when the guest reserves their spot, or enter a percentage of the total cost that requires payment when a reservation is set. If the entered number is a percentage, check Percentage.

    Remainder Due

    The number of days prior to a guest's stay that they must pay the rest of the balance due for the reservation. For example, if the Remainder Due is set to 2 and the reservation's Arrival date is on 5/20/2026, then the remaining balance must be paid on or before 5/18/2026.

  5. In the Advanced Rules section, click in the section header to open the Add Rule pop-up. You can create more specific rules for certain situations, such as requiring a different amount Due at Booking for reservations longer than a week.
    The following fields are available in the Add Rule pop-up:

    Field Description

    Due at Booking

    A flat dollar amount the guest is required to pay when the guest reserves their spot, or a percentage of the total cost that requires payment when a reservation is set. If the entered number is a percentage, check Percent.

    Remainder Due

    The number of days prior to a guest's stay that they must pay the rest of the balance due for the reservation. For example, if the Remainder Due is set to 2 and the reservation's Arrival date is on 5/20/2026, then the remaining balance must be paid on or before 5/18/2026.

    Apply Rule When

    Indicate whether you would like to apply charges for when there are long term reservations (over a certain number of days) made and/or reservations made a certain amount of days in advance.

    Number of nights in stay is greater than

    This rule applies to stays of a set minimum length. Enter a value which determines the length of stay necessary for the advanced rule charges to be applied.

    For example, enter 8 to apply this rule to any reservations associated this payment policy so that when a guest reserves a stay for longer than eight days, the advanced rule charge is applied.

    Number of days before arrival date is greater than

    This rule applies to stays reserved a set length of time in advance. Enter a value which determines the number of days before an arrival date that triggers the advanced rule charges.

    For example, enter 8 to apply this rule to any reservations associated with this payment policy so that when the guest reserves a spot 8 days in advance, the advanced rule charge is applied.

  6. To finish adding rules, click Add & Close, or to create additional rules, click Add & New.

  7. Click Save.
    The payment policy is added to the list.
