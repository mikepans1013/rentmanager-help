# Resubmit a Screening

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Screenings-Resubmit.htm

In the event that you need to generate an upgraded screening or recheck a prospect's information, you can resubmit an AmRent screening. After performing an initial screening, you can upgrade the screening once (for additional AmRent fees) by ordering a manual verification of up to 15 pieces of information. Alternatively, if the financial status of a prospect or tenant who was previously declined has changed (e.g., they have corrected any errors with the credit bureau(s) and/or increased their credit score), a screening can be resubmitted to retrieve their updated data.

 To resubmit a screening, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Screenings and select the screening you need to resubmit from the list.
The Screening Detail page displays.

 -
 At the bottom, click Resubmit .
The Run Screening pop-up displays.

 -
 Review the screening information.

 Warning

 Information from the original screening cannot be changed when resubmitting a screening. If any details have changed, such as an increase in annual income or a change of address, you must generate a new screening. For more information, refer to Run a Tenant or Prospect Screening .

 -
 Click Submit .
The screening is resubmitted to AmRent and can be tracked on the Screenings page.

 More Information

 If all required information is entered but some data is incomplete, such as a missing Middle Name or Annual Income , a pop-up displays warning you that submitting a screening without that information may result in inaccurate data. You can still complete the screening by clicking Yes .
