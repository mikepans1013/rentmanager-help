Clear Transaction Allocations

If a payment from a tenant or prospect is applied to the wrong charge, you can clear the payment allocation to apply the payment to the appropriate charge. You can clear the allocations from either the payment or charge transaction.

While an allocation affects both a payment and a charge, typically, you only need to clear one. To ensure unrelated transactions are not affected, base your selection on how each transaction type is affected when you clear their allocations. Clearing allocations from a payment removes any allocations applied to charges. Clearing a charge's allocations removes any payments applied to that specific charge without affecting a payment's allocations to other charges.

To clear allocations on a transaction, do the following:

  1. Go to the corresponding page below and select the tenant or prospect that needs their transaction(s) updated:

    Entity Type Description

    Tenant

     arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants

    Prospect

     arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects

    The details page for tenant or prospect displays.

  2. On the Transactions tile, click .
    The View Transactions pop-up displays.

  3. On the transaction you want to clear allocations from, click arrow_forward Clear Allocations.

  4. On the Clear Allocations pop-up, click Yes to confirm.
    The allocations associated with the transaction are removed.

    More Information

    To allocate the amounts you cleared to open charges, from the tenant or prospect details page, click  arrow_forward Add Payment and select the Apply Credits tab. For more information, refer to Apply Credits to Open Charges.
