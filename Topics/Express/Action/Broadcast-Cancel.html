Cancel a Broadcast

With Phone Broadcasting and Text Broadcasting, you can send audio and text messages to owners, tenants, prospects, or vendors. Additionally, you can build Numbers Lists of phone numbers that make it easier to broadcast your messages to multiple recipients at once. If a message you scheduled no longer needs to go out, Rent Manager allows you to cancel the broadcast.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

To cancel a text or phone broadcast message, do the following:

  1. Navigate to the broadcast page.

    Broadcast Type Navigation Path

    Phone Broadcasting

    arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward Phone Broadcast

    Text Broadcasting

    arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward Text Broadcast

  2. On the broadcast page details page, click .
    The Broadcast Batches pop-up displays.

  3. Click and select Cancel Broadcast on the broadcast you want to cancel.
    The Cancel Broadcast pop-up displays.

  4. Click Yes to confirm the cancellation.
    The broadcast is canceled and is not sent to tenants.
