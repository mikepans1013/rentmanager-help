# Move a Contact

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Contacts-Tenant-Prospect-Move.htm

An existing contact may be moved to a different tenant account, or, for prospect accounts, to a tenant or different prospect account. Moving a contact removes them as a contact on the original account and adds them to the selected account. To retain the contact on the original account as well, copy the contact.

 More Information

 Tenant Web Access (TWA) accounts and screenings are not copied to the destination account. A new TWA account for the copied contact must be created.

 Primary contacts cannot be moved. If necessary, assign another contact as the Primary .

 To move a contact's information, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants | Prospects and select a tenant or prospect from the list.
The tenant or prospect's details page displays.

 -
 On the Contacts tile, click .
The View Contacts pop-up opens.

 -
 On the desired contact, for tenants, click arrow_forward Move to Different Tenant . Or for prospects, arrow_forward Move to Different Prospect or Move to Tenant .
The Move Contact pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Tenant or Prospect field, begin typing the name of the account to which you are moving this contact. The field updates based on your entry.

 -
 Click Assign .
The tenant or prospect contact is moved to the selected account and removed from the source account.
