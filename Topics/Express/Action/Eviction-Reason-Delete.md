# Delete an Eviction Reason

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Eviction-Reason-Delete.htm

Eviction reasons allow you to create categories to track the reasons for a why a tenant is in the eviction process. Providing standardized reasons (such as Lease Violation or Non Payment of Rent ) to select from during an eviction process makes it easy to provide reporting and track trends on why tenants are being evicted. If you have an unused eviction reason that is not needed, you can delete the reason from the Eviction Reasons page.

 Warning

 You must have at least one active eviction reason. If you only have one active reason in your list, it cannot be deleted. Additionally, you cannot delete an eviction reason that was used in a completed eviction process.

 If you no longer wish to use an eviction reason, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain historical data attached to evictions that used the reason type while removing it from available eviction reasons in the future. You can mark the reason as inactive by going to the Eviction Reason Details pop-up and unchecking Active .

 To delete an eviction reason, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Evictions arrow_forward Eviction Reasons .
The Eviction Reasons page displays.

 -
 Next to the eviction reason you want to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete Eviction Reason displays.

 -
 On the pop-up, click Delete .
The eviction reason is deleted and can no longer be assigned to eviction workflows.
