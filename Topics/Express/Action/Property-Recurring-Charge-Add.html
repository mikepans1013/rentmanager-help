Add a Property Recurring Charge

Recurring charges are charge templates that are assigned to properties, unit types, units, and tenants and for charges that are posted regularly. When you post recurring charges, these charges post to the associated tenant as one time charges. Property-level recurring charges are posted to all tenants at the property. This is helpful when you have a charge that applies to all tenants at a particular property, as it allows you to set up a single recurring charge at the property level rather than setting up a recurring charge on each individual tenant account.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Properties/Units Properties View
Recurring Charges/Market Rent Recurring charges Add

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a recurring charge to a property, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties and select an property from the list.
    The property's details page displays.

  2. On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forwardAdd Recurring Charge.
    The Recurring Charge Details pop-up displays.

  3. Enter information in the following fields.

    Field Description

    Amount

    The dollar amount associated with the charge.

    Calculation

    A custom formula that use scripts to calculate the amount tenants are charged.

    Charge Type

    The category used to link transactions to the recurring charge.

    Comment

    A phrase or note to show on the View Recurring Charges pop-up, if applicable.

    Frequency

    How often this recurring charge posts to tenant accounts.

    More Information

    The Frequency is calculated as the number of months, weeks, or days that should elapse between each posting of the recurring charge. The duration of month, week, or day is determined on the tenant's details page in the Rent Period field.

    For example, a tenant with a Monthly rent period, a frequency of 1 means this recurring charge posts once every month, and 3 means it posts once every three months.

    If no value is specified, the Frequency defaults to 1.

    From

    A date to establish when this recurring charge begins posting to tenant accounts. If no date is entered, the charge is considered infinite, and begins posting to the account the next time recurring charges are posted.

    To

    A date to establish when this recurring charge expires and stops posting. If no date is entered, the charge is considered infinite, and is posted perpetually.

  4. Click Save & Close to complete the recurring charge creation process and close the pop-up. Alternatively, click Save & New to continue adding recurring charges.
    The recurring charge is applied to the property and available for all tenants at the property.
