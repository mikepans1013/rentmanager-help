# Add a Vendor Contact

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Vendor-Contact-Add.htm

Vendor contacts allow you to view and manage information about the people associated with the vendor, such as a sales representative or a contractor. If a vendor gets a new office manager or new technician, you can add additional contacts to the account.

 More Information

 Instead of recreating a contact from scratch, save time by duplicating an existing contact. To do this, click on the Contacts tile, then select an existing contact from the list on left side of the pop-up. Next, click   Duplicate . The details from the selected contact populate on the new contact where you can edit the information as you wish.

 To add a new contact to the vendor account, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Vendors and select a vendor from the list.
The Vendor details page displays.

 - On the Contacts tile, click  Add Contact .

 -
 If this is the main contact for this vendor, check Primary . A vendor contact can be made Primary to make them the main point of contact. Only one contact can be selected as Primary for each vendor account. Primary is checked by default for the first contact entered for the vendor.

 -
 Enter any of the following Personal Information for the contact:

 Field
 Description

 Name

 The full name of the contact to display in Rent Manager .

 Email

 The contact's primary email address through which they can be reached.

 Type

 The vendor contact type that best describes the contact. For more information, refer to Contact Types (Page) .

 -
 In the Phone Numbers section, enter phone numbers (including area code) and extensions associated with user-created phone number types, such as Office . Next to the number that you want Rent Manager to use in reports, bills, letters, scripting, or when you use rmVoIP to call the vendor, check Default . If a phone number can be used for text messaging, check the box in the T  column.

 -
 Enter details about the vendor contact in the Comment field, such as the contact's work hours and availability.

 -
 Click Save .
The contact is added to the vendor account.

 More Information

 Once the contact is saved, you can add an image of the contact. Back on the on the Contacts tile, click and select the contact from the tabs on the left. Click When yu and drag an image to the center section, or click Upload to browse your computer for a file to attach. When finished, click Save .
