Reverse a Journal Entry

A reversal journal entry is a mirrored copy of an existing journal entry in Rent Manager, but the credit and debit allocations are reversed. Reversal entries can be used to undo the effect of a previous journal entry without deleting it, or they can be used to make accounting adjustments between two periods.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting Journal entries View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Warning

Please speak with your accountant about the financial implications to ensure this is the best course of action for your business.

To reverse a journal entry, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Journals arrow_forward Journals and select a journal entry.

  2. Click arrow_forward Reverse Journal.
    The Confirm Reversal pop-up displays.

  3. Enter the Journal Reversal Date for this new journal entry.

  4. Click Accept.
    The reversal journal entry is created on the specified Journal Reversal Date. If any changes are made to the original journal entry, this reversal entry automatically updates as well.

More Information

To remove a reversal, click arrow_forwardRemove Reversal.
