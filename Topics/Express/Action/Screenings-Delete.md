# Delete a Screening

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Screenings-Delete.htm

Rent Manager has partnered with AmRent to provide the ability to check a prospect's credit, criminal, and eviction history and verify their claimed income prior to entering a rental agreement. Performing screenings and submitting income verifications requests for applicants are important steps in determining if an individual has a stable financial and behavioral history.

 Occasionally, you might submit a screening in error (for example, perhaps you inputted the prospect's information incorrectly). If this happens, you can chose to delete the screening. Once a screening is deleted, it is no longer visible in Rent Manager .

 Warning

 Deleting a screening is permanent and cannot be undone. As there may be legal considerations to deleting the record of a screening, it is strongly encouraged that you consult with a lawyer before permanently removing screenings from your database.

 To permanently delete a screening, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Screenings .
The Screenings page displays.

 -
 On the screening you want to delete, click arrow_forward   Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The screening is removed from Rent Manager .
