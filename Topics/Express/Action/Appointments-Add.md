# Add an Appointment

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Appointments-Add.htm

Appointments allow you to maintain your busy schedule with Rent Manager . You can even create appointments to recur on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. You can then easily view or access your calendar appointments on your Rent Manager and your dashboard's Appointments tile.

 Related Privileges

 Privilege

 Add, View, Edit

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To add a new appointment, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Services arrow_forward Calendar arrow_forward Appointments .
The Appointments page displays.

 -
 Click Add Appointment .
The Add Appointment pop-up displays.

 -
 In the General section, enter information into the available fields described below.

 Field
 Description

 Subject

 The topic of the appointment. This also displays on the calendar and on the Appointments dashboard tile.

 Description

 A detailed explanation of the appointment.

 Display

 The color of the Subject when the appointment displays on the calendar and in tiles. For more information, refer to Colors (Page) .

 Users

 The user(s) who are allowed to view this appointment on their calendar(s).

 -
 In the Links section, you can link to specific accounts or issues in Rent Manager , and a history/note item regarding the appointment is added to the specified account or issue. To do this, select an entity from the first drop-down list, then search for a specific account or issue number for that entity in the second drop-down list and then click . If necessary, click next to the name of the linked entity to delete it.

 -
 In the Schedule Options section, enter information into the available fields described below.

 Field
 Description

 Calendar Integration

 Check to allow the appointment to be synced with an external calendar. The option is checked by default when adding an appointment.

 Related Preferences

 This option displays only if the Calendar Integration system preference integration option is enabled. Additionally, in personal preferences, you can enter the email address to receive appointment notifications and override the system default Integration Method established in system preferences for only your account. For more information, refer to Calendar Integration (System Preferences) and Calendar Integration (Personal Preferences) .

 Start Time

 The date and time of the day the appointment occurs.

 End Time

 The date and time of day the appointment ends.

 Reminder

 Check to enable reminders for the appointment. Enter a value for one or more of the three provided increments ( Days , Hours , and Minutes ) to determine when the reminder occurs.

 Recurring

 Check to have this appointment to repeat on a regular schedule in the future. Select from one of the four provided patterns ( Daily , Weekly , Monthly , or Yearly ) and the coordinating increment. If applicable, select an End from the drop-down list.

 -
 In the Attachments section, click Upload Files to browse your computer for a file, or drag and drop a file into the upload box, to attach to the appointment.

 -
 Click Save .
The appointment is created.
