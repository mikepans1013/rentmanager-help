# Sync Text Numbers

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Broadcast-Text-Numbers-Sync.htm

On the Manage Text Numbers page, the Sync Numbers option allows you to manually update all numbers listed. This button is necessary only if new text numbers are activated for your database while the Manage Text Numbers page is open.

 The list of numbers on the Manage Text Numbers page updates automatically when the following actions are performed:

 -
 You log in to Rent Manager .

 -
 You open the Manage Text Numbers page.

 -
 You updated your text broadcasting credentials in system preferences. For more information, refer to Phone Broadcast (System Preferences) .

 More Information

 To sync text numbers, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Customization arrow_forward Manage Text Numbers .
The Manage Text Numbers page displays.

 -
 Click Sync Numbers .
The new text numbers are activated for your database.

 -
 Click Save to confirm any changes made on the Manage Text Numbers page.
Any new numbers added to your Rent Manager account display.
