# Delete a GPR Posting

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/GPR-Posting-Delete.htm

Gross potential rent (GPR) is the maximum revenue that a property could generate if it was rented at full capacity and at full market value. When posting GPR, Rent Manager creates a journal entry that reduces the rental income general ledger (GL) account to $0 and then adjusts other designated income GL accounts to project maximum revenue through metrics such as market rent, loss to lease, and vacancy loss. Deleting a GPR posting removes it from the GPR Postings page and deletes the associated journal entries that were created when the post was made.

 Warning

 Deleting a GPR posting is permanent and cannot be undone. Speak with your accountant about the financial implications of deleting postings to ensure this is the best course of action for your business. If a GPR posting needs to be corrected or was posted accidentally needs to be undone, it is typically recommended to roll back the GPR posting rather than deleting it. For more information, refer to Roll Back a Posting .

 To delete a GPR posting, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Gross Potential Rent arrow_forward GPR Posting History .
The GPR Postings page displays.

 -
 To the right of the posting you wish to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The GPR posting and all associated journal entries are deleted from Rent Manager .
