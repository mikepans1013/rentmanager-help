Forgot Rent Manager Password

If you forget your Rent Manager password, you can reset your password from the login screen.

To reset your Rent Manager password, do the following:

  1. On the Express login screen, click Forgot Password?.

  2. Enter your username and the email address associated with your account. If required, complete the CAPTCHA.

  3. Click Send Reset Link.

  4. Check your email for a reset link from Rent Manager. In the email, click Reset Your Password.

  5. Enter a new password in both fields. Your new password must meet the following criteria:

    • At least 8 characters long.

    • Contain at least 1 number (0-9).

    • Contain at least 1 lowercase alphabetic character (a-z).

    • Contain at least 1 uppercase alphabetic character.

  6. Click Change Password and then click Return to Login on the next pop-up.
    You can now log into Rent Manager with your new password.
