# Delete a General Ledger Account

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/COA-Delete.htm

You can delete a general ledger (GL) account only if no charge types are linked to the account and no transactions are tied to the account. For example, you might choose to delete a bank account that has never been used.

 If a GL account has a charge type and/or transactions linked to it, you cannot delete the GL account. You must transfer transactions to another bank account and link the charge type to a different account before you are able to delete the GL account.

 Warning

 Deleting a GL account is permanent and cannot be undone. If you no longer wish to use an account, instead of deleting it, consider marking it as inactive. This allows you to retain financial data attached to that GL account while removing it from available GL accounts for future transactions. For more information on how to make a GL account inactive, refer to Chart of Accounts (Page) .

 If you accidentally made a duplicate account and now have two, you also have the option to merge them. For more information on how to merge accounts, refer to Merge GL Accounts .

 To permanently delete a GL account, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Chart of Accounts .
The GL Accounts page displays.

 -
 To the right of the GL account, click   arrow_forward   Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The GL account is removed from Rent Manager .
