# Void a Payment

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/Transactions-Payment-Void.htm

You can reverse payments recorded in Rent Manager to reflect real-world transaction activity, such as insufficient funds or bank errors. Voiding keeps a record of the payment on the account, but it also reverses the payment so a balance is still due. This option is primarily used as an alternative to deleting a payment.
