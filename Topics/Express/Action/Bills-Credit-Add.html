Add a Credit to a Bill

Open credits on the selected tenant or prospect account display in this section. Review or enter the following information:

Column Description

Date

The date of the open credit.

Property

The property associated with the open credit.

Unit

The unit associated with the open credit.

Description

The description and charge type of the credit.

Open Amount

The current amount of the credit.

Allocated Amount

The amount you intend to refund to the tenant or prospect.

If there is not an existing credit on the selected account, on the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward Add Credit.

On the Add Credit pop-up, follow the steps below:

  1. Select a charge type from the drop-list.

  2. Optionally, select the Unit to associate with the credit.

  3. Enter the Date of the credit.

  4. Enter the Amount of the credit.

    More Information

    To prorate this credit, enter the full amount of the credit and then click Prorate.

    Rent Manager automatically prorates the credit based on the charge date. In the Days Prorated field, the number of days left in the month automatically populates. This number can be adjusted if needed. The Full Charge Amount displays the credit entered with the prorated calculation appearing in the Prorated Amount field. If the Full Charge Amount is changed, the Prorated Amount updates to show the calculated amount that will be credited to the tenant.

    When finished, click Save.

  5. Enter an optional Comment or Reference # to further explain the nature of this credit.

  6. Click Save.
    The credit is added to the Open Credits section, and can be entered in the Allocated Amount field.
