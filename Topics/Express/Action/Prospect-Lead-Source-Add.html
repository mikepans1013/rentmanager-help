Add a Prospect Lead Source

Adding a prospect lead source in Rent Manager helps you track how prospects found your business. Tracking these sources can be beneficial for certain prospecting reports (e.g., Prospect Box Score) that assess the marketing strategies you have employed.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Lead sources Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To add a prospect lead source, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Lead Sources.
    The Lead Sources page displays.

  2. Click Add Lead Source.
    The Prospect Lead Source Detail pop-up displays.

  3. Enter a Name to describe the lead source.

  4. Enter an optional Call in DID for the lead source. This is the phone number associated with the lead source, allowing Rent Manager’s integrated phone service, rmVoIP, to track call activity with the lead source.

    5. More Information

    This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

  5. Verify that Active is checked in order to make this lead source active.

  6. To finish, click Save & Close or to create additional lead sources, click Save & New.
    The new lead source is added to the list.
