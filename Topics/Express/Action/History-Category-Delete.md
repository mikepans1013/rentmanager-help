# Delete a History Category

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Action/History-Category-Delete.htm

You can delete a history category when there are no history/note items assigned to the category. You may want to delete a history category that you no longer want to use or that you wish to replace. If you want to delete a history category with history/note items currently assigned, you must reassign or remove the history category before you are able to delete it.

 Warning

 Deleting a history category is permanent and cannot be undone. If a history category is linked to a history/note item, consider deactivating it instead. That way, the category remains associated with the existing history/note items, but can't be assigned to future items. For more information, refer to History Categories (Page) .

 To delete a history category, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Rental Info arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward History Categories .
The History Categories page displays.

 -
 Next to the category you want to delete, click arrow_forward Delete .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes on the confirmation pop-up.
The category is removed from Rent Manager and no longer displays in the History Category drop-down list on history/note items.
