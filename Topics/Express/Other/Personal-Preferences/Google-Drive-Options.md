# Google Drive Options (Personal Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Personal-Preferences/Google-Drive-Options.htm

This personal preference allows you to save files to and access files from your Google Drive account directly from Rent Manager 12 .

 More Information

 This setting applies only to Rent Manager 12 . For Rent Manager Express , Google Drive files must be saved and/or uploaded manually.

 To set these personal preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward Personal Preferences arrow_forward Google Drive Options .
The Personal Preferences: Google Drive Options page displays.

 -
 In the Google Drive Settings section, click Sign in with Google .
A Sign in with Google browser window opens.

 -
 Enter the email address or phone number and password associated with your Google account. Alternatively, if you are already logged into a Google account, select the Google account you are currently logged into or select Use another account and log in to a different Google account.

 -
 Click Save .
The Google Drive account is linked and can be used to attach files in Rent Manager 12 .
