# Reminders (Personal Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Personal-Preferences/Reminders.htm

Reminder personal preferences allow you to disable pop-up notifications for task and appointment reminders. By default, pop-up notifications display in the bottom right of your Rent Manager page to alert you of upcoming tasks and appointments.

 To set this personal preference, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward Personal Preferences arrow_forward Reminders .
The Personal Preferences: Reminders page displays.

 -
 Check Disable Reminder Pop-Ups to remove pop-up notifications for tasks and appointments assigned to your user account. Notifications still display in the list.

 -
 Click Save .
The system preference configuration is updated.
