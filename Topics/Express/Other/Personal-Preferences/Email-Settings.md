# Email Settings (Personal Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Personal-Preferences/Email-Settings.htm

In personal preferences, you can override the default email setup in system preferences to display your own email address in Rent Manager . You can either set your email address as a mask that displays in place of the system email address (email@rentmanager.com) or, when using an external server, connect to your email account to use it in place of the system email account. Both options display your correspondence as coming from you and redirect replies to your email address.

 The email settings set in system preferences determine the email server users must use when adding an email account to personal preferences. For example, if your business's email account uses Gmail, the email account you add in personal preferences must also be through Gmail. In addition, system preferences determine whether features like email reply tracking are used with your emails. Email reply tracking affects if replies to your emails go back to your email account or pull into Rent Manager .

 To set these personal preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward Personal Preferences arrow_forward Email Settings .
The Personal Preferences: Email Settings page displays.

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The personal preference configuration is updated.

 Email Signature & Subject

 Rent Manager allows you to create custom messages that are automatically added to the bottom of emails generated in Rent Manager . Most people use signatures to include personal contact information, a special message, a motto, or a business-related quote to their emails.

 More Information

 For best results, consider sending a test email to yourself after making changes to ensure your signature displays as intended and that all formatting and images display correctly.

 If you want the subject line for emails you generate from a letter batch to automatically populate with the name of the letter template, check Prefill subject line with letter name when emailing letter batches .

 Connect to an Email Account

 If you are connected to an email provider using Modern Authentication in system preferences, this section displays with the account connected and allows you to connect or disconnect your individual email account. You can click on a specific provider, like Microsoft, to follow their steps for connecting your account in Rent Manager .

 Related Preferences

 The settings in this section are available only if, in system preferences, you have the setting Allow user to enter their credentials in Personal Preferences enabled. For more information, refer to Email Settings (System Preferences) .

 Setting

 Description

 Disconnect

 Disconnects from the current email account and reverts you back to using the default account set in system preferences.

 Email Provider

 A button that represents the email provider, such as Google or Microsoft, set up in system preferences using Modern Authentication. Clicking this button opens a pop-up window that walks you through that provider's authentication process.

 Override system default credentials

 Allows you to use your own email account in place of the default set up in system preferences.

 Email Credentials

 If you are connected to an email provider using SMTP settings in system preferences, this section displays with the account connected and allows you to connect or disconnect your individual email account.

 Related Preferences

 The settings in this section are available only if, in system preferences, you have the setting Allow user to enter their credentials in Personal Preferences enabled. For more information, refer to Email Settings (System Preferences) .

 Option
 Description

 Override system default credentials

 If checked, the Username and Password fields become available, allowing you to set up your own email.

 Username

 The username for your email account. This is typically the email address.

 Password

 The app password generated by your email client for the email account entered in the Username field. An app password is a unique account password that provides a secure way to give outbound email access without requiring a personal password.

 Warning

 Depending on your email client and its settings, you may need assistance from your IT representative or administrator to generate an app password.

 Test Connection Email

 The email address used as the recipient when testing your SMTP connection. Click Test Connection to test your SMTP connection. If successful, the test email is sent to the inbox of the email address entered in this field.

 Email Options

 This section determines the name and email address that display to recipients when sending emails from Rent Manager . These settings do not display when connected to an external email provider using Modern Authentication, such as Microsoft or Google.

 Option
 Description

 Default From Email

 The sender email address to display on your emails sent from Rent Manager .

 More Information

 Changing the displayed email address is referred to as masking the email. Masked emails still send from the email account email@rentmanager.com but display the email address entered in this field to recipients and reroutes replies to that email. On opened emails, the email@rentmanager.com email address is visible after the masking address.

 You can override the masked from address on individual emails you send by adjusting the From Address field at the top to enter or change the email address as necessary.

 Default From Name

 The sender name to display on your emails sent from Rent Manager .
