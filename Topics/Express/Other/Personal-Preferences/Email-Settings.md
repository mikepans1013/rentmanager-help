# Email Settings (Personal Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Personal-Preferences/Email-Settings.htm

In personal preferences, you can override the default email setup in system preferences to display your own email address in Rent Manager . You can either set your email address as a mask that displays in place of the system email address (email@rentmanager.com) or, when using an external server, connect to your email account to use it in place of the system email account. Both options display your correspondence as coming from you and redirect replies to your email address.

 To set these personal preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward Personal Preferences arrow_forward Email Settings .
The Personal Preferences: Email Settings page displays.

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The personal preference configuration is updated.

 System Email Account

 To configure which format emails sent from Rent Manager are sent in, select from the following options:

 Option
 Description

 From Email

 The email address that displays on all emails sent from your Rent Manager account. If you are connected to Microsoft or Google with Modern Authentication, this option is read-only.

 More Information

 Changing the displayed email address is referred to as masking the email. Masked emails still send from the email account established in system preferences or in the user-specific settings described below, but display the email address entered in this field to recipients and reroutes replies to that email. On opened emails, the actual email address is visible after the masking address.

 You can override the masked from address on individual emails you send by adjusting the From Address field at the top to enter or change the email address as necessary. For more information, refer to Send an Email .

 From Name

 The name that populates in the From Name field on emails sent from your Rent Manager system account.

 Make Default

 Check this option to populate this system email setup for outgoing emails by default.

 This option displays only if an additional email connection is set up in the Other Email Account section.

 Override system default credentials

 Select to use your user-specific email credentials for your company's SMTP email provider, then enter your credentials in the available fields below.

 More Information

 This setting is available only if, in system preferences, you have established a connection using Connect to SMTP Provider and the setting Allow users to override system default credentials in Personal Preferences is enabled. For more information, refer to Email Settings (System Preferences) .

 Username

 The username for your user-specific email account associated with your company's SMTP email provider. This is typically the email address.

 Password

 The app password generated by your email client for the email account entered in the Username field. An app password is a unique account password that provides a secure way to give outbound email access without requiring a personal password.

 More Information

 Depending on your email client and its settings, you may need assistance from your IT representative or administrator to generate an app password.

 Test Connection Email

 The email address used as the recipient when testing your SMTP connection. Click Test Connection to test your SMTP connection. If successful, the test email can be seen in the inbox of the email address entered in this field.

 Other Email Account

 In this section, you can select an option to set up another email connection. Once a provider is connected, the following fields display in this section. This email account can be selected in the From field when composing an email.

 Related Preferences

 The settings in this section are available only if, in system preferences, you have the setting Allow users to add an email account in Personal Preferences enabled. For more information, refer to Email Settings (System Preferences) .

 Option
 Description

 Make Default

 If checked, this connection populates by default for emails sent from your user account in Rent Manager . This option is only available if you are connected to an external provider in this section.

 From Name

 The name that populates in the From Name field on emails sent from your Rent Manager account when this account is selected. If you are connected to Microsoft with Modern Authentication, this option is read-only.

 From Email

 The email address that display on all emails sent from your Rent Manager account when this account is selected. If you are connected to Microsoft or Google with Modern Authentication, this option is read-only.

 More Information

 Changing the displayed email address is referred to as masking the email. Masked emails still send from the email account established in system preferences or in the user-specific settings described below, but display the email address entered in this field to recipients and reroutes replies to that email. On opened emails, the actual email address is visible after the masking address.

 You can override the masked from address on individual emails you send by adjusting the From Address field at the top to enter or change the email address as necessary. For more information, refer to Send an Email .

 Disconnect

 Disconnects from the current email account and reverts you back to using only the default account set in system preferences.

 Modern Authentication Provider

 If you are connected to an email provider using Modern Authentication in system preferences, this section displays with the account connected and allows you to connect or disconnect your individual email account. You can click on a specific provider, like Microsoft, to follow their steps for connecting your account in Rent Manager .

 SMTP Provider

 Conversely, you can use the settings in this section to set up SMTP communication between Rent Manager and your personal email account by clicking Connect To SMTP Provider . If a provider is already connected, click to view the following SMTP fields.

 Option
 Description

 Mail Server (SMTP)

 The server used by your email provider to send and receive email correspondence.

 More Information

 Your email provider may be different from your email client. For example, you may use GoDaddy as your email provider, but use Outlook for your email client.

 Mail Port (SMTP)

 The port number for the external server entered in the Mail Server (SMTP) field. The standard mail port for SMTP is 587 . The port defines a direction for the data being transferred.

 More Information

 In the rare occurrence that port 587 does not work, try using mail port 2525 . You may need assistance from your IT representative or administrator to test your port connection using the telnet command in the Windows or Mac operating systems.

 Enable Encryption (via STARTTLS)

 A command that tells the receiving end that the email data needs to be encrypted and checks for encryption support. If supported, the email is sent and the receiving end handles the encryption.

 Enable SMTP Authentication

 The system default email account used for emails from Rent Manager when using an external server.

 Username

 The username for the email account to use as the system default email account. This is typically the email address.

 Password

 The app password generated by your email client for the email account entered in the Username field. An app password is a unique account password that provides a secure way to give outbound email access without requiring a personal password.

 More Information

 Depending on your email client and its settings, you may need assistance from your IT representative or administrator to generate an app password.

 Test Connection Email

 The email address used as the recipient when testing your SMTP connection. Click Test Connection to test your SMTP connection. If successful, the test email can be seen in the inbox of the email address entered in this field.

 Use Proxy from internet access in Rent Manager

 This is available for email accounts or servers that require third-party connections to be passed through a proxy server instead of connecting to them directly.

 Proxy Server

 The name or IP address used to connect to the proxy server provided by your IT representative or administrator.

 Proxy Port

 The port number for the server entered in the Proxy Server field.

 Email Signature & Subject

 Rent Manager allows you to create custom messages that are automatically added to the bottom of emails generated in Rent Manager . Most people use signatures to include personal contact information, a special message, a motto, or a business-related quote to their emails.

 More Information

 For best results, consider sending a test email to yourself after making changes to ensure your signature displays as intended and that all formatting and images display correctly.

 If you want the subject line for emails you generate from a letter batch to automatically populate with the name of the letter template, check Prefill subject line with letter name when emailing letter batches .
