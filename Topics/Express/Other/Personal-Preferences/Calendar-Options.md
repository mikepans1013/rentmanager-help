# Calendar Options (Personal Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Personal-Preferences/Calendar-Options.htm

Rent Manager includes a calendar to keep track of your appointments and tasks. In personal preferences, you can choose how printed calendars display, determine the user(s) that can view your calendar, and select a default follow-up time for history/note items.

 To set these personal preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward Personal Preferences arrow_forward Calendar Options .
The Personal Preferences: Calendar Options page displays.

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The personal preference configuration is updated.

 Preference Descriptions

 Each section and their settings are described below:

 Section
 Description

 Work Week

 Determines which days are considered part of a work week and when the work week begins.

 Work week

 The day(s) of the week that you work. This determines which days of the week display in the Week view of your calendar.

 First day of the week

 The day of the week your work week begins. This day displays as the first column of your calendar.

 Month View

 If Print Landscape is selected, calendars printed in Month view are generated with landscape page orientation. Otherwise, calendars in Month view are printed with portrait page orientation.

 Week View

 If Print Landscape is selected, calendars printed in Week view are generated with landscape page orientation. Otherwise, calendars in Week view are printed with portrait page orientation.

 Day View

 Determines the times, time interval, and page orientation for calendars printed in Day view.

 Start Time

 The earliest time that displays for each day when printing the calendar.

 End Time

 The latest time that displays for each day when printing the calendar.

 Time Scale

 The interval of time used when displaying events and tasks. For example, if you select 60 minutes , each time block that displays is one hour long.

 Print Landscape

 If selected, calendars printed in Day view are generated with landscape page orientation. Otherwise, calendars in Day view are printed with portrait page orientation.

 Share Calendar

 In the Users field, the selected Rent Manager users can view the appointments and tasks on your personal calendar. To share your calendar with all current and future users, select *** All Users .

 Default History/Note Follow-up Time

 The default time of day that populates when adding a follow-up to a history/note item. This time can be changed as needed in the history/note item's Follow-up Date field, as it directly affects when scheduled follow-up reminders display.
