# Reminders (Dashboard Tile)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Dashboard-Tiles/Reminders.htm

The Reminders dashboard tile allows you to track important business tasks and deadlines so that you can ensure each one is completed in a timely manner.

 The information on this dashboard tile is represented in a list.

 Option
 Description

 Setup Options

 The following reminders can be displayed on your Reminders tile.

 Appointments

 The upcoming appointments with the Reminder option checked.

 Bills to Pay

 The total dollar amount of all unpaid bills past the due date and future unpaid bills that are due within X amount of Days .

 Checks to Print

 The total dollar amount of all checks that have the Check will be printed option checked but are not yet printed from the Print Checks page.

 Current Tenant Delinquencies

 The total dollar amount of all unpaid charges determined by the date entered on each charge that is owed to you by current tenants that are at least X amount of Days old as determined by the date entered on each charge that is owed to you by current tenants.

 Delinquencies

 The total dollar amount of all unpaid tenant charges that are at least X amount of Days old as determined by the date entered on each charge.

 ePay Posting

 The total dollar amount of tenants who have recurring ePay enabled and who owe you a balance within the next X amount of Days .

 Lease Dates

 The total number of units where a tenant's lease begins or ends within the next X amount of Days .

 Loan Insurance

 The total number of loans where the insurance has expired or will be expiring within the next X amount of Days based on the Insurance Expiration date entered on loans.

 Metered Utility Posting

 The total number of utilities that have at least one Current Reading entered and the reading(s) are not yet posted.

 Move In/Move Out

 The total number of units associated with a tenant who has a Move In or Move Out date entered on their lease that falls within the next X amount of Days .

 Online Application

 The total number of prospects who submitted applications but are not linked to a tenant or prospect account. This reminder applies only to applications submitted before Apply Now was available.

 More Information

 If you use Apply Now applications, use the Recent Online Applications dashboard tile instead. For more information, refer to Recent Online Applications (Dashboard Tile) .

 Owner Expirations

 The total number of owner contracts that are expiring within the next X amount of Days based on the contracts entered on property's Owners page.

 POs Assigned to Me

 The total number of purchase orders (POs) that are assigned to the currently logged-in user.

 POs Pending Approval

 The total number of purchase orders (POs) that have a status of Pending on purchase order details.

 Post Loans

 The total number of loans with an outstanding balance that are past due to be posted or whose posting is due within the next X amount of Days .

 Post Recurring Charges

 The total number of properties that have recurring charges due to be posted based on the property's Posting Date and the most recent date recurring charges were posted.

 Previous Month Sales

 The total number of commercial tenants who have retail sales tracked on their lease.

 For a tenant to display, Track tenant retail sales must be enabled in system preferences and the tenant must have retail sales amounts entered on their commercial lease.

 Reorder Items

 The total number of inventory items where the quantity was reduced to less than the Reorder Qty. amount.

 Scheduled Asset Maintenance

 The total number of assets with scheduled maintenance on asset history notes that are either past due or are due within the next X amount of Days .

 Security Deposits

 The total number of held security deposits associated with a lease that has a Lease End or Move Out date in the past or is within the next X amount of Days .

 Tasks

 The current number of open tasks.

 Undeposited Funds

 The total dollar amount that was paid to you and was not yet deposited in bank deposits and/or ePay deposits.

 Vacancies

 The total number of units that are not being leased by a tenant as of the current date.

 Vendor Insurance

 The total number of vendors with insurance that has expired or will be expiring within the next X amount of Days days based on the Insurance date entered on the vendor's details page.

 Vendor Workers Compensation

 The total number of vendors with workers' compensation that has expired or will be expiring within the next X amount of Days based on the Workers Comp date entered on the vendor's details page.

 Waiting List Availability

 The total number of units where a prospect is on the waiting list within the next X amount of Days based on information entered on prospect details.

 Web Service Issues

 The total number open service issues created from Tenant Web Access .

 Work Orders Due

 The total number of service issues that are overdue or have a due date within the next X amount of Days .

 Property Filter

 Each properties' reminders are included in the tile results. To include all current and future properties, select <All Properties> . Alternatively, select a property Group from the drop-down list.

 If <All Properties> is selected, you can also check Include inactive if 'All Properties' selected to calculate averages for all current and future properties whether they are active or inactive.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Ignore dashboard property filter

 If checked, override the property filter configured on the Dashboard .
