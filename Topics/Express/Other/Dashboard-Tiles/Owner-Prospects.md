# Owner Prospects (Dashboard Tile)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Dashboard-Tiles/Owner-Prospects.htm

The Owner Prospects dashboard tile helps you track owner prospects that were created in Rent Manager . The tile defines contact as a call, email, or visit entered in the owner prospect's history/notes, as well as the original date the owner prospect was created.

 The information on this dashboard tile is represented in a list.

 To filter the information that displays in the dashboard tile, click arrow_forward Settings to open the Owner Prospects Data Filters pop-up. The available filter options are listed below.

 Option
 Description

 First contact X days ago

 The number of days in the past that owner prospects were first contacted are included in the tile results. For example, entering 14 displays all owner prospects whose first contact took place in the past two weeks.

 Exclude follow-ups in the last X days

 The number of days in the past that owner prospects were contacted are excluded from the tile results. For example, entering 14 displays only owner prospects who were not contacted in the past two weeks.

 Exclude last contact > X days

 The number of days in the past that owner prospects were not contacted are excluded from the tile results. For example, entering 30 displays only owner prospects who were contacted in the past month.

 Property Filter

 Each property whose units are included in the tile results. To include all current and future properties, select <All Properties> . Alternatively, select a property Group from the drop-down list.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Sales Rep Filter

 Each sales rep's whose owner prospects you want to view in the tile results. Users assigned the Sales Rep/Leasing Agent role populate in the list of agents. To include owner prospects who have not been assigned to a sales rep in the tile results, select No Sales Rep .

 More Information

 Owner prospect accounts created through the submission of guest cards have no sales rep by default. To display these accounts, No Sales Rep must be included in your filter configuration.

 Created By

 Determine which owner prospects are displayed in the tile results.

 Guest Card

 Include owner prospects who were created in Rent Manager by submitting a guest card.

 User

 Include owner prospects who were created in Rent Manager manually by a user.

 Column Descriptions

 The information that displays on the tile is organized into the following columns.

 Column
 Description

 Date

 The date on which the owner prospect was created.

 Name

 The name of the owner prospect.

 Sales Rep

 The sales representative as specified on the owner prospect's account.

 Last Contact

 The date on which the owner prospect was last contacted by a user. The last contact date is determined by the call, email, or visit history/note item on the account with the most recent Start Date .

 More Information

 Call history/note items must have Spoke with owner prospect checked to be considered when determining last contact date.
