# Market to Actual Rent Comparison (Dashboard Tile)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Dashboard-Tiles/Market-to-Actual-Rent-Comparison.htm

The Market To Actual Rent Comparison dashboard tile compares the differences between the market rent and the actual recurring rent charges for all occupied units and home-type assets, which include all active rent charges for tenants and homeowners whether those charges are established at the tenant, unit, unit type, or property level. This comparison identifies the gross potential rent (GPR) for each unit and home-type asset, which allows you to track lost profits based on the amounts that are being charged to tenants and homeowners through their leases.

 More Information

 The Market To Actual Rent Comparison dashboard tile always makes calculations based on lease and market rent values as of the first day for each month within the selected filtering period.

 The information on this dashboard tile may be represented in a graph or a list.

 To filter the information that displays in the dashboard tile, click arrow_forward Settings to open the Market to Actual Rent Comparison Data Filters pop-up. The available filter options are listed below.

 Option
 Description

 Property Filter

 Each property whose market and actual rent values are included in the tile results. To include all current and future properties, select <All Properties> . Alternatively, select a property Group from the drop-down list.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Period(s)

 The number of months, quarters, semiannual periods, or years (as defined by the Period Category ) to include in the tile results.

 Period Category

 The unit of time to use for the tile results ( Month(s) , Quarter(s) , Semiannual(s) , or Year(s) ).

 Graph Type

 The desired display option.

 Line

 The total dollar amount of market and actual rent display in a line graph, with market rent in blue and actual rent in orange. The exact dollar amount for each date can be viewed by hovering your cursor over each point on the graph.

 Bar

 The total dollar amount of market and actual rent display in a bar graph, with market rent in blue and actual rent in orange. The exact dollar amount for each date can be viewed by hovering your cursor over each bar on the graph.

 None

 The total dollar amount of market and actual rent display in columns.

 Ignore dashboard property filter

 If checked, override the property filter configured on the Dashboard .

 Axis Descriptions

 The information that displays on each axis in the graph is described below.

 Axis
 Description

 X
(Horizontal)

 The period being examined in a MM/YY format or, if you selected Year(s) as the period category, YYYY format.

 Y
(Vertical)

 The amounts of market rent and actual rent charges as of the period specified on the horizontal axis.

 Column Descriptions

 The information that displays on the tile is organized into the following columns.

 Column
 Description

 Date

 The period being examined in a MM/YY format or, if you selected Year(s) as the period category, YYYY format.

 Market Rent

 The total market rent for the units and/or home-type asset at each selected property as of the specified period.

 Actual Rent

 The actual recurring rent charges for all occupied units and/or home-type assets at each selected property, which include all active rent charges for tenants and homeowners whether those charges are established at the tenant, unit, unit type, or property level, as of the specified period.
