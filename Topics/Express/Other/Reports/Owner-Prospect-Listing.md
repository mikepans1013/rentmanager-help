# Owner Prospect Listing (Report)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Reports/Owner-Prospect-Listing.htm

The Owner Prospect Listing report displays information about your owner prospects based on when they were first and/or most recently contacted.

 Report Options

 The report options described below determine what data displays in the report.

 First Contact Date Range

 Enter a date, or click to select a date from the calendar to display first contact with prospects that occurred during that date range. Alternatively, adjust the dates using the available preset buttons or click to open more options for setting a date range.

 Only accounts that made first contact with your business during the selected date range display. The original contact date displays on the account's History/Notes tile in the earliest history note on the account.

 More Information

 Either the First contacted date or the First & last contacted dates must be selected in the Date Ranges to Use section for this option to affect the report results.

 Last Contact Date Range

 Enter a date, or click to select a date from the calendar to display last contact with prospects that occurred during that date range. Alternatively, adjust the dates using the available preset buttons or click to open more options for setting a date range.

 Only accounts whose last contact with your business during the selected date range display. The last contact date is determined by the most recent call, email, text, or visit history note that displays on the account's History/Notes tile.

 More Information

 Either the Last contacted date or the First & last contacted dates must be selected in the Date Ranges to Use section for this option to affect the report results. Additionally, call history/note items must have Spoke with owner prospect checked to be considered when determining last contact date.

 Date Range to Use

 Select an option to determine if the First Contact Date and/or the Last Contact Date are examined in the report results. The First Contact Date and Last Contact Date are determined by the earliest and most recent call, email, or visit history/note that displays on the account's History/Notes page.

 More Information

 Call history/note items must have Spoke with owner prospects checked to be considered when determining Last Contact Date .

 Option
 Description

 First contacted date

 Accounts which made first contact with your business within the date range selected in the First Contact Date section display.

 Last contacted date

 Accounts which last made contact with your business within the date range selected in the Last Contact Date section display.

 First & last contacted dates

 All accounts which made first contact with your business within the date range selected in the First Contact Date section and last made contact with your business within the date range selected in the Last Contact Date section display.

 Sales Rep

 Select a user from the drop-down list to display only owner prospects who are associated with that sales representative. You can select the sales representative for an owner prospect on the owner's details page on the Miscellaneous tile. Alternatively, to display all owner prospects regardless of the sales rep associated with the account, select All .

 More Information

 Only users who have the option Sales Rep/Leasing Agent checked on their user account display in this drop-down list.

 Sort Options

 Select one of the following options to determine how the report results are organized:

 Option
 Description

 Date Created

 Owner prospects are sorted chronologically by the date their accounts were created in Rent Manager in ascending order (oldest to newest).

 Date Created - Desc

 Owner prospects are sorted chronologically by the date their accounts were created in Rent Manager in descending order (newest to oldest).

 Last Contact

 Owner prospects are sorted chronologically by the date of the most recent history/note item where you were in contact in ascending order (oldest to newest).

 Last Contact - Desc

 Owner prospects are sorted chronologically by the date of the most recent history/note item where you were in contact in descending order (newest to oldest).

 Last Name

 Owner prospects are sorted alphabetically by their Name

 Report Results

 The report results are described below including, if applicable, section, column, and subreport descriptions.

 Report Header

 The report header displays the report name and key information selected in the report options, such as date information and which properties were examined in the report.

 Column Descriptions

 The columns that display in the report are described below.

 Column
 Description

 Owner Prospect

 The Name of each owner prospect.

 First Contact

 The date that contact was first made with this owner prospect as determined by the earliest history/note item on the owner prospect's details page.

 Last Contact

 The date that contact was last made with this owner prospect as determined by the most recent call, email, text, or visit history/note item displayed on the owner prospect's details page.

 Property

 The name(s) of each property defined for the owner prospect on the owner prospect's details page.

 Property Type

 The Property Type selected for each property on the owner prospect's details page.

 Unit Count

 The number of units at each property as entered in the Unit Count column for each property on the owner prospect's details page.

 Interest

 The owner prospect's estimated interest in your management services as selected on the owner prospect's details page, on the Miscellaneous tile, using the Interest Level slider.

 Source

 The name of the lead source or marketing strategy used to attract the owner prospect, as selected on the owner prospect's details page.

 Sales Rep

 The name of the Rent Manager user who acts as your company's main point of contact for the owner prospect as selected on the owner prospect's details page.

 Phone Number: Default

 The owner prospect phone number that is marked as the default on the owner prospect's details page.

 Status

 The status currently assigned to the owner prospect displays as Owner Prospect , Owner , Rejected , or Lost .
