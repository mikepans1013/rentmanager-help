Prospect Listing (Report)

The Prospect Listing report displays prospect information, unit preference, and information on your company's marketing efforts for prospects interested in the selected properties over a time range. Though this report is most commonly used by users with multiple leasing agents, it is also great reference to initiate follow-ups with prospects.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Letter/Email Templates/Reports/Packets Run reports Enabled

Additionally, on the Reports tab, you must have access to Prospect Listing.

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To view the Prospect Listing report, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Prospects arrow_forward Prospect Listing.
    The Reports: Prospect Listing page displays.

  2. Adjust the report options as desired. Each option is described below.

  3. Generate the report in your desired file format(s).

Related Preferences

Reports may look different depending on whether or not the Use modernized report style system preference is enabled. For more information, refer to General Report Options (System Preferences).

Report Options

The report options described below determine what data displays in the report.

Properties to Include

Select each property or a property Group to be included in the report.

More Information

This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property.

Optionally, check Show Inactive Properties to include properties that are no longer active.

Date Range to Use

Select an option to determine if the First Contact Date and/or the Last Contact Date are examined in the report results. The First Contact Date and Last Contact Date are determined by the earliest and most recent call, email, or visit history/note that displays on the account's History/Notes page.

Related Preferences

Call history/note items must have Spoke with prospect checked, and/or the system preference to Always update Last Contact Date when a call note is added enabled, to be considered when determining Last Contact Date. For more information, refer to Prospect (System Preferences).

Option Description

First contacted date

Accounts which made first contact with your business within the date range selected in the First Contact Date section display.

Last contacted date

Accounts which last made contact with your business within the date range selected in the Last Contact Date section display.

First & last contacted dates

All accounts which made first contact with your business within the date range selected in the First Contact Date section and last made contact with your business within the date range selected in the Last Contact Date section display.

Leasing Agent

Select an active user to display only prospects assigned to those leasing agent(s) in the report results. Optionally, select All to view all prospects, regardless of their leasing agent assignment. Only users with Leasing Agent selected on their User details page display in the list.

Run Properties Separately

Check to generate a separate report for each selected property. Otherwise, all selected properties are combined into a single report.

Prospects to Include

Select which prospects to include in this report, based on the status defined on the Prospect details page.

Option Description

Prospect

Prospect accounts who are interested in renting from you and have not signed a lease display.

Lost

Prospect accounts that are marked Lost because they no longer plan to lease from you display.

Lost - Rejected

Prospect accounts that are marked Lost-Rejected because you no longer plan to lease to them display.

Tenant

Prospect accounts who are renting from you or are renting from you in the future and are converted to tenants display.

First Contact Date Range

Enter a date, or click to select a date from the calendar to display first contact with prospects that occurred during that date range. Alternatively, adjust the dates using the available preset buttons or click to open more options for setting a date range.

Only accounts that made first contact with your business during the selected date range display. The original contact date displays on the account's History/Notes tile in the earliest history note on the account.

More Information

Either the First contacted date or the First & last contacted dates must be selected in the Date Ranges to Use section for this option to affect the report results.

Last Contact Date Range

Enter a date, or click to select a date from the calendar to display last contact with prospects that occurred during that date range. Alternatively, adjust the dates using the available preset buttons or click to open more options for setting a date range.

Only accounts whose last contact with your business during the selected date range display. The last contact date is determined by the most recent call, email, text, or visit history note that displays on the account's History/Notes tile.

Related Preferences

Either the Last contacted date or the First & last contacted dates must be selected in the Date Ranges to Use section for this option to affect the report results. Additionally, call history/note items must have Spoke with prospect checked, and/or the system preference to Always update Last Contact Date when a call note is added enabled, to be considered when determining last contact date. For more information, refer to Prospect (System Preferences).

Sort Options

Select one of the following options to determine how the report results are sorted:

Option Description

Date Created

Prospects are sorted chronologically by the date their accounts were created in Rent Manager in ascending order (oldest to newest).

Date Created - Desc

Prospects are sorted chronologically by the date their accounts were created in Rent Manager in descending order (newest to oldest).

Last Contact

Prospects are sorted chronologically by the date of the most recent history note where you were in contact in ascending order (oldest to newest).

Last Contact - Desc

Prospects are sorted chronologically by the date of the most recent history note where you were in contact in descending order (newest to oldest).

Last Name

Prospects are sorted alphabetically by their Last Name.

Report Results

The report results are described below including, if applicable, section, column, and subreport descriptions.

Report Header

The report header displays the report name and key information selected in the report options, such as date information and which properties were examined in the report.

Column Descriptions

The columns that display in the report are described below.

Column Description

Prospect

The full name of each prospect with the last name first.

First Contact

The date the contact was first made with this prospect as determined by the earliest history note on made for the prospect.

Last Contact

The date the contact was last made with this prospect as determined by the most recent call, email, text, visit, or application history/note displayed on the prospect's History/Notes page.

More Information

For calls, the option Spoke with prospect on the history/note item must be checked for the call to display.

Property

The short name of the prospect's default property. A default property is selected in the prospect add wizard and can be edited on the prospect's details page.

Unit

The name of the unit where a prospect who signed a lease and was converted into a tenant is now leasing. For all other prospects, this column is blank.

Unit Types

The unit type(s) that a prospect is interested in renting as entered on the prospect's Preferences tile.

Interested Units

The name of the unit that the prospect is interested in renting as identified in the Interested Properties / Units section, including units indicated by the Wait List column, of the prospect's Interested Properties / Units tile.

Source

The name of the lead source or marketing strategy used to attract the prospect as selected on the prospect's Lead Information tile.

Agent

The name of the user who is associated with the prospect either because they made first contact with the prospect or they were responsible for attracting the prospect to one of your properties.

Phone Number: Default

The prospect phone number that is marked as Default on the prospect's View Contacts page.

Status

Indicates one of the following statuses to help you track the status of prospects interested in leasing from you. Each status is described below.

Pros

A prospect who is interested in leasing from you but has not yet signed a lease.

Lost

A prospect who is no longer interested in leasing from you.

Rej

A prospect that you are no longer interested in allowing to lease from you.

Tena

A prospect who has signed a lease and is now leasing from you or will be renting from you in the future.
