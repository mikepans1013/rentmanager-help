# Flexible Rent Status (Report)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Reports/Flexible-Rent-Status.htm

The Flexible Rent Status report displays a list of tenants who have enrolled in Flexible Rent via Tenant Web Access (TWA) or rmResident , the status of their enrollment, and the date that their status was last updated.

 More Information

 The Flexible Rent service is free to Rent Manager customers, but tenants must pay a monthly fee and a bill payment fee of 1% of their total rent to Flex to use the program. For more information, refer to Flexible Rent .

 Report Options

 The report options described below determine what data displays in the report.

 Properties to Include

 Select each property or a property Group to be included in the report.

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 Optionally, check Show Inactive Properties to include properties that are no longer active.

 Status

 Select the desired Flexible Rent enrollment status(es) from the drop-down list so only tenants with the selected Status display. To include tenants with all statuses, check the box next to the search bar.

 Option
 Description

 Deactivated

 The tenant was enrolled in Flexible Rent, but their account was disabled due to a change made in Rent Manager that changed their eligibility, such as ePay being disabled for the tenant or property, or the options Do Not Accept Payments or Do Not Accept TWA Payments being checked on the tenant's account.

 Enrolled

 The tenant is currently enrolled in Flexible Rent and Flex pays the tenant's balance after their upfront payment clears.

 Ineligible

 The tenant attempted to enroll in Flexible Rent, but was not approved.

 Paused

 The tenant is enrolled in Flexible Rent, but their account is frozen by Flex while they work with the tenant to resolve an issue, such as the tenant being behind on payments. While this status is set, Flex does not pay the tenant's balance.
