# History Privilege Group

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Privileges/History.htm

History privileges provide access to history/note features for tenants, prospects, properties, units, vendors, owners, service issues, estimates, purchase orders, assets, jobs, and violations. They also provide access to add or update history categories.

 To manage these privileges, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Users arrow_forward Users . Select a user and click the Privileges tab.

 -
 Click next to the privilege group to expand the privilege list.

 -
 To enable a privilege, check the box. Each privilege is described below.

 -
 Click Save .
