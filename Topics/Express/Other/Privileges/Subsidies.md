# Subsidies Privilege Group

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Privileges/Subsidies.htm

Subsidies privileges provide access to the Subsidy feature, which allows you to define subsidies (for example, for entities such as FEMA or HUD) and then receive a large single Subsidy payment and distribute it to the customers associated with that subsidy.

 The subsidy payment forms may also be used to receive and distribute unrelated multi-tenant payments you have filtered by property, balance due, and/or tenant status.

 -
 To enable a privilege, check the box. Each privilege is described below.

 -
 Click Save .

 Privilege Descriptions

 Each privilege is described below.

 Privilege
 Description

 Manage

 Allows a user to add, view, edit, and delete subsidies. When you select a subsidy during a subsidy payment, that subsidy's definitions ( Charge Type , Payment From , and associated customers) automatically populate the payment form.

 Payment List

 Allows a user to view and delete (roll back) Subsidies and multi-tenant payments that you have received and paid (distributed) on the subsidy posts form.

 Payment Detail

 Allows a user to double-click a subsidy listed on the subsidy posts form and view its payment detail. Note that no changes can be made to the Subsidy Payment detail page.

 Payment Form

 Allows a user to perform Make a Subsidy Payment . Single subsidy or multi-tenant payments can be received and distributed to selected customers through this feature.
