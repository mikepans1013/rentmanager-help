Publish Other Blue Moon Lease Forms

Blue Moon is a software from the National Apartment Association (NAA) that provides industry-standard leasing documents that are compliant with your state and/or industry association (e.g., NAA, TAA, GAA, AANC). Blue Moon can be integrated with Rent Manager to export your tenant and prospect information directly into Blue Moon leasing documents.

More Information

In order to use Blue Moon to generate leasing documents and create leases, you must first purchase a license(s) from the NAA , then enable and configure your Blue Moon integration. For more information, refer to Set Up Blue Moon.

Your contract with Blue Moon determines if you can also generate other lease forms that are not required by the state or NAA and which ones are available for you to generate, such as authorization forms or guarantees of payment.

Related Preferences

In order to generate these other lease forms, you must enable the Allow Other Lease Forms option in system preferences. For more information, refer to Blue Moon General (System Preferences).

Step 1: Select a Form

To generate an other lease Blue Moon form, do the following:

  1. Navigate to the desired entity and start the lease creation using the table below.

    Entity Navigation

    Prospect

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects. For the prospect receiving the lease document, click arrow_forward Blue Moon: Create Other Lease Forms.

    Tenant

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant. Then on the Leases tile next to the icon, click the drop-down arrow and select Create Other Lease Form.

  2. In the Form field, select the Blue Moon form you wish to generate.
    The pop-up expands to display the fields.

Step 2: Enter Field Values

On the second page of the pop-up, a list of all the Blue Moon fields for the selected form displays. In the Value column, the value of the mapped Rent Manager field, user-defined field, or charge type(s) displays.

More Information

The information that populates in the Value column is based on your Blue Moon field mapping settings in system preferences. For more information, refer to Blue Moon Form & Fields Setup (System Preferences).

To edit or add field values, do the following:

  1. For the fields named Resident #, enter the names of the tenant or prospect contact(s) who are signing the lease.

  2. Review all fields that pull information from Rent Manager data to ensure the correct information is populated.

  3. If there are any remaining blank fields, enter the applicable information into those fields. The fields available vary depending on the selected Form.

  4. Click Create.
    The form is generated and can be viewed on the tenant's details page in the Leases tile by clicking View Documents, or on the prospect's or tenant's History/Notes pop-up by clicking for that history/note item.
