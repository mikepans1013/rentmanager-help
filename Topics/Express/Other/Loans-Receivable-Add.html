Add a Loan Receivable Loan

The Add Loan pop-up is an integrated series of sections designed to help you easily add a new owner-financed loan into Rent Manager. The loan created on this page is added to the Loans Receivable page where you can manage, track, and report the loan through its scheduled process, default the loan, or pay off the loan.

Step 1: Enter Tenant and Interest Method Information

To create a new loan receivable, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Loans Receivable arrow_forward Loans Receivable and click Add Loan.

  2. In the Tenant Information section, enter general information about the tenant responsible for the loan.

    Field Description

    Tenant

    The tenant responsible for this loan. You can type in the field to narrow the list to match your search criteria.

    Unit

    The unit associated with this loan. By default, the unit associated with the first lease listed for the tenant on the View Leases pop-up.

  3. In the Interest Method section, select which method by which the loan calculates interest.

    Field Description

    Based on Current Principal Balance

    Calculated every month based on remaining principal.

    The interest portion of each payment installment is recalculated when you post based on the current remaining principal. Therefore, paying more than expected on an installment (or making extra payments) reduces the remaining principal and results in paying less than expected interest on the next recalculated installment.

    Straight Amortization

    Calculated every month based on a standard amortization chart, regardless of remaining principal.

    The interest portion of each payment installment is a fixed amount each month based on the original calculation using standard amortization. Actual payments (even extra payments) do not affect the calculated interest portion of each installment.

    Daily Interest

    Calculation is based on remaining principal and days in period.

    This type of loan is not posted on the Post Loans page like other loans. Its transactions (interest and principal charges) are automatically entered on the tenant's Transactions tile at the time you make a payment via the tenant's Add Payment pop-up.

    The interest portion of this charge is the total of daily interest calculated at the time of payment and based on the remaining principal and the number of days since the last payment. The following options are available when this field is selected.

    Days before next due date to display payments

    Rent Manager makes the next loan installment available to be paid on the tenant's Add Payment pop-up this many days before the scheduled installment date. For example, if the next installment is due on 5/1 and you specify 10 days in this Days before field, that installment is displayed in the Add Payment pop-up beginning on 4/21 (i.e., 10 days prior to May 1st).

    Days for interest calculation

    Specify if daily interest is calculated based on 360 days (360 Days = 30 days per month x 12 months) or 365 days per year.

Step 2: Enter Loan Details

Once you've entered the tenant and interest information, select the Loan Details tab on the left. Enter the information below to define important dates regarding the loan. Some fields pre-populate with values from the selected tenant and the designated interest method.

Field Description

Reference

The loan number automatically generated by Rent Manager.

Close Date

The date the loan was signed; the default is today's date.

Origination Date

The date on which to begin calculating interest; the default is today's date or the Close Date, if the date in that field has been changed.

Payment Start Date

The due date of the first payment; the default is one month from the Origination Date.

Payment Due Day

The day of the month on which the loan payment is due (a number between 0 and 31). The default is the same day of the month as entered for the Payment Start Date.

Acquisition Date

The date on which the loan was purchased from another lender. If the loan was not acquired from another lender, this field should be left blank.

Insurance Expiration Date

The date on which the insurance expires. Maintaining insurance coverage, such as homeowners' insurance or private mortgage insurance (PMI), may be a condition of the loan.

Step 3: Enter the Loan Amount

Once you've entered the loan details, select the Loan Amount tab on the left. Enter the information below to define important payment information regarding the loan's full amount.

Field Description

Sale Amount

The full amount of the sale for the loan.

Down Payment

The amount of money applied toward a down payment on the sale.

Loan Amount

The amount of the loan being financed, calculated using the following formula:

Loan Amount = Sale Amount - Down Payment

Initial Interest Rate

The beginning Interest Rate of the loan as a percentage (e.g., 7.0000 means 7%). You have the opportunity to change the interest rate, as desired, throughout the loan later on the pop-up.

More Information

If this loan's interest rates vary over time, then after you create the loan, you can add a rate schedule. For more information, refer to Add a Loans Receivable Rate Schedule.

Specify By

The way the loan length of the loan is determined. The following options are available:

Term

The length of the loan in number of months.

Payment Amount

The length of the loan based on the monthly payment amount.

Term of Loan (months)

The length of the loan in months. To get an estimate for how long the length should be based on the payment, click Estimate from Payment.

Extend loan term until balance is fully paid

When enabled, if a loan's final payment exceeds the regular monthly amount, the loan's term extends using the regular monthly amount until all principal has been charged. Otherwise, the full remaining principal is posted on the last payment period of the loan.

Increase principal to cover interest

When enabled, a credit covering the unpaid interest is posted against the principal to increase the balance. Otherwise, the unpaid interest remains open on the tenant's Transactions tile because the whole interest charge was posted but only partially paid.

This option is typically for daily interest used to account for late payments made long after the scheduled payment date. In such cases, the interest amount may be more than the scheduled payment amount.

Users also often check the Extend loan term until balance is fully paid option when this option is checked.

Extend loan term until balance is fully paid

When enabled, if a loan's final payment exceeds the regular monthly amount, the loan's term extends using the regular monthly amount until all principal has been charged. Otherwise, the full remaining principal is posted on the last payment period of the loan.

Use average principal to calculate interest

When enabled, the loan's average principal balance since the last payment is used to calculate the next payment's interest. Otherwise, the loan's current principal balance is used to calculate the next payment's interest.

Step 4: Enter Information In User Defined Fields

Once you have filled in the information related to the loan's full amount, click the User Defined Fields tab on the left. All required loan-type user defined fields (UDFs) display in a list. If applicable, enter any needed information in the associated Value field. If you need to view or edit values for optional UDFs, click View All UDFs.

More Information

If the loan was established prior to your using Rent Manager and you are managing the loan in Rent Manager going forward, complete step 5. If this is a new loan you are adding to Rent Manager and you need to add additional charges to the loan, skip step 5 and proceed to step 6.

Step 5: Add a Pre-Existing Loan

After entering any UDF values, select the Pre-existing Loan tab on the left. Marking a loan as preexisting applies the appropriate credits and adjustments for previously collected payments so that Rent Manager knows that those payments were already made.

To set up a pre-exisiting loan, check Pre-existing Loan and enter the following information:

Field Description

Starting Payment Number

The payment number of the first payment to be collected in Rent Manager. For example, if four installments have been previously collected outside of Rent Manager, enter 5 as the Starting Payment Number.

Based on how you have structured your loan thus far, Rent Manager then supplies values for Prior Interest Year and Prior Interest Amount.

Adjusted Principal

The outstanding remaining principal amount of the loan.

Adjusted Payment Start Date

The loan's new start date in Rent Manager.

Prior Interest Year

The year of the previously collected interest as it should display on the current year's 1098 form.

Prior Interest Amount

The amount of the previously collected interest as it should display on the current year's 1098 form.

Step 6: Enter Charge Types

When you have finished entering any information about the pre-existing loan, click the Charge Typestab on the left. Enter information about the charge types associated with the loan principal, interest, and principal prepayment.

Field Description

Principal

The charge type for allocating a payment amount to a loan's principal charge after your monthly loan postings. This charge type is typically linked to an asset chart account.

Interest

The charge type for allocating a payment amount to a loan's interest charge after your monthly loan postings. This charge type is typically linked to an income chart account.

Principal Prepayment

The charge type for allocating a payment amount to a loan's prepaid principal (credit) between your monthly loan postings. This charge type is typically linked to an asset chart account.

Notes Receivable

The charge type used in conjunction with the Sales charge type to automatically record the loan in Rent Manager.

This charge type represents the credit offsetting the Sales charge type and is typically linked to the same asset chart account used by the Principal and Principal Prepayment charge types. In that way, the negative amount recorded with the initial loan is reduced with positive payments throughout the duration of the loan.

Sales

The charge type used in conjunction with the Notes Receivable charge type to automatically record the loan in Rent Manager.This charge type represents the loan and is offset by the Notes Receivable charge type. The Sales charge type is typically linked to an income chart account.

To determine how Rent Manager records a new loan transaction, check the Do not create sales transactions option. When enabled, the tenant's Sales charge and offsetting Notes Receivable charge credit is not be created. It is up to the user to manually create the transactions if they are not already accounted for in current balances.

More Information

If you do not need to add any additional charges, click Save to complete the creation process and skip the remaining step below.

Step 7: Enter Other Charges

After completing the information about the loan's charge types, click the Other Charges tab on the left. Enter additional charges, if any, that are posted on the loan, such as escrow and PMI.

To add other charges, do the following:

  1. Click Add Other Charge and enter information into the following fields:

    Field Description

    Charge Type

    The charge type to be associated with the additional charge.

    Comment

    Additional information, such as why the charge has been added.

    Amount

    The total dollar amount of the charge.

  2. Repeat the process for all additional charges.

  3. Click Save to complete the creation process of the additional charges and the loan.
    The pop-up closes and the loan is added to Rent Manager.
