Short Term Rental (STR) Reservation Check-In

When guests are ready to check into their reservations at your short-term rental (STR) properties, you can use the Check In wizard to easily check them in. Information entered when the guest made the reservation populates in this wizard by default. Before checking in your guests, you can use this wizard to review and edit information about vehicles, pets, number of guests, and payments.

Warning

To access the STR tool, at least one of your properties in Rent Manager must have the Short Term Rental or RV/Campground property type assigned.

Step 2: Enter Reservation Info

In this section, enter general information about the reservation, such as the property, arrival date, and number of guests.

Field Description

Property

The property where the guest made a reservation.

Unit

The unit the guest reserved.

Arrival

The date the guest plans to arrive.

Departure

The date the guest plans to leave. Rent Manager automatically ends the rental on the specified departure date.

# of Nights

The calculated number of nights between the Arrival date and the Departure date. If no Departure date is entered, this value displays 0.

# of Adults

The number of adults associated with the reservation.

# of Children

The number of children associated with the reservation.

# of Pets

The number of pets associated with the reservation.

Comment

Any additional notes or comments about the reservation.

Lead Source

How or where the guest learned about your property or business.

Related Preferences

When guests make reservations, the STR Online Booking Portal displays only the lead sources selected in system web preferences. For more information, refer to RV/Campground Online Booking Guest Info (System Web Preferences).

Source

Where the guest reservation was created. When manually adding a reservation, Rent Manager displays. If the guest made the reservation online, STR Online Booking displays.

Confirmation #

A system-generated eight-digit random code used to identify a specific reservation.

Step 3: Enter RV/Vehicle Details

In this section, enter information about the vehicle or other equipment the guest is using during their reservation.

Field Description

On-site

A  indicates that the guest plans to bring their vehicle or equipment to the specific unit they are renting.

Type

The guest's RV type (Fifth Wheel, Hybrid Trailer, Pop-Up Camper, and so on). Guests with an RV type that is listed on the unit's RV/Campground Information tile in the Prohibited Eqpt. field cannot reserve the unit.

Make

The brand of the guest's RV or vehicle.

Model

The model of the guest's RV or vehicle.

Length

The length of the guest's RV or vehicle. The RV length must be equal to or less than the value entered on the unit's RV/Campground Information tile in the Length field.

Width

The width of the guest's RV or vehicle. The RV width must be equal to or less than the value entered on the unit's RV/Campground Information tile in the Width field.

Year

The manufacture year of the guest's RV or vehicle.

License

The license plate number of the guest's RV or vehicle.

Amps

The amperage(s) of the electrical hookup that the guest's RV requires.

Slide Outs

The type(s) of room extenders on the guest's RV or vehicle. Guests can only reserve units that allow their type of slide out, as listed on the unit's RV/Campground Information tile in the Slide Outs field.

# of Slide Outs

The number of room extenders on the guest's RV or vehicle.

Step 4: Enter Pet Information

In this section, enter information about pets the guest is bringing to their reservation.

Column Description

Pet Type

The kind of animal, such as a dog or cat, the guest is bringing. The pets that are available to add depend on the pet types for the property.

Name

The name of the pet associated with the guest.

Description

An optional message about the pet (e.g., Does not play well with other dogs).

Breed

The specific group of animals the pet belongs to. For example, American Bulldog can be entered as a dog breed.

Color

The color of the pet associated with the account (e.g., White with light beige spots).

Age

The age of the pet associated with the account. Age can be entered alphanumerically for accuracy (e.g., 5 and 1/2 years).

Size

The weight or size of the pet associated with the guest. This can be helpful for properties that may have weight limits to abide by.

Step 5: Enter UDF Information

In this section, enter a Value for any tenant-type user defined fields (UDFs) to record additional information about the guest. Only UDFs marked as required display. Click View All UDFs to display all of the available UDFs that can be used for this guest.

Step 6: Enter Transactions

In this section, any open transactions linked to the guest's account display. You can add one-time charges from this screen. Or, if you set up STR rate schedules for this property, those charges display here.

Column Description

Date

The date that the charge or payment was processed.

Reference #

An optional reference number used to organize transactions in Rent Manager.

Description

The description of the charge type associated with the transaction.

Comment

An optional comment explaining the transaction.

Amount

The total dollar amount of the payment or charge.

Balance

The running total of all guest charges, minus any payments they already made.

One-Time Charges

One-time charges are single charges or credits you can add to the guest account. When checking in a reservation, they are useful for situations such as pet deposits, promotional credits, or prorating for long term reservations if they checked in after the first day of the month.

To add a one-time charge for the guest to pay, do the following:

  1. Click Add Charge.

    The Transaction Detail pop-up displays.

  2. Enter or select the information for the one-time charge or credit in the available fields described below.

    Field Description

    Charge Type

    The charge type to use for this transaction. The posted charge expenses the GL account linked to the charge type.

    Date

    The date on which the transaction is applied to the tenant account regardless of when the tenant account is created.

    Amount

    The dollar amount charged to the tenant for this transaction. To credit the tenant, enter a negative amount.

    If charging the tenant for a partial month, enter the full amount for the month and then click Prorate.

    On the Prorate Transaction pop-up, the current charge information displays. In the Days Prorated field, enter how many days for which to prorate the transaction based on a thirty-day period. If needed, you can edit the total amount to prorate in the Full Charge Amount field to adjust the calculation. The Prorated Amount field displays the calculated amount for the remaining days of a thirty-day month.

    For example, the tenant's reservation begins on June fourteenth and their monthly fee is $1,200. In the Days Prorated field, enter 14 and in the Full Charge Amount field, enter 1200.00. The Prorated Amount field displays 560.00, which is the calculated amount for the remaining sixteen days.

    Comment

    A note to display for this recurring charge and on the associated charges on the tenant's View Transactions page when posted.

  3. If you are adding a credit with a negative Amount, the Preallocations section displays. Click Add Item to preallocate this credit to a specific Charge Type for a specified Amount. When other charges are added to this tenant, this credit is applied to those charges only if it has the selected charge type, up to the entered dollar amount.

  4. In the Other Information section, enter a Reference # to further identify the transaction.

  5. Click Save.

    The charge is created and added to the guest's details page.

Stay Charges

If the guest's reservation uses an STR rate schedule to determine charges, you can quickly add all scheduled charges by clicking Add Stay Charges. For example, if the rate schedule was made active after the guest made their reservation and you want to add the scheduled charges to an existing reservation, you can use this option to add all applicable charges at once.

Member Discounts

Member discounts can be a flat amount or as a percentage to reduce the reservation fees for stays that occur during seasonal events, advertisement campaigns, or based on the duration of the stay or the time of the year the guest reserves the unit. To apply a member discount to a guest's reservation charges, check Apply Member Discount and select a member discount from the drop-down. For more information, refer to Property STR Settings (Pop-Up).

More Information

If a member discount has already been applied to the reservation before check in, the checkbox is disabled. Member discounts cannot be applied if a promotion has been applied to the reservation. For more information, refer to Short Term Rental (STR) Promotions (Page).

Step 7: Finish the Check in

After all details are entered, do the following to finish the check in:

  1. If your STR property uses a preset payment policy, in the Payment Policy section, review the total Due at arrival.

  2. At the bottom of the Check In wizard, click Check In.
    The Check In confirmation pop-up displays.

  3. On the confirmation pop-up, click Finish to complete the check in, or click Finish & Pay to open the Add Payment pop-up and enter the guest's payment information for any open charges.
    The guest is checked in.
