# RV/Campground Online Booking General (System Web Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Web-Preferences/STR-Online-Booking-General.htm

These preferences allow you to select which properties are enabled for Short Term Rental (STR) Online Booking as well as define your payment booking policy. When a property is selected, its associated Online Booking URL becomes active.

 More Information

 The STR Online Booking feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 Preference Descriptions

 This preference group is divided into multiple sections. Each setting is described below.

 Enable Properties

 The Enable Properties section lists all active properties with the RV/Campground property type.

 When a property from the list is selected, the STR Online Booking button is enabled on the property's details page in the Rate Schedule pop-up. Additionally, a sharable URL that enables guests to book online is added to the property's Marketing Setup pop-up.

 More Information

 To allow your guests to book online, ePay must be enabled at the property. To learn how to enable ePay for a property, refer to Property ePay Settings (Pop-Up) .

 Default STR Payment Policy

 To ensure your property is ready to be booked by guests, it is important to establish a payment policy. By default, Rent Manager populates the Payment Policy field with 100% Due at Booking .

 The option selected here applies to all properties you have enabled for STR Online Booking. Payment policies can also be set at the property level.

 Payment Link Message

 To customize the email and text message guests receive when they are sent a link to make a payment, click Customize Payment Link Message . Use the Customize Payment Link Email/Text pop-up to edit the default message when sending payment links via text or email.
