# Pay Off Loans Receivable

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Loans-Receivable-Pay-Off.htm

The Loan Payoff pop-up allows you to pay off a tenant's loan before the end of its term. Additionally, this pop-up lets you view the loan payoff details (and determine what is to be included) as of a specific date before performing the actual payoff.

 Step 1: Start the Payoff Process

 To perform a payoff, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward Loans Receivable arrow_forward Loans Receivable and select the loan you want to pay off.
The Loans Receivable page displays.

 -
 At the top of the page, click Pay Off .

 Step 2: Include Other Charges

 Optionally, on the Included Other Charges tile, select one or more charge types of existing charges on the account that are not necessarily related to the loan. These charges are posted whenever the loan is posted.

 The value of any charge type(s) selected on this tile display on the Payoff Breakdown tile's Included Other Charges field.

 Column
 Description

 Amount

 The total dollar amount of the charge.

 Charge Type

 The name of charge type associated with the existing charge.

 Date

 The date the charge is applied to the tenant's account.

 Step 3: Add Additional Charges

 The Additional Charges tile allows you to add charges at the time of payoff at your discretion. For example, charges added here can be related to the payoff process or could be other outstanding charges from the borrower's account.

 The value of any charge type(s) selected on this tile display on the Payoff Breakdown tile's Additional Charges field.

 To add other charges, click Add Additional Charge and enter information into the following fields:

 Field
 Description

 Amount

 The total dollar amount of the charge.

 Charge Type

 The charge type to be associated with the additional charge.

 Comment

 Additional information, such as why the charge has been added.

 Step 4: Review the Payoff Breakdown

 The Payoff Breakdown tile displays the total amount needed to pay off the loan's obligations and other charges you include.

 If Include Daily Interest is checked, daily interest is calculated from the last loan posting to the payoff Date . The resulting value is displayed in the Accumulated Interest field.

 Step 5: Enter Payoff Options

 After you have made adjustments (if any) to the Payoff Breakdown tile's Total field, you may make a loan payoff payment.

 To make the loan payoff payment, in the Payoff Options tile, do the following:

 -
 In the Date field, select the payment date.

 Warning

 Changing the payment Date may recalculate some Payoff Breakdown categories (e.g., Accumulated Interest ) and adjust the Total .

 -
 In the Reference field, enter the check number or select Cash , MO , or CC as the payment type from the drop-down list.

 -
 In the Comment field, enter additional information that appears with the payment transaction on the tenant's View Transactions pop-up.

 -
 Click Save .
The Loan Default pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
 Rent Manager makes the payment and changes the loan's Status from Open to Closed .
