# Make a Manual Owner Contribution

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Owners-Contributions.htm

When an owner wants to put money into their portfolio, such as to cover the cost of expenses or to assist with improvements to a property, this is called an owner contribution. You can record contributions for each property in the portfolio as needed. Properties must have an active ownership to receive a contribution.

 Step 1: Filter Owners

 To filter owner contribution payments, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward Owner Payments arrow_forward Owner Contributions .

 -
 Select the Owner making the contribution.

 -
 Enter the post Date for the contribution.

 -
 Enter an optional note in the Comment field that provides additional information about the contribution.

 -
 If you have a Zego ePay account, you can check Use ePay to transfer the funds electronically.

 Step 2: Make Contributions

 After filtering by owner, the listing updates with the selected owner's ownerships. To update and record an owner's contribution payment, do the following:

 -
 Review the information in the following columns that display on the page:

 Column
 Description

 Partners

 If a property has split ownership between the primary owner and any other owners, the additional owners display in this column.

 Property

 The full name of the properties where the owner is selected as the primary owner.

 Property Short Name

 The abbreviated name of the properties where the owner is selected as the primary owner.

 Bank Balance

 The current dollar amount of funds that the owner has available to spend on the property.

 Security Deposits

 The total dollar amount of any security deposits held for this owner.

 Own %

 The percentage of the property that belongs to the owner.

 Reserve

 The minimum amount of money that must remain in the bank to cover expenses for each property.

 Comment

 An optional note specific to the contribution for this property that provides additional information about the contribution, such as what the money is to be used for.

 Contribution Amount

 The amount of the total contribution to add to the Bank Balance for the ownership of the property.

 -
 For each property to which the owner is making a contribution, enter the Contribution Amount .

 -
 Click Make Contribution .
The Make Contribution pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
A green check displays in the bottom right of the page, indicating that the contribution was successful.
