# Post eChecks

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/eChecks-Post.htm

Safeguard eChecks allows you to write checks and pay bills electronically through Rent Manager and deliver the payment to the intended recipient(s) through email. The email includes a link that allows the recipient(s) to retrieve and print the check themselves on any printer. You can manually process eChecks on the Post eChecks page.

 If eChecks are set up to be manually processed, writing an eCheck or paying a bill with an eCheck is recorded in Rent Manager but the payment is not processed by Safeguard until you post eChecks .

 Related Preferences

 eChecks are processed manually if you don’t have the eChecks system preferences option to Automatically process eChecks when paying for Tenant Bills , Owner Bills , or Vendor Bills enabled. For more information, refer to eChecks (System Preferences) .

 To post eChecks , do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Checks arrow_forward Post eChecks .
The Post eChecks page displays.

 -
 Review the information in the following columns.

 Column
 Description

 Date

 The date on which the eCheck was issued to the payee.

 Payee

 The recipient of the eCheck payment.

 Payee Email

 The vendor, owner, prospect, or tenant email address that is associated with the payee of the eCheck.

 Bank Account

 The bank from which funds are drawn.

 Memo

 An optional message providing additional information about the eCheck.

 Check No.

 The identifying number of the eCheck.

 Amount

 The total dollar amount of the eCheck.

 -
 Select which eChecks you wish to post. By default, all available eChecks are selected.

 -
 Click Post eChecks .
The confirmation pop-up displays.

 -
 Click OK  to confirm the posting.
The eChecks are sent to Safeguard for processing.
