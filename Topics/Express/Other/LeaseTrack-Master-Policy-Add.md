# Add a LeaseTrack Insurance Master Policy

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/LeaseTrack-Master-Policy-Add.htm

Insurance tracking and Master policy enforcement is available through an integration with LeaseTrack . Additionally, you can require tenants and manufactured housing homeowners to be included on a Master policy insurance plan if they currently have no insurance, or if their insurance coverage is inadequate. Your Master policies can be added to your insurance settings after tracking is enabled.

 More Information

 Before adding a Master policy, you must first enable insurance tracking. You can opt to add a Master policy during this setup process. For more information, refer to Activate and Set Up LeaseTrack Insurance .

 The steps to add a Master policy vary slightly depending on whether or not you already have a Master policy set up in Rent Manager . Refer to the headings below for each process.

 Add Your First Master Policy

 If this is your first time adding a Master policy after you have enabled insurance tracking, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then select Rental Info arrow_forward Properties/Units arrow_forward Insurance .
The Insurance page displays.

 -
 In the Master Policies tile, click Add Master Policy .

 -
 In the LeaseTrack Policy Number field, enter the policy number for your Master policy. This policy number is provided by LeaseTrack and should begin with MSIRM , but do not enter these letters in the field.

 -
 Click Verify .
The Master Policy field populates with the liability covered by the Master policy (e.g., 100K Renters/Homeowners Insurance , 300K Renters/Homeowners Insurance ).

 -
 Enter information in the available fields.

 Field
 Description

 Default Amount

 The default dollar amount of the charge posted each month from LeaseTrack to apply to all properties. The maximum amount you can enter is 50 .

 After you complete this setup process, you can adjust the amount for each property individually as needed.

 The recommended amount to charge for a Master policy is dependent on the policy option you establish with LeaseTrack . For example, the recommended amount to charge for a 100K Master policy is between $14 and $20, the recommended amount to charge for a 300K Master policy is between $17 and $23, and so on

 Default Charge Type

 The charge type for Rent Manager to use for the charges from LeaseTrack .

 Properties to include in master policy

 Each applicable property the Master policy applies to. Properties of the following property types are not included in the list: Short Term Rental , RV/Campground , and Association .

 More Information

 This field populates only with the properties to which you have access. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 -
 To complete adding the Master policy and make further customizations to it, click Save & Finish . To refresh the pop-up and add another Master policy, click Save & New and enter the information for that policy.
The pop-up closes and the Master policy's details display in the Master Policies tile.

 More Information

 If you need to set a different fee amount for any specific properties on the Master policy, you can enter that custom amount in the property's Charge Amount column. Then click Save .

 Add an Additional Master Policy

 If you have one Master policy in place and need to add an additional Master policy, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then select Rental Info arrow_forward Properties/Units arrow_forward Insurance .
The Insurance page displays.

 -
 In the Master Policies tile, click Add Master Policy .

 -
 In the LeaseTrack Policy Number field, enter the policy number for your Master policy. This policy number is provided by LeaseTrack and should begin with MSIRM , but do not enter these letters in the field.

 -
 Click Verify .
The Master Policy field populates with the liability covered by the Master policy (e.g., 100K Renters/Homeowners Insurance , 300K Renters/Homeowners Insurance ).

 -
 Enter information in the available fields.

 Field
 Description

 Default Amount

 The default dollar amount of the charge posted each month from LeaseTrack to apply to all properties. The maximum amount you can enter is 50 .

 After you complete this setup process, you can adjust the amount for each property individually as needed.

 The recommended amount to charge for a Master policy is dependent on the policy option you establish with LeaseTrack . For example, the recommended amount to charge for a 100K Master policy is between $14 and $20, the recommended amount to charge for a 300K Master policy is between $17 and $23, and so on.

 The charge type for Rent Manager to use for the charges from LeaseTrack .

 -
 If you do not need to add an additional renters or homeowner Master policy, skip this step. If you do wish to add an additional renters or homeowners Master policy, click Add Master Policy and repeat steps 3 - 5.

 -
 Click Next .

 -
 If you do not need to move any properties from another Master policy to this one, skip this step. If there are any properties currently associated with another Master policy that need to be moved to this new policy, select those properties in the list and set the Transition Date for each property to the date on which the associated tenants or homeowners are officially covered by this new policy, and set any custom fee amounts in the Charge Amount column for that property. These properties are still temporarily listed in their prior Master policy with (Transitioning) appended until the transition is complete.

 More Information

 If one of your properties is currently associated with a Master policy but needs to be moved to a different policy you already established (such as upgrading a property's coverage from 100K to 300K), you can transition the properties. On the Insurance settings page in the Master Policies tile, click the drop-down arrow to the right of Add Properties and select Transition Properties .

 -
 Click Next .

 -
 In the list below, select each property to add to this Master policy. If that property should have a fee amount that is different from the Default Amount , enter the custom amount in the Charge Amount column for that property.

 -
 Click Add Policy .
The Master policy is added, and any property transfers to the new policy are scheduled to transfer on their entered transition date.
