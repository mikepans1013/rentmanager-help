# Send Tenant Self-Inspection

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Inspection-Send-Tenant.htm

Tenant self-inspections in Rent Manager allow tenants to complete unit inspections via rmResident . Tenant self-inspections can be added for individual tenants or for multiple tenants at a time on the Tenants page.

 Send a Single Tenant Self-Inspection

 To send a tenant self-inspection, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants .
The Tenants page displays.

 -
 On the tenant you want to send an inspection to, click arrow_forward Send Tenant Self-Inspection .
The Send Tenant Self-Inspection pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Inspection For section, verify the Tenant , Property , and Unit are correct.

 More Information

 The fields in the Inspection For section can be edited only when sending tenants inspections from the Inspections page.

 -
 In the Inspection Details section, make selections in the following fields:

 Field
 Description

 Expiration Date

 The date on which the tenant can no longer access or submit the inspection in rmResident .

 Inspection Description

 Additional details regarding the type of inspection the tenant is being sent. This information displays on the tenant's History/Notes tile.

 Inspection Type

 The category of inspection to be completed, such as Move-Out .

 Template

 The templated checklist of areas in the unit to review as part of the inspection.

 -
 Click Send .
The inspection is sent to the tenant for them to complete in rmResident .

 Send Multiple Tenant Self-Inspections in Bulk

 If you have more than one tenant that need to complete inspections at the same time, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants .
The Tenants page displays.

 -
 Click Bulk Actions arrow_forward Add Tenant Self-Inspection .

 -
 Select the tenants you wish to send inspections to.

 -
 Click Add Tenant Self Inspection .
The Send Tenant Self-Inspection pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter information in the following fields:

 Field
 Description

 Expiration Date

 The date on which the tenant can no longer access or submit the inspection in rmResident .

 Inspection Description

 Additional details regarding the type of inspection the tenant is being sent.

 This information displays on the tenant's History/Notes tile.

 Inspection Type

 The category of inspection to be completed, such as Move-Out .

 Template

 The templated checklist of areas in the unit to review as part of the inspection.

 -
 Click Send .
The inspection is sent to the selected tenants for them to complete in rmResident .
