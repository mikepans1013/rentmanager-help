# Assign Unit Licenses

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Units-Licenses-Assign.htm

If you use per unit licensing, Rent Manager is billed by purchasing a subscription for the number of units that you own. Each unit requires one license. Depending on the number of units in your database, you may need to unassign licenses from units you no longer wish to use (e.g., you've sold a property and purchased another) in order to assign those licenses to other units. Unlicensed units still exist in Rent Manager for reporting purposes.

 When you license a unit, the following actions may be taken:

 -
 Tenants can occupy the unit.

 -
 Prospects can reserve the unit.

 -
 Transactions can be associated with the unit.

 -
 The unit can be marketed using unit marketing and licensing system web preferences.

 You can purchase additional unit licenses through the LCS  Direct portal or by contacting sales@rentmanager.com .

 More Information

 You may be using the legacy option to pay for Rent Manager which is based on the number of users who log into the program concurrently. If you pay for Rent Manager through this per-user method, unit licenses are not required. To learn more about per unit licensing, refer to Rent Manager Online Licensing .

 Step 1: Unassign a Unit License

 More Information

 If you have purchased more licenses, you do not need unassign licenses from other units before assigning them to new ones. You can proceed to Step 2: Assign a Unit License.

 To unassign a license from a unit, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration . Then go to Licensing arrow_forward Assign Unit Licenses .

 -
 Select Unlicense Units at the top of the page.

 -
 In the Properties drop-down list, select the property where the units you want to unlicense are located.

 More Information

 Only properties to which you have access display. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 -
 To filter the list for specific types of units you wish to unlicense, select a Unit Type .

 -
 To filter the list for units with specific statuses, select a Unit Status .

 -
 Click the checkbox next to the unit whose license you wish to remove.

 -
 When you are satisfied with your selections, click Unlicense .
The units are unlicensed and cannot be reserved, occupied, marketed, or have associated transactions.

 Step 2: Assign a Unit License

 To assign a license to a unit, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Licensing arrow_forward Assign Unit Licenses .

 -
 Select License Units at the top of the page.

 -
 In the Properties drop-down list, select the property where the units you want to license are located.

 More Information

 Only properties to which you have access display. Your access to properties can be managed from your user account or on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property .

 -
 To filter the list for specific types of units you wish to license, select a Unit Type .

 -
 To filter the list for units with specific statuses, select a Unit Status .

 -
 Click the checkbox next to the units you wish to license.

 -
 When you are satisfied with your selections, click License .
The units are licensed and can be reserved, occupied, marketed, and have associated transactions.
