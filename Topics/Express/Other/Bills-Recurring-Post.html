Post Recurring Bills

Recurring bills are bill templates assigned to owners and/or vendors that are posted regularly as one-time bills. Recurring bills can be posted as often as needed, and you can apply filters to control which recurring bills you post at any point in time.

Related Preferences

If Task Automation is enabled in Rent Manager for recurring bills, you do not have to manually post the recurring bills that are automated. For more information, refer to Task Automation (System Preferences).

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Recurring Bills View
Post recurring bills Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To post recurring bills, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Post Recurring Bills.

  2. In the Filters section, make any of the following selections to choose which bills display:

    Filter Description

    Memo

    Recurring bills that include the message or reference number providing further context for the bill, as entered in the Memo field on the Bill details page, populate on the page.

    Properties

    Recurring bills associated with the selected properties populate on the page. Alternatively, select a property Group from the drop-down list.

    Vendor

    Recurring bills associated with the selected vendor populate on the page.

  3. Check each bill that you wish to post. The following columns display on the page. By default, some columns display only by using .

    Default Column Description

    Amount

    The value entered in the Amount field of the bill. This is the total dollar amount of the bill.

    Bank

    The bank account from which the recurring bill is paid.

    Created Date

    The date on which the recurring bill was created.

    Due Date

    The calculated due date of a bill. This date is calculated by adding the Terms to the Post Date.

    For instance, a bill that is posted on 10/13 with a Term of NET 15 (payment is due within fifteen days of the bill date) shows a due date of 10/28. A bill that is posted on 8/1 with a Term of ON THE 15th shows a due date of 8/15.

    Invoice #

    The reference or invoice number on the recurring bill.

    Last Post Date

    The last time this recurring bill was posted.

    Post Date

    The date the recurring bill is recognized in the general ledger on an accrual accounting basis. By default, the Post Date is the same as the Bill Date.

    Property Short Names

    The short name of the properties associated with the bill.

    Terms

    The day or time-frame that determines when a bill is considered due. For example, ON THE 15th indicates that the bill is due on the 15th day of the month.

    Times Left

    The number of remaining times the bill is created before the recurring bill posting ends. This number decreases by 1 every time that recurring bill is posted.

    Updated Date

    The date on which the recurring bill was most recently updated and saved.

    Vendor

    The owner, vendor, or Vendor Payee Name who is paid by this bill.
    Available Column Description

    Created By

    The user who created the recurring bill.

    Detail Memo

    Additional information regarding the recurring bill as entered on the recurring bill's details page in the first Memo field.

    Expense Account

    The expense account debited for the bill. If multiple expense accounts are debited, this column displays <Multiple>.

    Expense Account Number List

    The general ledger (GL) account number for the associated expense account.

    Memo

    Additional details regarding each line item of the recurring bill as entered in the line item Memo field.

    Properties

    The full name of the properties associated with the bill.

    Unit

    If applicable, the unit(s) associated with the line items on the bill.

    Updated By

    The user who most recently updated the recurring bill.

  4. Adjust the Post Date to set the day when the bill is posted. By default, this is today's date. If necessary, change the Post Date back to the original date by selecting arrow_forward Reset Post Date.

  5. Adjust the Due Date to set the day when the bill is considered due. By default, this is calculated using the post date and the bill terms.

  6. Click Post Recurring Bills.
    The Post Recurring Bills pop-up displays.

  7. Click Yes.

  8. Click OK on the pop-up.
    New one-time bills are created for the selected recurring bills and can be viewed on the Bills page.
