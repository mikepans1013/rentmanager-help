# CashPay (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/ePay-CashPay.htm

CashPay settings allow you to control whether you accept partial payments or payments in excess of the balance due from tenants using the Zego CashPay service, and whether or not that balance due includes all members of an account group.

 Related Preferences

 This page displays only after CashPay is enabled in system preferences. For more information, refer to Advanced ePay (System Preferences) .

 Warning

 When setting up ePay for the first time, a Zego representative will schedule a call to help you set these system preferences and acquire additional information to enable the feature. It is not recommended that you adjust any of these settings prior to that call.

 Amount Due

 This section determines the balance that is considered due for tenants using CashPay and whether account group balances are combined. Each option is described below.

 Option
 Description

 Send the following balance as the amount due

 Establish how Rent Manager calculates the balance that tenants are required to pay via CashPay .

 Current balance

 Allows tenants to pay only their current balance due and not prepay future charges.

 Total balance

 Allows tenants to pay their full balance due for all charges on their account, including future charges.

 Sum the balance of all tenants in an account group

 If enabled, the balances of each tenant account in an account group are consolidated. For example, if three roommates are set up as individual tenants in an account group and this option is enabled, any payments made via CashPay are applied to their shared balance rather than any individual account. Additionally, if you do not permit partial payments or overpayments, any tenant in that account group would have to make a CashPay payment equivalent to the combined Current balance or Total balance , depending on the option you selected. For more information, refer to Manage Account Groups .

 More Information

 If you selected the Current balance option, this setting combines the balances of each tenant in an account group, excluding future charges.

 Payment Settings

 This section determines whether tenants can make payments less than and/or greater than their total amount due, as determined by the options selected in the Amount Due section. Each option is described below.

 Option
 Description

 Allow partial payments

 Tenants can make payments that are less than their total amount due through CashPay .

 Allow payments over amount due

 Tenants can make payments that are greater than their total amount due through CashPay .
