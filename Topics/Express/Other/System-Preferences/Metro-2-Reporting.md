# Metro 2 Reporting (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/Metro-2-Reporting.htm

The Metro 2 feature provides the ability to export loan data in Metro 2 format, which is suitable for import by the major credit reporting bureaus. The Metro 2 options selected in system preferences identify the credit bureaus and their agency account numbers to be included in the reporting, details about your organization's contact information as known by the bureaus, and the options to display as defaults in Metro 2–related fields in loans.

 Preference Descriptions

 This preference group is divided into multiple sections. Each setting is described in the corresponding section below.

 Credit Bureaus

 In this section, enter the account information you wish to include in Metro 2 reports.

 Options
 Description

 Credit Bureau Account Information (TransUnion, Equifax, Experian, Innovis)

 Check and enter the account number you have with the selected credit bureau to include that credit bureau in Metro 2 reporting. These credit information service providers store and maintain repositories of data about individuals' financial transactions.

 Reporter Details

 In this section, enter your organization’s contact (reporter) information as known by the credit bureaus.

 Option
 Description

 Name

 Enter your organization's name as known by the credit bureaus.

 Phone

 Enter your organization's phone number as known by the credit bureaus.

 Address

 Enter your organization's address as known by the credit bureaus.

 Identifier

 Enter your organization's identifier as known by the credit bureaus.

 The Identifier uniquely designates the data provider for your organization and must be at least five characters in length.

 Default People Fields

 In this section, select the option (if any) displayed as default for the Indicator and ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) fields on the Loan Contacts pop-up. The options available are defined by the Metro 2 standard.

 Option
 Description

 Indicator

 Select the Indicator that describes the account Address .

 ECOA

 Select the ECOA that defines the relationship of the primary borrower to the account and designates the account as joint, individual, and so on, in compliance with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act.

 Default Loan Fields

 Select the option (if any) displayed as default for the Account Type and Export Inclusion fields on the loan's Metro 2 tile.

 Option
 Description

 Account Type

 Select the code that best identifies the account classification.

 Export Inclusion

 This option determines whether to include or exclude this loan's information in the Metro 2 file generated from the Export to Metro 2 function.
