# VPO General (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/VPO-General.htm

Virtual Post Office (VPO) is a Rent Manager feature that allows you to outsource the process of printing and mailing letters, statements, and reports to the appropriate recipients.

 More Information

 You must first set up a VPO account with LCS and provide a credit card to use for VPO billing. Then set these system preferences relating to the functionality of the feature. For more information, refer to Set Up Virtual Post Office (VPO) .

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward Virtual Post Office arrow_forward General .
The System Preferences: Virtual Post Office - General page displays.

 -
 In the Account Settings section, enter the Username and Password of your VPO account as provided by LCS .

 -
 Click Test Settings to verify your connection to the Virtual Post Office .

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described below.

 -
 Click Save .
The system preference configuration is updated.

 Max Fee Settings

 In this section, enter the Max. Per Print Job , which is the maximum fee allowed per VPO print job. Once an amount is established in this section, Rent Manager does not allow any VPO print jobs that exceed this amount.

 Email Settings

 In this section, determine the email account used for VPO alerts and confirmations.

 Option
 Description

 Email Address

 The email address you wish to use for alerts regarding your VPO jobs. You may enter additional email addresses into this field provided that each email address is separated by a semi-colon (;).

 Send email when a job is canceled

 If checked, send a confirmation email when a previously submitted VPO job is successfully canceled.

 Send email when a job is sent for fulfillment

 If checked, send a confirmation email when a VPO job arrives at the post office.

 Send email when a job is submitted

 If checked, send a confirmation email when a VPO job is successfully received by LCS and ready to be sent to the post office.
