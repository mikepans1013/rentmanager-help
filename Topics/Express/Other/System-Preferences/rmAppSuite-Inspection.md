# rmAppSuite Inspection (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/rmAppSuite-Inspection.htm

These system preferences allow you to create default email messages sent from rmAppSuite Pro inspections and enable emailed inspection reports. When these system preferences inspections are enabled, the inspections and inspection templates menu options display in rmAppSuite Pro .

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward rmAppSuite arrow_forward Inspection .
The System Preferences: rmAppSuite - Inspection page displays.

 -
 Check Enabled .

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The system preference configuration is updated.

 General

 These settings allow you to determine the email and video inspection settings for rmAppSuite Pro inspections.

 Option
 Description

 Allow to send to email addresses

 Each entity to which users can send a copy of the inspection via rmAppSuite Pro .

 Custom

 A copy can be sent to an email address manually entered in the inspection's Email Other field.

 Owner

 A copy can be sent to the owner of the property linked to the inspection.

 Tenant

 A copy can be sent to the tenant linked to the inspection.

 Company Code

 The unique database identifier provided by LCS to identify the database for logging into rmAppSuite Pro .

 Default email message for Owners

 The email message sent to owners upon the completion of the inspection on rmAppSuite Pro . An inspection is considered complete when the Status has been marked as Closed .

 Default email message for Tenant

 The email message sent to tenants upon the completion of the inspection on rmAppSuite Pro . An inspection is considered complete when the Status has been marked as Closed .

 Licensed

 Displays Yes if the rmAppSuite Pro feature is licensed for the database, allowing you to enable the feature.

 Video Inspections

 Determines if or how videos can be recorded and/or attached to inspections.

 Disabled

 The option to attach or record videos for inspections is not available in rmAppSuite Pro or Rent Manager .

 Manual

 Users can attach videos to inspections in rmAppSuite Pro or Rent Manager by manually entering the URL of a video they recorded.

 rmAppSuite Pro

 Users can record a video of an inspection directly in rmAppSuite Pro , and the video is automatically saved to the inspection area where the recording was initiated. They can also manually attach video URL to an inspection area in Rent Manager .
