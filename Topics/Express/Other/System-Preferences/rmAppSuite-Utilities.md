# rmAppSuite Utilities (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/rmAppSuite-Utilities.htm

These system preferences allow you to determine whether rmAppSuite Pro users can view previous Metered Utilities reading information when entering new readings for Metered Utilities .

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward rmAppSuite arrow_forward Utilities .

 -
 By default, Show previous meter reading and usage when entering new readings is selected and historical information displays in rmAppSuite Pro when technicians update meter readings. To hide historical reading information, disable this option.

 -
 Click Save to accept your changes.
