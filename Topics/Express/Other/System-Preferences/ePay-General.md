# General ePay (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/ePay-General.htm

ePay is a feature for automated clearing house (ACH) and credit card transactions through third-party processors. Currently, Rent Manager uses Zego , which combines online payment gateways, real-time credit card processing, and merchant account services. These system preferences allow you to integrate your ePay account with Rent Manager and determine how platform fees and phone payments are handled.

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward ePay arrow_forward General .
The System Preferences: ePay - General page displays.

 -
 Check Enable ePay to display the fields required to integrate your ePay account with Rent Manager .
Click to view Zego 's phone number, which you can call for more information about ePay .

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The system preference configuration is updated.

 Merchant Information

 In this section, enter the Merchant ID , Username , and Password provided by Zego . In the Merchant ID field, use one terminal ID for multiple banks as described in your documentation from Zego .

 General

 In this section, determine how you manage platform fee charges and whether users can receive one-time ePay payments over the phone.

 Option
 Description

 Disable “Payment by Phone”

 Check to prevent users from receiving one-time ePay payments over the phone.
When adding a ePay payment to a tenant or prospect account in Rent Manager , the option Payment by Phone is disabled. For more information, refer to Process a Manual ePay Payment .

 Have Zego manage platform fees

 If you do not want to manage your own platform fee charges, check this option to allow Zego to automatically collect its platform fees for online processing directly from different types of ePay transactions.

 If this option is unchecked, you are responsible for collecting platform fees from transactions and must pay those fees when invoiced by Zego . If you uncheck this option, a warning pop-up displays asking if you wish to pay all of the platform fee charges from Zego . Click OK to confirm.
