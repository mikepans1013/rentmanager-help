# General Ledger System Accounts (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/General-Ledger-System-Accounts.htm

These system preferences allow you to set up default general ledger (GL) system accounts for reporting purposes.

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 System
 System Preferences
 View, Edit

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward General Ledger arrow_forward System Accounts .

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described below.

 -
 Click Save to accept your changes.

 Preference Descriptions

 Each setting is described below:

 Option
 Description

 Accounts receivable for accrual method

 The accounts receivable account to record the amount of money owed by tenants. The accounts receivable total is determined by unpaid charges on tenant accounts.

 Undeposited funds account

 The account to record income received but not yet deposited. This field can be linked to a bank account.

 Accounts payable for accrual method

 The accounts payable account to show all expenses that are due to be paid but have not yet been paid or cleared. Accounts payable is automatically calculated by referencing unpaid bills on the Pay Bills page.

 Cash prepay liability account

 The account to be used to track payments that are not allocated to a specific charge type when using cash-basis accounting.

 Related Preferences

 If the system preference option to Record cash preallocations as a liability (applies to new payments and credits) is checked, this account also records prepaid income that is allocated to a specific charge type when using cash-basis accounting. For more information, refer to General Ledger Settings (System Preferences) .

 Accrual prepay liability account

 The account to record prepaid income when using accrual-basis accounting if the system preference option to Record accrual prepayments as a liability (applies to new payments) is checked.

 Net income account

 The account to record the total net income. Net income is calculated by subtracting the total expenses from the total income for the current fiscal year.

 Retained earnings rollover account

 The account to record the previous fiscal year's income on the balance sheet and trial balance reports if the system preference option to Roll “Net Income” from prior fiscal years to “Retained Earnings” on the balance sheet is checked.

 Account for not linked charge types

 The account to record income that is not linked to another account.

 Tenant check pass-through account

 The account to record the refund amount as well as the charge against open credits when checks are written directly to tenants for refunding deposits or overpayments.

 Loans payable refinance clearing account

 The account to record journal entries created by the Refinance Loan wizard.
