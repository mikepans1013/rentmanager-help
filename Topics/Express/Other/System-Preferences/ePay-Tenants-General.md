# Tenant ePay (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/ePay-Tenants-General.htm

Tenant ePay system-level settings control default bank accounts, warning limits, and platform charges that should be applied to tenant and prospect accounts.

 More Information

 These settings can be overridden for tenants and/or prospects of an individual property. For more information, refer to Property ePay Settings (Pop-Up) .

 System Defaults

 In this section, you can select a default bank account for receiving tenant and prospect ePay payments and set a warning limit when receiving a tenant ePay payment equal to or beyond the specified threshold.

 Option
 Description

 Platform Fee Information

 Click this link to view a disclaimer about the application of platform fees. Rent Manager cannot provide specific guidance on the implementation of platform fees, as these requirements vary by contract and local, state and federal laws.

 Default Bank Account

 Select the bank account that receives tenant ePay payments when Use settings from System Preferences is checked on the ePay Settings pop-up on the property's details page. For more information, refer to Property ePay Settings (Pop-Up) .

 ePay Banks

 Assign ePay IDs (provided by Zego ) to any of your bank accounts that are involved in ePay transactions, such as receiving tenant and prospect payments. Only banks assigned an ePay ID can be used for tenant and prospect ePay payments.

 Warning Limit

 Enter a value to display a warning message when receiving a tenant or prospect ePay payment equal to or beyond the specified threshold. For example, to display a warning when a Rent Manager user attempts to process a tenant ePay payment of five hundred dollars or more, enter 500 .

 Resident Payout

 In this section, check Allow security deposit refunds to tenants via electronic payout to integrate Zego Resident Payouts with Rent Manager . This payment option transforms the security deposit refund process from a manual, check-based workflow into a secure, digital experience. You can initiate refunds directly within Rent Manager , and tenants receive an email invitation to choose how they want to receive their funds. For more information, refer to Zego Resident Payout .

 Zego Platform Fees

 Platform fees are collected for transactions that are processed differently than traditional methods, such as cash or checks. Electronic payments in Rent Manager are processed through Zego ePay , and platform fees are collected by Zego . The fields below customize how the platform fees are collected.

 Related Preferences

 If the Have Zego manage platform fees option is enabled in system preferences, the fields below are replaced by a list of platform fees Zego automatically collects directly from the ePay transaction. Click Update ePay Fees to generate the most up-to-date platform fee information directly from Zego . For more information, refer to General ePay (System Preferences) .

 Preference
 Description

 Charge Type

 Select the charge type to assign for platform fee charges (i.e., an additional cost for processing payments via ACH or credit cards).

 Charge platform fees in Rent Manager for

 Check the following options to determine how platform fees are charged on tenant and prospect ePay payments in Rent Manager . Enter the flat fee dollar amount(s) in the field(s) to the right. Optionally, check Percentage and enter a value to charge a percentage of the amount collected instead of a flat fee.

 ACH

 Check to charge platform fees on tenant and prospect ACH payments in Rent Manager .

 Credit Card

 Check to charge platform fees on tenant and prospect credit card payments in Rent Manager .

 Debit Card

 Check to charge platform fees on tenant and prospect debit card payments in Rent Manager .

 Charge platform fees in Tenant Web Access for

 Check the following options to determine how platform fees are charged on tenant and prospect ePay payments in Tenant Web Access (TWA) . Enter the flat fee dollar amount(s) in the field(s) to the right. Optionally, check Percentage and enter a value to charge a percentage of the amount collected instead of a flat fee.

 ACH

 Check to charge platform fees on ACH payments in TWA.

 Credit Card

 Check to charge platform fees on credit card payments in TWA.

 Debit Card

 Check to charge platform fees on debit card payments in TWA.
