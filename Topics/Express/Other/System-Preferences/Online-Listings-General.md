# Online Listings General (System Preferences)

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/System-Preferences/Online-Listings-General.htm

These preferences set up the internet listing service (ILS) marketing integration, which allows you to create online listings for the available properties in your Rent Manager database. Once enabled, you can set up these listings to be pushed to ILS sites in the Zillow Group (Zillow, Trulia, and HotPads) and the Apartments.com network.

 Warning

 Some ILS sites may require a paid account before your listings can be posted.

 More Information

 Before enabling online listings, ensure that you have entered marketing information for the properties and units you intend to market on ILS sites. For more information, refer to Property Marketing Setup (Pop-Up) .

 These system preferences can be overridden for an individual property's marketing setup. For more information, refer to Property Marketing Advanced Settings (Pop-Up) .

 To set these system preferences, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Administration , then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward Online Listings arrow_forward General .
The System Preferences: Online Listings - General page displays.

 -
 Check Enable Online Listings to allow Rent Manager to publish to ILS feeds based on the settings you define.

 More Information

 Whenever Enable Online Listings or a provider is unchecked, the Provider Contact Information pop-up displays with the user's information automatically populated in the fields. Verify or edit the contact information, then click OK to submit the automatic opt-in or opt-out email to the corresponding provider(s).

 -
 Edit the settings as desired. Each setting is described in the headings below.

 -
 Click Save .
The Online Listing Disclaimer pop-up displays, prompting you to authorize the data sharing from your company's Rent Manager database to the selected ILS sites.

 -
 Read the disclaimer, enter your Name , Email , and Phone Number in the fields provided, and check I agree to move forward with enabling Online Listings . Then, click OK to authorize the disclaimer.
The system preference configuration is updated.

 Provider Settings

 In these settings, check the provider(s)to which ILS feeds are published. The available ILS providers are described below.

 More Information

 ILS providers may have unique requirements or conditions for properties and units to be posted. For more information, refer to the help text below the provider name.

 Provider
 Description

 Apartments.com

 List Multi Family property types on ILS sites in the Apartments.com network. Properties with a unit count less than five or a Commercial or a Single Family property type, as listed on the property's details page, will not be listed by this provider.

 Zillow, Trulia, Hotpads

 List Single Family and Multi Family property types to ILS sites, as listed on the property's details page, associated with the Zillow Group network.
