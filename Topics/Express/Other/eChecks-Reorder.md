# Reorder eChecks

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/eChecks-Reorder.htm

Safeguard eChecks is an integration that electronically processes and delivers your check payment to the email associated with the recipient. For each bank set up to use eChecks , you must have enough digital checks to complete your eChecks payments.

 Related Privileges

 Group
 Privilege
 Column

 eChecks
 Reorder eChecks
 Enabled

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 Related Preferences

 You can have your eChecks reorders automated by enabling the Automatically reorder eChecks system preference. For more information, refer to eChecks (System Preferences) .

 To reorder checks, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Checks arrow_forward Reorder eChecks .
The Reorder eChecks page displays.

 -
 Each bank set up with eChecks displays with the following information.

 Column
 Description

 Bank

 The name of the bank for which you wish to reorder eChecks .

 eChecks Remaining

 The number of eChecks the associated Bank has left.

 Reorder Quantity

 The amount of eChecks to purchase from the associated Bank .

 -
 In the Reorder Quantity column for each bank, select the amount of eChecks to purchase for that bank.

 More Information

 eChecks can be reordered only if the Reorder Quantity is greater than the eChecks Remaining . For example, if a bank has 201 remaining eChecks , 500 eChecks can be ordered, but an order for 200 can’t be processed.

 -
 Click Reorder .
The eChecks Reorder Request pop-up displays.

 -
 Click  Yes to confirm your order.
The order is submitted to Safeguard .
