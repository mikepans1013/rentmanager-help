# Create a Phone Broadcast

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Other/Broadcast-Phone-Create.htm

Send a mass audio message to multiple owners, tenants, prospects, or vendors at once with Phone Broadcasting . Compose outgoing mass phone messages and build one or more Numbers Lists of phone numbers to receive an outgoing message. The maximum number of messages sent per day and per month is determined by your service agreement.

 More Information

 This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 Step 1: Create an Outgoing Message

 Related Preferences

 To perform a phone broadcast, ensure you enter the setup information in system preferences. This information includes a username and password that are initially provided to you by LCS . For more information, refer to Phone Broadcast (System Preferences) .

 To start a new phone broadcast, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward Phone Broadcast .

 -
 Compose your phone message using the following fields in the Outgoing Message tile.

 Field
 Description

 Message

 The name of a previously saved message template. By default, <New Message> is selected.

 To create a new phone broadcast template, click .

 Name

 The name of the phone broadcast. If you save this message, this name is added to the Message drop-down list so you can select it again.

 Description

 A comment or note about when to use this broadcast message.

 Number to Dial

 The phone number (including area code) in which the system is to call so you can record the message. Once you click Record , the phone number entered receives an automated phone call allowing you to record the message you wish to broadcast.

 When you end the call, the Phone Broadcast page automatically updates to display a playback option for the recorded message. If you want to record it again, click Re-record .

 More Information

 When you playback your recording, there is five seconds of silence before your actual recording begins. This is intentional, as it is meant to wait for the recipient's voice mail recording to finish before playing the broadcast.

 Step 2: Add Numbers to the Broadcast

 The Broadcast to Numbers section is where you compose the Numbers List of phone numbers to receive the broadcast. There are a variety of ways to create a list of broadcast recipients and to customize the broadcast to fit the needs of your business and recipients. Filter options change depending on the entity you have selected to include in your phone broadcast.

 More Information

 Additionally, you can manually add phone numbers when you click   Add Item . Then, click Link Contacts and if Rent Manager recognizes the phone number as being defined within the system, the Link type is automatically filled in.

 Filter by Tenants

 To add numbers when filtering by tenants, do the following:

 -
 Click Add Recipients .

 -
 In the Filter Type field, select Tenant .

 -
 To further filter the entity list, use the Saved Filter drop-down to select a saved or system filter. Alternatively, select One Time Filter to create a filter specific to this broadcast.

 -
 Check any applicable of the following options in the Status section.

 Field
 Description

 Past

 Filters list by tenants that have moved out.

 Current

 Filters list by tenants that have an active lease.

 Future

 Filters by tenants with upcoming move-in dates.

 Exclude Subaccounts

 Filters list by tenants to include only tenants that are the master account on the account group.

 -
 To add only primary tenant accounts, select Primary Contacts . Alternatively, to select all contacts associated with a specific contact type, select Contacts By Type and choose types from the drop-down.

 -
 Click Add Results to add the filter's results to your broadcast list.
The pop-up closes and the applicable accounts are added to the Numbers List .

 Step 3: Send the Broadcast

 Once your number list is complete, do the following:

 -
 In the Saved List section, to save the current Numbers List for future use, click .

 -
 To schedule the broadcast for a later time, enter the date and time in the Schedule Time section. By default, Now is checked and the broadcast is released once you click Broadcast on the bottom of the page.

 -
 Confirm on the Phone Broadcast pop-up that you wish to send the broadcast by clicking Yes .

 -
 The broadcast is sent immediately or scheduled to send at a specific time.

 Step 4: Review the Broadcast

 Once you proceed through both confirmations, the Phone Broadcast Batches pop-up opens. The Scheduled Broadcast Batches section lists the broadcast, and the Batch Call Status section displays all the recipients of the selected batch.

 Scheduled Broadcast Batches

 This section displays a listing of sent broadcasts. The default view for this pop-up is to display Current broadcasts, which includes any scheduled broadcast that are not sent as of the time the page generated. You can select Past to show broadcasts that were already sent. Select All to view both current and past broadcasts.

 The following columns display in the section:

 Column
 Descriptions

 Create Time

 The date and time the broadcast was created.

 Run Time

 The date and time the current broadcast was sent. For scheduled broadcasts, it displays the date and time the broadcast is scheduled to run.

 Sound File

 The name of the outgoing audio file.

 Submitted

 A Check displays when the broadcast is sent successfully.

 Batch Call Status

 In this section, review the following information to ensure the messages in the broadcast are being received. As the broadcast progresses, a Check displays in the Queued , Sent , and/or Received columns to inform you of the message's status.

 More Information

 Rent Manager adds a history/note to the appropriate account when the broadcast is initiated.
