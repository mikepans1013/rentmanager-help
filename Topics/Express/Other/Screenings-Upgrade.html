Upgrade a Screening

After submitting an initial AmRent screening for a prospect or tenant, you can upgrade the screening once (for additional AmRent fees) by ordering a manual verification of up to 15 pieces of information. For example, you may want to request a manual criminal search or a verification of employment.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Tenants/Prospects Access Screenings View, Edit
Access other users' screenings Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To upgrade a screening, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Leasing arrow_forward Screenings and select the screening you want to upgrade.
    The Screening Detail page displays.

  2. At the bottom of the page, click Upgrade.

  3. In the Contact Information section, enter information in the following fields:

    Field Description

    Email

    The email address that is notified when the upgraded screening can be viewed in Rent Manager.

    Name

    The full name of the person requesting the upgraded screening, or the name of the person who should be notified when the screening is complete.

    Phone

    The phone number of the person requesting the upgraded screening, or of the person who should be notified when the screening is complete.

  4. Click Add Item, then in the Request column, enter the additional information you want from AmRent, such as a request for criminal history.

  5. Click Submit.
    The screening upgrade request is submitted to AmRent.

  6. Once AmRent confirms that the upgraded screening is ready, resubmit the screening to receive the updated details. For more information, refer to Resubmit a Screening.
