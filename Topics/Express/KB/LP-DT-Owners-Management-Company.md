# Owners and Management Company Dashboard Tiles

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-DT-Owners-Management-Company.htm

Rent Manager includes several dashboard tiles that provide at-a-glance data and analysis to help you track information about expiring owner contracts and management company revenue status. With these dashboard tiles, you can view a list of expiring owner contracts that need attention from the owner.

 Add the following owner- and management company-related tiles to your dashboard to ensure you never miss any updates regarding owner contracts and unit revenue information at your properties.

 Dashboard Tile
 Description

 Expiring Owner Contracts

 A list of owners with contracts that have a contract end date within a specified number of days.

 Mgmt Company Profit Per Unit

 A graph or list of the management company's average earnings for each unit over time. The average is calculated by dividing your net income by the total number of available and occupied unit.

 Mgmt Company Revenue Per Unit

 A graph or list of the management company's average units over time. The average is calculated by dividing your management company's gross income by the total number of active units at your properties.

 Owner Prospects

 A list of owner prospects created in Rent Manager in addition to the last time they were contacted.
