Payables Dashboard Tiles

Rent Manager includes several dashboard tiles that provide at-a-glance data and analysis to help you track bills, credit card transactions, vendors, and outstanding security deposits. With these dashboard tiles, you can view the total balance of open (unpaid or partially paid) bills, the amount you owe vendors, and the billable expenses for which you have not yet created invoices.

Add the following payables-related tiles to your dashboard to help track unpaid bills:

Dashboard Tile Description

Open Billable Expenses

The date the bill or check was issued, expense type, the tenant or owner to whom the expense was billed, and the dollar amount of the expense that is owed display. This tile helps you track billable expenses for which you have not yet created invoices.

Open Payables

The name of the vendor, the date that the bill is due to be paid, and the dollar amount owed for the open bill display. This tile helps you track the amount you owe a vendor as well as the due date for open (unpaid) bills.

Open Payables Trend

The total balance of open (unpaid or partially paid) bills over time displays. This tile helps you understand the total amount owed from all open bills during the period.

Pending Smart Bills

The invoiced vendor name, the date and time the bill was added to the Smart Bills page, and the dollar amount owed display. This tile allows you to view a list of Smart Bills ready for review on the Smart Bills page.

Pending Smart Receipts

The amount of the transaction, the date and time the receipt was added to the Smart Receipts page, and the vendor account associated with the Smart Receipt transaction display. This tile allows you to view a list of Smart Receipts ready for review on the Smart Receipts page.

Security Deposit Refunds Due

The name of the tenant who moved out and has not yet received their security deposit refund, property, unit, move out date, and the amount of the security deposit being held display. This tile helps you track outstanding security deposits.

Vendor Expiration Listing

The name of the vendor, type of expired or expiring document, and the insurance or workers' compensation expiration date display. This tile helps you track vendors who have expired or imminently expiring insurance or workers' compensation entered on the vendor's details page.
