# Delete a Prospect

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Prospect-Delete.htm

Rent Manager gives you three options to handle prospects you no longer manage in your database. The first option, and best practice, is to make the prospect inactive. A prospect is considered inactive with a status of Lost or Lost/Rejected . This can be managed on the prospect's details page. Information about inactive prospects remains in Rent Manager and may be included in reports wherever the Prospects to Include option is available and Lost and/or Lost/Rejected is selected. For example, you can use the inactive option for prospects that choose not to rent with you in order to preserve valuable historical and financial data. Additionally, you can also track which prospects ultimately choose not to rent with you and use reports like Lost Prospect Breakdown and Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

 Should you find that deleting a prospect is the best option for your business, there are two deletion methods available in Rent Manager : complete or merge.

 If you created a prospect account by mistake and do not need to maintain any historical data or financial records for the prospect, the complete method is the best option. Alternatively, if you have duplicate prospect accounts for the same individual, those accounts can be merged together. Rent Manager can combine accounts using the Merge Prospects pop-up. The Merge Prospects pop-ups' main purpose is to merge a prospect account into a tenant account or another prospect account.

 Option 1: Make a Prospect Inactive

 Making a prospect inactive ensures that all the information regarding the prospect remains in Rent Manager for you to access if you need it again. Inactive prospects can also be included in reports wherever the Prospects to Include option is available and Lost and/or Lost/Rejected is selected. Additionally, you can also track which prospects ultimately choose not to rent with you and use reports like Lost Prospect Breakdown and Box Score to track the effectiveness of your leasing agents and marketing campaigns.

 Option 2: Merge Prospect Accounts

 Rent Manager provides the ability to copy one prospect and all contacts on that account into another current prospect or current tenant account. The source prospect and each associated contact are transferred as new contacts on the Contacts tile of the destination account, and after the merge is completed, history/note items are generated on both accounts to document the merge. Additionally, Tenant Web Access (TWA) users who do not exist on the destination account have their web user credentials transferred.

 This tool is helpful if, for example, you have contacted a prospect and taken detailed notes and then that same person submits an online application and becomes a second prospect in your Rent Manager database. If a prospect account was created in-office or via Apply Now that has a screening or charges/payments (e.g., an application fee) tied to it, but a separate tenant account was created when they signed their lease, you can use this tool as a way to merge these accounts.

 Option 3: Delete a Prospect

 If you created a prospect by accident and do not wish to maintain any historical data or financial records for the prospect, the complete method is the best option. Deleting a prospect from Rent Manager is final and cannot be undone. Prospects can be deleted only if they do not have financial data, issues, screenings, or utilities associated with their account.

 To delete a prospect, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects .

 -

 -
 Click Yes .

 To bulk delete multiple prospects, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects .
The Prospects page displays.

 -
 At the top left, click Bulk Actions arrow_forward Manage Prospects arrow_forward Delete .

 -
 In the far left column, check each prospect you wish to delete.

 -
 Once you are satisfied with your selections, click Delete Prospects .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The prospects are deleted from your Rent Manager database.
