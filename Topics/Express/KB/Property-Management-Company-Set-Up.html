Set Up Property Management Company

Like any other property in your portfolio, your management company generates income and accrues expenses; consequently, you should establish it in Rent Manager. In fact, to collect management fees and track related financial data, you must enter your management company as both a property and a vendor. After establishing your company's associated property and vendor, you must configure your system preferences to complete the setup process. With these steps complete, your Rent Manager database is ready to begin recording your management company's financial activity.

Step 1: Add Management Company as a Property

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Properties/Units Properties Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

In Rent Manager, all accounting is property-based. Therefore, to ensure effective tracking of financial data for your business, you must create your management company as property.

To add your management company as a property, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties.
    The Properties page displays.

  2. Click Add Property.
    The Add Property wizard displays.

  3. In the Add Property wizard, complete the appropriate fields for your management company:

    More Information

    Because this property represents your company, some of the fields that are relevant for real properties, such as Property Amenities or Rent Charge Type, do not apply. For more information on fields not covered in this topic, refer to Add a Property.

    Field Description

    Addresses

    The mailing address for your management company. Optionally, you can add a billing address which is used for owner statements.

    Default bank

    Your management company's operating bank account. Select the bank account that is typically associated with paying expenses and receiving payments for your management company.

    Property Name

    The full name of your management company.

    Property Tax ID

    Your management company's tax ID number.

    Short Name

    An abbreviated name for your management company (ten characters or less) which displays on reports and drop-down lists in various areas of Rent Manager, such as bills and checks.

    Users with Access to Property

    The users that have access to your management company's property. Their level of access is determined by their user privileges. For more information, refer to Limit Access to a Property.

  4. After completing the appropriate fields, click Save and Finish.
    The property for your management company is created and can be found on the Properties page.

Step 2: Add Management Company as a Vendor

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Payables Vendors Add, View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To ensure your business can collect management fees and receive payment for other services, you must add a vendor to represent your management company.

To add your management company as a vendor, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Vendors.
    The Vendors page displays.

  2. Click Add Vendor.
    The Add Vendor wizard displays

  3. In the Add Vendor wizard, complete the appropriate fields for your management company:

    More Information

    Because this account is for your company and not a typical vendor or contractor, some of the fields in the wizard do not apply. For more information on fields not covered in this topic, refer to Add a Vendor.

    Field Description

    Addresses

    The address(es) associated with your management company. The address marked as Default will populate on checks written to your company.

    Associated property

    The properties at which the vendor is available to render services. Since your management company is responsible for managing all properties in your portfolio, select <All Properties>.

    Check Payee

    The name to use when writing checks to your management company.

    Vendor Name

    The name of your management company.
  4. After completing the appropriate fields, click Save and Finish.
    The vendor for your management company is created and can be found on the Vendors page.

Step 3: Add Management Company in System Preferences

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
System System Preferences View, Edit
Owners Owners View

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Once you have established a property and vendor, identify them as your management company in system preferences. This ensures that the financial tracking of fees and expenses associated with your management company, including the collection of management fees, is set up accurately.

To add your management company in system preferences, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration arrow_forward Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward Management Company.

  2. In the Management Company section, in the Property and Associated Vendor fields, enter and select the name of your management company's property and vendor, respectively.

    More Information

    If your company is a fee-based management company, the Income Chart Account and Income Charge Type fields are important for tracking finances associated with management fees. For more information, refer to Management Company (System Preferences).

  3. After completing both fields, click Save.
    Your management company is now established in Rent Manager.
