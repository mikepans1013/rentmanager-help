# AvidXchange

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-AvidXChange.htm

AvidXchange is a provider of accounts payable (AP) and payment automation solutions. There are two services available for importing and paying bills using AvidXchange 's integration with Rent Manager : AvidInvoice and AvidPay .

 AvidInvoice allows you to take bills submitted by vendors to AvidXchange and import them to Rent Manager in batches, which automatically creates one-time bills in Rent Manager to ensure accurate financial records. Those bill payments can be made directly to vendors using AvidPay , either automatically or manually depending on your approval procedure for real-world payments.

 Once a check payment is submitted through AvidPay , it is assigned a status by AvidXchange directly on the check's details page so you can track it as processing is completed and verify that the payment reached the vendor. To view check details, go to either the Checks page or the vendor's View Transactions pop-up.

 More Information

 AvidXchange is a licensed feature. To use AvidPay and AvidInvoice in Rent Manager , you must set up an account with AvidXchange and enter that information into Rent Manager . For more information, contact sales@rentmanager.com .
