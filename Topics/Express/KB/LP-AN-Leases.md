# Automated Notifications for Leases

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-AN-Leases.htm

Automated notifications allow you to send alerts to a customizable list of recipients when a certain set of conditions are met to help streamline your workflows and processes. These notifications can be customized using plain text and scripting for personalized text and/or email messages.

 There are many options for automated notifications regarding leases to inform you of notices, move ins and move outs, and expirations for leases. These notifications are described in the table below.

 Attention

 Ensure the table is the full and current list of automated notifications related to leases.

 Notification
 Description

 Lease Expiring, No Renewal

 Informs tenants when their Lease End date is approaching and they have not yet signed a renewal.

 Move In Completed

 Informs users—such as property managers or leasing agents—when a prospect account is converted to a tenant account through the Move In wizard.

 Move In Scheduled

 Informs users, tenants, owners, or other external recipients as a future tenant's move in date approaches.

 Move Out Completed

 Informs property managers, leasing agents, other Rent Manager users, or external recipients when a tenant is moved out of their unit through the Move Out wizard.

 Move Out Scheduled

 Informs users, tenants, owners, or other external recipients as a tenant's move-out date approaches.

 New move-in to a unit with a broken lease

 Informs users when a tenant is being moved into a unit that was previously occupied by a tenant who broke their lease.

 Notice Date Set

 Informs users or external recipients once a tenant's Notice date has been set, which indicates when the tenant informed the property management company that they intend to move out.

 Renewal Offer Aging

 Informs users (e.g., property managers), tenants, or external recipients that a lease renewal offer has been opened for a specific number of days.

 Renewal Offer Status Updated

 Informs users, tenants, or owners when the status of a lease renewal offer changes. This occurs when a user manually updates the status on the Renewal Offers page or when the offer receives a response from the tenant in the Tenant Web Access (TWA) portal.
