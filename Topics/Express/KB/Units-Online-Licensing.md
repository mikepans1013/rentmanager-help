# Rent Manager Online Licensing

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Units-Online-Licensing.htm

Use of Rent Manager Online requires a subscription. The per-unit licensing model requires you to purchase a license for each unit you actively manage in your Rent Manager database with a minimum of 200 licenses. Units that are not actively being managed but you wish to keep the historical data can be unlicensed, and that license made available for another unit. Additional licenses are sold in 50-unit blocks. Once unit licenses are purchased, they may not be canceled for one year.

 More Information

 Previously, Rent Manager Online was available with a per-user subscription, where licenses were purchased for the number of active Rent Manager users logged into Rent Manager concurrently. You may have this legacy subscription; and in that case, the terms of unit licensing do not apply.

 How It Works

 Per unit licensing gives your employees the freedom to use the version of Rent Manager that best suits their preferences and roles. For customers with a unit licensing subscription, any number of user accounts can be created, and any number of users can log in to Rent Manager concurrently.

 How are unit licenses purchased?

 Additional unit licenses can be purchased through LCS Direct or by contacting a sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 How do you license units?

 Licenses are assigned to units automatically when you use the unit add wizard in Rent Manager , or can be assigned to units that already exist in your database on the Assign Unit Licenses page.

 How do you remove licenses from units?

 Licenses can be unassigned from units in Rent Manager through the Assign Unit Licenses page.

 What if I use locations?

 Each unit in each location uses one license.

 How do you manage licenses for individual locations?

 Licenses for individual locations can be managed by enabling the system preference to Manage unit licenses by location . Additionally, the Manage Location Unit Licensing Limits page allows you to set a maximum number of licenses for each location in Rent Manager . This is useful for companies that manage franchises and maintain those entities in separate locations.

 What does not require a license?

 -
 Assets do not require an assigned unit license.

 -
 Units with the unit type Other Rentable Item do not require a unit license.
