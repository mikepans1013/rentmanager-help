Homeowner Statuses Available on Move In

Depending on the active current homeowner status for a home-type asset, the ability to set a new status in the Homeowner Status field may or may not be available when adding a new tenant, moving a tenant in, creating a new lease for a unit with a home-type asset, or performing tenant transfers using the Transfer a Tenant wizard.

The following table lists whether the Homeowner Status field can be set manually during those processes, based on the status the home-type asset has at the time the process is performed:

Current Status Homeowner Status Field Default and Available Options

Abandoned (Community Owned, Dealer Owned, etc.)

Enabled

Set to the active version of the status by default. For example, if the home-type asset has an Abandoned - Resident Owned status, the status updates to Resident Owned automatically. The following options are available from the drop-down list:

  • Active, non-abandoned version of the status

  • Change of Ownership

Change of Ownership

Enabled

Set to Change of Ownership by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Change of Ownership

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

If there is not a current or future lease on the home-type asset, the following additional options are available:

  • Change of Ownership

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Dealer Owned

  • Employee Owned

  • Lender Owned

  • Resident Owned

Community Owned

Enabled

Set to Community Owned by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Change of Ownership

Community Owned - Employee

Enabled

Set to Community Owned - Employee by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Change of Ownership

Community Owned - Lease to Own

Enabled

Set to Community Owned - Lease to Own by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Change of Ownership

Dealer Owned

Enabled

Set to Dealer Owned by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Dealer Owned

  • Change of Ownership

Employee Owned

Enabled

Set to Employee Owned by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Employee Owned

  • Change of Ownership

Lender Owned

Enabled

Set to Lender Owned by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Lender Owned

  • Change of Ownership

No homeowner status set

Enabled

Set to blank by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Blank (no status)

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Change of Ownership

If there is not a current or future lease on the home-type asset, the following additional options are available:

  • Blank (no status)

  • Community Owned

  • Community Owned - Employee

  • Community Owned - Lease to Own

  • Dealer Owned

  • Employee Owned

  • Lender Owned

  • Resident Owned

  • Change of Ownership

Resident Owned

Enabled

Set to Community Owned by default, with the following options available from the drop-down list:

  • Resident Owned

  • Change of Ownership
