# Delete an Owner

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Owners-Delete.htm

Rent Manager gives you two options to handle owners you no longer manage in your database. The first option, and best practice, is to end the owner's contract dates, making them inactive. This allows you to keep all the owner's historical data for record keeping purposes. The second option is to delete the owner from Rent Manager . This is permanent and cannot be undone so it's only advised in situations where an owner was added in error. Additionally, if an owner has transactions tied to their account, they cannot be deleted.

 Owners are considered active when they have an active ownership contract for a property. If an owner has no contracts, or all of their contracts have ended on or before the current date, they are considered inactive. To see which properties an owner has a contract for, go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owners and select an owner account. On the owner's details page, on the action bar on the right, select arrow_forward Ownership . The Ownership pop-up displays the properties that the owner has had an ownership contract for and the contract's details.

 To set an owner as inactive, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Properties and select the property you want to update ownership contracts for.
The property's details page displays.

 -
 Select arrow_forward Owners .
The Owners pop-up displays.

 -
 On the row for the owner you are updating, enter the contract's End date.

 -
 Click Save .
The contract dates are set.

 Repeat this for any other properties where the owner has ownership. Once the current date is on or past the date set in the End date field for each of their properties, the owner is then considered inactive.

 It is suggested to set owners with historical data as inactive rather than deleting them. However, if you have an owner account that was added in error and it doesn't have any transactions associated with the account, you can delete the owner account.

 To delete an owner, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward General arrow_forward Owners and select the owner account you want to delete.
 The owner's details page displays.

 -
