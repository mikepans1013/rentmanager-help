# Delete a Vendor

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Vendor-Delete.htm

Rent Manager gives you two options to handle vendors you no longer use for certain products or services. The first option, and best practice, is to make the vendor inactive. This is done on the vendor details page's General tile by unchecking the Active box. Information about inactive vendors remains in Rent Manager and may be included in reports wherever the Include Inactive Vendors option is available and selected. For example, you can use the inactive option for a vendor that provided poor service in order to preserve valuable historical and financial data.

 Should you find that deleting a vendor is the best option for your business, there are two deletion methods available in Rent Manager : merge or delete.

 If you have created a vendor by mistake and do not need to maintain any historical data or financial records for the vendor, the delete method is the best option. Alternatively, choosing the merge method allows you to combine financial and historical data from the vendor with another existing vendor in your Rent Manager database. This option ensures that no important information is permanently deleted from Rent Manager and is the preferred method if you would like to maintain records for the source vendor. You may wish to use the merge option for a duplicated vendor.

 Make a Vendor Inactive

 Making a vendor inactive ensures that all the information regarding the vendor remains in Rent Manager for you to access if you need it again. Inactive vendors can also be included in reports wherever the Include Inactive Vendors option is available and selected.

 To make a vendor inactive, do the following:

 -
 Go to   arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Vendors and select the vendor you wish to deactivate.
The Vendor details page displays.

 -
 On the General tile, uncheck the Active box.

 -
 Click Save .
The vendor is now inactive and cannot be selected throughout Rent Manager for items like service issues, bills, checks, and so on, but Rent Manager maintains any historical data related to the vendor.

 Merge Vendors

 Rent Manager provides the ability to merge one vendor's transaction history and contacts with another current vendor. This action copies the applicable information and contacts from the source vendor to the destination vendor's record and then removes the source vendor from the Vendors page in Rent Manager . For example, if you accidentally have one vendor listed under multiple vendor names and you want a continuous and consolidated history of the vendor's transaction history, merging vendors can be a useful action to save the time it would take to correct the mistake.

 To merge vendors, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Merge Vendors .
The Merge Vendors pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Source Vendor field, select the vendor whose transaction history and contacts you want to merge to a destination vendor.

 -
 In the Destination Vendor field, select the vendor who inherits the transaction history and contacts of the source vendor.

 -
 When you are satisfied with your selections, click Merge .

 -
 Select Yes on the Merge Vendors confirmation pop-up.
The vendors are merged successfully and can be viewed on the Vendors page.

 Delete a Vendor

 If you created a vendor by accident and do not wish to maintain any historical data or financial records for the vendor, the complete method is the best option. Deleting a vendor from Rent Manager is final and cannot be undone. Additionally, you cannot delete a vendor with transactions on the account.

 Warning

 Choosing this option means that no data about this vendor (transaction history and contacts) can be accessed again.
