Zego Utility Expense Management

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Rent Manager's integrated partner Zego offers the Utility Expense Management feature, which allows Zego to collect property-wide bills directly from your utility providers and pay them on your behalf. This offloads the process of utility billing, invoicing, and vendor payments for your properties to Zego, reducing errors that can arise from manual entry. With Utility Expense Management, Zego creates one check in Rent Manager for each matching check number, bank account, and vendor, ensuring that your financial records include checks that more accurately reflect the payments issued to your utility vendors. This allows multiple bills paid by the same check to be correctly associated, simplifying bank reconciliation while letting Zego and Rent Manager handle the intricate details of utility expense management.

Additionally, Utility Expense Management allows Zego to identify utility usage discrepancies and monitor utility theft, while also providing an online portal where you can generate reports and set up notifications to review your utility billing process.

More Information

Zego's Utility Expense Management feature is designed to manage property-level utility expenses on a wide scale. Zego also offers the Resident Utility Billing feature, which focuses on tenant billing with customized statements. For more information on that feature, refer to Zego Resident Utility Billing.
