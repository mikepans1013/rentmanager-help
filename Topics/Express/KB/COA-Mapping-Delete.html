Delete Chart Account Mappings

Through chart accounts mapping, users can customize the appearance and naming structure of individual general ledger (GL) accounts to meet the needs of various report recipients. When these specific needs no longer need to be met or a mapping was added in error, the mappings can be deleted. This can reduce clutter and remove mappings that should not be selected. Alternatively, you can delete a single mapped account within the mapping to make minor changes without removing the entire mapping.

Delete a Chart Accounts Mapped Account

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting Chart mappings View, Edit, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To delete an individual mapped account from a chart of accounts mapping, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General arrow_forward Chart Accounts Mapping.
    The Chart Accounts Mapping page displays.

  2. Select a mapping from the list at the left.

  3. On the Mapped Account View tab, click arrow_forward Delete for each Mapped Account you wish to delete.
    The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

  4. Click Yes to confirm the action.
    The mapped account is removed from both tabs of the chart account mapping.

More Information

Mapped accounts that are the parent account of any mapped accounts cannot be deleted until all subaccounts are either deleted or moved to another parent account.

Delete a Chart of Accounts Mapping

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting Chart mappings View, Delete

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To delete a chart accounts mapping and all of its mapped accounts, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward General Chart arrow_forward Accounts Mapping.
    The Chart Accounts Mapping page displays.

  2. Select a mapping from the list at the left.

  3. Click Delete.
    The Delete pop-up displays.

  4. Click Yes to confirm the action.
    The mapping setup is permanently deleted from Rent Manager.
