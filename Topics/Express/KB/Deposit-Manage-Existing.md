# Manage Existing Deposits

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Deposit-Manage-Existing.htm

After making a bank deposit in real life, you should make a matching bank deposit in Rent Manager to accurately record and maintain your financial data. If you need to edit already posted deposits, such as to fix a payment or update information for deposited funds, you can do so from the deposit's details page. From here, you can add or remove a payment from a deposit, or update other income included in the Other Items tab of the deposit, such as coin laundry and vending machine income.

 The Bank field populates with only banks to which you have access. Your access to banks can be managed on the user's details page. For more information, refer to Control User Access and User Details (Page) .

 To manage an existing deposit, go to arrow_forward Accounting arrow_forward Banking arrow_forward Bank Register . In the Bank field, select the bank account where the payments were deposited, then click on the deposit you wish to update.

 More Information

 To locate a deposit that contains a specific payment, you can click the link in the Deposit # field on the payment's details.

 Add a Payment to the Deposit

 To add a payment to an existing deposit, do the following:

 -
 In the Payments section, select Add Payments .

 -
 Check the box next to each payment to add to the deposit.

 More Information

 Only undeposited payments display in the list. To move a deposited payment from one deposit to another, you must first remove that payment from the deposit where it is currently located. You can then add that payment to another deposit.

 -
 Click Add .
The deposit details page displays with the new payments added in the Payments tile.

 -
 Click Update .
The payment(s) is added to the deposit. The Deposit Detail report displays, showing a summary of your deposit. For more information, refer to Deposit Detail (Report) .

 The Other Income tile allows you to track any other additional income you need to deposit that is not accounted for in a payment, such as concessions, vending machines, and coin laundry.

 To add other income to an existing deposit, do the following:

 -
 In the Payments section, select Add Other Income .

 -
 For the new line item, enter the applicable information for each column below.

 The dollar amount of the income for this line item.

 Remove Transactions from the Deposit

 You can also remove any payments or income from the deposit. Payments removed from the deposit become undeposited payments and can be added to another deposit—new or existing—when needed. Any line items for additional income entered on the Other Items tab is deleted from Rent Manager .

 Upon saving the deposit, the Deposit Detail report displays, showing a summary of your deposit.

 Remove Payment

 To remove a payment from the deposit, in the Payments section next to the payment you want to remove, click arrow_forward Remove . Then, click Update to save the change.
