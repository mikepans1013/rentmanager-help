# Allocate Bill Payments to Owner Invoices

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Owners-Billable-Expenses-Allocate.htm

When paying bills from your property management company's vendor account in Rent Manager , you can bill your owners for any charges that they are responsible for paying. If the owner has multiple open receivables, you can choose to have Rent Manager automatically allocate the bill payment or manually allocate the payment to open owner invoices.

 More Information

 To ensure that owner open receivables are available to pay bills, the system preferences Allow manual allocation of payment at the time bills are paid and Add a payment from the owner when a bill is paid to the management company must be enabled. For more information, refer to Management Company (System Preferences) . Additionally, a bill where the owner is selected in the Billable field must be created. For more information, refer to Bill Back an Owner for Property Expenses .

 Automatically Allocate Bill Payments to Open Owner Invoices

 You can opt to have Rent Manager automatically select which open owner receivables are paid by this bill. This is most useful for scenarios where there are multiple open invoices and you don't want to sort through them to find the oldest charge for the allocation to be applied to.

 To have Rent Manager automatically allocate bill payments to owner invoices, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills .
The Pay Bills page displays.

 -
 In the Pay column, check the bill(s) you want to pay for the property management company's Vendor account.

 -
 Click Pay Bills .
The Payment Info pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter information for the following options.

 Manually Allocate Bill Payments to Open Owner Invoices

 You have the option to manually select which of your owner's open invoices you apply a bill payment to. This is useful for scenarios where you want to apply the allocation to a specific invoice instead of the oldest charge.

 To manually allocate bill payments to owner invoices, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills
The Pay Bills page displays.

 -
 In the Pay column, check the bill(s) you want to pay for the property management company's Vendor account.

 Click Pay Bills .
