Site Classification

For manufactured housing-type properties, the site classification tool allows you to monitor units (or sites) by considering several data points and assigning a status based on those data points. The site classifications that display in the related reports are determined by the site's legal owner, whether the site can be rented, the occupancy status, and the type of asset(s) and/or resident-owned RV present at that site.

To ensure that these classifications are correct, manufactured homes must be created and maintained as home-type assets that is added to a unit. If residents reside in their own RVs on one of your sites, instead of creating an asset, the RV must be added directly to the tenant's lease.

Warning

Before Rent Manager can assign classifications sites, you need to apply homeowner statuses for any home-type assets or resident-owned RVs associated with those sites. For more information, refer to Homeowner Statuses (Pop-Up).

More Information

A site classification Name and Short Name can be customized. For more information, refer to Homeowner Statuses & Site Classifications.

Community-Owned Site Classifications

The following classification statuses are assigned to sites owned by the manufactured housing community based on the described criteria:

Classification Description

Abandoned - Community Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Abandoned - Community Owned, regardless of whether the site is occupied.

Community Owned - Employee

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Community Owned- Employee and is associated with an active tenant lease.

Community Owned - Occupied

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Community Owned and is associated with an active tenant lease.

Community Owned - Vacant

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Community Owned and is not associated with an active tenant lease.

Lease to Own

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Community Owned - Lease to Own and is associated with an active tenant lease.

Other Owned Site Classifications

The following classification statuses are assigned to sites that are not community-owned based on the described criteria:

Classification Description

Abandoned - Dealer Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Abandoned - Dealer Owned, regardless of whether the site is occupied.

Abandoned - Employee Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Abandoned - Employee Owned, regardless of whether the site is occupied.

Abandoned - Lender Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Abandoned - Lender Owned, regardless of whether the site is occupied.

Abandoned - Resident Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Abandoned - Resident Owned, regardless of whether the site is occupied.

Dealer Owned - Non Revenue Producing

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Dealer Owned and is either vacant or has an occupant with an active rent free period on their lease.

Dealer Owned - Revenue Producing

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Dealer Owned and is occupied without an active rent free period on their lease.

Employee Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets or resident-owned RVs whose current homeowner status is set to Employee Owned and is associated with an active tenant lease.

Lender Owned - Non Revenue Producing

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Lender Owned and is either vacant or has an occupant with an active rent free period on their lease.

Lender Owned - Revenue Producing

Assigned to sites with home-type assets whose current homeowner status is set to Lender Owned and is occupied without an active rent free period on their lease.

Resident Owned

Assigned to sites with home-type assets or resident-owned RVs whose current homeowner status is set to Resident Owned and is associated with an active tenant lease.

Transitional and Unavailable Classifications

The following classification statuses are assigned to sites that are not available for rent or are in the process of changing ownership:

Classification Description

Change of Ownership

Assigned to all sites with a home-type asset or resident-owned RV whose current homeowner status is set to Change of Ownership.

Inactive

Assigned to vacant sites with an active unit status that does not have Show As Vacant and Show As Available selected.

Occupied

Assigned to sites that have an active tenant lease, but have neither a homeowner status nor a home-type asset or resident-owned RV associated with them.

Undeveloped/Unrentable

Assigned to vacant sites with an active Unrentable/Undeveloped unit status.

Vacant Lot

Assigned to sites that do not have an active lease, a homeowner status, and a home-type asset or resident-owned RV associated with them.

<Unassigned Homeowner Status>

Assigned to sites that have a home-type asset or resident-owned RV associated with them but do not have a homeowner status.
