# Delinquency Dashboard Tiles

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-DT-Delinquency.htm

Rent Manager includes several dashboard tiles that provide at-a-glance data and analysis to help you track and minimize delinquent payments. With these dashboard tiles, you can view trends such as the average time it takes tenants to pay open charges, the percentage of posted charges you successfully collect, and the total dollar amount of delinquent charges over time.

 Add the following delinquency-related tiles to your dashboard to help reduce losses:

 Dashboard Tile
 Description

 Average Days to Collection

 Calculates the average number of days it takes tenants to pay open charges on their accounts. This tile helps you understand when tenants tend to pay their open charges.

 Collection Rates (Dashboard Tile)

 Calculates the percentage of posted charges that you successfully collect. This tile helps you identify which periods of the year are more likely to have delinquent charges.

 Delinquency By Property (Dashboard Tile)

 The count of tenants with open charges and the total amount of those charge at each property displays. This tile helps you identify which properties are more likely to have delinquent charges.

 Delinquency List (Dashboard Tile)

 The tenant's name, the unit they are leasing, and the date and amount of their delinquent charges displays. This tile helps you track which tenants have not paid open charges on their account.

 Delinquency Trend (Dashboard Tile)

 The total balance of delinquent charges over time displays. This tile helps you understand the total amount of income you may be losing to delinquency.
