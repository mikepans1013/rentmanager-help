Set Up Virtual Post Office (VPO)

Virtual Post Office (VPO) allows you to outsource the expensive and time-consuming process of printing, packaging, stamping, and mailing through the US postal system. Any letter-sized statement, invoice, report, or letter under 300 MB can be submitted to the Virtual Post Office for automatic fulfillment and delivery.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Every document is classified under one of the following categories:

Category Description

VPO-Ready

These documents have a reserved area on their first page that contains the To address of the recipient and, when applicable, the From address of the sender. This area displays through the envelope window to facilitate the shipping process.

Rent Manager allows you to design custom forms and letter templates that are VPO-ready with the reserved space on the front page.

Not VPO-Ready

These documents can still be submitted to the Virtual Post Office, but they do not have a reserved area on the first page for addresses. Rent Manager creates an additional cover page that contains the To address of the recipient and, if applicable, the From address of the sender which is formatted to display through the letter envelope window.

The fee associated with each VPO submission is based on the total number of recipients in the submitted batch and the page count of each letter/report. Rent Manager calculates this fee and asks you to approve the transaction before the VPO submission is officially processed. Approved transactions are billed to the credit card you have on file with LCS. Your submitted document(s) are postmarked for the same business day, unless it is a federal holiday.

More Information

VPO documents originate in the United States and are sent via the United States Postal Service (USPS), which delivers to Canada. As a best practice, generate reports intended for Canadian delivery in a separate batch, because postage rates for Canadian delivery differ from rates for mail within the United States.

Enable VPO in Rent Manager

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
System System Preferences View, Edit
VPO Manage VPO Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Once you receive the confirmation email containing your Virtual Post Office user credentials, you can enable the feature in Rent Manager. To enable your VPO account, do the following:

  1. Go to  arrow_forward  Administration, then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward Virtual Post Office arrow_forward General.

  2. In the Account Settings section, enter the Username and Password as listed on your confirmation email.

  3. Optionally, click Test Settings to make sure your database connected to your VPO credentials correctly.

  4. Click Save to save your changes.
    Your VPO connection is complete and you are able to send documents through VPO.

Next Steps

The following settings are not required but allow you to customize your VPO workflow by setting up default options and enabling notifications.

Settings Description

VPO Defaults (System Preferences)

These system preferences allow users to save time by establishing custom forms and letter templates that are VPO-ready, with a reserved area on their first page that contains the To address of the recipient and the From address of the sender. For more information, refer to VPO Defaults (System Preferences).

VPO General (System Preferences)

These system preferences allow users to establish the username and password for their VPO account, the maximum fee allowed per print job, and the email account used for VPO alerts and confirmations. For more information, refer to VPO General (System Preferences).
