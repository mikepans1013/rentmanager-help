Set Up a Tenant or Prospect with a Tenant Web Access and rmResident Account

Tenant Web Access (TWA) and rmResident are part of the Web Portal Suite package. TWA is a website that allows your tenants or prospects to manage various aspects of their leases, such as view their balance due, transaction history, meter readings, history/notes, and service issues. Additionally, tenants can make online payments, submit service issues, and edit their account information. TWA users also have the ability to view and sign lease documents or a collection of documents in a packet electronically. For tenants or prospects on the go, rmResident is a mobile app with similar capabilities to TWA that residents can use from a mobile device. If you have TWA, rmResident is included with no additional setup needed and your tenants can download the app from the App Store or Google Play and log in using their TWA credentials.

If you are just getting started with Web Portal Suite, refer to Set Up Web Portal Suite for more information on setting up the feature.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

Set Up TWA User

There are two ways you can set up your tenant or prospect for Tenant Web Access (TWA): you can create their account in Rent Manager and provide them with their TWA account information, or you can have the tenant sign up for their account directly in TWA.

Create TWA Account via Rent Manager

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

Creating a TWA account for your tenants or prospects via Rent Manager is often the quickest and easiest method for both parties. Just enter their email into TWA settings and the tenant or prospect receives an email that allows them to create their account. To create a TWA account for a tenant or prospect directly from Rent Manager, do the following:

  1. Account Navigation

    Prospect

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select the prospect's account.
    The prospect's details page displays.

    Tenant

    Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select the tenant's account.
    The tenant's details page displays.

  2. On the action bar to the right, click arrow_forward TWA Settings.
    The Web Access Settings pop-up displays.

  3. Click Add Web User.
    The Add Web User pop-up displays.

  4. In the Email Address field, enter the tenant or prospect's email address that they wish to use for Tenant Web Access.

  5. Click Save & Close to complete adding a user or Save & New to create additional users.
    The pop-up closes and a verification email is sent to that email address.

  6. Once they receive the email, the tenant or prospect must click Finalize.
    They are redirected to TWA's account creation page.

  7. The tenant or prospect must enter their information and create a password, entering it in both the Password and Confirm Password fields.

  8. The tenant or prospect must click Verify.
    Their TWA account is created and they are automatically logged in.

Tenants and Prospects Create Their Account via TWA

Alternatively, you can provide your tenants and/or prospects with their account number and they can create their TWA accounts at their leisure directly from the TWA website.

More Information

To streamline this process, you can create a letter template using scripting to quickly generate and send instructions for your tenants to set up or access their TWA accounts. Alternatively, you can use the Online Template Library to download one of our example TWA setup letters and customize it to suit your needs. For more information, refer to Online Template Library (Page) and Letter/Email Templates (Page).

For a tenant or prospect to create their Tenant Web Access account via TWA, they must do the following:

  1. The tenant or prospect must go to https://YourCompanyCode.twa.rentmanager.com, replacing the red text with your company's Company Code, which can be found in the top right of Rent Manager Express.

    For example, if your Company Code is EX-123, the web address for TWA would be https://EX-123.twa.rentmanager.com.

  2. At the bottom of the login section, they must click Sign Up.

  3. In the Profile Information section, they must enter their information in the available fields.

  4. In the Account Validation section's Account # field, they must enter their system-generated Rent Manager account number, which you must provide to the tenant or prospect. This number can be found on their tenant or prospect account's details page on the scoreboard at the top.

    Related Preferences

    If there are any additional verification options set as required in your system web preferences, the tenant or prospect needs to provide additional validation information, such as a phone number, email, or social security number. In order to validate their account, this information must be entered in their Rent Manager account and it must match the information the tenant or prospect enters in the Account Validation section of TWA. If it does not match, the account cannot be validated or created. For more information, refer to Tenant Web Access Accounts (System Web Preferences).

  5. The tenant or prospect must click Sign Up.
    A verification email is sent to their entered email address.

  6. Once they receive the email, the tenant or prospect must click Finalize.
    They are redirected to TWA's password creation page.

  7. The tenant or prospect must create a password and enter it in both the Password and Confirm Password fields.

  8. The tenant or prospect must click Verify.
    Their TWA account is created and they are automatically logged in.

Set Up rmResident User

After a tenant or prospect is set up for Tenant Web Access, they can access their account and manage their lease from the rmResident mobile app.

More Information

To streamline this process, you can use the Announcing the rmResident App letter template created by LCS. Once you download the template from the Online Template Library, you can customize it to suit your needs. For more information, refer to Online Template Library (Page) and Letter/Email Templates (Page).

For the tenant or prospect to download rmResident and log in, they must do the following:

  1. From either Google Play or the App Store, search for rmResident.

  2. Verify that the publisher is LCS and install the app.

  3. When rmResident has finished installing, open the app.

  4. In the Company Code field, the tenant or prospect must enter your Rent Manager company code, which can be found in the top right of Rent Manager Express. You must provide this information to the tenant or prospect.

  5. Tap Next.

  6. The tenant or prospect must enter the Email Address and Password they use to log into Tenant Web Access. Both TWA and rmResident share the same login information.

  7. Click Login.
    The tenant or prospect is now ready to use rmResident.
