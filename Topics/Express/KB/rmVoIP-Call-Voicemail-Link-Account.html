Link an Unlinked Call or Voicemail to an Account

rmVoIP is the fully independent, business-class phone system offered by LCS. rmVoIP provides enterprise-grade phone features like auto-attendants, hunt groups, conference calls, call forwarding, call recording, and more. If you receive a call from a phone number not listed on an account in Rent Manager, you can manually link the call from the Unlinked Calls page.

The Smart Voicemails feature—powered by Orion AI—allows you to manage all your voicemails in one spot in Rent Manager. Smart Voicemails organizes your voicemails by category and priority, providing a streamlined communication management process that reduces the chance of missed issues or follow-ups. Each voicemail is recorded, transcribed, and summarized by Orion AI. If you receive a voicemail from a phone number not listed on an account in Rent Manager, you can manually link the voicemail to the related entity, such as a tenant or owner.

More Information

This feature is licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com.

To access the details page for a call or voicemail, do one of the following:

Page Navigation

Call Details

Go to  arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward rmVoIP Unlinked Calls and select an unlinked call from the list.

Voicemail Details

Go to  arrow_forward Communication arrow_forward Texting/Phone arrow_forward Smart Voicemails and select an unlinked voicemail from the list.

Link to New Account

To create a new account in Rent Manager to associate the call or voicemail with, do the following:

  1. From the call or voicemail's details page, select Add New Account.
    The Add New Account pop-up displays.

  2. In the Select Account Type field, choose the entity (Tenant, Prospect, Property, Vendor, Owner, or Owner Prospect) to assign the new account to. Then, click Next.
    The wizard to add the entity's new account displays.

  3. Complete the wizard by entering and saving the new account's information.
    The account is created and linked to the call or voicemail.

Link to Existing Account

To associate the call or voicemail with an existing account, do the following:

  1. From the call or voicemail's details page, select Link to Existing Account.

  2. Select the account to link to the call or voicemail. Use the Search option or Account Type drop-down to filter the list of accounts.

  3. Click Link.
    The call or voicemail is linked to the selected account in Rent Manager.

More Information

To unlink an rmVoIP phone call, go to the entity's details page. On the History/Notes tile, click and, on the history/note item where the call is logged, click arrow_forward Unlink rmVoIP Phone Number.

To unlink a Smart Voicemail, go to the Smart Voicemails page and on the voicemail you wish to unlink, click  arrow_forward Unlink. For more information, refer to Orion Smart Voicemails (Page).
