Zego Resident Utility Billing

Rent Manager's integrated partner Zego offers the Resident Utility Billing feature, which allows you to automate the process of billing your tenants. Zego mails out a monthly customized statement that consolidates the tenant's rent and utility charges. In certain circumstances, Zego can fully take over the payment process as well.

More Information

ePay is a licensed feature by Zego. To use Zego ePay, you must set up an account with Zego and enter that information into Rent Manager. For more information, refer to Set Up Zego ePay.

Step 1: Enable Zego Resident Utility Billing Integration

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
System View Available Integrations Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To enable Zego Resident Utility Billing in Rent Manager, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com for more information on licensing the feature.

Alternatively, to request the activation of Resident Utility Billing directly within Rent Manager, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then go to Integrations arrow_forward Available Integrations.
    The Available Integrations page displays.

  2. In the Categories field, uncheck All Categories and select Utility Management from the drop-down list.

  3. On the Zego tile, click Contact Sales.
    The Contact Sales - Billing-Expense Management pop-up displays.

  4. Enter your contact information in the available fields.

  5. In the Notes field, enter any additional information you wish to provide to Zego.

  6. Click Contact.
    Your message is sent to Zego's sales department.

Step 2: Establish System Preferences with Zego

Once Zego's sales team contacts you and the Resident Utility Billing integration is licensed for your company, you need to enable the feature in your Rent Manager database. When setting up Resident Utility Billing for the first time, a Zego representative schedules a call to help you set these system preferences and acquire additional information to enable the feature.

Warning

Do not adjust any of these settings prior to your setup call with Zego. The preferences in Rent Manager must match the settings in Zego exactly, and if they do not, Resident Utility Billing will not function correctly.

To set up Resident Utility Billing preferences, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Administration, then go to Preferences arrow_forward System Preferences arrow_forward Zego Resident Billing.

  2. Select Enable Zego Resident Billing.

  3. Edit the settings as specified by Zego. For more information, refer to Zego Resident Billing (System Preferences).

  4. Click Save.
    Zego Resident Utility Billing is enabled and your settings are saved.
