# rmAppSuite Pro

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-rmAppSuite-Pro.htm

The rmAppSuite Pro app allows you to take Rent Manager with you in the field. From your mobile device, you can capture images and videos, perform inspections, read utility meters, accept payments, and manage information related to service issues, assets, and violations.

 Additionally, if you have rmVoIP ( Rent Manager 's integrated phone system) or the Text Messaging feature, you can call or text prospects, tenants, vendors, and owners directly through the app without using your personal phone number. As an added benefit, records of calls placed through rmVoIP and text conversations are saved to Rent Manager .

 More Information

 rmAppSuite Pro and many features within it are licensed and must each be purchased separately. For more information, contact a representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 The following modules are available in rmAppSuite Pro :

 Tile
 Description

 Assets

 The Assets module allows you to quickly view the account information of any asset in your Rent Manager database. You can view details specific to the asset, such as the manufacturer, the status, and service life dates. You are also able to view history/note items for the asset, as well as add and view images related to the asset. Locations and asset statuses can also be edited directly in rmAppSuite Pro .

 Call

 From the Phone Dialer , users can place calls through rmAppSuite Pro . Users with rmVoIP extensions can make these calls using their rmVoIP phone number instead of their personal phone number.

 Capture

 The Capture tool allows you to take pictures in the field using your mobile device, and then upload them to your Rent Manager database. These photos can be attached anywhere images are accepted in Rent Manager , such as user-defined fields or service issues.

 Contacts

 The Contacts module allows you to quickly view the account information of any tenant, prospect, owner, owner prospect, or vendor in your Rent Manager database. After locating the desired contact, you can communicate with them by phone or email, view their address on a map, receive payments, review their account details, as well as contact information of additional people associated with that account.

 Inspections

 The Inspections feature simplifies the inspections process by allowing you to perform, document, and complete an inspection in the field using your mobile device, and then upload the results into Rent Manager .

 Related Preferences

 To begin using the inspection module in rmAppSuite Pro , you must enable it system preferences. For more information, refer to rmAppSuite Inspection (System Preferences) .

 Additionally, to access the inspections module, users must have the Inspector option enabled on their user account's General tab in Rent Manager . For more information, refer to User Details (Page) .

 Readings

 The Meter Readings module allows you to easily enter meter data while in the field using your mobile device. If your meters have a bar code, the app utilizes your device's camera to read that bar code and enters usage data for that meter. rmAppSuite Pro also displays historical readings of each meter, which helps you identify irregularities on the spot.

 Receipts

 The Smart Receipts feature allows you to take pictures of expense receipts for purchases made while in the field. These expenses can be classified as a company credit card charge or a personal charge that needs reimbursement. Orion AI then extracts transaction information from the image(s) and autofills the relevant fields in rmAppSuite Pro . The receipt is then submitted to Rent Manager for further review.

 Service

 The Service module allows you to add new issues and access existing service issues in your Rent Manager database and update those issues in real time. Users may clock in and out to document their hours, contact tenants or vendors, or close issues from this screen.

 Related Preferences

 To begin using the service module in rmAppSuite Pro , you must enable it system preferences. For more information, refer to rmAppSuite Service (System Preferences) .

 Additionally, to access the service module, users must be added as maintenance techs in Rent Manager . For more information, refer to Add Maintenance Techs .

 Settings & Uploads

 From the side menu ( ), Settings allows you to manage Account Settings that determine various user preferences, such as how rmAppSuite Pro uses your mobile device's storage and what notifications your receive for Rent Manager text messages.

 The Settings menu also allows service techs to manage work availability in On Duty Schedule .

 From the side menu ( ), Uploads allows you to track the status of inspections and service issues as they are uploaded to Rent Manager and displays any errors preventing them from uploading.

 Text

 The Text feature allows you to send text messages from rmAppSuite Pro to contacts in your Rent Manager database. Texts are sent to the tenant from their property's designated Text Numbers , rather than your personal phone number.

 Violations

 The Violations module allows you to add new violations and access existing violations within your Rent Manager database and update the status of those violations from your mobile device. Users can add images, escalate and close violations, and assess charges and fines from this screen.

 Related Preferences

 Additional display settings for violations in rmAppSuite Pro can be configured in system preferences. For more information, refer to rmAppSuite Violations (System Preferences) .
