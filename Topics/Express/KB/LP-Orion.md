# Orion AI

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/LP-Orion.htm

Rent Manager 's Orion AI powers a variety of features to help streamline and expedite your daily tasks, including a suite of accounts receivable (AR) and accounts payable (AP) processing tools, automatic call summaries, simplified report analysis, and writing assistance.

 More Information

 These features are licensed and must be purchased separately. For more information, contact your sales representative at sales@rentmanager.com .

 The following features are powered by Orion AI :

 Feature
 Description

 Audit Trail Analysis

 The Audit Trail Analysis is a feature that provides in-depth analysis of the Audit Trail report, which can be reviewed and refined to help you identify important trends. This feature takes the Audit Trail report, a long, line-by-line log of Rent Manager user actions with little context or insight, and generates a detailed system history with actionable insights instantly.

 For more information, refer to Audit Trail Analysis Powered by Orion AI .

 Call Summaries

 The Orion Call Summary feature generates concise summaries of call recordings created using the rmVoIP phone service. You can use this feature to quickly scan call records, review the important points of each conversation, effectively triage issues, and be well-informed in your responses. Whether you receive a high or low call volume, this feature helps improve your team's efficiency in managing each phone call you receive.

 For more information, refer to Call Summaries Powered by Orion AI .

 Report Analysis

 The Orion Report Analysis is a feature that helps you analyze the data in your Rent Manager reports by summarizing their results in clear overviews that highlight key information. You can review and refine the analysis, and also copy, download, or attach the analysis directly to the report when you export it to save time, improve clarity, and make your reports easier to understand.

 For more information, refer to Report Analysis Powered by Orion AI .

 Smart Suite

 Using the Smart Suite, Rent Manager can streamline a variety of accounts receivable (AR) and accounts payable (AP) processes. Smart Suite features can automate data entry for bills, invoices, and checks, process reimbursements or company credit card expenses with rmAppSuite Pro , and synchronize transaction history with your real-world banks and cards. Additionally, the Optical Character Recognition (OCR) feature allows you to scan checks and convert the image text into machine-readable text.

 For more information, refer to Smart Suite Powered by Orion AI .

 Smart Voicemails

 The Orion Smart Voicemails feature can automatically transcribe and categorize voicemails. This feature creates a Smart Voicemails register in Rent Manager that displays all voicemails that need to be addressed and categorizes them by subject and/or urgency, allowing for immediate follow-up action. This feature also creates concise summaries of the voicemail transcript so the user can quickly glean an general idea of the content of the message.

 For more information, refer to Smart Voicemails Powered by Orion .

 Writing Assistant

 The Orion Writing Assistant is a helpful tool to help you improve your writing, whether you're composing a professional email, refining your grammar in a letter, or trying to convey a more persuasive tone in your marketing materials.

 For more information, refer to Writing Assistant Powered by Orion AI .
