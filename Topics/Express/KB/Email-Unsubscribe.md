# Unsubscribe From Emails

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Email-Unsubscribe.htm

Rent Manager allows you to easily manage which email categories (general, notifications, and confirmations) that contacts are subscribed to. For example, if a tenant requests to no longer receive confirmation emails, you can manage their subscriptions and unsubscribe them from the Confirmations category. Additionally, if the tenant is unsubscribed to a specific category and wishes to receive those emails again, you can just as easily resubscribe them to that category.

 System-type history/note items are created when email categories are subscribed to or unsubscribed from, as well as when emails that are normally tracked in history/notes are not sent to the entity due to them being unsubscribed to the associated category.

 Subscription Categories

 There are three email categories that an email address can be subscribed to. Email categories cannot be added, edited, or removed. Each category is detailed below.

 Category
 Description

 Confirmations

 Emails sent as receipts and confirmations when an action is completed, such as making a payment, submitting a service issue, or signing a document.

 General

 Emails that are manually created by the property manager. For example, sending bulk emails with a letter template, emailing a group of roommates, or composing an email to one recipient. When sending emails of this category, property managers can override email addresses that are unsubscribed.

 Notifications

 Emails sent as automated notifications or alerts. For example, notifications sent when an issue is scheduled, documents are published to TWA, or there is an upcoming amenity reservation. For more information, refer to Automated Notifications .

 Unsubscribe or Subscribe from Categories

 Warning

 You must have the recipient's permission, per federal regulations, before subscribing any email address to any of the email categories.

 When you attempt to subscribe an email address to an email category, a confirmation pop-up displays that requires you to confirm that you have obtained permission from the recipient to subscribe them.

 Related Privileges

 In order to update the email subscriptions for any entity, you must have the View and Edit privileges for the associated entity type. For example, to update the email subscriptions for a tenant, you need the Tenants privileges for View and Edit .

 For more information, refer to Control User Access .

 You can manage email subscriptions from an entity's details page, contacts, Owner Web Access (OWA) settings, and Tenant Web Access (TWA) settings. To access the Manage Email Subscriptions pop-up in these locations, locate the Email Address field and click Manage Subscriptions or click arrow_forward Manage Email Subscriptions .

 More Information

 If a recipient's email provider (e.g., Google) has an unsubscribe option, and they choose to unsubscribe from an email, that unsubscribed status is updated in Rent Manager and can be viewed in the Manage Email Subscriptions pop-up.

 On the Manage Email Subscriptions pop-up you can manually subscribe or unsubscribe for each email address on the entity's account.

 Override Unsubscribed Email Addresses

 If you attempt to manually send a necessary email to a single email address that is unsubscribed from the General category, the Override Unsubscribed Email pop-up displays and requires you to check the box stating I confirm that this message is necessary, non-promotional, and permitted under email compliance rules before overriding the unsubscribed status and sending the necessary email to the recipient.

 In instances where you are sending an email to multiple email addresses and there is one or more email addresses that are unsubscribed from the General category, the Override Unsubscribed Emails pop-up displays with multiple options on how to process the email.

 The following options are available:

 Option
 Description

 Send to all recipients

 The email is sent to all of the email addresses regardless of subscription status for the General category. Before you are able to use this option, you must check the box stating I confirm that this message is necessary, non-promotional, and permitted under email compliance rules before overriding the unsubscribed status and sending the necessary email to the recipients.

 Send to subscribed recipients only

 The email is sent to only the email addresses that are subscribed to the General category.

 View Unsubscribed Emails

 Generates a report that lists the email addresses (from the email you are attempting to send) that are unsubscribed from the General category, as well as their associated entity account and account number. This list gives more insight of who you may or may not be contacting based on the option you select, so you can confirm if you still want to override subscription settings or if you need to contact unsubscribed accounts via another method.
