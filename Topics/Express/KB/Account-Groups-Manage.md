# Manage Account Groups

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Account-Groups-Manage.htm

Account groups allow you to group together two or more tenant accounts for transactional and reporting purposes, while maintaining individual account records for each tenant. Each account group has a tenant account that serves as the master account while the other tenants in the group are considered subaccounts.

 For example, you may manage student housing and have four students living as roommates in a single unit. Creating an account group allows you to set up each tenant with their own Rent Manager account while also generating a single statement for all of their combined transactions. You can also receive a single payment from one roommate that can be applied against the charges of the other roommates.

 More Information

 If you wish to assign one lease for the entire unit regardless of however many roommates live there, you can create a primary tenant account and list any additional roommates as contacts on the account. For more information, refer to Set Up Roommates .

 When you use account groups, the View Transactions pop-up option to Show Account Group Items , the Add Payment pop-up Account Group Items option, and the History/Notes pop-up Account Group Notes option are available for each tenant in the group.

 From the Account Groups page, you can change the master account as well as remove (i.e., delete) tenants from an account group. You only need to change the master account if the tenant assigned as the Master is being removed from the account group. Otherwise, you can just remove the tenant from the group.

 To add a tenant to an account group, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants .
The Tenants page displays.

 -
 For the tenant you wish to place in an account group, click arrow_forward Add To Account Groups .
The Add Account Group pop-up displays.

 More Information

 If you have more than one tenant that needs to be added to an account group, you can do this by clicking Bulk Actions and going to Account Groups arrow_forward Add Account Group .

 -
 Select one of the following options depending on the action you wish to take:

 Option
 Description

 Use current account as the master account

 Create a new account group, using the selected tenant Account Name to serve as the master account. This option can be performed only on the Tenants page from the Bulk Actions menu, and only when multiple tenants are selected.

 Create a new account to be used as the master account

 Create a new tenant account to be used as the master account in the account group. This option can be performed only on the Tenants page from the Bulk Actions menu, and only when multiple tenants are selected.

 Add the selected account to an existing account group

 Add the selected tenant to an existing account as a subaccount. From the drop-down list, select an existing account group.

 -
 Click Save .
The tenant is added to the new or designated account group and the tenant's account includes additional fields related to their account group.

 Remove Tenants From an Account Group

 If the tenant assigned to the master account needs to be removed from the account group, you first must selected another tenant to be the master account otherwise you cannot remove the tenant from the group. Selecting which tenant to use for the master account can be based on which tenant in an account group is the preferred contact. Common charges are also typically posted to the master account.

 Change the Master Account of an Account Group

 To change the master account on an account group, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Rental Info Setup arrow_forward Tenants/Prospects arrow_forward Account Groups .
The Account Groups page displays.

 -
 Select the account group you wish to update from the list at the left.

 -
 Uncheck Master next to the tenant account currently assigned to the master account.

 -
 Check Master next to the tenant account you wish to make the master account.

 -
 Click Save .
The name of the account group is immediately updated in the left panel to reflect the new master account.

 Remove Account Group Tenants

 For the tenant(s) you wish to remove from the account group, click arrow_forward Delete and then click Save . The tenant is removed from the account group. Additionally, the View Transactions pop-up option to Show Account Group Items , the Add Payment pop-up Account Group Items option, and the History/Notes pop-up Account Group Notes option are removed for that tenant.
