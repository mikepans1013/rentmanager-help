Loans Payable Variable Rate Calculations

When you add one of your loans to Rent Manager, you can choose to make it a Variable Rate loan. This means the interest rate changes on a set schedule throughout the life of the loan. For example, for a 5/1 adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM), the rate is fixed for the first five years of the loan, then changes once every year for the remainder of the loan. Each year that the interest rate changes, the loan's monthly payment amount also changes.

Variable loan rates are often used so the borrow can have lower initial payments than a fixed-rate loan, and potentially lower them further if interest rates drop in the future due to market fluctuations. Variable rates are commonly used for home equity lines of credit, student loans, and mortgages.

When you first add the loan to Rent Manager, you select only the initial interest rate percentage because the future interest rates are not generally known at the time of the loan's creation. After the loan is added to Rent Manager, you can edit the details of the loan to establish the new variable rates when needed.

  • Initial Interest Period = 3/1/2024–2/28/2029

  • Initial Interest Rate = 5%

  • Total Monthly Payment (principal + interest) = $536.82
