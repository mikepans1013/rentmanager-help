# How to Access Rent Manager Express

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Access-RMX.htm

Rent Manager Express is the online companion product to our flagship software, Rent Manager 12 . Rent Manager Express , or Express , is an application that allows you to access and manage your Rent Manager data from any web browser and keep your data synced between both products regardless of which you are currently using.

 If you are a Rent Manager 12 customer with Rent Manager Online , then there are no additional fees or contracts needed to use Express ; you just need the correct website address, and your Rent Manager 12 login credentials.

 Step 1: Open the Express Website

 You can open Rent Manager Express by going to the website specifically tied to your database. The direct link to the Rent Manager Express website is here: https://www.rentmanager.com/rmx/ .

 From the direct link, you will be prompted to enter your organization's Company Code , which can be found at the top of your Rent Manager 12 window.

 Step 2: Log in to Express

 On the web page, a login screen displays.

 -
 Enter the same Username and Password that you use to log into Rent Manager 12 .

 -
 Click Sign In .
You are now logged in and ready to start using Rent Manager Express .
