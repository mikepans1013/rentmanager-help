Blue Moon

Blue Moon is a software that provides industry-standard leasing documents that are compliant with the specific rules and regulations of your state and/or industry association (e.g., NAA, TAA, GAA, AANC) leaves nothing for the property management company to do before issuing the lease to a tenant. Blue Moon can be integrated with Rent Manager to export your tenant and prospect information directly into Blue Moon leasing documents. Your contract with Blue Moon determines which leasing documents are available for you to generate.

More Information

Published Blue Moon leases can be viewed from the History/Notes tile on the tenant's details page.
