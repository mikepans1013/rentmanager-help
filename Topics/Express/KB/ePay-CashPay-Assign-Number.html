Assign a CashPay Account Number

Zego offers the CashPay feature, which allows tenants and prospects to pay rent and other charges in cash at more than 25,000 retail locations within the United States. There are two versions of CashPay: Affinity CashPay, which provides tenants and prospects with physical cards to use for direct payments; and CashPay Basic, which requires payees to provide their account information when making CashPay payments.

More Information

Before you can assign a CashPay numbers, you must first enable the feature in Rent Manager. For more information, refer to Zego CashPay.

To assign a CashPay account number, go to the tenant or prospect's details page using one of the following navigation paths.

Entity Navigation

Prospect

Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Prospects and select a prospect.

Tenant

Go to arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward General arrow_forward Tenants and select a tenant.

Then, on the action bar to the right, select arrow_forwardCashPay.

Refer to the headings below to assign your number based on the type of account you have.

Affinity CashPay

If you are utilizing the paid Affinity version, Zego supplies your company with Affinity cards to distribute to your tenants and prospects. This Affinity card must be activated before being given to tenants or prospects. When making a payment at a retailer, this card (or its electronic version) must be presented.

To activate an Affinity card, do the following:

  1. On the CashPay Account pop-up, select Issue New Account Number.

  2. In the New Account Number field, enter the account number shown on the Affinity card.

  3. In the Confirm Account Number field, reenter the account number shown on the Affinity card.

  4. Click OK.
    The Affinity account number is now associated with that tenant or prospect in Rent Manager, and the card may be given to the tenant or prospect.

CashPay Basic

If you are using the free CashPay Basic service, you need to generate CashPay account information within Rent Manager and print or email the CashPay Card with that information to the tenant or prospect.

To generate a CashPay Card document, do the following:

  1. On the CashPay Account pop-up, select Issue New Account Number.

  2. Click Generate.
    A CashPay account number is created and the Print CashPay pop-up displays.

  3. Click Yes to generate the CashPay Card.

  4. Provide the CashPay Card to the tenant or prospect to use for CashPay payments.
