# Send Estimates for Approval

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Estimate-Send.htm

Estimates allow you to record the anticipated parts and labor needed to complete a project, itemize the costs, and provide a total amount due, including any markups and/or taxes you add. After you have saved the estimate, you can send it to an owner, tenant, or prospect for their approval. The recipient can then review the estimate and either approve it or reject it.

 Send the Estimate

 Both tenant, prospects, and owners can receive estimates via email. In addition to email, owners can receive estimates via Owner Web Access (OWA) .

 To send an estimate for approval via email, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Receivables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Estimates .
The Estimates page displays.

 -
 For the estimate you wish to send, click arrow_forward Email Estimate .
The Email Custom Form pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Select a Report field, select the estimate format you wish to send.

 -
 Click Continue .
The Compose Email pop-up displays.

 -
 Fill out the fields and body of the email. By default, The email address on the recipient's account populates, and the estimate is automatically added as an attachment. For more information, refer to Send an Email .

 -
 Once the email is written, click Send .
The email is sent to the owner, tenant, or prospect for them to review. When they respond with their decision, you can update the Status on the estimate to Approved or Rejected .

 Send Estimate via Owner Web Access

 Related Preferences

 To enable owner's approval of estimates via Owner Web Access (OWA) , you must first check the options for Show estimates and Enable owner estimate approval and rejection in system web preferences. For more information, refer to Owner Web Access Estimates (System Web Preferences) .

 To send an estimate to an owner via Owner Web Access , from the estimate's details page, set the Status field to Awaiting Approval . Then click Save .

 The estimate is made available in the owner's OWA portal where they can review it and select Approve or Reject . Their selection automatically updates the Status on the estimate's details to Approved or Rejected .

 More Information

 If you want to receive notifications to monitor the status of estimates sent to owners via Owner Web Access , you can configure automated notifications that let you know when the estimates have been approved or rejected by the owner. For more information, refer to Estimate Approved via OWA (Automated Notification) and Estimate Rejected via OWA (Automated Notification) .
