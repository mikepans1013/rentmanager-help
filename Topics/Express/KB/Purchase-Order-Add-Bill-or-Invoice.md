# Add a Bill or Invoice to a Purchase Order

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Purchase-Order-Add-Bill-or-Invoice.htm

Purchase orders (POs) are used to request permission to purchase items, prevent unauthorized purchases, and maintain a historical record of items purchased. All purchase orders are assigned statuses to identify rejected, approved, pending approval, or closed POs.

 Once a PO is approved, it can be linked to any related bills or, if a tenant or owner is selected on the PO, an invoice.

 To link a PO and a bill, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Bills .
The Bills page displays.

 -
 Select or add a bill associated with the PO's vendor. For more information on creating vendor bills, refer to Add a One-Time Vendor/Owner Bill .

 -
 On the bill's details page, select Link Existing PO .

 -
 In the Link Existing PO pop-up, select the PO associated with the vendor and bill.

 -
 Click Link .
If the selected PO is already approved, the PO and bill are linked. Otherwise, the second page of the Link Existing PO pop-up displays.

 -
 In the Selected Bill Information section, verify that the PO information is correct.

 -
 In the Purchase Order # Billing Quantity section's Billing Qty column, enter the quantity of items on the PO that this bill is paying.

 -
 Click Link .
The PO and bill are linked.

 To create a new invoice linked to a PO, do the following:

 -
 Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Purchase Orders and select a tenant or owner PO.
The PO's details page displays.

 -
 In the Links tile, click Add Invoice .
The Create A Link pop-up displays.

 -
 Click Yes .
The invoice is created and automatically linked to the PO.

 -
 Click Save .
The PO is saved with the attached invoice.

 -
 Click the newly created Invoice # .
The invoice's details page displays.

 -
 Verify the invoice's information and, if needed, make any necessary changes.

 -
 Click Save .
The linked invoice is updated.
