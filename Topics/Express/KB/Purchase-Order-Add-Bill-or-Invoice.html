Add a Bill or Invoice to a Purchase Order

Purchase orders (POs) are used to request permission to purchase items, prevent unauthorized purchases, and maintain a historical record of items purchased. All purchase orders are assigned statuses to identify rejected, approved, pending approval, or closed POs.

Once a PO is approved, it can be linked to any related bills or, if a tenant or owner is selected on the PO, an invoice.

Link a Bill

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Accounting View bill register Enabled
POs/Estimates Purchase orders View, Edit
Payables Bills View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To link a PO and a bill, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Bills.
    The Bills page displays.

  2. Select or add a bill associated with the PO's vendor. For more information on creating vendor bills, refer to Add a One-Time Vendor/Owner Bill.

  3. On the bill's details page, select Link Existing PO.

  4. In the Link Existing PO pop-up, select the PO associated with the vendor and bill.

  5. Click Link.
    If the selected PO is already approved, the PO and bill are linked. Otherwise, the second page of the Link Existing PO pop-up displays.

  6. In the Selected Bill Information section, verify that the PO information is correct.

  7. In the Purchase Order # Billing Quantity section's Billing Qty column, enter the quantity of items on the PO that this bill is paying.

  8. Click Link.
    The PO and bill are linked.

Link an Invoice

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Sales/Invoicing Invoices Add, View, Edit
POs/Estimates Purchase orders View, Edit

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

To create a new invoice linked to a PO, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward General arrow_forward Purchase Orders and select a tenant or owner PO.
    The PO's details page displays.

  2. In the Links tile, click Add Invoice.
    The Create A Link pop-up displays.

  3. Click Yes.
    The invoice is created and automatically linked to the PO.

  4. Click Save.
    The PO is saved with the attached invoice.

  5. Click the newly created Invoice #.
    The invoice's details page displays.

  6. Verify the invoice's information and, if needed, make any necessary changes.

  7. Click Save.
    The linked invoice is updated.
