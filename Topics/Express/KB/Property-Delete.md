# Delete a Property

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Property-Delete.htm

Rent Manager gives you two options to handle properties you no longer manage in your database. The first option, and best practice, is to make the property inactive. This is done on the property details page's General tile by unchecking the Active box. Information about inactive properties remains in Rent Manager and may be included in reports wherever the Include Inactive option is available and selected. For example, you can use the inactive option for a property that has been sold in order to preserve valuable historical and financial data.

 Should you find that deleting a property is the best option for your business, there are two deletion methods available in Rent Manager : complete or merge.

 If you have created a property by mistake and do not need to maintain any historical data or financial records for the property, the complete method is the best option. Alternatively, choosing the merge method allows you to combine financial and historical data from the property with another existing property in your Rent Manager database. This option ensures that no important information is permanently deleted from Rent Manager and is the preferred method if you would like to maintain records for the source property. You may wish to use the merge option for duplicated properties.

 Make a Property Inactive

 Making a property inactive ensures that all the information regarding the property remains in Rent Manager for you to access if you need it again. Inactive properties can also be included in reports wherever the Include Inactive option is available and selected.

 To make a property inactive, go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Properties and do the following:

 -
 Select the property you wish to deactivate.
The Property Details page displays.

 -
 On the General tile, uncheck the Active box.

 -
 Click Save .
If there are residents with active lease for the property, the Active Leases pop-up displays.

 -
 Enter a move-out date to modify the leases. Optionally, leave the Move Out Date field blank to make no adjustments to the active leases.

 -
 Press Save to close the Active Leases pop-up.
The property is now inactive. As such, ePay for this property is disabled, and the property is inaccessible from other places in Rent Manager .

 Delete a Property

 If you created a property by accident and do not wish to maintain any historical data or financial records for the property, the complete method is the best option. Alternatively, choosing the merge method allows you to combine financial and historical data from the property with another existing property.

 Deleting a property from Rent Manager is final and cannot be undone.

 To permanently delete a property, go to   arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Properties and do the following:

 -
 Select the property you wish to delete and on the action bar to the right, click .
The Confirm Delete pop-up displays.

 -
 Select Yes to confirm that you want to delete the property.
The Delete Property pop-up displays.

 -
 Choose from the following options for Delete Method .

 Option
 Description

 Complete

 Select to remove all information about the property, including financial records, from Rent Manager . This is final and cannot be undone. All records pertaining to the property, including bank transfers, journals, checks, bills, credits, and credit card transactions are deleted and cannot be recovered.

 Warning

 Choosing this option means that no data about this property can be accessed again. In the event of a financial audit, historical data cannot be generated.

 Merge

 Select to combine records with another property in Rent Manager . In the Property to merge to field, select the property you wish to merge records with. Records to be merged include bank transfers, journals, checks, bills, credits, and credit card transactions.

 More Information

 Rent Manager will retroactively change the historical data of the deleted property to match that of the property it is being merged with, which may lead to inaccuracies in your financials. To avoid this, make the property inactive by unchecking the Active box on the General tile. This will ensure all records remain tied to the correct property in Rent Manager .

 -
 Once you make your selection, click Accept .
If you selected Complete , the property is deleted from your Rent Manager database. If you selected Merge , the property is merged with an existing property in your Rent Manager database.
