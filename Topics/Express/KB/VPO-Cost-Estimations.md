# Estimate the Cost of a Virtual Post Office (VPO) Job

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/VPO-Cost-Estimations.htm

Virtual Post Office (VPO) allows you to outsource the expensive and time-consuming process of printing, packaging, stamping, and mailing through the United States Postal Service (USPS). Any letter-sized statement, invoice, report, or letter under 300 MB can be submitted to the VPO for automatic fulfillment and delivery.

 Before submitting a VPO job in Rent Manager , you are prompted with the actual amount that the job costs. Jobs are priced per envelope based on the number of pages in each envelope and the number of envelopes being mailed via VPO. This cost includes paper, postage, and labor.

 You can calculate the approximate cost of the job using the following formula: Price per envelope * Number of recipients = Estimated job cost .
