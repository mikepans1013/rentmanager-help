Allocate Bill Payments to Owner Invoices

When paying bills from your property management company's vendor account in Rent Manager, you can bill your owners for any charges that they are responsible for paying. If the owner has multiple open receivables, you can choose to have Rent Manager automatically allocate the bill payment or manually allocate the payment to open owner invoices.

Related Privileges

Group Privilege Column
Properties/Units Properties View
Receivables Take owner Payments Enabled
Banks/Checks Bank deposits Add
Payables Allow user to pay bills Enabled

For more information, refer to Control User Access.

More Information

To ensure that owner open receivables are available to pay bills, the system preferences Allow manual allocation of payment at the time bills are paid and Add a payment from the owner when a bill is paid to the management company must be enabled. For more information, refer to Management Company (System Preferences). Additionally, a bill where the owner is selected in the Billable field must be created. For more information, refer to Bill Back an Owner for Property Expenses.

Automatically Allocate Bill Payments to Open Owner Invoices

You can opt to have Rent Manager automatically select which open owner receivables are paid by this bill. This is most useful for scenarios where there are multiple open invoices and you don't want to sort through them to find the oldest charge for the allocation to be applied to.

To have Rent Manager automatically allocate bill payments to owner invoices, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills.
    The Pay Bills page displays.

  2. In the Pay column, check the bill(s) you want to pay for the property management company's Vendor account.

  3. Click Pay Bills.
    The Payment Info pop-up displays.

  4. Enter information for the following options.

    Option Description

    Comment

    An optional note that provides further context for this payment. The note displays in the Comment column on Vendor Transactions pop-up and in the check's Memo field.

    To select a memorized comment, click .

    Consolidate By Vendor

    Combines bills from a specific vendor into a single payment. The selected bills with the same Vendor, Bank, and Pay Method are paid together with a single check or payment transaction.

    Mark check(s) to be printed

    Indicates you want to print the check(s) from Rent Manager for the bill(s) being paid.

    Payment Date

    The date on which the payment was issued. This date should match your real-world financial records to prevent any issues with reconciliation. By default, the current date populates.

  5. Click Pay. If Mark check(s) to be printed was checked and you wish to print the checks now, click Pay & Print Checks.
    The Management Company Receivables pop-up displays.

  6. Click Yes.
    The bill is paid and an owner payment is allocated to the oldest open owner invoice.

Manually Allocate Bill Payments to Open Owner Invoices

You have the option to manually select which of your owner's open invoices you apply a bill payment to. This is useful for scenarios where you want to apply the allocation to a specific invoice instead of the oldest charge.

To manually allocate bill payments to owner invoices, do the following:

  1. Go to arrow_forward Payables arrow_forward Bills arrow_forward Pay Bills
    The Pay Bills page displays.

  2. In the Pay column, check the bill(s) you want to pay for the property management company's Vendor account.

  3. Click Pay Bills.
    The Payment Info pop-up displays.

  4. Enter information for the following options.

    Option Description

    Comment

    An optional note that provides further context for this payment. The note displays in the Comment column on Vendor Transactions pop-up and in the check's Memo field.

    To select a memorized comment, click .

    Consolidate By Vendor

    Combines bills from a specific vendor into a single payment. The selected bills with the same Vendor, Bank, and Pay Method are paid together with a single check or payment transaction.

    Mark check(s) to be printed

    Indicates you want to print the check(s) from Rent Manager for the bill(s) being paid.

    Payment Date

    The date on which the payment was issued. This date should match your real-world financial records to prevent any issues with reconciliation. By default, the current date populates.

  5. Click Pay. If Mark check(s) to be printed was checked and you wish to print the checks now, click Pay & Print Checks.
    The Management Company Receivables pop-up displays.

  6. To manually allocate the payment, click No on the pop-up.
    The Owner Payment Allocations pop-up displays.

  7. In the Open Charges section, in the Allocate? column, check each open charge to apply an owner payment towards those open invoices.

  8. Optionally, in the Allocated field, adjust the amount being allocated to that open charge.

  9. When you are satisfied with your payment allocations, click Save.
    The bill is paid and an owner payment is allocated to the selected owner invoices.
