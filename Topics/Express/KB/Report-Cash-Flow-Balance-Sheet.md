# Cash Flow and Balance Sheet

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/KB/Report-Cash-Flow-Balance-Sheet.htm

Both the Cash Flow report and the Balance Sheet report provide the balance of cash available. The Cash Flow examines cash at the end of period value while the Balance Sheet 's total bank value, including undeposited funds, is examined.

 Warning

 The information contained here does not replace the advice of an accountant. If you are unclear about how to interpret the data presented in the related reports, please consult your accountant.

 The Cash Flow report is used to track the movement of cash over a period of time. It starts with the beginning cash balance and reports all changes that were made to result in the ending cash balance. When properties receive money, such as collecting rental income, receiving owner contributions, or selling assets, positive totals display in the report. When properties spend money, such as paying maintenance fees, providing owner distributions, or purchasing assets, negative totals display in the report. Additionally, this report's totals include deposited and undeposited funds and provides users with a clear picture of how the company is receiving and spending cash.

 The Balance Sheet report displays the financial position of a property or business as of the report date. This report can be used to view the assets of one or more properties and the claims (liabilities and equities) against those assets.
