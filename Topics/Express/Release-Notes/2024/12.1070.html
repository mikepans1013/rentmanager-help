12.1070 Release Notes

The release begins rolling out on April 17, 2024.

New Features

Coming soon to Rent Manager, Income Verification, powered by AmRent! This new resource is designed to simplify how applicants submit their income details during the screening process. The licensed feature uses a built-in integration, which means you can run income verification from your Prospects and Tenants pages. From there, the applicant completes a few steps authorizing data from their bank accounts to be reported directly to Rent Manager for your review. This eliminates paper documentation so you can quickly and confidently verify income.

If you’re interested in learning more about the Income Verification feature, call (800) 324-3681 option 3, or email rmsales@amrent.com.

The Import Bills tool was added to Express, which allows you to import new bills into your database, greatly reducing the amount of time spent on data entry.

Enhancements

To make viewing and managing service issues in Express more intuitive and user-friendly, several changes were made to the Issue details page. These changes include improvements that make important issue information easier to read and update. Tiles were resized and revamped to make performing popular actions—like updating checklists and adding work order items—much easier and more efficient by requiring fewer clicks.

As previewed in last month’s release notes, 92 reports (including at least 60 financial reports) received a modern, streamlined design in this release. Headers that include the report’s name, date information (if applicable), and specified report options were added to all pages of the updated reports. Changes were also made to the way data is displayed in these reports, improving organization, spacing, shading, and legibility.

In addition to the new styling, we’ve significantly improved the format of Excel exports—removing merged cells and improving the overall alignment—making it much easier to tweak the exported data to fit your needs.

The Owner and Owner Prospect layout types were added to System Layouts and My Layouts. This addition makes it a breeze to create an owner or owner prospect layout that optimizes your workflows and meets your individual business needs!
