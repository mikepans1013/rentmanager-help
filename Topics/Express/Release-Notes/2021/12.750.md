# 12.750 Release Notes

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Release-Notes/2021/12.750.htm

The release begins rolling out on August 17, 2021.

 New Features

 The Budget feature was added, which allows you to document your projected income and expenses, and then enter your actual revenue and costs throughout the year. On the Budget page, the following actions can be taken:

 -
 You can Import budgets from external files.

 -
 Click on a cell in the budget to access details about that amount, such as the GL Account , Amount budgeted, and relevant Comments .

 -
 Quickly populate a budget with information from another year by using the Fill From Previous action.

 -
 Click on a cell and select Copy Across on a GL account row to repeat the same amount for each month in that year’s budget. Alternatively, you can choose to change the amount by a percentage or specified amount each month.

 -
 Select cells and use the Modify Selected function to quickly update budget amounts based upon a percentage or specified amount.

 -
 Enter an amount in the Total column for a GL account, and quickly divide that total evenly across 12 months or based on a previous year’s budget.

 The Management Fee Templates page was added, where you may establish how fees should be assessed and collected from properties that you manage. Creating templates of your fee structures allows you to quickly assign them to each property in your portfolio. Additionally, you can copy an existing fee template to use as a starting point for a new template.

 Path: arrow_forward Rental Info arrow_forward Owners arrow_forward Management Fee Templates

 On the Property Details page, the Fees Setup slide-up page was added. On this page, you can view or edit the management fee structure currently assigned to the property. Additionally, you can convert the fee structure from advanced to standard, or vice versa.

 On the Property Details page, you can now view a breakdown of fee details for a specific management fee posting. This is available for standard management fee structures only.

 The Management Fee Breakdown report was added, which details how calculations in management fee charges and owner checks were made for properties using standard management fee structures.

 Enhancements

 The Add Payment page for tenants and prospects was updated with an option to Collapse Invoices . Check this option to show a single row with the total of an invoice, instead of each line item.
