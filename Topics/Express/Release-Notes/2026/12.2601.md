# 12.260100 Changelog

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Release-Notes/2026/12.2601.htm

The release begins rolling out on January 22, 2026.

 New Features

 Revolutionize how you manage renters insurance with LeaseTrack's newest options!

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The LeaseTrack renters insurance integration was updated, allowing you to maintain multiple master policies with different liability coverage amounts. This update gives you greater control over your renters insurance settings, making setup more intuitive and boosting security with strategic reporting updates. These enhancements streamline your insurance policy management, provide greater flexibility, strengthen administrative control, and improve reporting and data visibility.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Setup privilege group was updated with a Manage Renters Insurance Setup privilege, which, when Enabled , grants the user the ability to manage renters insurance settings without having the System privilege group System Preferences ability to Edit .

 -
 The Renters Insurance page was added, which allows you to enable and set up renters insurance tracking through the LeaseTrack integration. Additionally, you can require tenants to be included on a Master Policy insurance plan if they currently have no renters insurance, or if their insurance coverage is inadequate.

 -
 The Insurance History pop-up was added, which allows you to view a detailed breakdown of renters insurance setting changes, separated by property.

 -
 The Audit Trail report was updated with a Renters Insurance audit type report option.

 -
 The Add Master Policy wizard was added, which allows you to add a new master policy and manage property assignments (when transitioning or adding new properties), keeping your account in sync with LeaseTrack contacts and policy changes.

 -
 The Renters Insurance Breakdown report and Renters Insurance Breakdown dashboard tile were updated, allowing you to easily monitor enrollment statuses by liability amount.

 -
 The Tenant details page's Leases tile was updated. allowing you to view the Insurance master policy's liability limit and type.

 -
 The Lease Details pop-up was updated, allowing you to view the Insurance master policy's liability limit and type.

 Gain complete control of your payables process with work order links on bills, Smart Bills, and Smart Receipts.

 Express Rent Manager 12 rmAppSuite Pro

 The Smart Bills and Smart Receipts features were updated, allowing you to easily link a Smart Bill or Smart Receipt to an issue and work order directly from the review process. Additionally, you can retroactively link an existing bill to a work order from both the Issue page and Bill details page. These updates make approvals faster, expense tracking more accurate, and give you complete command of your payables workflow with no manual entries or missing links. For more information, refer to the Add Links to a Bill topic in Express help.

 Coming soon to rmAppSuite Pro , the ability for your technicians to select an issue to link when they are scanning receipts in the field.

 Maximize your returns and track where every dollar goes with the new utility cost recovery tool!

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The utility cost recovery tool was added, allowing you to easily add master meters, track monthly consumption in seconds, and generate reports that show you exactly how much you are spending on utilities, compared to what you are charging your tenants, so you can easily see what you are really recovering each billing period.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Utilities page was updated with Available columns for Default Vendor , Default Expense Account , and Master Meters .

 -
 The Add Utility wizard was updated, improving navigation and adding or repositioning tiles, sections, and fields to simplify the add utility process.

 -
 The Utility Detail page was updated with a Master Meters tile, allowing you to add and manage the utility's master meter.

 -
 The Meter Readings Setup page was updated, making filtering meters and editing meter types a breeze.

 -
 The Meter Readings page and View Consumption History pop-up were updated, allowing you easily view and edit the utility's billing period.

 -
 The Utility Cost Recovery report was added, allowing you to compare the consumption and charges between your individual meters for each unit and your master meters to review how much you are recovering over a date range.

 Take control of your data entry with Custom Wizards!

 Express

 The Custom Wizards tool was added to Rent Manager Express , allowing you to create streamlined data entry processes tailored to your exact needs. With this new tool, you can fully customize your Express add wizard workflow, guiding your team step-by-step, displaying only the essential fields for each task. You can design wizards from scratch, customize existing ones, or import wizards from Rent Manager 12 . Unleash new levels of productivity and ease for your entire team by eliminating sifting through unnecessary fields and tedious training.

 This release introduces the Tenant custom wizard, with custom wizards for Prospect , Property , Unit , Asset , Move In , Move Out , and Issue coming soon!

 Enhancements

 Easily view, manage, and track your email subscriptions with new enhancements!

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The email tool was updated with enhanced visibility and control for your email subscriptions. In order to give you complete visibility of each entity's (tenant, prospect, vendor, owner, or owner prospect) subscription status, new status icons were added to let you instantly see which email list a recipient is subscribed or unsubscribed from.

 We've also made managing your subscription statuses easier by allowing you to unsubscribe individuals from some or all email lists, ensuring you are honoring the entity's preference and following compliance standards. In addition, you can effortlessly resubscribe emails when they are ready to hear from you again. For more information, refer to the Unsubscribe From Emails topic in Express help.

 Introducing improved styling and usability for contact tracking.

 Express

 In our continuing efforts to enhance the Rent Manager Express user experience, several exciting changes were made to the Contacts tile and View Contacts pop-up for tenants, prospects, and vendors. These updates have made information easier to read, improved navigation, and added or repositioned sections and fields to simplify performing popular actions.

 Quickly add and view scanned documents associated with an invoice via the new Attachments field.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 An Attachments field was added to the Add Invoice pop-up and Invoice details page, enabling you to upload and store scanned originals directly from the invoice record.

 Updated and expanded filtering options have come to the Tenants page.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The filter options on the Tenants page were expanded, giving you the ability to filter your tenant list to identify tenants who entered the eviction process but were not evicted. Additionally, the Exclude Assets and Exclude ORI filters were added, letting you exclude leases for other rentable items (ORIs) and assets when filtering the tenant list.

 Effortlessly assign issues to your technicians with the new maintenance scheduling enhancements!

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The maintenance scheduling tool was updated, allowing you to limit scheduling options based on each technician's availability. This enhancement also allows you to assign emergency issues to technicians outside of their scheduled work hours.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Service Manager privilege group was updated with a Schedule issues outside a tech's working hours privilege, which, when Enabled , allows you to override the schedule established for a maintenance group and assign issues to technicians when they are not scheduled to work.

 -
 The Add Maintenance Group and Group Settings pop-ups were updated with an Only schedule issues during a tech's working hours field, which allows you to control whether issues can be assigned to technicians anytime or only during their scheduled hours.

 Streamline your unit turnover and home tracking process with the new Move Out wizard updates!

 Express

 The Move Out wizard's Homeowner Status tile was renamed Home and now includes an option to either set a Location End Date for a home-type asset that is no longer located on a lot or update the homeowner status if the home is changing ownership.
