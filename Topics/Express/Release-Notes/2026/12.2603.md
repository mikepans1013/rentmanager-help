 New Features

 Flag, verify, and post with confidence with the new meter exceptions review workflow.

 Express

 Meter exceptions and the approval workflow were enhanced, making managing your utilities easier than ever. With these updates, abnormal readings can be flagged at the meter by your technicians, who provide context using standardized exception reasons to give you a complete picture of potential issues. With designated approvers, you can determine who reviews exceptions before readings can be posted.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Setup privilege group was updated with a Manage High/Low Settings privilege, which allows you to access the High/Low Settings pop-up, where you can add, view, edit, and delete the setup of a multitiered approval process for meter reading exceptions. For more information, refer to the Setup Privilege Group topic in Express help.

 -
 The High/Low Settings pop-up was added, which allows you to create and manage consumption groups, exception reasons, and approval workflows. For more information, refer to the Manage Metered Utilities High/Low Settings topic in Express help.

 -
 The Meter Exceptions page was added, which displays all unposted meter readings flagged as exceptions for the selected property, utility, and billing period. You can click an exception to view and manage additional details, such as adding images, an exception reason, and history/note items. For more information, refer to the Meter Exceptions (Page) topic in Express help.

 -
 The Meter Readings Statuses page was added, which allows users designated as a Reviewer or Approver to view the meters that need a reading, review, approval, follow up, or are ready to post. For more information, refer to the Meter Readings Statuses (Page) topic in Express help.

 Get a complete view of how your entire portfolio aligns with market expectations with the new market rent tracking and reporting updates.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 Rent Manager 's market rent tools have been expanded to cover all types of rentable assets (e.g., community-owned homes, equipment, appliances). The gross potential rent (GPR) tool has also been enhanced, allowing you to set up multiple GPR configurations that tie specific income accounts to selected unit types. Additionally, each created configuration compares income and market rent separately and posts results to dedicated accounts for market rent, vacancy loss, loss to lease, and more. For more information, refer to the Asset Market Rent (Pop-Up) topic in Express help.

 Find everything you need in one place with Rent Manager Express 's new global search tool.

 Express

 The Global Search tool was added to Express , allowing you to search your entire database—from contacts and properties to transactions, service issues, and history/note items—all in one place. This update eliminates fragmented search tools and navigation frustration, while streamlining access to your information with greater control over the scope of your search. For more information, refer to the Global Search (Page) topic in Express help.

 The new Asset Vacancy by Status report allows you to evaluate how close your asset is to generating revenue through occupancy.

 Express

 The Asset Vacancy by Status report was added, which provides details on the current status of your assets, including information on revenue potential and identification of potential issues in the process of getting an asset back to market. For more information, refer to the Asset Vacancy by Status (Report) topic in Express help.

 The Apply Now feature now includes the ability to incorporate income verification with applications.

 Express Rent Manager 12 Tenant Web Access (TWA)

 The Tenant Web Access Apply Now General system web preferences were updated with income verification options, which, when enabled, requires prospects to complete an income verification as the last step of their application. Additionally, this update makes it easy to monitor the progress of the application from the Recent Online Applications dashboard tile, and removes back-and-forth communication for expired links by allowing the applicant to request a new one directly from Apply Now .

 For more information, refer to the Set Up Apply Now Application Settings topic in Express help.

 Take control of your data entry with Custom Wizards.

 Express

 The Custom Wizards tool was updated in Rent Manager Express , allowing you to create streamlined data entry processes tailored to your exact needs. With this update, you can fully customize your Express prospect add wizard workflow, guiding your team step-by-step, displaying only the essential fields for each task. You can design wizards from scratch, customize existing ones, or import wizards from Rent Manager 12 . Unleash new levels of productivity and ease for your entire team by eliminating sifting through unnecessary fields and tedious training.

 This release introduces the Prospect custom wizard, with custom wizards for Property , Unit , Asset , Move In , Move Out , and Issue coming soon.

 Improve the effectiveness of your rent quotes with the addition of other rentable items (ORI).

 Express

 The rent quotes tool was updated so you can easily track your full rent quotes, including ORIs like parking spaces, storage units, or any other rentable areas. These enhancements allow you to view all your prospects potential units and recurring and one-time charges, in one easy to use tool.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Add Rent Quote pop-up was updated with an Other Rentable Item (ORI) Unit section, which allows you to add and/or view units with a unit type marked as Other Rentable Item .

 -
 The Prospect details page's Rent Quotes tile was updated with an ORI Unit field, and the Reservation/Expected Lease Information tile was updated with ORI Unit field and ORI unit selector button.

 -
 The ORI Unit Selector pop-up was added, which allows you to select the desired ORI units to be included in rent quotes and lease renewals.

 -
 The ORIUnit and ORIProperty functions are now available for RentQuotes scripts. Additionally, the RecurringChargesList, AdditionalRecurring, QuoteList, and ReservedAdditionalRecurring functions were updated to pull ORI unit information.

 -
 The Unit Types page was updated with an Other Rentable Item column, which displays a if the unit type is marked as an Other Rentable Item .

 Transform your lease renewal process with out latest enhancements.

 Express

 The lease renewal tool was updated to include other rentable items (ORI) and rentable assets, giving your renewal workflow even more control, clarity, and organization. This powerful tool allows you to incorporate all home-type assets and rented spaces (e.g., parking spaces, storage lockers, etc.) when creating renewal offers and setting rent increases. With this enhancement, you can now create a single, all-encompassing renewal offer for each tenant, significantly improving efficiency and streamlining operations.

 For more information, refer to the Create Renewal Offers topic in Express help.

 Easily track home-type asset financials for non-community owned homes with the site classification tool updates.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The site classification tool was updated, allowing you to track financials when a home-type asset is assigned to a non-community owned homeowner status. This improvement allows you to continue recording late bills, sales commissions, and final transactions even after a home is sold. As a result, Rent Manager maintains accurate financial records while keeping the home’s setup aligned with its current ownership status. Additionally, the Move Out wizard's homeowner status options were updated to better support scenarios where a tenant assumes ownership of the home.

 The Vacancy report includes updates to analyze occupancy trends and make informed operational decisions.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The Vacancy report was updated with an Include Site Classification report option, which, when enabled, adds a Site Classification column and a Site Classification Breakdown subreport. The subreport displays unit counts and vacancy percentages per classification, improving visibility into occupancy and vacancy trends. Additionally, the report can be organized/sorted by Site Classification short name. For more information, refer to the Vacancy (Report) topic in Express help.

 Apply Now templates were updated with expanded pet matching options.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 Apply Now templates were updated, allowing you to match Pet fields to a specific pet (i.e., Pet 1 , Pet 2 , Pet 3 , etc.).
