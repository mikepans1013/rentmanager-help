 New Features

 Effortlessly manage your technicians' locations and improve response times with the new Service Tech Map enhancements!

 Express rmAppSuite Pro

 The Service Tech map was updated, allowing you to locate your technicians on demand and manage service issues more efficiently. Additionally, your maintenance techs can easily toggle their availability with the new rmAppSuite Pro On Duty feature, which can also be automated based on predefined schedules.

 As a part of these updates, the following changes were made in Rent Manager Express :

 -
 The rmAppSuite Pro Service system preferences were updated with an Allow techs to be located while On Duty option, which allows your maintenance techs to indicate when they are on duty in rmAppSuite Pro . Once the technician is on duty, you can locate them on the Service Tech Map . Additionally, enabling this system preference allows you to customize the name for the On Duty field in rmAppSuite Pro .

 -
 The Service Tech Map has the following new and updated tabs:

 Tab
 Description

 Locate Techs

 This tab was added to help you quickly find and assign the nearest technician to both existing and emergency service issues.

 Current Issues

 This tab was updated to provide a comprehensive view of each of your technician’s assigned tasks and schedule, streamlining route planning and day-to-day operations.

 Check-In Review

 This tab was added to help you monitor and verify the accuracy of your technician check-ins and check-outs with acceptable distance thresholds.

 For more information, refer to the Service Tech Map topic in Express help.

 Additionally, the following changes were made in rmAppSuite Pro :

 -
 When the Allow techs to be located while On Duty system preference is enabled, an On Duty toggle displays at the top of the rmAppSuite Pro home screen for your maintenance technicians.

 -
 The Settings screen in rmAppSuite Pro was updated with an On Duty Schedule option, which allows technicians to create a schedule that automatically turns location tracking on or off.

 -
 A green indicator dot displays on all screens in rmAppSuite Pro when the tech is On Duty .

 Easily bill for in-house maintenance with the new option to establish your maintenance company as a vendor in Rent Manager .

 Express Rent Manager 12

 If you use Rent Manager to track the financials and services provided by your in-house maintenance company, the Service Issues General system preferences were updated with an In-House Maintenance Company section. When the option is enabled, you can create a vendor bill from a work order for your in-house maintenance technicians using the item's Sale Price .

 Check Set an in-house maintenance company and select a Vendor from the drop-down list so, when new vendor bills are added to work order line items associated with the selected vendor, the in-house maintenance company is billed using the Sale Price instead of the base Cost . For more information, refer to the Service Issue General Options (System Preferences) topic in Express help.

 You now have more control over how rent amounts are calculated for your renewal increases and offers.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The Set Renewal Rent Increase , Rounding Rules , and Lease Term Rent Offset pop-ups' Round to Nearest field was updated, allowing you to select a whole number from a drop-down menu that correlates with new, secondary Dollars or Cents options. For example, select the value to use for rounding ( 1 , 5 , 10 , 25 , or 50 ) in the first drop-down, then select the monetary unit ( Dollars or Cents ) to use in the second drop-down.

 If you select Cents , calculations include a thousandth decimal place used for rounding. For example, if the calculation produces a value of $425.766, and you selected Round Down round to the nearest 1 Cents , the calculated value is $425.76.
