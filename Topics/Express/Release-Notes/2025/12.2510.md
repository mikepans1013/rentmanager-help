# 12.251000 Changelog

Source: https://rmxhelp.rentmanager.com/Topics/Express/Release-Notes/2025/12.2510.htm

The release begins rolling out on October 27, 2025.

 New Features

 Say goodbye to missed requests, delayed readings, and prolonged unit turnover with off-cycle meter readings.

 Express Rent Manager 12 rmAppSuite Pro

 The off-cycle meter readings tool was added, which allows you to instantly trigger a utility meter read when residents move in or out, or a meter is swapped. As soon as the request is submitted, your maintenance tech is automatically notified to capture the meter reading in rmAppSuite Pro . In addition, this new tool provides the ability to select the type of reading, improving data integrity, reducing operational overhead, and enhancing tenant confidence in their usage data. With off-cycle meter readings, everyone stays in sync, readings happen faster, and the whole turnover process (move ins, move outs, billing, and resident communication) becomes smoother and less error-prone.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Move In wizard was updated with a Move In Reading tab, which displays when the Last meter reading was posted for the unit and allows you to request a move in meter reading and which utility you would like to request an off-cycle reading for.

 -
 The Select Off-Cycle Reading Method pop-up was added, which allows you to select the user(s) who should be notified for an off-cycle read request and set its Due Date .

 -
 The Meter Readings page was updated, allowing you to easily track and manage reading types and statuses.

 -
 The Move Out wizard was updated with a Move Out Reading tab, which displays when the Last meter reading was posted for the unit and allows you to request a move out meter reading and which utility you would like to request an off-cycle reading for.

 -
 The Off-Cycle Readings dashboard tile was added, which displays all pending off-cycle readings, the type of reading, the name of the utility, and due date.

 Additionally, the Metered Utilities (MU) tool received several updates in Rent Manager 12 . These updates have improved navigation and added or repositioned sections, filters, and fields to simplify off-cycle meter readings.

 Coming soon to rmAppSuite Pro , the ability for your technicians to receive and view meter reading requests, perform the reading, and send them directly to Rent Manager .

 Keep your utility billing precise and transparent with meter swaps.

 Express Rent Manager 12 rmAppSuite Pro

 The meter swap tool was added, which allows you to accurately track every meter change and bill without gaps. Whether it's swapping out a faulty meter, installing a smart upgrade, or preparing for a new tenant, Rent Manager 's meter swap eliminates the need to manually track two sets of readings—one from the meter being removed, and one from the new meter. In addition, you can now link meter swaps directly to service issues, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks. With meter swaps, you can say good bye to mid-cycle changes leading to billing errors, unclear usage, and dissatisfied tenants.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Meter Readings and the Meter Readings Setup pages were updated with a Swap Meter row action, which allows you to record and assign a meter swap, ensuring your billing and consumption records stay accurate and transition smoothly from the old meter to the new meter. Additionally, both pages were updated to make tracking the status of the swap (pending or complete) and if it's linked to a service issue a breeze.

 -
 The Swap Meter wizard was added, which allows you to enter current and new meter information and create a service issue for the meter swap with just a few clicks.

 -
 The Issue details page was updated with a option to Link Meter Swap , which allows you to select which utility you would like to swap the meter for.

 -
 The Utility Consumption , Utility Tenant Consumption , Utility Historical Consumption , Utility Consumption with Ranges , and Utility Reconciliation reports were updated with Swap subsections, which display data from both the old and new meters after the swap.

 Additionally, the Metered Utilities (MU) tool received several updates in Rent Manager 12 . These updates have improved navigation and added or repositioned sections, filters, and fields to simplify the meter swap process.

 Coming soon to rmAppSuite Pro , the ability for your technicians to receive and view meter swaps tied to service issues and track the maintenance costs associated with the swap.

 Enhancements

 Make your meter readings smarter and tracking seamless with Rent Manager 's metered utility estimate and rollover enhancements.

 Express Rent Manager 12 rmAppSuite Pro

 The utility billing tool has been updated, allowing you to instantly estimate unreadable meters with standardized, administrator-defined methods—ensuring every estimate aligns, audits run smoothly, and surprises disappear. Get auto-warnings before repeated estimates stack up, confirm every meter rollover with a prompt, and validate negative readings for situations like meter resets or corrections.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Setup privilege group was updated with a Manage Meter Estimates Settings privilege.

 -
 The Meter Estimates pop-up was added, which allows you to enable meter estimates, select the estimate methods used to automatically calculate the estimated reading, and establish the number of consecutive estimates allowed before users are warned.

 -
 The Meter Readings page was updated with new fields and icons, simplifying the estimate and rollover process.

 -
 The Manage Consumption Groups pop-up was updated with an Include range for negative consumption field, which allows you to add negative values to the Consumption Ranges section.

 -
 The Utility Consumption , Utility Tenant Consumption , Utility Historical Consumption , Utility Consumption with Ranges , and Utility Reconciliation report's Current Reading values were updated to display ( Est. ) when the meter reading was estimated and ( Rollover ) when the reading was part of a rollover.

 Coming soon to rmAppSuite Pro , the ability for your technicians to estimate unreadable meters with a single click. Rent Manager handles the calculation automatically based on your configuration methods. If the estimate count exceeds the value determined on the Meter Estimates pop-up, a warning displays. Additionally, the Meter Readings screen will be updated to visually separate standard, rollover, and negative readings.

 Get high-level tracking and reporting for your manufactured housing properties with the new site classification tool updates.

 Express Rent Manager 12

 The site classification tool was updated for manufactured housing-type properties, enhancing site classification connectivity, streamlining data entry, and introducing customizable scripting and reporting. These new updates allow you to easily future-date and update homeowner statuses, avoid double-booking by reserving both the home and lot for a prospect, and effortlessly record RV information for prospective tenants. Additionally, these new upgrades ensure that ownership records and occupancy types remain accurate and audit-ready through enhanced tracking and reporting.

 As part of this update, the following changes were made:

 -
 The Add Homeowner Status and Homeowner Status Details pop-ups were updated to allow for future dating.

 -
 The Prospect details page and Add Prospect wizard were updated, allowing you to reserve both the home and lot for a prospect, guaranteeing placement and preventing double booking. In addition, you can now record RV information for prospective tenants, guaranteeing compliance and streamlining resource planning.

 -
 The Move In , Add Tenant , and Transfer a Tenant wizards and the Add Lease pop-up were updated, allowing you to set the Homeowner Status and other key details of a tenant's home at move in and when adding a new lease. Additionally, the Move In , Add Tenant , and Transfer a Tenant wizards now allow you to track the period of time rent is not being charged for a site to a lender or dealer for a promotion, establishing accurate financial reporting and budgeting.

 -
 The Move Out wizard was updated, allowing you to track if a home is in a pending ownership change and log the type of occupant in the home (e.g., general renter, employee, lender, dealer).

 -
 The Transfer a Tenant wizard was updated, giving you the ability to update the Homeowner Status during a transfer, ensuring accurate home turnover records.

 -
 The Tenant details page and Lease Details pop-up were updated with Home links, allowing you to easily reference the home a tenant is leasing or owns, ensuring operational tasks are streamlined and time is not wasted searching for connections across records.

 -
 The Import Tenants and Import Prospects tools were updated, allowing you to import tenant- and prospect-owned RV data, greatly reducing the amount of time spent on data entry.

 -
 The Unit details page was updated, allowing you to view a unit's current Site Classification and easily access the Site Classification History pop-up.

 -
 The Asset details page was updated, allowing you to view an asset's current Homeowner Status and easily access the Homeowner Statuses pop-up.

 -
 The Asset/Home Profile report was updated with a streamlined design, allowing you to easily confirm the accuracy of important home details.

 -
 The Rent Roll Analysis , Market Rent Analysis , Rent Roll Comparison , Site Classification Listing , Rent Roll & Recurring Charges , and Summary Rent Roll reports were updated, allowing you to track site classifications and asset/home types.

 -
 The SiteClassification, SiteClassificationList, LoanNumber, Lienholder, HomeownerStatus, and HomeownerStatusList scripts were added, allowing you to expand your reporting and customize your letters and notices.
