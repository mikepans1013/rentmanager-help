12.1030 Release Notes

The release begins rolling out on December 12, 2023.

New Features

Please note, the IRS has lowered the e-filing requirement threshold through their IRS FIRE system from 250 returns to 10 returns for the 2023 tax year. To simplify your tax season, several exciting updates were made to the Export 1099 and Export 1098 tools.

  • Both export options were redesigned as step-by-step wizards to easily guide you through the exporting process.

  • The Export 1099 wizard was updated with two distinct workflows for owner and vendors.

  • A Getting Started section was added to both wizards, which details the setup required before you can begin the export process.

  • The Export 1099 Summary and Export 1098 Summary reports were added, which provide a breakdown of the options you used to generate the export file.
